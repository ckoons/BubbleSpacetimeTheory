# Lecture 6 — Three Generations, at the Floor

## The question

Why three copies of everything — and why can we not yet say how heavy each is?

## For the reader in a hurry

Count the ways the shape's boundary can be reached: there are three, and they are the same three however you count them, because the count is the shape's rank plus one. That is why there are three generations, and it is a theorem. Then ask the shape which generation is heaviest and by how much. We tried the obvious mechanism — a thermal ordering — and computed it two ways with the answer hidden. It came out exactly backwards. Then we proved that no mechanism of that whole family can work, because the very thing that defines the three strata makes every such weight zero or infinite on them. So the *count* is derived and the *masses* are at a floor: closed by a theorem, reopenable only by an idea of a kind we can name.

## What this lecture will derive (from the register — prose to be written)

1. The count: orbit-indexed ⟺ AV-invariant ⟹ swept; frame-indexed ⟺ Weyl-covariant ⟹ exactly degenerate; $r + 1 = 3$ classes against $r = 2$ slots. One invariant read twice.
2. $m_1 = 0$ as the same theorem's corollary; the $\nu_R$ pair.
3. The negative, told as the result it is: the pre-registered thermal ordering, two independent computations, identical exact triples $(0, 9/4, 25/4) = \nu^2$, the strict reverse of the frozen Šilov-first order. It could have succeeded and did not.
4. The floor theorem: the strata are *defined* by the degeneration of the invariant measure, so any weight derived from that measure is $0$ or $\infty$ on them. Not "we have not found the weight" — "no weight of this family exists." The door: a weight not derived from the invariant measure, and whoever brings one must say what it is.
5. Koide's relation: its source is provably under-hierarchical; closed permanently.

## What would make this lecture wrong

A fourth generation would falsify item 1 outright; it is excluded to high confidence by the $Z$ width, which is the kind of falsifier we like. The floor cannot be "falsified"; it can be *opened*, by a named weight source, and we have said what kind.
