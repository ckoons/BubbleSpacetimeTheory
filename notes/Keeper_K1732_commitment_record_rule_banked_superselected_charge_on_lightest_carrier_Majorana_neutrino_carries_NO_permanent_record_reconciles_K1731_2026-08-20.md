## The rule (banked, Derived-given-BST)
**A particle carries a permanent commitment record IFF the record is a superselected charge AND the particle is the lightest carrier of that charge** (nothing to decay into that preserves the charge). This unifies the proton and the electron — stable for the *same* reason: sitting at the bottom of a superselected ladder. Permanence is a property of the **charge, not the particle.**
