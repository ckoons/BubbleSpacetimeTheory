## CMB discipline: the prediction must be BLIND-PRE-REGISTERED (why this target is uniquely dangerous)
Casey, 2026-07-27: *"The CMB is like the bible — you can quote it to say anything."* Exactly. The CMB is the most dangerous evidence source in the program: many low-significance anomalies (~2–3σ), each post-hoc interpretable, with a huge look-elsewhere effect. So a *fit* to some anomaly is worth nothing — the CMB's degrees of freedom guarantee you can always find one. **The ONLY honest path to CMB evidence is blind pre-registration, the same discipline that governed the threshold (§105), E₀ (Fernando–Günaydin), and the generation invariant (§110):**
- BEFORE looking at the data, the competition dynamics commits — in writing — the SPECIFIC predicted signal: which observable (e.g. bubble-collision circular scars), the angular scale, the number density, the morphology, and **what would FALSIFY it.**
- THEN compare. A hit on a pre-registered signal is real evidence; a hit found by scanning anomalies is confirmation bias wearing a result's clothes.
- **Retrofitting flags (reject the "evidence" if any occur):** choosing which anomaly to match after seeing it; tuning the predicted angular scale/amplitude to a known feature; counting a marginal, unpredicted anomaly as confirmation; reading the sign of a result off the data rather than off the dynamics.
- Corollary: the fluctuation-operator computation (which geometries are unstable) must be done FIRST and independently — it is the source of the pre-registered prediction. The prediction flows dynamics → CMB, never CMB → dynamics.
