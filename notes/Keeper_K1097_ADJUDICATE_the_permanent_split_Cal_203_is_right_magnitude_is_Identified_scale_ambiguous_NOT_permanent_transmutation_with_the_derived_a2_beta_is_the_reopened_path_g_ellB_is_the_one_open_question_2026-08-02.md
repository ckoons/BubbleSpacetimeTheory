# K1097 — Adjudicating "Identified-permanent": Cal §203 is right; the magnitude is Identified, forced-μ open

## The split (my job to rule)
- **Grace + Elie:** banked "Identified-permanent."
- **Cal §203:** rejected "permanent" — scale-ambiguous is earned, un-forcible is not shown.
- **Lyra:** "permanent" earned *iff* BST does not force the boundary coupling g(ℓ_B).
- **Keeper (K1096, earlier today):** already retracted "permanent" per K1073 (Casey-driven).

## Ruling: Cal §203 is right
**The magnitude is IDENTIFIED (scale-ambiguous) — earned.** ζ(0)=−0.7691≠0 proves a genuine scale anomaly: ρ_Λ runs with the subtraction scale μ. That half is computed and solid; all five of us agree on it.

**"PERMANENT" is NOT earned.** The decisive distinction (Cal's, and it's correct):
> **free-scale** (the ambiguity *exists*) **≠ un-forcible** (no mechanism *fixes* it).

A scale anomaly is exactly the **precondition dimensional transmutation uses** to *produce* a forced scale: μ ~ exp(−∫dg/β) (Coleman-Weinberg). So ζ(0)≠0 is the **raw material for forcing μ**, not evidence that μ can't be forced. Declaring the magnitude *permanently* un-derivable claims something the computation does not show — the strict-pessimism mirror of declaring it Derived. Both need proof; neither has it.

## Two steelmen of "permanent," both rejected
**(a) Grace: "it needs ℓ_B, so it's permanent."** The magnitude factors as (ℓ_B⁻⁴ dimensionful UNIT) × (exp(−S) dimensionless SUPPRESSION). Needing ℓ_B is true **only of the unit factor** — every dimensionful quantity needs a unit, so this can't be what "permanent" means (else every magnitude is permanent). The **meaningful** object is the dimensionless suppression exp(−S) (the ~122 orders / the −280 exponent) — that is what a derivation *produces*, and it is forcible-in-principle via transmutation.

**(b) Grace: "the forced-μ path is closed because the b=0 α-tower isn't the vacuum."** The α⁵⁶ **form** (one candidate *value* for ρ_Λ) used the b=0 tower and is downgraded — true. But transmutation μ~exp(−∫dg/β) is a **separate mechanism** that does not route through the b=0 vacuum tower. **One candidate-form closing ≠ the path closing.** (This is exactly K1073's "old forms become testable candidates for ρ_Λ(μ_forced)": the α⁵⁶ candidate weakened, the *mechanism* did not.)

## Grace credited (progression, not flip-flop)
Grace is right that her K1073 retraction had two blockers and one genuinely resolved: **blocker-1 (a₅ uncomputed) → now computed** (ζ(0)=−0.7691). That's real progress and I bank it. Only **blocker-2 (forced-μ path)** remains — and it is *open*, not closed, per the above. So the tier didn't drift back by convenience; it advanced by one blocker, and the remaining one is correctly named.

## Lyra's condition is the reopened forward lane
Lyra named the single load-bearing condition exactly: **"permanent" iff BST does not force the boundary coupling g(ℓ_B).** I rule that condition **OPEN** — the infinite-volume argument stays *calibrated, not banked* (K1095), and transmutation is a live mechanism that could force it. So we cannot bank "permanent"; we hold "Identified, forced-μ open."

**And here is the key connection that makes it a real lane, not a hope:** the **a₂ rung → strong-sector β-function is already Derived** (Grace's own tier-split bridge). The transmutation integral ∫dg/β uses **that derived β**. So μ is determined **once g(ℓ_B) is fixed.** The whole promotion of the cc-magnitude reduces to one target-blind question:

> **Does BST force the boundary coupling g(ℓ_B)?**
> - **Forced** → μ forced → **magnitude Derived** (and may land target-blind on the old exp(−280)=2^{N_c}·n_C·g — vindicating that banked form).
> - **Unforced** → magnitude stays **Identified** (free boundary value).

This is exactly the derivation path Casey asked for ("what can we do to derive lambda?"), and it's now well-posed: a Derived β + one open boundary coupling.

## Honest tier for Casey's sign-off
**cc-magnitude = IDENTIFIED (scale-ambiguous), forced-μ OPEN via transmutation — NOT "permanent."** Structure Derived (det Δ_full → Jordan norm) unchanged.
