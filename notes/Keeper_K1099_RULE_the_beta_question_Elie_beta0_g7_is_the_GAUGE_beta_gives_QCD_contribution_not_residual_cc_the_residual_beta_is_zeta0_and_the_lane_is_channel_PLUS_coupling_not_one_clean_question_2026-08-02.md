# K1099 — Rule the β: the gauge β₀=7 gives the QCD contribution, not the residual cc

## The error propagated (now into a concrete setup)
K1098 flagged that the vacuum transmutation β is not the strong-sector a₂. It didn't fully land — **Elie "set β" at β₀=g=7** (the a₂ gauge slope, S=2π/(β₀·g(ℓ_B))) and **Lyra reasoned about g(ℓ_B) as "α_s run to Planck."** Both put the strong/gauge-sector β behind the vacuum transmutation. Ruling it now, precisely.

## Ruling: β₀=7 is the wrong β (contribution, not residual)
The observed cc is the **tiny residual after cancellation** among all sector contributions:
- QCD contribution ~Λ_QCD⁴ ~ **exp(−182)** (density) — a large individual term.
- Observed residual cc ~ **exp(−283)** (density) — ~100 orders smaller.

**β₀=g=7 governs the GAUGE COUPLING → produces Λ_QCD → the QCD CONTRIBUTION** to the vacuum, one of the large individual terms — **NOT the residual observed cc.** Using it conflates a sector contribution with the residual. The **observed residual's μ-running is governed by the WHOLE-GEOMETRY conformal anomaly = ζ(0)=−0.7691** (the a₅ term), not by any single gauge sector's β₀. So:
- **Elie:** the β integral uses the **vacuum ζ(0)-β**, not β₀=7.
- **Lyra:** g(ℓ_B) is the **vacuum-sector coupling** at ℓ_B, not the strong α_s (so "α_s run to Planck" isn't the right prior — that's the gauge sector).

## Self-own (my K1098 reasoning was imprecise)
K1098 said "wrong by ~11 orders" and compared the cc scale to Λ_QCD as a naive gap. The **precise** statement is **contribution-vs-residual**: β₀=7 produces a large individual sector term; the residual cc's β is ζ(0). The conclusion (β=ζ(0)) holds; the reasoning is now correct.

## Un-over-compress (calibrate both ways, on my own framing)
I've called this promotion "**ONE clean question — does BST force g(ℓ_B)? — with the β already Derived.**" That's too tidy, and I should hold my own compression to the same bar I hold others'. Honestly:
- **Transmutation itself is a CANDIDATE mechanism** (Cal/Lyra's proposal), not established — K1067 warned the exponent-form route is target-aware/unwinnable, and the transmutation mechanism has not been shown to be *the* one that sets the residual cc.
- **β=ζ(0) is the *principled* channel**, but the **channel selection** (which coupling's transmutation fixes the residual vacuum scale) retains openness alongside the **boundary value g(ℓ_B)**.
- So the honest lane is: **"Identified, forced-μ open via a candidate transmutation mechanism, principled channel β=ζ(0), open boundary coupling g(ℓ_B)"** — **two partly-open pieces (channel + coupling), not one settled-β-plus-one-question.**
