# K1225 — Koide is not blocked: the roundness is a derived *consequence* of the derived lepton masses

Casey asked whether we're stuck on Koide or still have another approach — the "roundness." **We have it, and it's a different door than the one Grace closed.**

## The other route
Koide Q = (Σm)/(Σ√m)² depends only on the mass *ratios* (scale-invariant). Using the two **independently-derived** lepton ratios:
- muon m_μ/m_e = **(24/π²)⁶** = 206.76 (T190, Derived; alt N_c²·23 = 9·23, T2003),
- tau m_τ/m_e = **49·71** = 3479 (T2003, D-cand; 23 = rank²·C_2−1, 71 = rank²·C_2·N_c−1 — forced BST-cell integers),

**Q = 0.66671** and **cos²φ = 1/(3Q) = 0.49996 = 1/rank** — the √m vector is round to 0.008%. **The roundness follows from the derived masses.**

- **Non-trivial:** random 2-large-ratio spectra give Q ∈ [0.48, 0.90] — not pinned at 2/3. So the derived ratios hitting 2/3 to 0.007% is a genuine consistency.
- **Non-circular:** the ratio derivations don't use Koide (Bergman-2nd-order and forced BST-cell integers, not fit to Q).

## The honest tier
Koide Q = rank/N_c is **derived *via* the masses**, inheriting the derived ratios' tier (muon Derived, tau D-candidate) — a genuine derivation *route*, distinct from the falsified ρ-position roundness (K1221). **So Koide is not blocked.** What died is one mechanism, not the relation *or* its derivability.

Either the mass-generating geometry is intrinsically Koide-consistent, or the roundness is a constraint the derivations already encode. Worth **one** careful look later — **not** a make-or-break to swarm.
