# K1003 — The pre-registered scorecard (committed before the numbers)

Companion to K1002. The blind bar is committed; this pins the concrete targets and — the load-bearing honesty point — **separates what the fire actually proves from what it merely re-confirms.**

## ★★ THE EVIDENTIARY-VALUE RULE (read first)
Most of the ~22 outputs are **ALREADY banked as separate BST derivations** (m_s/m_d=20, V_us=1/√20, m_c=α·v/√2, the individual quark masses, sin²θ₁₃=3/137, etc.). So when the ONE kernel fires and reproduces them:
- **Reproducing a banked number = a CONSISTENCY check, NOT new evidence.** It shows the one matrix is consistent with the piecemeal derivations. Real, but not 22 new confirmations.
- **The genuine new evidence is TWO things:** (1) **UNIFICATION** — that they ALL fall out of ONE object with no per-observable dial (the "one matrix, not nine derivations" claim); (2) **any output not previously derived that falls out blind.**
- **Do NOT let the fire be reported as "22 blind predictions confirmed."** It is "our piecemeal results are mutually consistent under one matrix" + "N genuinely new outputs." Name N explicitly.

## ★★ THE SHARPEST BLIND TESTS (least-pinned outputs — where the fire earns or fails)
- **δ_CKM and δ_PMNS (CP phases):** currently only IDENTIFIED, and **δ_PMNS is a MISS** (banked N_c·π/g ≈ 1.35 rad vs observed ~3.44 rad). The kernel produces the phases from the off-diagonal binomial structure. **If δ_PMNS falls out near 3.44 blind → strong new evidence. If it reproduces the 1.35 miss → honest, report the miss.** This is the single sharpest discriminator: the kernel can't have been tuned to it (we don't even match it now).
- **The joint off-diagonal texture:** the mixing angles come out *together* from one off-diagonal table, not fitted one-by-one. Whether the whole CKM+PMNS texture is self-consistent (unitarity, the Gatto-type relations) is genuinely new.
- **sin²θ₂₃ vs π/4:** observed 0.572 (upper octant); does the kernel force maximal (0.5) or the observed 0.572? The duality claims θ₂₃=π/4 — if the kernel gives 0.5 and data is 0.572, that's a ~3σ tension to state, not bury.
