## The premise correction (the load-bearing point)
**Global energy conservation is not a law of general relativity.** By Noether, energy conservation is the conserved charge of **time-translation symmetry**; a dynamical/expanding spacetime has no global timelike Killing vector, so **there is no globally conserved total energy and no global ledger.** Standard, measured:
- **Cosmological redshift** — CMB photons lose energy as space expands; not conserved.
- **The cosmological constant** — constant density × growing volume ⟹ total vacuum energy **grows** (dU = −p dV, p = −ρ ⟹ dU = +ρ dV > 0). Expansion "creates" energy against negative pressure.

The only surviving law in curved dynamical spacetime is the **local** one, ∇_μT^{μν} = 0 — **automatic** from the Bianchi identity ∇_μG^{μν} ≡ 0 once Einstein's equation holds. It governs the **local** exchange of energy-momentum between matter and geometry; it asserts no fixed global total.
