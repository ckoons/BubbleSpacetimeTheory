# K1028 — Adversarial audit of K1026: it leans against my own candidate

A found-result of the auditor's gets the hardest scrutiny. I stress-tested K1026 (θ₂₃=4/7 via the Grimus–Lavoura sum rule) trying to KILL it, and it doesn't survive cleanly.

## ★ Test 1 — the sum rule is NON-trivial (K1026 not empty)
cos2θ₂₃ = sinθ₁₃·cosδ **fails for 6/6 random PMNS matrices** (diffs 0.14–0.64). So it's a genuine constraint, not a general identity — K1026 isn't vacuous. Good so far.

## ★★ Test 2 — but it assumes a symmetry BST does NOT have
Exact **μ-τ REFLECTION** symmetry (S₂₃ + CP) forces **maximal θ₂₃ AND δ=±90° (cos δ=0)** — verified (6/6 random reflection-symmetric M_ν give θ₂₃=45°, |sin δ|=1). The Grimus–Lavoura sum rule is the *linearized statement of how breaking μ-τ reflection shifts θ₂₃ and δ together.*
- **BST has cos δ = −3√5/7, |cos δ| = 0.96 — FAR from 0.** So **BST is NOT near the μ-τ-reflection point** (which requires cos δ ≈ 0).
- **BST's maximal θ₂₃ comes from μ-τ EXCHANGE (the Shilov ℤ₂ swap) and Elie's PARITY theorem — NOT μ-τ reflection.** Exchange/parity force maximal θ₂₃ but do **not** constrain δ (so δ~180° is fine).
- **Therefore the reflection-breaking sum rule likely does NOT apply to BST**, and the clean **cos2θ₂₃ = −1/g (√5 cancellation) is more likely a numerical COINCIDENCE** of BST's independently-derived θ₁₃ and δ than a forced derivation.

## ★ Honest downgrade (calibrate both directions)
- **K1026 → downgraded from "strong candidate" to "likely coincidence."** The 4/7 is seductive (clean √5 cancellation, matches data, matches Route 1's 2√3), but the *mechanism* it invokes (the sum rule) requires μ-τ reflection, which BST's δ~180° contradicts. No-wave-through-on-a-perfect-number: the clean number confirms consistency, not the sourcing mechanism.
- **K1025 stands: θ₂₃ = maximal DOUBLY-DERIVED (exchange + parity); the small observed deviation is genuinely NOT forced.** This is the honest position, and both papers use it correctly.
- **The one thing that could revive K1026:** if BST's M_ν turns out to have a specific μ-τ-*broken* structure where θ₁₃, δ, and the θ₂₃-shift all come from ONE breaking (not reflection, but a variant that reproduces the sum rule). That's a real M_ν-structure question — **Lyra's final verdict**, not mine.
