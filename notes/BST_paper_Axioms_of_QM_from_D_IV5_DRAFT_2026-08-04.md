## 1. The claim

Quantum mechanics is usually handed to a student as a list of postulates — a Hilbert space, self-adjoint observables, unitary evolution, the Born rule, measurement collapse — to be granted, not explained. We show that on a single geometry, the rank-2 bounded symmetric domain **D_IV⁵ = SO(5,2)/[SO(5)×SO(2)]**, most of that list stops being postulates and becomes features of the shape. We keep an explicit scorecard, because the value of the claim is the honesty of the boundary: which axioms are *derived*, which is an *honest posit*, and exactly what would close the gap.

## 3. The input floor, benchmarked against GR

BST is not zero-input, and we state the floor plainly, the way general relativity states Newton's constant. What is remarkable is the *shortness* of the list:

- **One framework premise:** spacetime is the domain's conformal descent (SO(5,2) → SO(3,1)).
- **Two dimensionful scales:** the substrate tick and the cosmic age. (Every dimensionless quantity is independent of both.)
- **Boundary data:** per-instance initial conditions and single measurement outcomes — data every theory takes as given (GR evolves its Cauchy slice; it does not derive it).

Against this floor, **the entire dimensionless structure of the Standard Model and of quantum mechanics is forced with zero dimensionless free parameters.** The input list is *shorter* than GR's (which takes G, the metric signature, and the full initial data). The honest floor is a strength, not an apology.

## 4. The one posit — and the question that would close it

Item 10 is the whole gap, and it is sharply isolated. The mathematical scaffolding is forced: the superalgebra closes (the requirement that {Q,Q} close forces an su(2)_R and assembles the exceptional F(4), with the coupling set by N_c), so the domain genuinely carries a ℤ₂ super-grading with the geometry in the even sector and a spinor in the odd sector. What no computation settles is the *physical identification* — that the substrate's matter genuinely **is** the F(4) superpartner of its geometry. That is interpretive. And it is *operator-level*: it predicts **no** superpartner particles — BST's standing no-SUSY-spectrum prediction is untouched.

So the finish line for a posit-free "QM from one geometry" is a single, well-posed question: **why is matter fermionic — is physical matter the odd sector of the domain's super-grading, forced, or is that an identification we supply?** Until that closes, the honest scorecard is **nine derived, one posit** — and we say so.

## 5. What we do not claim

- **Not "all the axioms."** Item 10 is a posit; we mark it.
- **Not "measurement is solved."** The measurement *process and odds* are derived (item 8); the *single outcome* is a boundary datum — given, as every theory takes its initial data. The over-claim we refuse is that the specific outcome, or the fact of quantum randomness, is explained away; it is physical and irreducible.
- **Not "CPT is distinctive."** CPT holds, but it is universal (any connected/Lorentz theory has it); only uncertainty (the −2/g curvature) is distinctive among the support axioms.

**The claim, exactly:** most of the axioms of quantum mechanics are features of one shape — nine of ten derived on a floor shorter than general relativity's — and the tenth is a single, named, well-posed question. That is a stronger and more checkable statement than "quantum mechanics is derived," and it is the true one.
