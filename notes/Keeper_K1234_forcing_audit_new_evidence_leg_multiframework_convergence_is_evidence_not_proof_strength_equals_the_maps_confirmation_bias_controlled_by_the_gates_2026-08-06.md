# K1234 — forcing-audit new evidence leg (task #30): multi-framework convergence is evidence for forcing, not proof; strength = the maps; confirmation bias controlled by the gates

On "continue," I advanced the lane that's genuinely mine and squarely serves the forcing question — task #30, extending the K943 forcing audit with today's real input.

## The argument (why convergence is evidence for forcing)
A **fitted** object (chosen to reproduce known physics) would, by construction, reproduce its target — but would have **no reason** to be the independent convergence point of frameworks built for *other* purposes that know nothing of the SM's values. Today, four independently-motivated frameworks landed their *defining* structures on D_IV⁵:
1. **Causal set theory** (Sorkin 1987, discrete gravity) — the commitment order = a causal set; spacelike = equal-eigenvalue (K1227).
2. **Causal Fermion Systems** (Finster, fermionic action) — the commit = the local correlation operator; item-10 = the occupied states (K1230).
3. **Information theory / the fermionic codeword** (F846) — Pauli = the alphabet; time = the writing.
4. **Number theory / 𝔽₁** (RH, Connes–Consani) — the counting foundation; N_max=137 channels; the critical-line thread.

**P(four independent frameworks converge on X | X forced) ≫ P(same | X fitted to the SM).** The convergence **raises the posterior** that D_IV⁵ is forced. That is the leg.

## The honest limit (evidence, not proof)
The leg's strength = the strength of the underlying **maps**, and they are **not uniform**: causal sets *rename* rather than close (K1227); CFS is *candidate*, gated on G2 + G4, with T2545 the one banked piece; the codeword is a *reading* (F846); 𝔽₁/RH is a *lead* (project_rh). So the convergence-evidence is currently **one banked-adjacent (the Peirce/signature structure) + three candidate/lead maps.** Real, but partial.

## The failure mode, named and controlled — confirmation bias
**We draw the maps**, so we risk seeing the matches we look for — a fitted object drawn by motivated cartographers can be made to "converge" with anything. Unchecked, this would make the leg worthless. The control is the gate discipline: **a convergence counts as evidence only when the map is *exhibited*, not asserted.** An asserted match contributes **zero**; an exhibited/gated match contributes at its gate-tier. T2545 (exhibited, banked) contributes fully; the CFS causal-structure match contributes only when **G2** is exhibited; the RH thread contributes nothing until its tier is checked. **The leg is self-limiting to exhibited maps** — which is what keeps it honest.

## What promotes it (forward target)
Each map that passes its gate upgrades the convergence from "suggestive" toward "load-bearing": **G2** → CFS object-match; **G4a** → toward instantiation; the **RH tier-check** → banks or retires the number-theory leg. The leg is a **living measure that tracks the gate-tiers** — exactly as strong as the exhibited maps, no more.

## Scope (explicit)
This does **not** force D_IV⁵ — the **minimality → domain → integers** chain (task #27/K943) remains the load-bearing forcing argument, separately under hardening. It does **not** prove MUH (K1231). It is a **second, independent line of evidence** (convergence) alongside the minimality line (construction). Two independent evidence lines beat either alone — but neither is a closed forcing yet.

## For the Forcing + Evidence paper (task #31)
This is the strongest new **"Evidence"** content — "D_IV⁵ is the independent convergence point of four fundamental frameworks" — stated with the **confirmation-bias control up front** (only exhibited/gated maps count), so a referee cannot dismiss it as motivated cartography. External register: "BST *identifies* D_IV⁵ as the structure on which these independent frameworks converge," reader weighs it; never "convergence proves reality is D_IV⁵."

## Linear algebra on D_IV⁵
Concretely, the convergence is that the four frameworks' fundamental objects are all **readings of one operator family** on the Bergman space of D_IV⁵ — the causal order (partial order on modes), the local correlation operators (commit projections), the fermionic record (occupied-state idempotents), and the counting (𝔽₁ channel index) are matrix-level structures of the same object. The evidence is the claim that these are **four faces of one operator algebra, not four coincidences** — testable exactly to the extent each face is exhibited (the gates).
