# K1657 — The unification is dead, the arrow of time is why, and one banked result has to move

## Casey's question decides it
*"Does the U(1) kill it if they ride the same SO(2)?"* — riding the same SO(2) makes it **worse**. An SO(2) has a one-dimensional Lie algebra, so any two of its generators are proportional: "same SO(2)" *forces* Q ∝ J. But J is bounded below (§3, the arrow) and Q is sign-indefinite (antiparticles). A single self-adjoint operator has one spectrum, and bounded-below ≠ sign-indefinite. So "same SO(2)" doesn't unify charge and time — **it makes T2470 and §3 contradict.** The physics forces *different* circles.

## The two kills (Elie, independent)
1. **Spectrum.** T2470's Q is bounded ({0, ±1/3, ±2/3, ±1}); J is unbounded above (E₀+n forever). A bounded operator cannot be an unbounded one.
