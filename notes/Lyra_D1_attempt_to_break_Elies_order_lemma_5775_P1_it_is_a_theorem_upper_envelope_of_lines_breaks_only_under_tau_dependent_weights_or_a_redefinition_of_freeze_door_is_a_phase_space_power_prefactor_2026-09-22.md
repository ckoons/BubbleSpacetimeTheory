## 1. The lemma holds, and here is why (no sweep needed)
Occupations f_j(τ) = w_j e^(−τE_j), w_j > 0 constant, E_e > E_μ > E_τ = 0. Leadership = argmax_j L_j(τ), L_j(τ) = ln w_j − τE_j: **three straight lines in τ with slopes −E_j.** (i) For any pair with E_k > E_j, L_j − L_k = ln(w_j/w_k) + τ(E_k − E_j) is strictly increasing, so j can overtake k and k can never overtake j. (ii) Hence the sequence of leaders of the upper envelope has strictly decreasing E — the envelope of lines is convex and its pieces are ordered by slope. (iii) The line with slope 0 (Šilov, E = 0) is therefore the terminal leader for every weight triple: once it leads it never loses the lead, and if it leads at τ = 0 it leads forever. (iv) Under Elie's definition, freeze = lose the lead, the Šilov stratum can never freeze at all, let alone first. ∎ This is the same fact as "the Legendre transform orders by slope"; it holds for any number of channels and any positive constants. **P1 cannot fail inside its stated class; the 10⁴-triple sweep confirms a theorem.** P2's weights I re-derived: r² = x₁² + x₂² for a uniform point of S⁴ ⊂ ℝ⁵ is Beta(1, 3/2); moments 1, 2/5, 8/35 exactly; the phase e^(iθ) drops out of |w₁ + i w₂|² on Š. ## 2. Where it breaks — three relaxations, each an assumption the lemma does not state
- **(A) Constant weights.** The proof needs ln(w_j/w_k)(τ) + τ(E_k − E_j) monotone, i.e. **d/dτ ln(w_j/w_k) > −(E_k − E_j) for all τ.** A τ-dependent weight ratio whose log-derivative is more negative than −ΔE at some τ lets a low-E channel lose the lead to a high-E one. The structurally live case: **power-law prefactors** w_j(τ) = c_j τ^(−a_j) — what a Boltzmann integral over a stratum of continuous phase-space dimension d_j supplies (a_j = d_j/2). Then L_τ − L_e = c + (a_e − a_τ) ln τ + (25/4)τ, which is non-monotone whenever a_τ > a_e: it decreases at small τ and increases later, so the Šilov channel can lead at early τ, LOSE the lead to e, and regain it — **Šilov loses the lead first.** The log-derivative −(a_τ − a_e)/τ diverges at τ → 0, so any a_τ > a_e, however small, breaks the lemma at early enough τ — exactly where "first to freeze" is decided. Whether such prefactors are forced is a question about the strata (their degeneracy/dimension as Boltzmann phase space), not about the weight w_k of the exhibited F323 modes, which is a constant. **This is the door.** It is computable, it is not a relabeling, and it is not in the prereg.
- **(B) The word "freeze".** Elie: freeze = lose the lead. Lecture 6's R93 biconditional identified "first to freeze" with the largest E (fastest depletion), which under (i)–(iv) is the same thing for constant weights. But a third reading is in the corpus's own words — "the strata freeze out in sequence" — and under freeze = "occupation stops changing" the E = 0 stratum is frozen from τ = 0: Šilov-first, trivially, and vacuously (it is the E-order read backwards). **Convention-collision before contradiction: the floor must pin freeze = lose-the-lead, in those words, or the next reader reopens it with a dictionary.**
- **(C) Exponential occupations with fixed E.** Running exponents E_j(τ) (a temperature-dependent spectrum) make the L_j curves; crossings then obey no slope order. Outside Lane P's scope by its own statement (E = ν² certified constant, K1827/K1828), so this is a scope line, not a door.
