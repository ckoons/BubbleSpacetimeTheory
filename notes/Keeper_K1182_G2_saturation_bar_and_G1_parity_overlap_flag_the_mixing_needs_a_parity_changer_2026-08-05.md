# K1182 — the G2 saturation bar + the G1 parity-overlap flag (the mixing needs a parity-changer)

Two Keeper items filed **before** Grace/Lyra's structural work lands — non-blocked, and both help their math.

## (A) The G2 bar — "up-wavefunctions survive saturation at {0,2,4}" (pre-committed)
**Forced only if** top-Yukawa saturation (y_t=1) acts **diagonally on the mode's norm** in the shelf basis — it rescales the {0,2,4} mode's mass-*eigenvalue* without **rotating** the mode into a combination of other shelves.
- **PASS** (addresses intact → mixing rides clean {0,2,4}): saturation shown to be a **norm rescaling** (eigenvalue effect).
- **FAIL** (Cal's "built on sand"): saturation **distorts** the wavefunction (mixes shelves) → the mixing needs the distorted wavefunctions; the clean cross-shelf fire doesn't proceed.

Forced-vs-assumed: "addresses preserved" must be a **shown** property of saturation, not adopted because it lets the fire proceed. @Grace G2 + @Lyra rule; @Cal ratifies.

## (B) The G1 parity-overlap flag (the structural issue their math will hit)
Up sits on the **even** grid {0,2,4}, down on the **odd** grid {1,3,5}. On a parity-symmetric measure, **⟨even | odd⟩ = ∫(even)(odd) = 0 by parity.** So the *naive* ⟨up|down⟩ cross-shelf overlap plausibly **vanishes** → CKM = identity, no mixing — contradicting observed Cabibbo.

**Resolution (physics — Grace/Lyra verify; I flag the structure, don't over-specify):** the physical mixing is **not** the bare overlap; it's mediated by a **charge-changing operator** — the weak charged current / the Higgs Yukawa — which carries weight and therefore **shifts parity by the notch** (δ_m). That operator **bridges** the two grids: **mixing = ⟨even | (parity-changer) | odd⟩ ≠ 0.**

**The payoff (a lead):** if the mixing is a **parity-bridged** matrix element, it's **suppressed** by the bridging-operator strength — plausibly **why CKM is small** (Lyra's near-identity, now with a mechanism: small mixing = the parity gap must be bridged). So **G1 computes the notch-operator's shelf-to-shelf matrix** (linear algebra on D_IV⁵), not the bare Gram overlap — flagged now so a "bare overlap = 0" result is read as a *feature*, not a surprise.
