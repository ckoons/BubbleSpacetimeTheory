## The structure (sourced, K950-compliant)
- **The Gram matrix is DIAGONAL.** The ψ_k carry distinct so(2)-weights (the (z₁+iz₂) direction raises the weight by 1 per rung), and modes of distinct formal-degree level are orthogonal in L² (sourced: π_k ⊥ π_l for k≠l, BST_AlphaSquared_LayerProof; formal-degree orthogonality). So ⟨ψ_j, ψ_k⟩ = δ_{jk}‖ψ_k‖², and **b = #{k∈{0,1,2} : ‖ψ_k‖² > 0}** — no off-diagonal mixing to compute, just three diagonal norms.
- **Each ‖ψ_k‖² is a Shapovalov product of linear factors.** In the contravariant form of the real form so(5,2), the norm of the k-fold raise of the ground vector is a product over the non-compact positive roots of linear factors in the weight (the standard Shapovalov/Gindikin structure; the formal degree is a degree-n_C=5 polynomial, "each non-compact positive root contributing one linear factor" — BST_AlphaS_NonperturbativeRunning). **So ‖ψ_k‖² is positive/zero/negative according to how many factors are negative** — a finite sign count, K950's b.
- **Null (‖ψ_k‖²=0) = a reduction point** (the module becomes reducible there; the rung is on the boundary of the unitary range) → does NOT count (K950). Negative → non-unitary → does NOT count.
