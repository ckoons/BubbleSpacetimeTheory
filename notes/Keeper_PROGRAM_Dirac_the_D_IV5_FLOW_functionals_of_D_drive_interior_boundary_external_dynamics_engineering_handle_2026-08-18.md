# The D_IV⁵ flow — functionals of D promote the dynamics

**Casey, 2026-08-18:** *"If we look at the functionals related to the Dirac, we will see that they promote the dynamics of the boundary/continuum. We may be able then to 'engineer' functionals for various aspects of dynamics and refine them to have better predictability — or state clearly how the D_IV⁵ internal/boundary/external flow works."*

He's right, and it's precise: on a Hermitian symmetric domain the static geometry is inert (a container), and *every* dynamical object is a **functional of D**. It **extends** the banked Tier-0 framework (it doesn't replace it); the new content is that all four legs are functionals of *one* operator.

## The engineering handle (SP-30 on a rigorous footing)
To target a dynamical aspect, **engineer the functional** `f` in the spectral action `Tr f(D/Λ)`; different cutoffs `f` weight different heat coefficients. In BST this has a physical realization we already banked: **boundary conditions on the Shilov boundary select eigenvalues** (the Casimir / BaTiO₃-137-plane spectral-engineering idea). So:
- **"engineer a functional"** = choose the cutoff `f` = impose boundary conditions that select which part of `spec(D)` contributes;
- **"refine for predictability"** = sharpen `f`/the boundary condition to *isolate one heat coefficient cleanly*, so the target quantity comes out as a number with small ambiguity;
- **the deliverable** = a table `functional → dynamical aspect → number`, which is precisely PROGRAM-DIRAC's map with the flow legs attached.
