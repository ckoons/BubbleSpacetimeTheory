## Abstract (to write) — the one-paragraph thesis
A structure that *reproduces* N physical constants may still have *fitted* them; the question is whether the physics enters **upstream** (premise) or **downstream** (output). This paper states a seventeen-rule standard that separates the two, each rule with a **worked case where it caught a real, in-progress over-claim** — most of them the authors' own. The standard is falsifiable in use: it lowered our own honest "derived" count (21→12) while accuracy never moved, which is the signature of a working instrument rather than an advocacy device.
