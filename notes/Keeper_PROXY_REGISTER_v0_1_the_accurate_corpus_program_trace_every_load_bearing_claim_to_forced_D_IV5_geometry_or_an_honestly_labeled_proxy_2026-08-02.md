# Proxy Register — the accurate-corpus program

**Principle (Casey, 2026-08-02):** *"I don't want to throw away good geometry, and if there are proxies to be tested and reconsidered that's fine. What improves BST?"* — This register is the answer. The geometry (D_IV⁵, the five integers, the forced structure) is the durable asset; a **proxy** is any place a result rests on a toy model / scalar approximation / scale estimate / imported generic-physics result *instead of* a direct linear-algebra computation on D_IV⁵. Most of the corpus is forced-geometry; the proxies are a bounded set. Find them, mark them, recompute the load-bearing ones.

## The five audit questions (per load-bearing claim)
1. **Proxy or geometry?** Direct D_IV⁵ computation, or a stand-in?
2. **Honest tier?** Target-innocent + forced (Derived), or form-selected / target-aware (Identified/Structural)?
3. **Circular?** Does its support chain loop back on itself?
4. **Current?** Falsifier / "matches observation" scrubbed against today's numbers?
5. **Consistently cited?** Superseded results propagated, notation unique, theorems registered?

## Status tags
- **CONFIRMED** — established to rest on a proxy, not the operator.
- **LIKELY** — strong reason to think it's a proxy; needs verification.
- **TO-CHECK** — flagged, disposition open.
- **RESOLVED** — was a proxy, now recomputed from D_IV⁵ (the template cases).
- **FORCED** — verified to rest on a direct D_IV⁵ computation (validated; the durable core).
