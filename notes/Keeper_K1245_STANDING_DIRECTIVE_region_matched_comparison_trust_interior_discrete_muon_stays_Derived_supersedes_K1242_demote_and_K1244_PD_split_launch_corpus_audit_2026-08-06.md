# K1245 — standing directive: region-matched comparison + trust interior/discrete; the muon lands at Derived (interior)

Casey: *"Fix any mismatched comparisons. The interior compares only to interior, boundary to boundary, external to external. Otherwise we trust interior/discrete values."*

## The rule (standing, corpus-wide)
A σ/dev comparison is valid **only within one region** — interior↔interior, boundary↔boundary, exterior↔exterior. Across regions is a **category error** (the interior→exterior projection sits between them, K1215/K1243). With no same-region comparison, **trust the interior/discrete computation.** Demote a tier **only** for a reason internal to the value's own region (e.g. a free/named **input**), **never** for a cross-region σ mismatch.

## The muon — landed (supersedes K1242 and K1244)
(24/π²)⁶ is a **forced interior computation** (Grace: form forced — 24 = Γ_Ω, exponent = C₂ π-counting; forced form ⟹ forced interior **value**), with **no free input.** Per the directive we **trust it: const_010 = Derived (interior).** Not demoted (K1242 retracted), not PD-split (K1244 superseded). The 0.004% agreement with the **exterior** measurement is **region-crossing confirmation, orthogonal to the tier** ("confirmation ⊥ tier" — the entry's own long-standing note, now vindicated). Scope kept: "Derived (interior)" means the interior value is forced; it does **not** assert equality to the exterior measurement at exterior precision (that crossing needs the projection).

## Why the muon ≠ the tau (a real distinction, not a region one)
The **tau (const_039)** is Structure-Derived because its **value carries a named input** (the 71-type factor; 49·71 was refused K989) — an **input-dependence** demotion, internal to its own region, which **stands.** The muon has no such input → Derived. The lepton sector is honestly **differentiated**, not uniform-PD.

K1242 (flat demote — wrong, cross-region σ) → K1243 (no demote — right principle) → K1244 (PD-split, Grace's ledger-read — kept the "value structural" mindset) → **K1245 (landed: trust the interior value = Derived).** Driven by Casey's principle + the team's corpus-reconnect. Q=2/3=rank/N_c is **projection-invariant** (ratio-of-ratios, the running cancels) → directly exterior-comparable → σ-robust ~1σ (Tier-1 exact). It's the one lepton observable that's *legitimately* exterior-comparable, precisely because the projection cancels.
