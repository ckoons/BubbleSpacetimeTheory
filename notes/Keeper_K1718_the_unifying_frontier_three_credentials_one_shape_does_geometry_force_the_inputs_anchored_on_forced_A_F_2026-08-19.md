# K1718 — the unifying frontier: does the geometry force the inputs? (anchored on forced A_F)

## Cal's insight (§625): three frontiers, one shape
Three credential frontiers now share a single structure:
- **AF:** does anything **fix** N_c / n_f, or are they received?
- **Koide:** does the overlap give θ **without** Q=2/3 entering?
- **Central charges:** does the geometry **fix the field content** (# scalars/fermions/vectors)?

In all three the **machinery is demonstrably right**; the open is always **whether the inputs are forced.** Every BST credential = *(machinery proven) × (inputs received)* = **credential**; each upgrades to a **prediction** exactly to the extent its inputs are geometry-forced. This is Casey's forces-first framing at the *program* scale.

## The corpus anchor (the lever)
The field content isn't a free input — it's determined by the finite spectral-triple algebra A_F, and **T2551 states A_F = ℂ⊕ℍ⊕M₃(ℂ) is forced** (the endomorphism *-algebra of BST's finite space, read off one geometry: ℍ = Schur commutant of the Sp(2) spinor, M₃ = End(V₁₂ color), ℂ = SO(2)). **Connes assumes A_F; BST forces it.** So the content that fixes a,c largely comes from a *forced* algebra — the central-charge credential is closer to a prediction than "credential" alone suggests.

## The honest decomposition (K1683 — hold the line)
A_F's **real skeleton** is forced (M₃(ℝ)→SO(3), ℍ, ℂ); the **complexification** (M₃(ℝ)→M₃(ℂ), SO(3)→SU(3), the chiral/gauge promotion) is the SM/gauge **input** (imported, #108 floor). So the content decomposes: **forced** — A_F real skeleton (T2551), N_c=3, baryon#; **imported** — the complexification/chirality, the generation count, n_f.

## The forward frontier (tomorrow, well-posed)
A systematic **forces-vs-imports audit of the SM inputs, anchored on A_F.** For a,c: how much of the content (number/type of each field) is fixed by the forced A_F vs imported by the complexification? **To the extent it's forced, "BST computes a,c" upgrades from credential to prediction.** The same audit closes the AF scope (is n_f forced?) and frames Koide.

## The deep payoff
If the forced A_F fixes the content, then the a,c credential + the AF credential + the SM spectral triple are **one forced structure** — "the geometry forces the content whose local invariants are the SM's" is a **prediction**, not bookkeeping. Single most valuable thing to establish next; it has a banked anchor.
