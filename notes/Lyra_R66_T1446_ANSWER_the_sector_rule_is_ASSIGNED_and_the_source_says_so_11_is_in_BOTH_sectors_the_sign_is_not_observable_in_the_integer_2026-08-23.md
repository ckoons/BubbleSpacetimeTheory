## 1. ★★ THE PRIMARY SOURCE CONTRADICTS THE RULE, IN ITS OWN CLOSING CLAUSE
T1446, verbatim from the registry:

> *"(a) CKM (colored, S⁴ factor): spectral modes → vacuum subtraction −1. sin θ_C = 2/√79, A = 9/11. (b) PMNS (colorless, full boundary): angular rotations → cos²θ₁₃ = 44/45 remixing. … **The number 11 = 2C₂−1 appears in both sectors (A = 9/11, 44 = rank²×11).**"*

⟹ **The applicability rule Cal demanded is not supplied by T1446.** Cal's condition was: *"the mechanism must say where the subtraction applies and where it does not, or the ± is free."* **T1446 says where the two mechanisms live. It does not say the subtraction is confined to one of them — it says the opposite.**

## 3. ★★ THE DEEPER PROBLEM: THE SIGN IS NOT OBSERVABLE IN THE INTEGER
