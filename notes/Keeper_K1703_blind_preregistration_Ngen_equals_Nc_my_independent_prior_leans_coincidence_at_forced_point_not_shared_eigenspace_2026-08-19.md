# K1703 — blind pre-registration: my read on "is N_gen = N_c forced?"

Committed **before** Grace's linear-algebra result, so my ruling on her report is honest — not swayed by whichever answer is prettier. The team wants "forced"; Cal #27 fires hardest at peak convergence, and it should fire at me too.

## The question reduces (I did the algebra)
`N_gen = rank+1` (Korányi–Wolf boundary-orbit strata, T2527); `N_c = n_C − rank = a` (short-root multiplicity = dim of the Peirce off-diagonal block V₁₂, T1930). So `N_gen = N_c ⟺ rank+1 = n_C−rank ⟺ n_C = 2·rank+1 ⟺ N_c = rank+1` (using the genus identity `n_C = rank + N_c`). **The whole question is: is `N_c = rank+1` a shared structure, or two counts coinciding at the forced point?**

## My prior — leans coincidence-at-a-forced-point (~65/35)
The two integers come from **different kinds of object**:
- `N_gen = r+1` — a **count of boundary orbits** (the rank stratification: Shilov at rank r, then lower-rank faces; r+1 pieces).
- `N_c = a = dim V₁₂` — the **dimension of one off-diagonal Peirce block** (short-root multiplicity).

A count-of-orbits and a dimension-of-a-block are different *kinds* of thing; both equalling 3 at n_C=5 is a numerical meeting, not obviously a shared eigenspace. In the rank-2 Peirce decomposition there are 6 blocks {V₀₀,V₀₁,V₀₂,V₁₁,V₁₂,V₂₂}; the generation strata and the color block V₁₂ are *different pieces* of the same decomposition, equal only in cardinality.

## Why it still matters either way
`n_C=5` and `rank=2` are **both forced** (D_IV⁵ = unique smallest-that-does-physics). So even a "coincidence" is a coincidence **at a forced point** — the physical consequence (`N_gen=N_c ⟹ matter stable`, K1700b) holds robustly given the domain. The open question is *unification depth*, not stability.

## Acceptance for Grace's report (pre-committed)
To overturn my prior and rule **forced-by-shared-structure**, she must exhibit a genuine operator whose **eigenspaces are simultaneously the rank-strata AND the short-root directions** — the *same* eigenspaces, not two objects that happen to have cardinality/dimension 3. **Dimension-match alone = coincidence** (forced ≠ meaningful; two "smallest 3"s are not one object). If she finds only dim-match → **coincidence-at-5, honestly labeled**, and the headline is "given the forced domain, matter is stable" (real) rather than "one operator forces generations = colors" (deeper, but unearned).

## The false-neighbor test I want her to run
Does `N_gen = N_c` hold at any *other* rank-r type-IV domain, or only at rank-2/n_C=5? The equality `r+1 = n_C−rank` is the generic curve `n_C = 2·rank+1` (rank=3 ⟹ n_C=7, N_gen=4=N_c, …). If the physics-selection merely *lands on* that curve, it's coincidence-riding-selection; if n_C=5 is the *only* point where the equality **and** the physics-criteria both hold, that's tighter.
