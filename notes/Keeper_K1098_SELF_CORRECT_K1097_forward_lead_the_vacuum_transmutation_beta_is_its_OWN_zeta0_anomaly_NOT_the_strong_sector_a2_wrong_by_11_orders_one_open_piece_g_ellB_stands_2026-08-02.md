# K1098 — Self-correction: the vacuum transmutation β is its own ζ(0), not the strong-sector a₂

## The catch (on my own K1097 bridge, before the team built on it)
K1097 sharpened the cc-magnitude promotion to "one open question — does BST force g(ℓ_B)? — because **∫dg/β uses the Derived a₂ strong-sector β**." I asserted that bridge; I did not verify it. Verifying it now (grep/check binds hardest on my own claims): it's **wrong by ~11 orders.**
- cc energy scale: ρ_Λ^{1/4}/M_Planck ~ 1.9×10⁻³¹ = **exp(−71).**
- QCD/a₂ transmutation scale: Λ_QCD/M_Planck ~ 1.6×10⁻²⁰ = **exp(−46).**
- The vacuum scale is **not** the strong-sector transmutation scale (differ by exp(−25) ~ 10⁻¹¹). The a₂ β generates the QCD scale, not the cosmological one.

## The correction (it strengthens the lane, doesn't weaken it)
The vacuum has its **own** scale anomaly, and its coefficient **is ζ(0) = −0.7691** — the a₅ conformal/trace-anomaly coefficient is the **vacuum-sector β-function's leading coefficient** (the analog of b₀). So the **same ζ(0)** that establishes "scale-ambiguous" (K1095/K1097) **is also the β that governs the vacuum's own transmutation.** That's more self-consistent than the a₂ bridge, and ζ(0) is Derived/computed.

The **"one open piece = g(ℓ_B)" structure STANDS.** With transmutation S = 1/(2 b₀ g²(ℓ_B)) and b₀ ~ |ζ(0)|:
- S ≈ 280 → g(ℓ_B)² ≈ 0.0023, g(ℓ_B) ≈ 0.048 — a small boundary coupling, plausible.
- **Calibrated:** this arithmetic is **illustrative, not a derivation.** The exact ζ(0)↔b₀ relation and the transmutation normalization are **Lyra/Elie's to pin** — I do not claim g(ℓ_B)≈0.048 is forced, only that the structure is coherent and the required boundary coupling is in a sane range.

## What changes, what doesn't
- **Corrected (forward lead only):** the β in ∫dg/β is the **vacuum's own ζ(0)-anomaly**, NOT the strong-sector a₂. **Elie's thread updates:** set up the transmutation integral with the vacuum ζ(0)-β, parameterized by g(ℓ_B) — not the a₂ β.
- **Unchanged (the ruling):** K1097's adjudication — magnitude is **Identified (scale-ambiguous), NOT permanent** (Cal §203) — never depended on which β. A scale anomaly is the precondition for transmutation *whatever* the coupling; the correction is about *which* coupling, not *whether* the mechanism exists.
- **Unchanged:** structure Derived (det Δ_full → Jordan norm); operator ruling; both Λ/Ω Partially Derived.
- **Lyra's open question is the same:** does BST force g(ℓ_B)? — now with the correct (self-anomaly) β behind it, which if anything makes the lane cleaner (one Derived coefficient ζ(0) + one open boundary coupling).

## Discipline note
This is the second false bridge I've caught in my own audits today (the first: "three-rung value-lock," K1094; the "Identified-permanent" re-introduction, K1096; now the a₂-β bridge). The pattern is real and worth naming: **when I sharpen an adjudication into a clean "one question" forward lead, I reach for the nearest Derived object to anchor it — and the nearest isn't always the right one.** The a₂ β was Derived and convenient; the ζ(0) β is correct. Verify the bridge, not just the ruling. (This is Rule 16 applied to my own forward leads: the sharpening is blind to whether the object it grabs is the right one.)

## Disposition
Forward lead corrected; ruling intact. The cc-magnitude promotion lane: **one Derived coefficient (ζ(0)=−0.7691, the vacuum-sector β) + one open boundary coupling (g(ℓ_B), Lyra leads).** Elie sets up ∫dg/β with the ζ(0)-β. Both Λ and Ω stay Partially Derived.
