# Part B.1 — FROZEN v1

## 0. What is frozen, in one paragraph
A distant-source catalogue with per-source redshifts is cut into fixed redshift bins. In each bin, the source-count dipole vector is measured by a fixed estimator with a fixed mask and fixed weights. The dipole in every bin is modelled as ONE kinematic vector, common to all bins up to the bin's own Ellis–Baldwin factor, plus ONE intrinsic (clustering) vector with a fixed decreasing redshift profile. The fit returns the common boost. **P1 says the common boost is the CMB boost — amplitude and direction — and it is stated so that a large intrinsic dipole neither rescues nor condemns it.** The three landings partition every possible outcome; the hatch is a finite list of named tests with pass/fail written here; nothing in Sections 2–6 may be adjusted after a real dipole is seen.

## 1. The claim (P1), the presupposition, and the inputs
**P1.** Let $\mathbf{D}_i$ be the source-count dipole vector in redshift bin $i$ (Section 2), with Ellis–Baldwin factor $f_i = 2 + x_i(1+\alpha_i)$ (Section 3). Under the model of Section 4, the fitted common boost $(\beta, \hat{u})$ equals the CMB boost $(\beta_{\rm CMB}, \hat{u}_{\rm CMB})$ within the tolerances of Section 5. The CMB boost's values are **Grace's pin from Planck 2018** (the draft quotes $369.82 \pm 0.11$ km s$^{-1}$ toward $(l,b) = (264.02°, 48.25°)$; the pinned primary governs, and the frozen quantity is *whatever the pin says*, not these digits).
**Presupposition on the face of the claim:** the CMB dipole is read as purely kinematic. Support: Planck's aberration and modulation measurement (Grace pins the primary and its significance). A partly intrinsic CMB dipole is a named alternative OUTSIDE this test; if it is ever established, P1 is re-stated against the kinematic part and this freeze does not cover that case.
**Inputs, named:** T2564/K1522 (the frame is the exterior's state recorded through the photon channel — one exterior, no matter channel); T2565 (geometry alone does not select the frame; only the velocity is the missing input); Cal §957 (ε is the rapidity between $u_\gamma$ and $u_m$, each an ℓ = 1 moment on the induced slice). **Scope, as the rows license it:** the rows license ONE boost. They say nothing about the intrinsic component, which is astrophysics (P2 of the draft). Lyra's L1 row may narrow P1 before the run; it may not widen it.

## 2. Catalogue, mask, weights, bins — fixed now
2.1 **Catalogue:** Quaia (Storey-Fisher et al. 2024), the version, magnitude limit, selection function and mask **as pinned by Grace by DOI before the download**. Rule: the BRIGHTER of the catalogue's two published magnitude limits is used (the one with the published selection function); if only one is published, that one. CatWISE2020 is the named second catalogue, not opened in this run.
2.2 **Mask:** the catalogue's published mask, PLUS a Galactic-latitude cut $|b| > 30°$ for the primary analysis. (The $|b| > 20°$ variant is a hatch check, H1, not a second primary.)
2.3 **Weights:** each source is weighted by the reciprocal of the published selection function at its position, evaluated at the catalogue's published resolution; sources where the selection function is below 0.5 are excluded (a mask extension, applied identically in every bin). No other weight.
2.4 **Redshift used:** the catalogue's reported redshift for each source, as published. No reassignment, no outlier rejection beyond the catalogue's own flags as published.
2.5 **Bins (edges fixed now):** $[0.0, 0.8)$, $[0.8, 1.3)$, $[1.3, 1.8)$, $[1.8, 2.4)$, $[2.4, \infty)$. Five bins.
2.6 **Minimum count:** 100,000 sources (after mask and weight cut) per bin. Reason, written now: the shot-noise floor on a dipole amplitude is $\approx\sqrt{3/N}$, i.e. $\approx 0.0055$ at $N = 10^5$, against an expected kinematic amplitude of order $f\beta_{\rm CMB} \approx 5$–$8\times10^{-3}$ for $f \approx 4$–$6$ — about one sigma per bin, five bins jointly $\gtrsim 2\sigma$; the common-vector fit is what makes the test decidable, not any single bin. **Merge rule if a bin is under the floor:** it is merged into its lower-redshift neighbour (the highest bin into the one below it); the merged bin's $(x,\alpha)$ are recomputed under Section 3; no other edge moves. Merges are reported.

## 3. Per-bin $(x_i, \alpha_i)$ — computed within the mask, BEFORE any dipole, from the catalogue itself
3.1 **$x_i$:** the logarithmic slope of the integral counts in the selection band at the selection limit: with $m$ the selection-band magnitude and $N_i(<m)$ the weighted cumulative count in bin $i$, $x_i = \dfrac{1}{0.4}\,\dfrac{d\log_{10}N_i(<m)}{dm}$ evaluated as the least-squares slope over the last 0.5 mag below the limit. (For a flux-limited radio catalogue in a later run the standard $N(>S)\propto S^{-x}$ form replaces this; not this run.)
3.2 **$\alpha_i$:** the effective spectral index between the catalogue's two selection bands (Gaia $G$ and unWISE $W1$ for Quaia), $\alpha = -\log(S_G/S_{W1})/\log(\nu_G/\nu_{W1})$ per source from the published photometry, and $\alpha_i$ = the weighted MEDIAN over the bin. Uncertainty on $\alpha_i$: the bin's median absolute deviation scaled by 1.4826, divided by $\sqrt{N_i}$.
3.3 **$f_i = 2 + x_i(1+\alpha_i)$**, with its uncertainty propagated from 3.1–3.2. **Both $x_i$ and $\alpha_i$ are computed and posted (by Elie, on the board, with the run's hash) BEFORE the dipole estimator is run on the catalogue.** A value changed after a dipole is seen voids the run.

## 4. The estimator, the model, the fit, and the Landing-C criterion — fixed now
4.1 **Estimator (Elie's E1, validated on E2 before use):** the weighted unit-vector sum $\mathbf{D}_i = \dfrac{3}{W_i}\sum_{s\in i} w_s\,\hat{n}_s$ corrected for the mask's own dipole by the standard mask-mode correction (the same estimator applied to a uniform sky under the same mask returns the correction vector, which is subtracted). Uncertainty: the covariance of $\mathbf{D}_i$ from 1,000 isotropic Poisson mocks drawn under the same mask, weights and $N_i$. The estimator's code is retained with the run and is not edited after the catalogue is opened.
4.2 **Model (the separation):** $\mathbf{D}_i = f_i\,\beta\,\hat{u} + A\,w_i\,\hat{w}$, with ONE boost $(\beta,\hat{u})$ (3 parameters), ONE intrinsic direction $\hat{w}$ (2 parameters), ONE intrinsic amplitude $A \geq 0$ (1 parameter), and the FROZEN profile $w_i = (\bar\chi_1/\bar\chi_i)^2$, where $\bar\chi_i$ is the weighted median comoving distance of bin $i$ in flat ΛCDM with $H_0 = 67.4$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$, $\Omega_m = 0.315$ (Planck 2018 base; Grace pins). Reason for the profile, written now: the clustering dipole of a shell falls with the shell's distance roughly as the inverse square (a fixed local structure subtends a solid angle falling as $1/\chi^2$), and $w_1 = 1$ normalises it to the lowest bin, where it dominates. **Named alternative, reported but NOT deciding:** $w_i = \bar\chi_1/\bar\chi_i$. The landing is read from the primary profile only.
4.3 **Fit:** weighted least squares over the five bins (15 data, 6 parameters) with the covariances of 4.1; $\beta$ and $A$ bounded below by 0; the fitted $(\beta,\hat{u})$ carry a covariance from which the amplitude uncertainty $\sigma_\beta$ and the 95% and 99% positional regions of $\hat{u}$ (2D confidence regions on the sphere from the fit's direction covariance) are read.
4.4 **Landing C — NOT DECIDABLE — criterion (fixed now):** $\sigma_\beta \geq \beta_{\rm CMB}/2$ (the run cannot separate "no boost" from "the CMB boost" at $2\sigma$), OR the fit's 95% positional region on $\hat{u}$ covers more than 25% of the sphere, OR the profile-alternative fit of 4.2 moves $\beta$ by more than $2\sigma_\beta$ (the separation is profile-dominated). Any of the three → Landing C, reported with the numbers; the row stays A9-live; the next catalogue (CatWISE2020) is named.

## 5. Tolerances and the three landings — fixed now, partitioning every outcome
Let $\Delta\beta = \beta - \beta_{\rm CMB}$ with total uncertainty $\sigma = \sqrt{\sigma_\beta^2 + \sigma_{\rm CMB}^2}$.
- **Landing A — P1 HOLDS:** $|\Delta\beta| \leq 2\sigma$ AND $\hat{u}_{\rm CMB}$ lies inside the fit's 95% positional region AND, after subtracting the fitted intrinsic term, no bin's residual $\mathbf{D}_i - A w_i \hat{w} - f_i\beta_{\rm CMB}\hat{u}_{\rm CMB}$ has $\chi^2$ (3 d.o.f., bin covariance) with $p < 0.01$.
- **Landing B — P1 FIRES (A9's kill condition met; the Machian descent falsified):** NOT Landing C, AND ( $|\Delta\beta| \geq 3\sigma$ OR $\hat{u}_{\rm CMB}$ lies outside the fit's 99% positional region ), AND every hatch check of Section 6 PASSES (the discrepancy survives all of them). A boost consistent with ZERO ($\beta \leq 2\sigma_\beta$ while $\sigma_\beta < \beta_{\rm CMB}/2$) is a Landing B: no boost is not one boost.
- **Landing C — NOT DECIDABLE:** the criterion of 4.4, OR any outcome between A and B (a $2\sigma$–$3\sigma$ amplitude tension; the CMB direction between the 95% and 99% regions; an A-level fit with a failed per-bin residual), OR a B-level discrepancy that FAILS a hatch check (then "did not survive the hatch," reported with the failing check named — not a fire, and not a hold either).
- **Does NOT fire, by construction:** the amplitude of the intrinsic term $A$, whatever it is; the total dipole's amplitude; an angle alone; any single bin.
Every outcome is exactly one of A, B, C. There is no fourth word.

## 6. The hatch, closed into named tests (carried from 08-21 §5 and made finite)
A Landing-B discrepancy is a fire only if ALL of the following pass. Each is a test with a written criterion; none is "an astrophysical account."
- **H1 Galactic mask:** repeat the fit with $|b| > 20°$ and with $|b| > 40°$; PASS iff the fitted $\beta$ and $\hat{u}$ move by less than $1\sigma_\beta$ and less than the 68% positional radius respectively.
- **H2 Magnitude/flux limit:** repeat with the selection limit brightened by 0.25 mag; PASS iff the same stability holds and the recomputed $f_i$ change by less than their propagated uncertainties.
- **H3 Ecliptic / Gaia scanning-law imprint:** (a) the dipole of the published selection-function map itself is computed and reported; (b) the fit is repeated with an additional frozen nuisance template — an ecliptic dipole and ecliptic quadrupole (5 amplitudes, fixed basis) marginalised over; PASS iff $\beta$ and $\hat{u}$ move by less than $1\sigma_\beta$ and the 68% radius; (c) the fit is repeated WITHOUT the selection-function weights of 2.3; PASS iff the discrepancy that triggered B is present in BOTH the weighted and the unweighted fit (a fire that appears in only one is a systematic).
- **H4 Malmquist / redshift-error mixing:** repeat with all bin edges shifted by $+0.1$ and by $-0.1$ in $z$; PASS iff the B-level discrepancy persists in both shifted binnings.
- **H5 Stellar contamination and dust:** repeat with the catalogue's published quasar-probability threshold raised by 0.1 and with an additional $E(B-V) < 0.05$ cut (the dust map Grace pins); PASS iff the discrepancy persists.
- **H6 Photometric-redshift outliers:** repeat with the catalogue's published redshift-uncertainty column cut at its median; PASS iff the discrepancy persists.
A discrepancy that fails any of H1–H6 is reported as Landing C with the failing check named. The list is closed: no seventh check may be added after a real dipole is seen, and none of H1–H6 may be dropped.

## 8. C2 — the landings can fail (written before the hash)
- **P1 can fail.** Landing B is reached by any of: a common boost of amplitude $\geq 3\sigma$ from $\beta_{\rm CMB}$ (including $\beta \approx 0$: a sky with no kinematic dipole at all falsifies P1, since "one boost" is not "no boost"); or a direction outside the 99% region; each surviving H1–H6.
- **Landing A is not vacuous — the synthetic cases that produce B under this exact protocol:** (S1) an isotropic population boosted at $2\beta_{\rm CMB}$ toward $\hat{u}_{\rm CMB}$ plus a clustering dipole with the Section-4.2 profile: the fit returns $\beta \approx 2\beta_{\rm CMB}$, $|\Delta\beta| \gg 3\sigma$ → B. (S2) boosted at $\beta_{\rm CMB}$ toward a direction $60°$ from $\hat{u}_{\rm CMB}$: the CMB direction falls outside the 99% region → B. (S3) NO boost, clustering only (Elie's negative control): the fit returns $\beta$ consistent with zero with $\sigma_\beta < \beta_{\rm CMB}/2$ → B. Elie's E2 must show S3 lands B and the positive control (boost at $\beta_{\rm CMB}$ toward $\hat{u}_{\rm CMB}$ plus clustering) lands A; a protocol that lands A on S1–S3 is broken and is not run on data.
- **No clause lets a large intrinsic dipole rescue P1.** The intrinsic term enters ONLY as $A\,w_i\,\hat{w}$ with the profile frozen and decreasing; it cannot absorb a redshift-INDEPENDENT residual, because a vector constant across bins is exactly what the kinematic term is and the profile is not constant. The Landing-C criterion (4.4) catches the case where the profile and the kinematic term become degenerate and reports it as undecidable, never as a hold. The hatch (Section 6) is a closed list of tests, not an "astrophysical account": a B-level discrepancy is either a fire or a named failed check; "the intrinsic dipole is large" is not on the list and cannot be invoked.
- **What would make the freeze itself wrong:** the Ellis–Baldwin form not applying to a magnitude-limited optical/IR sample as prescribed in Section 3 — then E2's positive control fails to recover the injected boost and the run stops there; that is what the control is for.
