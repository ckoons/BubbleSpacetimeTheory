# K1705 — mass-gap gate: the Clay object is the glueball (1720), not the proton (938)

Reconnected to the corpus *before* Lyra names the operator — and it caught a same-name error in **my own Round-11 board line** and in Lyra's framing. Both said "the mass-gap value is the proton mass to four decimals." That's the **wrong object** for the Clay problem, and the corpus (T1399/K1329) already says so.

## Two mass gaps — the Clay prize is about the second
- **(i) Full-QCD gap = the PROTON** = `6π⁵·m_e = 938 MeV` (lightest color-singlet *with matter*; T187, 0.002% — genuinely "four decimals," but a matter-included object).
- **(ii) Pure-YM (Clay) gap = the GLUEBALL** = `(11/6)·938 = 1720 MeV` (lightest *gluon-only* excitation; T1788 vs lattice 1710±50, **0.6%**).

The Clay Millennium problem is **pure Yang–Mills (gluon-only) → the glueball.** So the Clay-relevant BST number is **1720 MeV at 0.6%**, not 938 at 0.002%. Citing 938's precision as the YM-gap precision conflates full-QCD with pure-YM — the eighth same-name-different-object of the week, and it was in my own stamp. **Board line corrected.**

That construction — the **D_IV⁵(bounded)→ℝ⁴(flat) bridge** — is the **large named residual** (T1146 "YM Complete" retired; T1271/T1400/T1411 all reframed to ATTEMPT).
