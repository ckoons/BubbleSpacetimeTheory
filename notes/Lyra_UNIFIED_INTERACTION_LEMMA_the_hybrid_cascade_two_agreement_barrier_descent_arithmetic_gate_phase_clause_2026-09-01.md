## 1. The cascade and the TWO-AGREEMENT BARRIER (derived — the confinement)

**Cascade rule:** while some edge (a, b), a ∈ A, b ∉ A has (w·c)(a) = c*(b), add b to A.
Termination: A grows monotonically in a finite graph; at termination ∂A is collision-free and
**h_A is PROPER — automatically** (the three interaction classes never need separate pages:
each was a species of boundary collision, and the cascade eats them uniformly).

**Two-Agreement Barrier (the confinement, forced).** The cascade CANNOT cross any edge where
both colorings agree with the base: if (w·c)(b) = c(b) and c*(b) = c(b), then a collision at
(a, b) says (w·c)(a) = c(b) — but a is adjacent to b and w·c is proper, so
(w·c)(a) ≠ (w·c)(b) = c(b). Contradiction: no collision reaches b. **Hence
A ⊆ N[ supp(w) ∪ diff(c, c*) ]** — the cascade is confined to the union of the word's support
and the target's difference region, and can never wander the bulk. (Forcing: properness of
w·c plus the definitions; no census, no cases.)

## 2. The descent arithmetic (one mechanical inequality)

H(w·c, h_A) = #{v ∉ A : (w·c)(v) ≠ c*(v)}
            = H(c, c*) − #(diff ∩ A) − #(diff ∩ S̄) + #{v ∈ S̄ ∖ A : (w·c)(v) ≠ c*(v)}
              [S̄ := supp(w) ∖ A; diff := diff(c, c*); each term by direct counting]

**Strict descent ⟺ the captured-or-agreeing count exceeds the exposed-support count:**
#(diff ∩ A) + #{v ∈ S̄ : c*(v) = (w·c)(v)} > #{v ∈ S̄ : c*(v) = c(v) ≠ (w·c)(v)} … reduced to
its final form in one line: **d decreases iff A ∪ supp(w) meets the difference region in more
vertices than supp(w) newly exposes off A.** Everything is finite, zone-tagged, and computable
per (word, target, cascade) triple — the existence claim quantifies over the family and the
target set, as everywhere else in the program.
