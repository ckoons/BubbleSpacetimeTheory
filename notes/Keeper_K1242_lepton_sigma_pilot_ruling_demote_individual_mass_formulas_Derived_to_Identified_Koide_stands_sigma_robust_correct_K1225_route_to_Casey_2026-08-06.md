# K1242 — lepton σ-pilot ruling: demote the individual mass formulas to Identified; Koide stands σ-robust; correct K1225

Elie fired the lepton pilot and scored it in σ (the standing "score σ, not dev%" discipline). The result is important and sobering, and it needs a careful tier ruling.

## The result (σ-scored)
- **Koide Q = 2/3 = rank/N_c** — dev 0.001%, **~1σ** (tau-precision-limited). **σ-robust. Stands.**
- **m_μ/m_e = (24/π²)⁶ (T190)** — dev 0.004%, but leptons are measured to ~1 part in 10⁸, so **~1580σ.** Not σ-exact.
- **m_τ/m_e = 49·71 (T2003)** — dev 0.05%, **~7.5σ.** Not σ-exact.
- Lepton ratios are **scale-clean** — no scale knob can rescue them.

## The ruling
The individual lepton-mass-ratio **formulas** are dev%-close **approximations** (~10⁻⁴–10⁻³), **not σ-exact derivations.** Their honest tier is **Identified / Tier-2-structural**, not Derived. This is **consistent with the corpus's own Two-Tier hypothesis** — BST masses carry a ~10⁻⁴ structural floor (Tier-2), not Tier-1 exact. So the pilot doesn't reveal a *new* failure; it **confirms the mass formulas were always Tier-2**, and the "Derived" labels were drift.

⟹ **Tier reduction:** T190 (24/π²)⁶ and T2003 (49·71) **Derived/D-cand → Identified.** Per governance (tier reductions route to Casey), **I recommend Casey accept it** — it's the honest σ-tier and a large pre-external save.

## Koide stands — the one exact geometric lepton relation
**Q = 2/3 = rank/N_c is σ-robust (~1σ) directly from the measured masses** (a scale-clean ratio-of-ratios; the geometric 2/3 is exact). Koide is **not** demoted — it's the one exact relation, and the pilot **sharpens** it by separating it from the approximation-formulas. Tier: **Identified-exact-observed with a rank/N_c structural reading** (the mechanism-forcing was falsified in K1221; the relation is exact + structurally read).

## Correct K1225
K1225 said "Koide is a **derived consequence of the derived ratios** (muon (24/π²)⁶ Derived + tau 49·71)." That route is **undercut** — the ratios are **not** σ-Derived. **Corrected:** Koide Q=2/3 stands on its **own** as σ-exact directly from the measured masses, with a rank/N_c structural reading — it does not need, and should not claim, a derivation via the individual formulas. (K1225's Q=0.66671 was a valid *consistency* check that the approximations are Koide-consistent; but "derived-via" over-stated the ratios' tier.)

## The honest — and optimistic — reframe
The lepton sector is **sharper, not weaker.** Before: "three Derived mass formulas" (which a σ-referee destroys). Now: **one exact geometric relation (Koide = rank/N_c, σ-robust) + honest structural ~10⁻⁴ approximations** for the individual masses. A referee respects that far more. This is exactly the separation the pilot was built to produce.

## Scope (contained)
