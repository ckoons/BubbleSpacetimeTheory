# The existence check (the two-line lemma)

**Lemma (Target-Existence is the theorem).** For a sphere triangulation T and v of degree 5:
𝒯(T,v) ≠ ∅ ⟺ T is 4-colorable.

*Proof.* (⟹) Let c* ∈ 𝒯. τ_v(c*) ≤ 5 means some pair (a,b) is not operationally tangled
(Definition 5, standalone paper): a single (a,b)-swap in T−v frees a or b at v; assign it to v.
T is 4-colored. (If 𝒯 is read to include directly insertable colorings, assign the absent
color.) (⟸) Restrict a 4-coloring of T to T−v: v's color is absent from its link, so v is
unsaturated and the coloring is directly insertable; under the τ-reading, for any pair
(a,b) with a the absent color, the empty set of a-colored neighbors is trivially isolated, so
(a,b) is not operationally tangled and τ_v ≤ 5. ∎

Inputs: Definition 5 (audit-verified, K1832). No census, no cases, no frame change: the whole
lemma lives in T−v with T's embedding present, exactly as A3(S4) supplies it.

**Consequence for the induction.** K1834 A3 inducts on |V|: T−v is 4-colored by hypothesis,
and the Insertion Theorem must produce a coloring of T−v with a color absent from the link.
At a hypothetical minimal counterexample T, 𝒯(T,v) = ∅ by the lemma. So d_gate is infinite
there, Step-Existence has no content there, and DGT is vacuously true there. **Any proof of
the Insertion Theorem that measures descent toward 𝒯 assumes T is 4-colorable — the
circularity guard already on the board ("no reduction step may assume the whole graph
