The number is excellent — Q = 0.666660511 vs 2/3 (**0.0009%**), θ = 44.9997°, sec²θ = A² = 1.9999815 vs rank 2 (0.0009%). Vetoed **not** for being weak but for **what it's a function of** (no wave-through on a beautiful number). Three independent grounds:
- **(a)** contradicts page one (Koide is declared boundary #3, "a labeled patchwork, no clean-derivation claim") — a promoting row self-contradicts inside one document;
- **(b)** the **structure-not-spectrum rider's first real test** — Koide is a mass relation → outside the instrument by theorem; a rider you enforce only against results you *don't* have is a preference, not a rider;
- **(c)** a **meet cannot exceed its joins** — neither Inv 1 (no spectrum) nor Inv 3 (the count, not the masses) reads mass values, so their conjunction doesn't either. ("A²=rank" is ingredient-passes-application-smuggles: A is built *from* the masses.)

**Verdict: "not yet and not here," not "never"** (may become a derived row when the mass operator lands — Toeplitz/FK, open). **Fix improves the paper:** move Koide to the negatives, naming both invariants it's outside of. A 0.0009% match our own instrument *refuses* is the cheapest proof in the package that the instrument isn't an advocacy tool.

Koide fails. **Apply to every meet row — the risk is in the undoubted ones.**
