# K1136 — pre-registered bars for the two promotion gates

Extends K1134 to the two make-or-break steps the team is now working. Committed **before** their results, to keep the ruling clean (the κ lesson).

## Frontier C — the ε-blind bar (Structure-Derived → Derived)
The promotion step is deriving **ε** (per-bond α-α energy, empirically ≈ 2.4 MeV — I acknowledge partial contamination on the value) **forward** from the α-α interaction geometry, not as a factorization of 2.4.
- **Structural routes to test (no targeting):** (i) ε shares the substrate energy-unit that already sets B_d and B_α — check whether **ε/B_d or ε/B_α is a forced ratio**, not whether ε hits 2.4; (ii) ε as a **geometric boundary-overlap** of two alpha clusters (an overlap integral on the domain).
- **★ The independent lock:** ε must *also* reproduce the sharp **⁸Be datum — the ground-state resonance +92 keV above 2α** — a number the 4-point ladder never used. **ε forward + the +92 keV ⁸Be → Derived; ε only by matching the ladder → Structure-Derived stands** (already a real, over-determined win).
- **Contamination guard:** the ε-deriver builds forward from the interaction; the +92 keV is the blind check.

## Frontier A — the per-particle gate reduces to a sector-level Z₂ assignment
A sharpening that makes the K279 D-tier gate closable. **The SM spins are not free:** every fundamental fermion is spin-½ (odd ℤ₂ half-twist), every gauge boson spin-1 (even), the Higgs spin-0.
- So Lyra's flag — "does the geometry force *which* particle gets which linking number?" — is **not a per-particle question.** It reduces to: **does the geometry force the matter-sector seats to carry the odd (half-twist) ℤ₂ and the force/scalar seats to carry even?**
- **The bar:** show the **fermion addresses** (the discrete-series / boundary seats already used for the mass + generation structure) **necessarily carry an odd ℤ₂ half-twist**, and the gauge/scalar seats carry even — **forward from which cycles close**, not by assigning spins to match.
- **The smuggle to watch:** giving "the fermions" the half-twist *because we know they're fermions*. It must fall out of the seat structure.
- **Payoff:** if matter-seats force odd-ℤ₂ and boson-seats force even, the gate **closes at the sector level** — which is all the SM needs, since spins are sector-uniform — and **spin-statistics promotes Identified → Derived**, the genuine second marble headline after Born.

Both bars committed blind. When Elie's ε and the Elie+Cal per-particle work land, Keeper/Cal rule against **these**.
