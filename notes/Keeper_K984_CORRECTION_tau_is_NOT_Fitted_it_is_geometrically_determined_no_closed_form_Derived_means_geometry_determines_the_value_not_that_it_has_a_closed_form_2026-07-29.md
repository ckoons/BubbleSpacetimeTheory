# K984 — Casey's correction: geometrically-determined ≠ Fitted; form ⊥ tier

## Casey (2026-07-29)
> "Tau is not just 'fitted' — it's well described, and does not have a closed form. We don't need to prove the closed form, we need the value **determined geometrically**."

Correct, and it corrects MY ruling. K981/K982 called the tau "Fitted (derived-final)" — anchoring the tier on the *absence of a closed form*. That is a category error.

## The methodology refinement (general — folds into the tier standard)
**DERIVED means the value is FORCED / DETERMINED by the geometry, target-innocently. It does NOT require a closed algebraic form.** A value produced by a geometric computation — an integral, a singular-measure norm, a transcendental limit — is **Derived even with no closed form.** Only a value **searched / fit** with no geometric determination is **FITTED.** *The form (closed or not) is orthogonal to the tier.* (Relates to [[feedback_limits_lossy]] — integrals preserve information a closed form may not.) Fold into Rule 8 / the Derived definition: do not demote a geometrically-determined value to Fitted for lacking a closed form.

## The tau, re-ruled
- **Home: DERIVED** — the tau is the ℓ=0 boundary mode (Test 1 Γ(0) pole proves it is not a smooth-spectral eigenvalue). This is *well described*, not a shrug. Banked.
- **Value: NOT Fitted.** It is **geometrically determined** — the mass is the norm of the boundary mode against the **singular Shilov-vertex measure**. That is a geometric quantity, not a searched number.
- **Current status: IDENTIFIED (geometrically-determined, pending the weight).** Elie's Move 2 found the ℓ=0 vertex measure has **one free weight**, and his BOUNDED (non-exhaustive) search didn't fix it. So the value is determined-up-to-one-canonical-normalization; the weight is not yet pinned to the geometry.
- **Promotion path (Casey's directive — the corrected Move 2):** **DETERMINE the value geometrically** = fix the ℓ=0 boundary-measure weight from its **canonical normalization** (the Rossi–Vergne / Berezin measure on the rank-0 orbit at ν=0 — a primary-source geometric object, NOT a search), compute the norm forward/blind, and match the observed tau mass. **We do NOT need a closed form** — we need the geometric value. If the canonical weight gives the mass → **tau value DERIVED (geometrically determined, no closed form)**, no 49·71 required.

- WRONG (retract): calling the value "Fitted." It is not searched — it is measure-set, i.e. geometrically determined. The absence of a closed form is a *feature of the boundary mode*, not a demotion to Fitted.
