## E1 — The rank of the global data of the height lift on the branched cover

**Line one.** The height lift of a proper 4-coloring of a sphere triangulation with k odd-degree vertices
carries global data of rank **2k − 4** (k ≥ 2; rank 0 at k = 0). It is one datum per dislocation, less one
relation, less the two-dimensional gauge. It is not one holonomy. In the 5-connected frame k ≥ 12, so the
rank is at least 20 and grows with n. **This is the count for the height lift of a GIVEN coloring; it is not
a count of what an observer needs to FIND a coloring.** The rung-1 claim was only ever supported by this
lift, and this lift does not support it.

### E1.1 Frame and conventions (T2577, pinned)
T a triangulation of S², f a proper 4-coloring with colors in V = GF(2)², edge labels ℓ(uw) = f(u)+f(w) ∈
{a,b,c}. Height lattice ℤ², steps A=(1,0), B=(0,1), C=(−1,−1); mod 2 they are a, b, c. A height is
h: V(T) → ℤ² with h(w) − h(u) = σ·L(ℓ(uw)), where σ ∈ {±1} is the sign of the face to the LEFT of u→w.
Closure on a face is automatic for either sign (σ(A+B+C) = 0); coherence across an edge forces the two
faces to carry OPPOSITE signs. So a height exists ⟺ the dual is bipartite ⟺ every vertex has even degree
(T2577 §3). The face sign σ is a gauge choice (two on a connected Eulerian object); it is NOT the Heawood
sign z_t, which is the cyclic order of the labels. (Both facts are already in the 08-30 dictionary; restated
so the count below is checkable from this page alone.)

### E1.2 The branched cover
Let k be the number of odd-degree vertices (k is even, by handshake). Let Σ → S² be the double cover
branched at those k vertices. It is unique: π₁(S² − k points) is generated by the puncture loops and each
must map to the nontrivial element of ℤ₂ (the face sign must flip once around an odd vertex). Concretely:
pick any face-sign assignment σ on S²; let F be the set of edges across which σ fails to alternate. F has
odd degree at every odd vertex and even degree at every even vertex (around a vertex of degree d the sign
sequence has d entries and d mod 2 same-sign adjacencies). Cutting along F and switching sheets gives Σ.
Riemann–Hurwitz: χ(Σ) = 2χ(S²) − k = 4 − k, so **genus g = k/2 − 1** for k ≥ 2; for k = 0, Σ is two
disjoint spheres. On Σ every lifted vertex has even degree (a branch point lifts to degree 2d; an even
vertex lifts to two copies), and the lifted sign σ̃(t̃) := σ(t)·(sheet) alternates across EVERY edge of Σ
(across F the sheet flips and σ agrees; off F the sheet holds and σ alternates). So the increment cochain
ω̃(e) := σ̃(left face)·L(ℓ(e)) is a well-defined closed ℤ²-valued 1-cochain on Σ: ω̃ ∈ Z¹(Σ; ℤ²).

### E1.3 The global data and its rank (three derivations, one number)
**(a) Cohomology.** A single-valued height on Σ exists ⟺ [ω̃] = 0 in H¹(Σ; ℤ²) ≅ ℤ^{2g} ⊗ ℤ² = ℤ^{4g}.
The class [ω̃] is determined by f; it is the lift's global datum. Rank of its home: **4g = 2k − 4**.
The deck involution ι negates ω̃ (sheet flip); since Σ/ι = S² has H¹ = 0, ι acts as −1 on H¹(Σ;ℤ) and no
further constraint appears. The invariant is [ω̃] up to sign.

**(b) Monodromy representation.** Off the branch points the height is a flat bundle with structure group
ℤ² ⋊ ℤ₂ (translations and the flip x ↦ −x). Around an odd vertex v the monodromy is x ↦ 2c_v − x, a POINT
REFLECTION through the height c_v ∈ ℤ² of v itself (v lies on Σ as a branch point and is fixed by the
flip). The k puncture loops satisfy γ₁⋯γ_k = 1; the product of k point reflections (k even) is the
translation by 2·Σ(−1)^i c_i, so the centers obey ONE ℤ²-relation. Global conjugation (c ↦ ±c + s) removes
two more integers. Free count: 2k − 2 − 2 = **2k − 4**.

**(c) Arc periods.** H₁(Σ) is generated by lifts of arcs joining consecutive branch points (k − 1 arcs, one
relation, rank k − 2 = 2g). Along the lift of an arc α from odd vertex p to odd vertex q (out on one sheet,
back on the other; the return increments equal the outgoing ones because reversing the edge and flipping
the sheet cancel), the period is **2·(h_α(q) − h_α(p))**, twice the sheet-1 height difference. k − 1 vectors
in ℤ², one relation: rank **2(k − 2) = 2k − 4**.

**Corollaries that can be checked by hand.**
- Every period is EVEN (lies in 2ℤ²). That is exactly why f = h̃ mod 2 is single-valued on Σ although h̃ is
  not: mod 2 the multivaluedness vanishes. The coloring is the mod-2 shadow of a multivalued height even
  off the Eulerian cell.
- The class is nonzero whenever two odd vertices are adjacent: the edge pq contributes the period
  ±2L(ℓ(pq)) ≠ 0. In frame, adjacent degree-5 vertices are common; the class is generically nonzero.
- k = 2: Σ = S², rank 0, so the height on Σ is single-valued and the two odd vertices sit at ONE height —
  hence they cannot be adjacent. Coloring-free check of the same fact: delete the edge pq; the graph is
  Eulerian with one quadrilateral face and triangles, dual bipartite, so 3n₊ + 4 = 3n₋, impossible mod 3.
  Two instruments, one fact. (Positive control on the derivation, not on Elie's instrument.)
- The Height Dictionary §4 pre-registration "m(v) ↔ ω(v) = ±1, constant magnitude, sign = ω" is a
  MIS-FRAME and I withdraw it: the monodromy at a dislocation is a reflection, not a translation; its
  translation part 2c_v is pure gauge; the invariant content is the RELATIVE position of the k reflection
  centers, which is (c) above. Elie's flagged computation of m(v) should compute centers, not vectors.

## E2 — Torus completions per sign record: the prediction, from T2574's clause as stated

**Line one.** From Lemma R's proof as written, I predict on the torus **exactly ONE completion per realized
sign record modulo A₄ — the same as the sphere.** The H¹(·;ℤ₃) in T2574's clause is NOT a multiplicity of
completions. It is an obstruction to REALIZATION: which locally-closed sign patterns come from a coloring at
all. And there is a second obstruction the clause does not name, in H¹(·; V), V = GF(2)². I am
pre-registering this against my own 08-30 torus remark, which said the opposite and which I now retract as
written.

### E2.1 Frame and conventions (pinned)
T a triangulation of the closed oriented torus (any orientable closed surface serves; the torus is the test).
Record: the Heawood sign z_t ∈ {±1} on every face, z_t = +1 iff the labels of the three edges read
(a, b, c) in the counterclockwise cyclic order under the fixed orientation. Reversing the orientation flips
every sign. "ℤ₃" means the cyclic group ⟨A⟩ ⊂ GL(V) generated by the rotation a → b → c → a (the order-3
element of GL(2,2) = S₃); it acts on labelings by ℓ ↦ Aℓ and preserves every cyclic order, hence every
sign. A₄ = V ⋊ ⟨A⟩ is the sign-preserving color group (T2574 (ii)). Colorings are counted as functions
V(T) → V, not mod any symmetry, and completions per record are then divided by |A₄| = 12; A₄ acts freely on
proper colorings (a permutation fixing three used colors is the identity), so "mod A₄" means literal
division by 12 on the raw count.

### E2.2 The derivation
Lemma R (i) propagates labels face to face: given the label on one edge of a face and the face's sign, the
other two labels are FORCED (the sign fixes the cyclic order). So a labeling is determined by its value on
one edge e₀ — three candidates — subject to consistency around every closed dual path.
1. **Realized records have trivial holonomy.** If the record is ε(f) for an actual coloring f, transport of
   f's own label ℓ_f(e₀) along any dual path reproduces ℓ_f edge by edge (the rule holds at every face for
   ℓ_f, and the rule's solution is unique). So the holonomy around every closed dual path is the identity —
   on the torus as on the sphere; H₁ = 0 was never used for this direction.
2. **The three labelings form one ⟨A⟩-orbit.** Transport is a rotation of labels and rotations commute, so
   the labeling from start Aℓ_f(e₀) is Aℓ_f. Three labelings: ℓ_f, Aℓ_f, A²ℓ_f.
3. **Each labeling gives exactly one V-coset of colorings.** δf = ℓ_f has the solution set f + V (δg = 0
   forces g constant on a connected graph): four colorings. Aℓ_f = δ(Af): four more; A²ℓ_f: four more.
4. Total 3 · 4 = 12 = |A₄|, all in the single orbit A₄·f.
**Prediction P1: completions per realized record on the torus = 12 raw, = 1 mod A₄.** Same for every closed
orientable surface. Kill: any torus triangulation with a realized record carrying 24 raw completions or
more falsifies step 1 or 3 and with them Lemma R off the sphere; 12 exactly on every record confirms.

### E2.3 Where the monodromy actually lives — the two realization obstructions (pre-registered structure)
Call a sign pattern LOCALLY CLOSED if Σ_{t∋v} z_t ≡ 0 (mod 3) at every vertex (Heawood closure).
- **Sphere (Lemma R's second half, and Heawood 1898 in substance):** every locally closed pattern is
  realized — propagation is consistent (dual cycles are generated by vertex cycles), and the resulting
  labeling is a V-valued cocycle (each face sums to a+b+c = 0) which is a coboundary because H¹(S²;V) = 0.
  So on the sphere colorings/A₄ ↔ locally closed patterns is a BIJECTION. Positive control for Grace.
- **Torus, obstruction 1:** the transport holonomy of a locally closed pattern descends to a homomorphism
  θ(ε) ∈ Hom(H₁(T²), ℤ₃) = H¹(T²; ℤ₃), order 9. A labeling exists ⟺ θ(ε) = 0. This is the H¹(·;ℤ₃) of the
  clause; it is coloring-independent (a property of ε alone).
- **Torus, obstruction 2:** given θ = 0, the labeling ℓ is a V-valued cocycle whose class [ℓ] ∈ H¹(T²; V),
  order 16, is independent of which of the three labelings is taken (A acts linearly, A[ℓ] = 0 ⟺ [ℓ] = 0).
  A coloring exists ⟺ [ℓ] = 0. **This obstruction is absent from T2574's clause and from my 08-30 remark.**
**Prediction P2 (structure, can-fail):** on a torus triangulation, N_realized ≤ N_{θ=0} ≤ N_locally-closed,
with N_realized = (number of proper 4-colorings)/12 exactly, and with BOTH inequalities strict on some
census members. No fraction is predicted; the heuristic 1/9 and 1/16 are not pre-registered. Kill for the
structure: a locally closed pattern with θ ≠ 0 that IS realized (impossible by step 1), or a pattern with
θ = 0 and [ℓ] ≠ 0 that is realized (impossible by step 3). Either would break the derivation, not the data.
