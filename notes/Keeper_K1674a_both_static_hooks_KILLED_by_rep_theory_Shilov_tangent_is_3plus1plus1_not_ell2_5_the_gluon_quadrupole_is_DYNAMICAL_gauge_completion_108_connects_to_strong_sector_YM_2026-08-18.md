# K1674a — both static hooks killed by rep-theory; the ℓ=2 gluons are dynamical, so #108 lives in the strong sector

## Hook 1 — Shilov boundary ↔ coset — killed
The five gluons are i·Sym²₀(V₁₂) = the **ℓ=2 irreducible 5** of the color SO(3). Lyra's hook was that the coset SU(3)/SO(3) is rank-2, dim-5 — the same rank and dimension as D_IV⁵'s Shilov boundary. But a forced intertwiner needs the *same representation*, not just the same dimension. The Shilov tangent is T(S⁴) ⊕ T(S¹) = 4 ⊕ 1, and SO(3) has **no 4-dimensional irreducible** (its irreps are odd: 1, 3, 5, …), so the S⁴ tangent must split 3 ⊕ 1. Under the color SO(3), the Shilov tangent is therefore **3 ⊕ 1 ⊕ 1 — not the irreducible 5.** Same dimension, different representation. **No forced SO(3)-equivariant map exists; it's a pure dimension coincidence.** Killed by rep-theory, not merely "unproven."

dim Sym²₀(ℝ³) = 5 for *any* 3-space (Grace, K1674). No link to n_C specifically. Coincidence.
