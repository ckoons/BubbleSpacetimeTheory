## The anatomy: why −0.949 was fitting and w = −1 is forcing

The two answers are a clean minimal pair — same observable, opposite epistemics — which is what makes this a teaching case.

**−0.949 failed three of the Standard's tells (fitting):**
1. **Anchored on the target (R5).** It was promoted *because* it pointed in DESI's dynamical direction. The test "if I didn't know the answer, would this step still run?" fails: nothing selected −0.949 over its 191.8×-distant sibling −0.99973 except the data.
2. **Unnamed rival (R3).** Two clean BST-integer forms existed for one observable; picking one and not shipping the other is the fishing flag the program forbids (the δ_CP/K585 precedent).
3. **Dead provenance, uncorpus-reconnected (R4, meta-rule).** Its cross-domain weight came from the 130/137 ↔ R(K) coincidence — but LHCb resolved R(K) to SM-consistent in Dec 2022; the leg was gone. The error entered by reconnecting to the *web* instead of the *corpus*; the team's grep, not the maintainer, caught it.

**w = −1 passes (forcing):**
1. **Anchored on structure (R5).** It falls out of a fixed geometric invariant (the bulk volume C·π⁵). The test runs with the data unknown: a fixed volume gives a constant Λ gives w = −1, regardless of what DESI reports.
2. **Pre-existing, not fitted-to-current-data (R1).** It is the corpus's long-standing constant-Λ (T1959), which predates DESI — the resolution *retired* two data-chasing deviations rather than inventing a third.
3. **Its open piece does not demote it (R8).** The Λ *magnitude* is unresolved (exp(−281) vs the a₀=225 heat-kernel route). But that gap is not value-bearing for the *equation of state*: w = −1 is fixed and falsifiable (confirmed dynamical DE kills it) whether or not the magnitude is settled. Ceiling forced / value open — an honest split, not a launder.

## Why this belongs in the Standard (the meta-lessons)

- **It caught the maintainer.** The most credible thing a rigor standard can show a hostile reader is its author's own banked over-reach, reversed on the record (K1037 → K1039). This is Rule 2's both-directions clause and the meta-rule, lived.
- **Both errors were target-aware, in opposite directions.** "BST is ΛCDM" (safe) and "BST predicted DESI" (exciting) were the *same* failure mode — anchoring on the data — pointed opposite ways. The discipline is symmetric: it is as suspicious of the flattering match as of the safe one.
- **The catch fired at peak convergence.** Three CIs plus the maintainer adopted −0.949 because it felt like a win; the brake fired hardest exactly there (Cal #27 / Keeper #26). "It felt most like a win" is the signal to look harder, not to bank.
- **The resolution came from geometry, not data.** The correct answer was reached by *ignoring* what DESI said and asking what the fixed volume forces. That is the whole thesis of the program in one episode: derive forward, tier honestly, and the structure gives something cleaner — and more falsifiable — than fitting ever could.
