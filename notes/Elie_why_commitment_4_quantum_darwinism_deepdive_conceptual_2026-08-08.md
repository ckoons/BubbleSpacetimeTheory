## The computable core (toy 5111 — grounds the "computable?" answer = YES)
- **I(S:F_m) rises to H_S and PLATEAUS.** A pointer record S (the committed bit) is imperfectly copied into N environment fragments (fidelity p). The mutual information I(S:F_m) between S and an m-fragment saturates at H_S — beyond a small m_δ, extra environment adds **observers, not information**. That plateau *is* objectivity: many observers read S, agree, without disturbing it.
- **Redundancy R_δ = N/m_δ is a definite computable number, and grows with copy fidelity:** R_δ = 1.5, 5.0, 10.0 for p = 0.75, 0.90, 0.97. As decoherence copies the record more faithfully, more disjoint fragments each carry a (1−δ)-complete record.
- So the answer to #4's physics sub-question is **YES: the redundant-record condition is computable**, with a definite redundancy.
