## Target-innocence (the referee's first question)
Nothing in the banked claim uses a mass number. The positions are derived from the ρ-vector; the Wallach set is proved rep theory; their coincidence is a value identity from the root data. The result would stand if the electron/muon/tau masses were never measured. This is the maximally target-innocent form — and it's exactly why it survives when the mass VALUES do not (see scope below).

## Scope discipline (what this paper does NOT claim)
- **It does NOT derive the lepton mass values.** (24/π²)⁶ = 206.77 is a striking identified coincidence (0.004%, T190), NOT a derivation — the rank bound (K864) proves the exponent can't come from the domain's residues. The mass-value mechanism (a localization width in the F585 overlap matrix, or the α-tower scale) is separate, open, and belongs to a companion paper.
- **The two are cleanly separable:** this geometry answers WHICH generations exist, how many, and their ordering; the SCALE and the precise ratios are a different mechanism. Keeping them separate is the honest structure, and it's a strength — the "why three" result doesn't wait on the "how heavy" result.
