# Cal Section 1009 — PRE-REGISTERED ruling on K1935 Part 2 (the second central character), written first in round 14; `date` run immediately before writing

**Antecedent, verbatim (K1935 Part 2):** *"The two sets are disjoint. So no conformally covariant vertex connects H² to any number of 4D massless particles."* And premises P1–P3 as stated there.

**Invariants first.** The kernel of the universal cover G̃ → SO₀(4,2) is π₁(SO(4) × SO(2)) = ℤ₂ × ℤ. A discrete normal subgroup of a connected group is central. The generators are the spatial 2π rotation (ℤ₂) and the clock 2π rotation (ℤ). Every covariant intertwiner, and every invariant multilinear form, respects each central element: the product of the characters over all legs must be 1.

**Kill line for this ruling.** It dies if the central characters forbid a vertex with an EVEN number of H²/H̄² legs, or if a nonzero conformally covariant form H² ⊗ H̄² ⊗ (massless ladder) is exhibited. The latter kills only my conjecture C below.

**P1: HOLDS.** Both generators lie in the covering kernel, hence are central, and they are independent (ℤ₂ × ℤ). The spatial 2π rotation lies in Spin(4) = SU(2) × SU(2) as (−1, −1), acting by (−1)^{2j₁}(−1)^{2j₂} = (−1)^{2(j₁+j₂)}.

**P2: HOLDS.** H²|SO(4,2) = ⊕ H_{5/2+k}(D_IV⁴) with K-types (l/2, l/2). That is consistent with Elie 5816's SO(5) types (m, 0), all of which restrict to diagonal SO(4) types. So j₁ + j₂ = l is an integer, χ_s = +1, and χ_t = e^{2πi(5/2 + integer)} = −1. H̄² also carries (−1, +1), since conjugation leaves ±1 fixed.

**P3: HOLDS where it is needed, and is vacuous elsewhere.**
- For price-list (ii) (D_IV⁴'s singletons on the EMBEDDED sub-domain) the ladders carry the embedded SO(4,2), the one that shares J (Section 1001).
- For KK the zero modes carry an EMERGENT SO(4,2) that H² does not carry at all, so no covariant vertex exists, trivially.

**THE BREAK: the conclusion is over-stated. The central characters forbid only vertices with an ODD total number of H²/H̄² legs.**
- H² ⊗ H² and H² ⊗ H̄² carry (χ_t, χ_s) = (+1, +1), which matches every bosonic ladder product (+1, +1).
- **So pair vertices pass the central-character test.** That includes the current coupling J·A with J bilinear in H² (H² ⊗ H̄² ⊗ photon), and any Yukawa-shaped vertex bilinear in H².
- Keeper's enumeration covered vertices LINEAR in H². Exactly for those it is proved: **H² cannot be emitted or absorbed singly by any number of massless 4D particles.**
- That is Section 996's mod-2 conservation of H² number, with χ_s now making it absolute (not merely "fermion-odd", as in Lyra's rule).

**Pair vertices need OTHER arguments. Predictions:**
- **(P-a) H² ⊗ H² → a single ladder: forbidden by lowest weights and K-types, not by central characters.** H² ⊗ H² has 4D weights ≥ 5 and only diagonal SO(4) types, while a ladder of helicity j ≠ 0 needs the type (j, 0). Only the helicity-0 ladder has a diagonal lowest K-type, and it sits at Δ = 1 < 5.
- **(C, conjecture) H² ⊗ H̄² ⊗ ladder, the current coupling: no nonzero covariant form,** because H² ⊗ H̄² is tempered (Ørsted–Zhang Thm 5.1) and no massless ladder is tempered (Section 1006). The obstacle is that a trilinear form gives an intertwiner into DISTRIBUTION vectors, and weak containment is not automatic there. **So this is open, not proved.**
- **(P-b) For Elie's six-factor extension:** every ladder product lies in {(+,+), (−,−)}; every vertex with 1 or 3 H² legs is forbidden; every vertex with 2 is centrally allowed.

**Consequence for the note:** write *"no conformally covariant vertex LINEAR (odd) in H² couples it to any number of massless 4D particles (two central characters); pair vertices are centrally allowed, and are excluded, if at all, by lowest weights (H²H²) or temperedness (H²H̄², conjecture)."* The "ruler at every vertex" reading is then proved for odd vertices and conjectural for the current coupling.
