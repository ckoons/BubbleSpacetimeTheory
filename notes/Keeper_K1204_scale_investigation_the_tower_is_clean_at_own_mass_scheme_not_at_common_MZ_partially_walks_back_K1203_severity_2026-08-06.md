# K1204 — scale investigation: the tower is clean at the own-mass scheme, not at common M_Z (walks back K1203's severity)

I ran the scale question instead of just flagging it — and it corrects my own K1203, in the tower's favor, while keeping the open items honest.

## The finding
K1203's caveat evaluated the up-quark at a common **M_Z** scale and found d ≈ 11.9 (out of window) → "scale is a hidden knob washing it out." But a common high scale is **the wrong frame** for a mass-generating overlap. The principled frame is **each fermion at its own mass scale** (m_f = y_f(m_f)·v/√2 — the particle's actual mass). At that scheme:

| scale frame | weights d/ln(n_C) {up, charm, top} |
|---|---|
| own-mass (2 GeV proxy for up; m_c(m_c), m_t(m_t)) | **{7.02, 3.06, 0.04} ≈ {g, N_c, 0}** ✓ |
| common M_Z (MS-bar m(M_Z)) | {7.35, 3.50, 0.01} ✗ |

The clean integers **live at own-mass and wash out at M_Z.**

## The scale-robust content
The up-tower **mass ratios** are n_C-powers spaced by the weight gaps {4, 3}: m_u : m_c : m_t = 5⁻⁷ : 5⁻³ : 5⁰ = 1 : 625 : 78125 (observed 1 : 588 : 75500 → 6%, 3%). The sharpest clean sub-result: **m_t/m_c = n_C^(N_c) = 5³ = 125** (observed 128, 2.6%; ln = N_c·ln(n_C) = 4.83 vs 4.855, 0.5%).

## Why M_Z breaks it (and why own-mass isn't a fudge)
In K1203 I asserted flavor-blind QCD running makes the ratios scale-invariant. True for QCD — but it **fails for the top**, whose running is dominated by its own large Yukawa (electroweak) and by thresholds. So m_t/m_c is *not* scale-invariant (4.855 own vs 5.627 at M_Z), and it matches the tower **only at the own-mass scheme**. That's exactly what "the overlap gives each mass at its own scale" (m = y(m)·v/√2, self-consistent) predicts — so own-mass is the *principled* frame, not one chosen to fit.

## Recalibrating K1203 (both directions)
K1203 caveat-2 was **too severe** — I picked the wrong frame (M_Z). In the own-mass frame the tower is clean to ~1-3%. I walk back "the scale washes it out." Still open from K1203:
- BST must **justify** the overlap gives the Yukawa at the mode's own mass scale (state it, don't just adopt the frame that works).
- The up-quark's own-mass scale is **non-perturbative** → its clean 7 rests on the 2 GeV proxy (a soft spot for the lightest rung).
- **Charm still degenerate**: at m_c, y_charm ≈ α = 1/137 (0.02%) competes with n_C^{−N_c} (2.6%).
- Cal's **landing ≠ forcing** stands (3·ln(42) still lands the up d-value).

## Tier
The tower moves from K1203's "suggestive" back up to **suggestive+/soft-Identified** — clean at the principled own-mass scheme (~1-3%), with a genuinely clean scheme-robust sub-result (m_t/m_c = n_C^{N_c}). **Not Derived** — owes the forward weight-derivation, the own-mass-scale justification, the light-quark handling, and breaking charm's α-degeneracy. Up-quark 5⁻⁷ stays Identified.
