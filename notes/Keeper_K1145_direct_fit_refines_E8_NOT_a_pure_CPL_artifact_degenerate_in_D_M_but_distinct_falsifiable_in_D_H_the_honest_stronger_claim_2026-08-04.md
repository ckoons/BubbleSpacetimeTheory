# K1145 — the direct-fit refines E8: not a pure artifact, a distinct D_H(z) prediction

The gate caught an overclaim before external — exactly its job.

## Grace's direct-fit (verified DESI DR2)
Verified: arXiv:2503.14738 / Nature Astronomy; all three SN combos prefer w₀>−1, wₐ<0 at **2.8–4.2σ**. The fit: BST's completely-monotone wₐ>0 shape and the CPL-phantom best-fit are **degenerate in the comoving/transverse D_M** (both ~1.5% from ΛCDM, near DESI precision) but **differ ~2–3% in the radial D_H(z)=c/H(z)** — which DESI directly measures via radial BAO.

## The decisive reframe (ruled)
**Not a pure parametrization artifact.** "The phantom crossing is just CPL's fault" is a *partial* truth (real in D_M — that projection is genuinely degenerate) but an **overclaim if stated flatly**, and it would fail Cal's hostile read. The honest claim is **narrower and stronger:** BST makes a **distinct, falsifiable prediction in D_H(z)** — no phantom crossing, a specific monotone-w(a) radial-BAO signature ~2–3% off the phantom fit — that DESI's radial BAO directly tests. BST is genuinely *exposed* there, which is better than "artifact."

## This refines Casey's K1144 (instinct right, fit sharpened it)
Casey's read — the wₐ<0 preference is parametrization-dependent — is **confirmed in D_M** (genuine degeneracy; and independent literature, arXiv:2502.04212, argues the DESI DDE evidence is biased by low-z, supporting the partial-artifact reading). But the **discriminator is D_H**, and there BST stands on its own distinct prediction. So the pre-registration is stronger than "it's an artifact": a distinct D_H(z) falsifiable claim, with the current CPL-space tension stated openly.
