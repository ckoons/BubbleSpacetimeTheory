## Item 2 — Pauli from commitment (Casey asked for this)

**(a) The operator statement.** Let c(w) be the ledger's operator "commit a write in direction w ∈ V₁₂". Two inputs, both stated:

- **(O) Once-only, for every direction:** c(w)c(w) = 0 for all w ∈ V₁₂. A second commit of the same content produces no new ledger state: the cell is already committed (positive-time: no re-commit, K1922's exclusion rule).

Then **polarization**: 0 = c(v + w)² = c(v)² + c(v)c(w) + c(w)c(v) + c(w)² = c(v)c(w) + c(w)c(v). So **c(v)c(w) = −c(w)c(v): the canonical anticommutation relations of creation operators.** The algebra the c's generate on the vacuum is the exterior algebra Λ•V₁₂. That is Pauli exclusion plus the exchange sign, from (L) and (O).
