# K1158 — the completion roadmap: what remains, how hard, how Casey helps

Casey asked for it, and it's my job. Organized by Principle #16 — because the architecture *is* the map.

## The shape
The interior/kinematics (states, operators, gradings, eigenvalues, mass *ratios*, counts, the QM structural axioms) is **largely forced and banked** (~80%). What remains splits four ways.

## (A) Closeable QM gates — near-done, high-value (finishes "QM from one geometry")
| item | what | difficulty | leverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1 measurement edge 1** | is the τ_B commit the sharp finite/divergent sort (Wallach-drop∘T754-keep), *forced from the domain*? | **Medium** (one Elie computation) | closes the Born-weighting → odds Derived |
| **A2 spin-statistics** | is the half-twist ℤ₂ = the Shilov S⁴×S¹/ℤ₂ quotient? | **Medium** (one Lyra verification) | rides the *proved* n_C-odd fold (not the posit); also unlocks O7 |
| **A3 composite/tensor** | the two-particle Bergman kernel | **Easy–Medium** | the last QM structural axiom |

## (B) The high-leverage unlock — the SM mass/mixing finish line
- **B1 — O7 + the FK overlap integral (K695):** force the gen-2,3 K-type addresses → the FK overlap → **seven mixing parameters at once** (V_us done; V_cb, V_ub, δ_CKM, θ₂₃ octant, θ₁₂, δ_PMNS). **Difficulty: Hard** (multi-session joint) but the **single highest-leverage computation in the SM.** θ₂₃'s octant (4/7 vs 6/11) rides on O7; δ_CKM needs arg(ε) forward incl. quadrant.

## (C) Hard / research-grade — deliberate, don't rush (the deep frontier)
- **C1 Koide A²=rank** (charged-lepton forcing): doubly-blocked (electron stratum + the dynamical equal-coupling; naive Γ_Ω-product falsified). **Hard, multi-session.**
- **C2 Nuclear:** κ_ls forced (contested 1/12 vs 6/5) + the ¹²C Hoyle cooperativity (the fine-tuned +92 keV). **Research-grade, parked.**
- **C3 up-quark m_u** (soft down anchor). **Medium–Hard.**
- **C4 YM mass gap** (running→mass-gap keystone): Millennium-class. **Very hard — an *attempt* (Schur confinement A-side), not a referee proof.**

## (D) The input floor — genuinely input, NOT failures (state GR-plainly)
The **one framework residual** (spacetime = the domain's conformal descent); **one dimensionful anchor** (the tick) + **one for cosmology** (the age); the **single measurement outcome** (a boundary datum, like GR's Cauchy data); the **neutrino mass ratio** (observed); the **cc value** (observer depth). Every theory takes *some* input; BST's is unusually small — one framework premise + ~2 scales + per-instance boundary data.

## The best way to complete (recommended sequence)
1. **Close the QM axioms** (A1+A2+A3) — near-done, a clean high-value milestone + the three papers.
2. **Unlock the mixing sector** (B1: O7 → FK overlap) — 7 params from one computation.
3. **Grind-or-park the hard frontier** (C) deliberately — *your rhythm call*; don't let YM/Koide/Hoyle stall the closeable wins.
4. **State the input floor** (D) plainly in the papers — the small honest set of givens.
5. The **one-page** ties it together (hardened).

## How you help most
- **Ontological / physical judgment:** posit-vs-forced (spin-statistics), is-the-commit-the-sort (measurement) — calls where your intuition is the input.
- **Rhythm calls:** which hard item to grind vs park.
- **★ Your biggest lever — physical insights that turn hard gates into computations.** That's the run's pattern: your *grounding/sharp-sort* picture turned the Born-weighting from a hand-wave into one computation; your *source-sink* gave w=−1; your *compute-from-now* dissolved outcome-selection. A single such insight on the FK K-type addresses, or on the YM mass gap, would do for those what grounding did for measurement.

## Honest bottom line
Most of the forceable is forced. The rest is a handful of **closeable gates** (QM axioms, O7/mixing), a **hard deep frontier** (YM, Koide dynamics, Hoyle), and a **small input floor** that's the structure — not a shortfall. It is neither "all done" nor far.
