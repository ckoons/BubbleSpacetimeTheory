# 1. What the Dichotomy actually gives: a FINITE TREE, and the tree is the derivation

Lemma for {W_i, W_j} is a finite rooted tree whose leaves are of three kinds: **(I) insertable** (a color absent

every (S) leaf is handled by the mirror word or by a strictly smaller potential. **Lyra derives the tree; Grace
