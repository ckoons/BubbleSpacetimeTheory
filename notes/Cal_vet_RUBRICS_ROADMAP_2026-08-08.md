## LAYER 1 (the Tegmark-facing Appraisal Rubric) — the crank-tell risks

**V1 (severity: HIGH) — Replace every self-scored "Strong" with an evidence-pointer.** The rubric warns "a crank scores all eight at 100%," then scores BST **Strong on 5 of 8** (C1, C2, C5, C7 + C6-ish). A hostile appraiser discounts self-adjectives on sight. Fix: the "BST current" column must be a LINK to the artifact that earns the score, never an adjective. "Strong" → "600+ preds, region-matched, [σ-table link]." No claim scores itself; the evidence scores it. This is the C1 "no dev%-inflation" discipline applied to the rubric itself.

**V2 (severity: HIGH) — C1 must lead with the DERIVED-STRONG σ-count, NOT "600+ predictions."** Raw prediction count is the match-cheapness trap at theory scale — a hostile reviewer reads "600+" as numerology-volume, the worst possible first foot. The honest C1 is "N predictions that are σ-MATCH ∧ target-innocent ∧ mechanism-forced (≈5 of 26 in the sample I've scored), M Identified, the rest structural." Lead with the *derived-strong* number and its σ; the 600 is context, not the headline. (My score-σ memory: 13 nominal MATCHes, ~5 derived-strong.)

**V3 (severity: HIGH) — Missing criterion: CONFIRMED-NOVEL prediction. BST is mostly POSTDICTIVE, and a hostile reviewer WILL say so.** C5 covers kill-conditions, but the strongest Kuhn/Popper virtue is a novel prediction *confirmed after the fact*. Almost all BST "predictions" are matches to already-known constants (postdictions). Add the criterion and answer it honestly: the forward bets are the Five Absences + DUNE θ₂₃-octant + the sin²θ₁₃ falsifier — name them as THE novel content, and concede the rest is postdictive fitting. Hiding this is the crank move; naming it is the credible one.

**V4 (severity: MED) — C3 (forcing) is not a peer of C7. It is the existential criterion.** If D_IV⁵ isn't forced, BST is "an excellent 5-integer *fit*," not "the selection principle." The rubric should say plainly: C1+C2 (empirical + parsimony) are strong and make BST *a* theory; C3 decides whether it's *THE* theory or a parameterization. Don't let it sit as one-of-eight — it's the load-bearing one, correctly already flagged "THE Tegmark question," but the framing should make its make-or-break status explicit.

**V5 (severity: MED — calibrate) — C4 (consistency) is currently the WEAKEST-in-practice, not "Strong-but-in-repair."** As of *yesterday* the corpus had a foundations paper asserting a retired result ("sin²θ_W=3/13, Derived"), 168 stale pointers, header-rot, and tier-monotonicity violations. (This cuts the honest direction: I'm not demoting the theory, I'm refusing to let the rubric flatter the thing we're mid-fixing.)

**V6 (severity: MED) — Coverage-breadth at Identified is DOUBLE-EDGED; state it.** "Nearly every domain covered at Identified/Framework" reads two ways: (a) broad reach, or (b) *fits everything, forces nothing.* Breadth is a strength ONLY when paired with C3 forcing; absent that, a hostile reviewer reads 14-domains-at-Identified as an over-flexible framework.
