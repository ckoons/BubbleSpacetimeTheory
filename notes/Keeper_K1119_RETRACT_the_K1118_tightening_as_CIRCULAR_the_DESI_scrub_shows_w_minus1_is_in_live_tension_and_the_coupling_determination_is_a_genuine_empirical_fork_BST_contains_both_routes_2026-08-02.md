# K1119 — Retraction and falsifier scrub: w=−1 is in live tension, and the seal is a fork

## (1) The K1118 tightening was circular — withdrawn (unconditional)
I wrote: "given banked w=−1, the null outcome is excluded because Hubble-coupling → w≈−0.89 ≠ −1." The coupling-determination is *choosing* between route A (age) and route B (Hubble/holographic). Using route A's output to exclude route B **presupposes route A wins.**

w=−1 is **not** coupling-independent: K1106's attractor banking already assumes the closed-equilibrium / proper-time picture. The Hubble-coupling is a *different* dynamics — a growing horizon with no fixed point, giving w≈−0.89. So **w=−1 and age-coupling are one assumption, not two independent facts**, and I used one to "prove" the other. This is the nearest-anchor reflex — grab the comfortable banked result to seal the wanted conclusion — and it was caught by an **external scrub, not self-vigilance.** The retraction stands on the logic alone, independent of any data.
