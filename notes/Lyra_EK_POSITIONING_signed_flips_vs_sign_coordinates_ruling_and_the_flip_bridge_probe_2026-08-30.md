# THE ELIAHOU–KRYUCHKOV POSITIONING

## 1. THE LANDSCAPE (per Kauffman's survey, math/0112266 — the map, so the ruling has context)

Four count-algebraizations: Penrose 1971 (tensor contraction counts colorings), Kauffman 1990
(SO(3) cross-product associations), Kauffman–Thomas (Temperley–Lieb), Bar-Natan 1997
(Lie-algebra weight systems). All algebraize HOW MANY colorings exist. One move-reformulation:
**Eliahou–Kryuchkov signed diagonal flips** (conjecture proved equivalent to 4CT by
Gravier–Payan) — signs decorate FLIPS, the moves of the flip graph whose vertices are the
TRIANGULATIONS themselves. Our program: signs decorate FACES of a fixed triangulation as a
function of the COLORING, and the algebra is the Kempe dynamics on coloring space. Keeper's
positioning line stands verified from this seat: nobody algebraizes the Kempe dynamics.

## 2. THE RULING

**Different objects, on three independent axes:**
1. **What varies.** EK: the triangulation moves, colorings are the target of an existence
   statement. Ours: the triangulation is fixed; the coloring moves.
2. **What carries the sign.** EK: a sign per FLIP — decoration of an edge of the flip graph
   (move-decoration). Ours: a sign per FACE per coloring — decoration of a state
   (state-decoration); moves ACT on the decoration by Straddle-Flip.
3. **What the main statement does.** EK reformulates 4CT itself (existence, equivalence proved
   by Gravier–Payan). Our candidate theorem is a COMPLETE INVARIANT for Kempe equivalence — a
   classification of the dynamics, which 4CT does not assert and which does not assert 4CT.
   Honest positioning: our lane is Kempe-dynamics theory ADJACENT to the reformulation
   landscape, not a fifth reformulation. (This is also why the field's Nov/Dec 2025 activity is
   the right comparison set, not the reformulation papers.)

**Novelty consequence:** the ε-coordinates and Lemma R are not EK's objects under any renaming;
the check the round demanded is passed at the structural level. The residual obligation is the
standing one (Fisk primaries for the sign-pattern lineage), unchanged by this pass.

## 3. THE BRIDGE PROBE (import channel, not threat — registered, one afternoon someday)

A diagonal flip in a COLORED triangulation (quad u,v,w,x, flip (u,w) → (v,x), legal when
c(v) ≠ c(x)) kills two faces and births two; both carry our z-signs, so the flip has a
computable z-signature — and EK assign that same flip a sign by their rule. **Probe: compute,
for all colored quad types, our z-signature of the flip vs EK's sign (from their primary — the
read this note flags).** If they agree up to convention on a natural sub-family, the bridge is
real and value flows BOTH ways: their 4CT-equivalence imports into the sign calculus, and our
Straddle-Flip machinery exports to flip sequences. If they disagree structurally, the ruling
above simply stands with a measured confirmation. Either way one small table settles it — Elie-
sized, filed for a quiet slot, no urgency (the ruling does not wait on it).
