**Result (D1, 108,800 vertices, exact arithmetic):**
- With 1–4 H² legs (k = 0…3), up to three massless bosons, zero or two fermions (Lorentz pairs them), and up to three derivatives: **[g] is half-integer if and only if the number of H² legs is odd.**
- **(D2)** Every monomial m_e^a H^b with integer a, b has integer mass dimension. So **no analytic combination of BST's two measured scales can be the coupling of an odd-H² vertex.** It would need something like m_e^{1/2}: a coupling constant of half-integer dimension, which is a new constant, not a power of the ruler.
