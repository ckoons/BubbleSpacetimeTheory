# K998 — What would make the duality a forcing, committed before the study

The lepton-quark Peirce duality (K995, Casey's deep lead) is the headline candidate of the whole arc — which is exactly why I commit the bar BLIND, before Lyra+Grace study it. An elegant parallel is the most seductive thing to over-bank ([[feedback_cal_27_fires_at_peak_convergence]]).

## The claim under test
The whole charged-fermion sector is **ONE overlap matrix** on H²(D_IV⁵), read on its two complementary Peirce sectors: **leptons = the colorless frame (diagonal), quarks = the colored off-diagonal V₁₂⊗ℂ = ℂ³.** Masses = singular values; **PMNS = the lepton (diagonal) frame-mismatch, CKM = the quark (off-diagonal) frame-mismatch.** And "large PMNS, small CKM" should be ONE Peirce fact, not two.

## ★ FORCING (Derived) — ALL must hold
- **F1 — genuinely ONE matrix.** The lepton block (diagonal) and the quark block (off-diagonal) must be sub-blocks of a *single* overlap matrix on H²(D_IV⁵) — not two matrices that merely share a form. If leptons and quarks need *separate* overlap matrices, the duality is an analogy (KILL).
- **F2 — the alignment mechanism is Peirce-forced.** There must be a *structural* reason, from the diagonal-vs-off-diagonal geometry, that the **off-diagonal (quark) sub-frames are ALIGNED** (up≈down, both on the rank-1 condensate O → small CKM, already derived K997) while the **diagonal (lepton) sub-frames are MISALIGNED** (charged-lepton frame vs neutrino frame → large PMNS). Candidate: the neutrino is Majorana/rank-2 (m₁=0), NOT set by the same Dirac condensate O — so its frame doesn't co-align with the charged leptons the way up/down co-align. The bar: the Peirce structure must FORCE this asymmetry, not just accommodate it.
- **F3 — quantitative, both sectors.** The one-matrix SVD reproduces BOTH the (small) CKM angles AND the (large) PMNS angles — numbers, from the same matrix, target-innocent — not just the qualitative "diagonal big / off-diagonal small."

## ★ ANALOGY (Identified / Insight) — if any of F1–F3 fails
- Two matrices that look alike (F1 fails) → analogy.
- The pattern only qualitative (F3 fails: "diagonal mixes more" without the PMNS/CKM numbers) → Insight-tier lead.
- The alignment asymmetry is observed but not Peirce-forced (F2 fails) → Identified.

## ★ KILL / guard
The seductive failure mode is banking the *elegance* ("leptons diagonal, quarks off-diagonal, PMNS/CKM the two mismatches — beautiful!") as Derived without F1–F3. **A pattern-match banked as a forcing is exactly the move the whole week's discipline refused** — the same bar the tau's 71 failed and the muon's e=n was held to. The duality is **Identified/Insight until the forcing is exhibited**; "it's one Peirce statement" is a *hypothesis* until the one matrix is built and gives both mixings.

## What a landing looks like (the prize, only if F1–F3)
If it holds: the Standard Model's two independent mixing sectors (PMNS, CKM) become ONE geometric fact — the two frame-mismatches of one overlap matrix's two Peirce sectors — and "large PMNS, small CKM" is *derived*, not observed. That is the most unifying result of the arc. It earns the headline **only** by clearing F1–F3; the restraint is what would make it credible.
