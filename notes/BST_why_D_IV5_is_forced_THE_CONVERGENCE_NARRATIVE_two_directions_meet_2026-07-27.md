## The question

Is D_IV⁵ **forced** (the geometry compels itself, physics downstream) or **fitted** (a domain reverse-engineered to match the Standard Model)? This is the question that killed Wyler — not a wrong number, an unanswerable "why this object?" We answer it now from two directions that meet.

## Direction 1 — Forward: minimality → the domain (physics downstream)

Start from what a self-observing substrate must be, not from any physics:
- It must support **stable, persistent structure** — and stable bound states exist only in 3+1 spacetime (Ehrenfest 1917 / **Tegmark 1997**, a standard theorem, not ours to argue).
- Among the Hermitian symmetric domains, the classification plus one proved, **physics-free** relation — N_c = rank²−1 from the scalar Hardy condition (T1829) — reads off the integers. The integer **3 falls out of the geometry (rank²−1), with zero Standard-Model input.**
- Physics enters only downstream, as output.

This direction is **forced in architecture** — but honestly *conditional*, with three named soft spots: the rank=2 premise (asserted-structural), the BST-constructed spectral genus, and the chirality→observed-parity node (derived-conditional). Forward alone does not close the case. It never did for Wyler either.

## Direction 2 — Inverse: the observed universe reads back to the domain (the new thread)

Here is the move we did not have before. Instead of pushing forward, **read the domain off the physics** — and do it with a *uniform* rule applied to every candidate domain, using only measured facts, never the Standard Model as a premise. Two independent measurements of the universe both point at D_IV⁵:

**Prong A — the shape of spacetime (Selector-2, framework tier).** Take only the observed fact that spacetime is **3+1 Lorentzian**. Every bounded symmetric domain has an isometry group; only **type IV** domains are conformal groups of *Lorentzian* spacetimes (SU(p,q), Sp, E6, E7 are not). And the uniform descent SO(n,2) → Lorentz group of (n−1)D fixes the dimension: 4D observed → n = 5. So from spacetime alone you get **type IV → rank 2 → n_C = 5 = D_IV⁵**, and the exceptional sibling **E7 is excluded** because its "spacetime" would be the 27-dimensional octonionic object, not 3+1 Minkowski. *(Tier: framework — it rests on the identification "physical spacetime = the domain's conformal-Lorentz descent," Casey #14. Clean at n=5; acknowledge the low-rank SO(4,2)≅SU(2,2) coincidence.)*

**Prong B — the number of generations (Selector-1, reduced tier).** Take only the observed **3 fermion generations**. A rank-r domain has rank+1 boundary strata (Korányi–Wolf, uniform). Observed 3 → rank+1 = 3 → **rank = 2**, and **E7 (rank 3, → 4 generations) is excluded** by a world that shows three. *(Tier: reduced, not eliminated — it rests on the occupancy identification "one generation per seat," which is the open fulcrum: a finite linear-algebra count on D_IV⁵ that will return 3 or 4. The count is what the geometry gives; we do not force it.)*

**The closer (census, §92).** Given rank = 2, the five integers follow (N_c = rank²−1 = 3, and the rest). So both prongs, having pinned rank 2, hand the integers to the census.

## The elegant part — the directions cover each other's weakness

The forward chain is **thinnest exactly where the inverse is thickest.** Forward's one asserted premise is rank = 2, and its one unexcluded rival is E7. The inverse prongs pile up *precisely there*: **rank = 2 now has two independent supports** (spacetime family + generation count), and **E7 is excluded three ways** — the asserted rank=2 premise, the reduced generation count, and the framework spacetime family. Where forward leans on an assertion, inverse brings measured data; where inverse leans on an identification, forward brings structure. Neither is unconditional; together they do not have a single shared point of failure on the load-bearing pieces.

## The name for this — well-posedness

Forward forcing is **existence** (the domain determines the physics). Inverse forcing is **identifiability** (the physics determines the domain). A structure where both hold is **well-posed** — you can enter from either end and land on the same object. That is a *mathematical* property a hostile reviewer can check, not a taste they must share — which is exactly what moves the panel that dismissed Wyler. Wyler had neither direction rigorous. We have the forward forced-in-architecture and the inverse on two independent prongs, and they converge.
