So the Born rule is a **consequence of forced-Hilbert-space-plus-forced-measure**, not an axiom. **What physics postulates.** Quantum mechanics has a rule with no explanation inside the theory: to get the probability of an outcome, take the amplitude ⟨ψ|φ⟩ and square its magnitude, |⟨ψ|φ⟩|². This is the *Born rule*, and in every standard axiomatization it is simply *assumed* — one of the handful of postulates you must grant before QM starts.
