# K1111 — T is moot (harmonic); the value collapses to the observer's depth; rule the n_s tier catch

## Elie's result — T is moot for the value (harmonic)
At a closed detailed-balance equilibrium with a **harmonic** potential V=(k/2)ρ²−Sρ, the Boltzmann average is **⟨ρ⟩ = ρ* = S/k exactly, independent of T** (T sets the variance T/k, not the mean). So my K1110 "new open piece: is the internal temperature T forced?" is **moot for the value** in the harmonic case — the value is the operator ratio S/k regardless of T, and closure adds **no temperature free parameter.** *(Caveat, Elie: holds iff V is harmonic; anharmonic V gives a T-dependent shift → then T-forcing matters. So "is V harmonic?" is a genuine open piece.)*

## This resolves Cal §215's exp-double-counting (a)
Cal worried: the Boltzmann exp(−V_gap/T) and the F200 exp(−rate·d*) are two independent exponentials both suppressing Λ (double-counting). **Resolution:** the vacuum energy *value* is the **mean ⟨ρ⟩=S/k** (the potential minimum), **not** exp(−V_gap/T) — the Boltzmann factor governs fluctuations, not the mean. The **one exponential lives in S itself**: S = Poisson-Szegő boundary transfer = a₀·exp(−μ·d*) — the bleed/depth-suppression. So **one exp (depth-suppression), Boltzmann-T variance-only, no double-counting.** (Exact normalization — μ vs rate, the k factor — is Elie/Grace's to pin; the structural one-exp point holds.)

## ★ The coherent collapse — every thread reduces to the observer's depth
With T moot (harmonic) and the exp in S, the value **ρ*=S/k = a₀·exp(−μ·d*)/k is forced iff d\* is forced** — and d* is **depth-degenerate** (K1109), pinned only by the **observer**. > **Is the observer's fixed depth d\* forced?** That is the *whole* remaining value-question. Everything else (a₀, μ, k) is forced; T is moot (harmonic); the exp is the depth-suppression; the depth is the observer's.
