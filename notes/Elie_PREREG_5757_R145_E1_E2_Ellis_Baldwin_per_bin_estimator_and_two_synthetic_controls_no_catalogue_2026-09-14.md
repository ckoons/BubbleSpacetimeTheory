# Elie PREREG — Toy 5757, Round 145 E1/E2: the per-bin Ellis–Baldwin estimator and its two synthetic controls. Written 2026-09-14 10:05 EDT, before the run. NO CATALOGUE IS TOUCHED.

## E1 — the instrument (what it takes, what it returns)
Input: a source list (Galactic l, b, redshift z, flux S), a mask (cell-level: |b| > b_cut plus named circular exclusions), redshift-bin edges, and per-bin (x, α). Sky pixelised into equal-area cells (rings uniform in sin b, cells uniform in l within a ring; ~2,900 cells). Per bin: weighted linear least squares of cell counts n_c = A_c [a + b·n̂_c] over unmasked cells (monopole and dipole fit jointly, so the mask's own dipole is absorbed by construction); D = b/a; β̂ = |D| / [2 + x(1+α)] (Ellis & Baldwin 1984); v̂ = β̂ c; direction = D/|D|. Uncertainties two ways: (i) analytic from the LS covariance (Poisson weights); (ii) bootstrap (multinomial resampling of the cell counts, 200 draws) — amplitude σ and the 95 % positional cone (95th percentile of the angular offsets of the bootstrap directions from the mean). The per-bin count slope x is ALSO measured from the synthetic's own counts at the flux limit (−d ln N(>S)/d ln S over [S_lim, 2 S_lim]) and compared with the generating x, since the frozen protocol takes x from the catalogue's own counts.

## E2 — the controls, as numbers
Synthetic sky "Quaia-like": 1.3 M sources, z ∈ [0, 4] with median ≈ 1.5, five bins [0, 0.75, 1.25, 1.75, 2.5, 4.0], mask |b| > 15° plus LMC (l 280.5°, b −32.9°, r 5°) and SMC (l 302.8°, b −44.3°, r 3°), per-bin generating x = 0.9, 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, α = 1.0 (one band; α is an input, not measured — a catalogue needs two bands for it). The boost is applied PHYSICALLY, not by the formula: each source's direction is aberrated and its flux Doppler-boosted by δ^(1+α), δ = γ(1 + β n̂·v̂), then the flux limit is applied — so the estimator's Ellis–Baldwin amplitude 2 + x(1+α) is TESTED, not assumed.
- Positive control, BLIND: (β, direction) drawn from a sealed seed (v uniform in [200, 600] km/s, direction isotropic), never passed to the estimator; per-bin estimates printed BEFORE the injection is revealed; the revealed values then compared. Same run repeated at 10× the counts (13 M) to show the instrument's own convergence apart from the catalogue's depth.
- Positive control, NAMED: the same at the Planck 2018 values quoted in the draft (369.82 km/s toward l 264.02°, b 48.25°) — not blind, reported as the number the freeze will see.
- Negative control: no boost; an intrinsic (clustering) dipole D_int(z) = 0.03·exp(−z/0.5) toward a fixed direction (l 90°, b 20°) applied as a density modulation; the separation step fits the per-bin dipole vectors to D_b = K + I·g(z_b) with a TEMPLATE g — run twice: (a) matched template g = exp(−z/0.5) and (b) mismatched template g = (1+z)^(−2) — and reports K (the kinematic residual) with its uncertainty, the K–I correlation per component (the degeneracy number Landing C needs), and whether the redshift dependence is detected (I ≠ 0).
