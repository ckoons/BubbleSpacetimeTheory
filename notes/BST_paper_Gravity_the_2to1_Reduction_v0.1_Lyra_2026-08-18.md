# Gravity: the 2→1 Reduction

## The story first

Every physical theory has to be told, once, how big things are. You can write down every rule about how a swing works, but until someone tells you the length of the rope, you cannot say how long a swing takes. Physics is no different. General relativity does not predict Newton's constant *G* — it takes it. The Standard Model does not predict the electroweak scale *v* — it takes it. Those are two separate numbers the world has to hand each theory before it can speak in kilograms and metres.

This paper asks one narrow, answerable question: **how many such numbers does BST have to be told?**

The answer is **one** — the mass of the electron, a number measured in a table-top ion trap with no reference to gravity anywhere in the measurement. Once BST is told the electron's mass, Newton's constant follows to **0.065%**.

That is the whole claim, and it is a *reduction*, not a free lunch: we trade a constant we can only measure by weighing the Earth for one we can measure by watching a single trapped electron go round in a circle. It is worth doing, and worth saying in exactly those terms.

## 1. The honest count: three relations, not four wins [the honest core]

It is tempting to present a table of four dimensionful quantities all landing under 0.1% and call it four successes. That would be dishonest, and the dishonesty is easy to see. Take the chain, each line built on **m_e** as the only dimensionful input:

| quantity | BST form | predicted | observed | deviation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| m_p/m_e | 6π⁵ = N_c!·π^{n_C} | 1836.1181 | 1836.1527 | **0.0019%** |
| m_Planck | m_e / (6π⁵ · α^{2C₂}) | 2.175727×10⁻⁸ kg | 2.176434×10⁻⁸ | **0.0325%** |
| **G** | ħc / m_Planck² | 6.678640×10⁻¹¹ | 6.674300×10⁻¹¹ | **0.0650%** |
| v (electroweak) | (6π⁵)² · m_e / g | 246.107 GeV | 246.220 | **0.0459%** |

Now look at the two middle rows. **G's deviation, 0.0650%, is exactly twice m_Planck's, 0.0325%.** That is not luck. Newton's constant is *defined* by the Planck mass, G = ħc/m_Planck², so G carries no information that m_Planck does not — and any fractional error in m_Planck shows up *doubled* in G, because G ∝ m_Planck⁻². **The G row is the m_Planck row squared. It is one fact stated twice, not two facts.** (This is Cal's third guard, and the table proves it on its own: a genuinely independent second prediction would not have its deviation pinned to exactly 2× the first.)

Applying the same discipline to *v* is where under-claiming would be as dishonest as inflating. The v row is $(6\pi^5)^2 m_e/g = m_p^2/(g\,m_e)$: it *reuses* the $6\pi^5 = m_p/m_e$ that heads the table, but it also introduces a **new integer, g = 7**, that appears nowhere else in the chain. So v is not redundant the way G is — it carries one genuinely independent piece of content (the factor 1/g). So of the four rows, the genuinely independent dimensionless content is **three numbers**:

- $6\pi^5 = m_p/m_e$ (0.0019%) — the proton–electron ratio;
- the exponent relation $m_e/m_{\text{Planck}} = 6\pi^5\,\alpha^{12}$ (0.0325%) — the one that reaches the Planck scale;
- $v = m_p^2/(g\,m_e)$ (0.0459%) — which adds the integer **g = 7** to fix the electroweak scale.

The one genuine redundancy is **G**: it is $m_{\text{Planck}}$ squared and adds nothing (its deviation is pinned to exactly 2× m_Planck's). So four dimensionful rows collapse to **three independent dimensionless relations, not four wins — but three, not two: v earns its place by bringing g.** Everything dimensionful is those three pure numbers redressed in units, once the single input m_e supplies the scale.

## 2. The reduction: 2 → 1 [the result]

In natural units (ħ = c = 1), count the dimensionful inputs each framework must be handed:

| framework | dimensionful inputs | count |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Model + general relativity | v *and* G | **2** |
| BST | m_e | **1** |

Both *v* and *G* become outputs of a single atomic anchor. **The reduction is 2 → 1**, and the surviving input is atomic — measured by trapping one electron, not by weighing a planet.

## 3. Guard 1 — one input is the floor, and the floor is a theorem [Derived]

**m_e is an input, not a prediction**, and any statement that BST has "zero free parameters" refers to its *dimensionless* content only — five integers, a geometry, ratios — and must never be written unqualified.

It is worth being exact about *why* this is not a shortfall (Elie 5340, ratified K1668). All of BST's content is dimensionless. **A dimensionless set cannot determine a length**: no combination of pure numbers has units of kilograms or metres. So nothing internal to BST can *ever* fix m_e in absolute terms — and asking it to is asking for something dimensional analysis forbids. The minimum number of dimensionful inputs for *any* theory that predicts a dimensionful quantity is **one** (GR takes G, the SM takes v, QCD takes Λ_QCD). **BST takes exactly one. It is at the floor** — and the floor is forced, a theorem, not a choice we settled for.

This reframes the open question rather than answering it. "m_e is an input" is **the theoretical minimum, already achieved** — not a gap waiting for a cleverer derivation. The door from "input" to "prediction" does not exist for a dimensionful constant, for BST or for anyone.

## 4. Guard 2 — α is Identified, not derived [tier-honest]

The Planck relation contains α, and **α is Identified in BST, not derived.** The Wyler route was retired (K676/K680); α enters as a measured number, currently identified with 1/N_max (mechanism-open). Auditing the ingredients of $m_e/m_{\text{Planck}} = 6\pi^5\,\alpha^{12}$:

| ingredient | status |
|---|---|
| $6\pi^5 = N_c!\,\pi^{n_C}$ | **Derived** (F402: baryon antisymmetrization × Plancherel volume), target-innocent, 0.0019% |
| exponent $2C_2 = 12$ | **Count mechanism-backed** (F426), target-innocent — the observed ratio forces 12.00, and 12 = 2·C₂ is a BST primary combination named independently |
| $\alpha$ | ***Identified — the one open ingredient*** (= 1/N_max, mechanism-open) |

α is measured in atomic physics — recoil, g−2, quantum Hall — with **no reference to G**, so the route from m_e to G stays genuinely G-independent. But α being Identified bounds what may be claimed as *explained*: BST currently predicts the *relationship* between the electron and the Planck scale, using a coupling it does not yet derive. Deriving α is the one thing standing between "BST relates m_e to G" and "BST explains why they are related by that number."

## 5. Why this route is non-circular [the load-bearing honesty]

A referee's first suspicion should be circularity — and it should be, because BST *had* a circular route to G, and we retired it. The old route ran substrate-scale → M_Planck → G through a length ℓ_B, and ℓ_B was obtained by inverting the *observed* G in the first place. So $G = \kappa\,\ell_B^2/\pi^{n_C}$ returned $G = 1.000000000\cdot\ell_{\text{Planck}}^2$ — a tautology to ten decimals — and the Kaluza–Klein reduction handed back $V_6/\ell_B^6 = \pi^5/n_C$, the mere *definition* of ℓ_B. **No rearrangement of the ℓ_B route produces G as an output** (proven two ways, K1408). We do not use it here, and we say so.

**The route in this paper never touches ℓ_B.** Read the chain of Section 1: its only dimensionful input is m_e (atomic); its other ingredients are α (atomic), $6\pi^5$ (derived rep-theory, F402), and the integer 12 (target-innocent). **Not one of these is calibrated from G.** The full m_Planck → m_e → m_p → v chain has been checked and is **non-circular by construction** (F509, verified — no gravitational quantity enters any step). That is precisely the difference between a *reduction* and a geometric renaming: trading G for m_e is real because m_e is pinned by an observable — the electron's mass — that carries no gravitational information, whereas trading G for ℓ_B was empty because ℓ_B carried nothing *but* G.

So the honest disposition splits cleanly by route:

- **Gravity-forward from geometry alone (zero dimensionful inputs):** *impossible* (Section 3) — and where a length was smuggled in (ℓ_B), *circular* (K1408). Do not claim it.
- **Gravity from one atomic input (m_e):** a genuine **2 → 1 reduction** (this paper, F509-non-circular). G follows to 0.065%, as the square of a dimensionless relation good to 0.033%.

## 6. The door, named honestly [open]

Could the one input ever become a prediction? For a *dimensionful* constant, no — Section 3 closes that door by dimensional analysis. What remains genuinely open is narrower and worth stating plainly:

1. **Derive α.** It is the single Identified ingredient in the chain (Section 4). Deriving it would not remove the m_e input, but it would turn "BST relates m_e to the Planck scale" into "BST explains the number that relates them."
2. **An independent route to m_Planck/m_e that does not pass through α**, which would let the Planck relation be *cross-checked* rather than assumed on one leg.

Neither is attempted here. Both are named so a referee sees exactly where the frontier is.

## 7. Summary

BST does not predict *G* from nothing — nothing does, and Section 3 shows nothing can. What it does is **reduce the dimensionful inputs from two to one, and make the survivor an atomic measurement rather than a gravitational one.** Newton's constant then follows to 0.065% — as the square of a single dimensionless relation good to 0.033% — with α entering as a measured, G-independent number. The whole table is three independent dimensionless relations and one reduction, honestly one input deep, on the non-circular route (G is the Planck relation squared, not a fourth win; v earns its place by bringing g = 7). Trading a constant you measure by weighing the Earth for one you measure by trapping a single electron is worth doing, and we have tried to say so in exactly those terms.
