# Why this table exists

Search disease is the dual of collision disease: one object under many names is missed by every requester who
searches under the wrong one; one name over many objects is banked twice. The four-color program now sits on a
frontier where the field's nearest objects (Tilley, Feghali, Florek, Mohar–Salas) share WORDS with ours and
differ in the ATTACHMENT (edge vs vertex), the BOUND (unbounded sequences vs one word of a frozen menu), and the
DOMAIN (degree 4 vs degree 5; sphere vs torus). Every row below pins: the object, the source's definition, our
nearest object, and the seam where the two names stop being the same thing (the false-neighbor check).

Source access, honestly: MDPI (Tilley) returned HTTP 403 twice; Tilley's definition below is quoted from the
Research Outreach overview of the paper and from Keeper's K1836 reading of Section 3 / Property 2, both marked.
Feghali, Florek, Mohar–Salas, Belavadi–Cameron were read from the arXiv PDFs (text extracted, quotes verbatim).

# The two projections, read off the table

**Rows 12–14 (added 09:2x on Casey's Round-97 order):** Kittell's eight chains ARE our eight distinct link-seeded chains (15 seeds mod coincidence, measured 8 on 2,927/2,927); Kempe's two-swap attempt IS the 2-prefix of W_i / W_j, irrevocable at every stuck configuration by Lemma D; the impasse is the algorithm's name for the state whose coloring-property name is insertion-stuck.

carry the other four as aliases in its spec header.

# What this table does NOT do

It does not import any theorem. Feghali's single-class result (degree 4, 4-critical) does not transfer to
degree 5 / 5-critical; Tilley's Theorem 1 constrains a minimum counterexample by EDGES and OWL's contrapositive
by a VERTEX — together a squeeze, not a proof; Florek's class count is on G_n with poles and his connectivity
result is at the pole. The only exact alias is row 4's vocabulary bridge, and it is exact only after restriction
to G−v and only at degree 4. Everything else is a rhyme, logged so nobody mistakes a rhyme for a citation.
