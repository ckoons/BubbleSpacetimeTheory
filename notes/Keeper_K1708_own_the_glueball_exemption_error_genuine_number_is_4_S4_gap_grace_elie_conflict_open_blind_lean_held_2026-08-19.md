# K1708 — own the glueball exemption; the genuine number is 4; the shift is open

## Own it (Cal §615, verified)
K1707 said "the glueball 1720@0.6% is **separate and untouched**." **That exemption fails.** The glueball is `Δ_YM = (11/6)·m_p = 1720`, and the **6 in 11/6 is C₂=6** — the held value, in the denominator (verified `1720/m_p = 1.8332 = 11/6`). So the glueball is not independent of the held gap:
- (a) 6 isn't the gap → "separate" holds, but the glueball **can't support λ₁=6** (doesn't contain a different 6);
- (b) 6 is the gap → the glueball **inherits the hold**; at λ₁=4 it gives `(11/4)·m_p = 2580 MeV`, a **51% miss** vs f₀(1710);
- (c) using 1720-vs-1710 to prefer 6 over 4 = **fitting to target** (forbidden; GATE-KOIDE keeps Q=2/3 out — same discipline).

⟹ **Pin the operator with the glueball number out of the room.** This is the **propagation failure**, 3rd of its shape (stale-Proved-after-retirement, stale-clause-in-retraction, now stale-exemption-in-hold). **Adopted standing (Cal §615):** when a quantity is held or corrected, **grep it through every downstream formula** before declaring anything exempt.

## The genuine number is 4 (team converged; my blind lean held)
I posted a blind lean at 10:26: "the honest gap is *not* 6; 6 is the definitional artifact; the genuine number is 4." The team landed there.
- **Grace (decisive):** the k(k+5) spectrum has degeneracies `1,5,14,30,55` = SO(5) harmonics on **S⁴** (our Wallach K-types), not S⁶'s `1,7,27,77`. So it's the Shilov-boundary **S⁴ Laplacian k(k+3), λ₁=4** (degeneracy n_C=5 = the vector), plus a conformal shift.
- **Cal (converges):** BST's Shilov boundary is `(S⁴×S¹)/ℤ₂` — **no S⁶ in BST**, so k(k+5)-as-S⁶ isn't our geometry; k(k+3)↔S⁴ matches.

⟹ **The genuine, non-tautological spectral number is `λ₁(S⁴)=4`** — geometry-forced, degeneracy exactly n_C=5, could-have-been-otherwise. "6" is the conformal-Casimir reading (4 + shift), and "6 = C₂ = n+1" is Cal's identity.

## But the shift is contested — Grace vs Elie, OPEN (verified)
- **Grace:** conformal Casimir = `k(k+3) + 2k = k(k+5)`, λ₁ = 4+2 = 6 (shift = 2k, **no k² term**).
- **Elie:** SO(2) part = `2νk + k²` (**has a k² term**, ν-dependent): λ₁ = 5+2ν → **4** (SO(2) suppressed), **8** (ν=3/2 Hardy), **15** (ν=5 genus), and **6 only at ν=1/2 — not a BST point**.

These **cannot both** be the SO(2) contribution (one has a k² term, one doesn't). So any value **above 4 is doubly open**: (i) is the conformal shift physical or dressing? (ii) what is ν? A gap quoted without ν is under-specified.

## Survives robustly (ratify Elie's safe sentence)
