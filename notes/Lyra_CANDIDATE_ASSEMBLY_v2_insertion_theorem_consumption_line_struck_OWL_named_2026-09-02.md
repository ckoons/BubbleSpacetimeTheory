# THE CANDIDATE ASSEMBLY v2

## 1. The statement assembled

**Insertion Theorem (CANDIDATE — conditional on OWL of Section 3).**
For every sphere triangulation T [if the frame of Section 5 is adopted: every internally
6-connected sphere triangulation T], every vertex v of T with deg(v) = 5, and every proper
4-coloring c of T−v: there is a sequence of at most ONE fully legal family word in T−v (four
Kempe swaps, from the frozen 186-word family 𝒜) followed by at most ONE freeing swap, whose
application yields a coloring of T−v in which some color is absent from v's link.

Case analysis carried by the assembly:
- some color absent from the link already: nothing to do;
- saturated with τ(v) < 6: one single swap (Definition 5);
- saturated with τ(v) = 6 (stuck): by the One-Context Lemma the configuration presents the
  canonical context; by OWL some fully legal family word's image is in the gate phase (τ ≤ 5 or
  directly insertable); if τ ≤ 5, one freeing swap.

Quantifier form: ∀ T ∀ v (deg 5) ∀ c (proper on T−v). No metric, no iteration, no count clause.
**Target-innocence by inspection:** no functional in the statement or the case list refers to
the set of good colorings; the only objects are c, the family, τ, and the link.

## 2. The struck line, and why (the record of the metric finding)

v1's count clause read "at most d_gate(c) gate words plus one freeing swap," with d_gate the
Hamming distance to the τ ≤ 5 colorings of T−v. **STRUCK.** 𝒯(T,v) ≠ ∅ ⟺ T is 4-colorable
(Lyra 08:06, Keeper 08:10, from Definition 5), so d_gate is finite exactly when the induction's
conclusion already holds; at a minimal counterexample d_gate ≡ ∞ and the clause has no content.
The assumption entered at the metric freeze (Cal §801, §811), not at a step, which is why
K1832–K1834 passed it. The census (54/54; 1,801/1,801) was an empty confirmation for that
clause, since every real T is 4-colorable. Rule adopted for this document from now on: **a
functional consumed by the count clause must be definable without reference to the set of
colorings the theorem is trying to reach.**
