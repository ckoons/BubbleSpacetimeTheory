# Time, Derived, in D_IV⁵

## Abstract
We show that in Bubble Spacetime Theory time is not a background parameter but the flow of a single operator — the **linear conformal Hamiltonian J** on the substrate Hilbert space H²(D_IV⁵). Time is emergent: it is the flow parameter of the commitment semigroup, its **arrow** is the positivity of J's spectrum, and the **generator itself is forced by the geometry** (the linear SO(2)-center charge, not the Casimir). The one operator has two faces — a real-time tick and an imaginary-time circle — the two boundary values (Wick pair) of one holomorphic semigroup. The paper's **one substantive result is that time is derived** — with an explicit generator, arrow, and Wick structure; everything else we label honestly as a consistency or a scaffold. An earlier draft made two over-identifications that we correct here, for *independent* reasons: electric charge is not the time-generator (it is not even a function of J — the muon and neutrino share a conformal weight but differ in charge), and Connes' spectral action is not Tr f(J) (it needs a Dirac operator whose square is Laplace-type, encoding curvature, which the positive J cannot supply). Accordingly the **causal-order↔spectral-action** connection (Section 11) is marked **Scaffold**, now with its real operator — the built, sign-indefinite **Kostant cubic Dirac D**, whose D² Laplace-type heat trace is the F60/F63 Sakharov induced gravity — leaving the causal-order *weighting* as the open piece.

The imaginary-time circle is represented on a **degree-2 cover**, and we are careful about what that is and is not. The condition is referee-checkable (T = 2π/gcd of the charges within a superselection sector; 4π ⟺ gcd = ½), and the parity content is an exact identity: exp(2πiJ) = (−1)^{#Rac}, the parity of a state's scalar-singleton (Rac) constituent count. On physical particles (two-singleton composites) this equals **(−1)^F in every dimension** — it is exactly **fermion parity**: fermions ride the cover (720°), bosons do not. **The degree-2 cover is therefore the geometric restatement of spin-statistics, not a novel prediction** — we retract an earlier draft's claim that it is "forced by n_C being odd" (the dimension-dependence cancels between constituents; what the odd dimension decides is only which *constituent* carries the half-odd weight, and constituents are not particles). We report it as a consistency: the theory reproduces spin-statistics, and the gravitational sector rides the single cover (the graviton is even-#Rac). We state plainly what we do not answer: the tick's numerical value, and the weighting in the spectral↔causal action equality. A paper on time earns a referee for one reason — time is derived, and the paper says what it does not know.

## 4. The two faces — the Wick pair [Derived; standard theorem, cited not claimed]
Because J is self-adjoint and bounded below, exp(−zJ) is a holomorphic semigroup on the closed right half-plane Re z ≥ 0 (Stone / Hille–Yosida). Its two boundary faces are:
- **Real-time face** exp(−τJ) (z = τ): the irreversible tick — a one-directional half-line.
- **Imaginary-time face** exp(iθJ) (z = iθ): the reversible circle.
These are the Euclidean and Lorentzian faces of the *one* generator, tied by the Wick rotation τ ↔ iθ. We stress that the existence of the two faces is a **standard** functional-analytic fact for any bounded-below generator; what is specific to BST is that the generator J is *forced by the geometry*, not that time has two faces.
