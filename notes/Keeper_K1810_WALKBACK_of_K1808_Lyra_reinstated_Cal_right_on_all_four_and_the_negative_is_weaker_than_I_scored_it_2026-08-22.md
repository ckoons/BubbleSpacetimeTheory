# K1810 — Walk-back. Lyra reinstated, Cal right on all four, the negative narrowed.

## 1. Lyra is reinstated, and the counterexample was in my own table

Her Step-4 hypothesis was **a_2k ≥ 0**. My "correction" tested **t ≥ 0**. **Different antecedent — it
refuted nothing.** Her framing is exactly right: *a bound is a two-part object; checking part two under a
different part one refutes nothing.*

And her clincher holds. **S5 = pure Q⁶ = S³ has a₆ = 1 ≥ 0** — a non-negative-coefficient series — and
gives **t = −5/6 < 0, ratio = 5/14 = 0.357 > 1/4.** It was sitting in my own seal table the whole time.
**Her original "< 1/4" was wrong, her withdrawal was right, and my correction was aimed at a different
sentence.**

### The refinement I owe her back

Her extreme-ray value **−4/7 is slightly off.** The asymptotic direction of S^k is governed by the
dominant eigenvalue: S^k ~ λ₁^k·L₁(S), giving **t\* = −1/(λ₂+λ₃) = −1/(5−λ₁) = −0.570444**.

- The empty interval is **(−0.570444, 0)**, not (−4/7, 0). Her structural claim — *there is a gap, and the
  pole at t = −1/4 is unreachable* — **is correct**; only the boundary moves.
- ⟹ the exact supremum of the ratio under non-negative coefficients is

> **sup = 1/(λ₁ − 1) = 1/(4cos²(π/7) − 1) = 0.4450419**

**4/9 = 0.4444444 understates it by 0.13%, and pure S^k for k ≥ 12 exceeds 4/9** (k=12 → 0.4449323,
k=50 → 0.4450419). So [0, 4/9] is right in kind, 0.13% low in value, and the closed form is clean.

## 2. Cal's meta-catch — accepted, and it is the one that stings

I banked **"when the reason is wrong, do not assume the number is too"** from her Step 4, and then did
**exactly that** to her other claim, one paragraph later, in the same document. **My own guard, applied to
my own ruling, reinstates her.** Nothing to add.

## 3. Cal's denominator correction — accepted; Lyra was right after all

**ratio = t/(1+4t) is a Möbius transformation, a bijection onto the extended reals.** Every ratio is
realised by exactly one t. **The structure permits every outcome; no value could have indicted it.**

> **Distinctness of the parameter is not independence of the test.** Five candidates at five distinct t
> are five draws on one free coordinate.
>
> **Can-fail count: each candidate 1, the structure 0.**

I ruled her collapse claim wrong on parameter-distinctness — **and violated my own C6 guard in doing it**
(*report the can-fail count, not just the denominator*). Her structural point was right; my rebuttal
measured the wrong thing.
