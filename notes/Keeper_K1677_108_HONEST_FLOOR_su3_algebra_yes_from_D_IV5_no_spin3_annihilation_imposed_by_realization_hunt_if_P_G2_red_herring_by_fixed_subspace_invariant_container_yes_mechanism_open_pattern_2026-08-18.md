# K1677 — #108 is an honest floor: su(3) the algebra, not su(3) from D_IV⁵

The last frontier resolved, and the team did it the right way — an invariant, a scored test, and a self-caught circularity.

## The Toeplitz test, scored (Elie) — 2 pass, 1 conditional, 2 not-derived
- **d-symbol (PASS):** max|d_abc| = 2.31 with 25 nonzero components on the geometric quadrupoles; the so(3) control returns *exactly* 0. So it is **not a disguised SO(3)** — the un-fakeable discriminator Lyra named, confirmed.
- **Compact (PASS):** Killing eigenvalues all −12 → the algebra genuinely **is su(3)**, not smaller or split.

So **su(3) the algebra is real.** But:
- **Closure (the crux — NOT derived):** Λ²(spin-2) = spin-1 ⊕ spin-3 (3+7); closure into so(3) needs the spin-3 (7 of 10) to vanish. In the 3×3 *matrix* realization it does — but only because [S_a,S_b] of symmetric matrices is antisymmetric 3×3, a 3-dim space with no room for spin-3. **The realization imposes the truncation; it isn't derived.** For genuine Toeplitz operators (symbols = functions on the shell), nothing kills the spin-3 for free.
- **Conditions 1 & 5 collapse into the assumption:** "finite closure" holds *iff* the operators are the 3×3 action on V₁₂ = "V₁₂ carries a 3×3 matrix algebra" = the SU(3) we set out to derive. **Hunt-if-P** — the mechanism that would deliver the result is the assumption that produces it. (Elie caught this in his own conditions; ratified.)

**Honest floor: we have su(3) the algebra (a container); we do not have su(3) from D_IV⁵ (a mechanism).**

## G₂ is a red herring — killed by a conjugacy invariant (Grace)
Decisive, not a judgment call: conjugate subgroups have isomorphic fixed subspaces on any representation, so the *dimension of an SO(3)'s fixed subspace inside the 14 is a conjugacy invariant* — unarguable by normalization. The geometric color SO(V₁₂) fixes **three** dimensions of the 14 (the time circle + core tensor modes); the SO(3) that G₂'s SU(3) uses fixes **zero**. 3 ≠ 0 → the two SO(3)s are **not conjugate in any containing group** → the identifying map does not exist. "14 = G₂ ⊃ SU(3)" is real group theory, but the SU(3) it delivers is bolted to a foreign SO(3) — a shared-structure coincidence, like 5 = n_C. (Lyra converges via the fixed-point count.) Retire the 14-shell-as-color's-home hook; keep T2495/T2085 as abstract group facts (g=7 is G₂'s smallest rep; 14 = dim G₂) — real, just not color's geometric home.
