# K1207 — what to do with the up-tower: it isolated the μ_geo matching crux

Casey asked what to do with the up-tower knowledge, and reminded me not to drop the directed product or the ChatGPT topics. The up-tower's real gift isn't 5⁻⁷ — it's that it drove us straight to the one hinge the whole fermion program turns on: **μ_geo**, now sharpened into a matching-scale question. Five moves.

## Move 1 — resolve μ_geo as a matching-scale question (the crux)
Lyra derived **μ_geo = v** (the condensate turns on at v; above the EW transition there's no v, no Yukawa masses) — physically motivated, and she honestly states it **disfavors** the naive tower (5⁻⁷ at v → m_u(v) ≈ 2.23 MeV vs measured ~1.1, a 2× miss).

But there's a load-bearing gap in "condensate at v ⟹ evaluate the coupling at μ = v": **the VEV v is nearly scale-fixed, while the Yukawa coupling y_f(μ) runs** and exists at all scales. So the geometric overlap (a pure number) matches the running coupling at *some* matching scale — which is a real EFT question, **not automatically v**. Two defensible answers, opposite verdicts:
- **single-scale v** (Lyra) → naive tower misses 2×;
- **per-fermion own-mass** (y_f = y_f(m_f), the standard self-consistent MS-bar mass) → tower **clean** {g, N_c, 0} (K1204).

This is exactly where Cal warned the scale-knob sneaks back. **Settle it by the matching physics — never by which scheme makes 5⁻⁷ land** (pushing own-mass because it's clean would be the retrofit). **Web needed:** EFT matching conditions — at what scale does a tree-level/geometric coupling equal the running MS-bar coupling? (Standard: match at the heavy/matching scale, run down.)

## Move 2 — pilot the protocol on the up-tower alone (3, not 9)
Cleanest, best-understood sector. Lyra predicts {w_u, w_c, w_t} forward at the derived μ_geo; Elie+Grace run down with measured RGE; score in σ. Survives → expand to nine; dies → we learned it cheaply, on 3 not 9. **Pilot before fleet.**

## Move 3 — harvest the relative structure now for the directed product (Casey's reminder)
The absolute masses are hostage to μ_geo, but the up-tower's **relative ladder** (shelf ordering, radial addresses) drives the **mixing** — and relative structure is more scale-robust than absolute normalization. So the ordered product **CKM = U_up† · J_W · U_down** can advance using the up-tower's relative structure *while μ_geo is still open*. This keeps the directed product moving and **connects Casey's two topics** — the up-tower feeds the mixing.

## Move 4 — isolate the one scale-robust nugget
**m_t/m_c = n_C^(N_c) = 5³ = 125** (observed 128, 0.5% at own-mass). A ratio — more robust than the absolute weights, and the cleanest single up-tower relation. Test it under proper RGE running; it may survive even if the absolute tower doesn't.

## Move 5 — parallel track: pick up ChatGPT item 4 (recover GR)
While the mass tower is (rightly) Lyra-gated on μ_geo, the team can advance item 4 — the earlier AI questions (how the Koons tick gets its scale, how D_IV⁵ shows Lorentz invariance, how GR is recovered; F60-F66, currently Framework tier). Move the rubric on two fronts.

## The honest headline
The up-tower's biggest contribution is not 5⁻⁷ — it's that it **forced the μ_geo/matching question into the open**, and μ_geo = single-scale-v likely buries the naive 5⁻⁷ as a 2-GeV coincidence. So "what to do with the up-tower" = use it to nail μ_geo (the crux for all nine *and* the mixing), pilot the protocol on it, harvest its relative structure for the directed product, and run the GR track in parallel. Forced either way — the up-tower is the pilot that tells us whether the whole spectrum program flies.
