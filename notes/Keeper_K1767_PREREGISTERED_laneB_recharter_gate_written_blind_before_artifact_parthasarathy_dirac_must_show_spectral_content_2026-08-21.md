## THE GATE — five pre-registered conditions. PASS requires all five.

**G1 — Operator named by spectral content, not label.**
The charter must identify the operator by *what its spectrum does* (varies, D² ∈ [−35/4, 61/4] across K-types), not by the word "Dirac." A restatement that could equally denote the Kostant operator FAILS. The tell of a pass: someone who has never seen the label can reconstruct which operator is meant from the spectral description alone.

**G2 — No two-operators-one-name re-fusion.**
The quark ladder must be attributed to the FK Pochhammer (ν)_λ (Bergman-norm, F506/T2529), NOT to the Dirac spectrum. If the charter says or implies "the Dirac spectrum IS the quark ladder," it FAILS — that is the exact collision that flattened the old charter. The Dirac and the Pochhammer ladder are allowed to be *related*; they are not allowed to be *identified*.

**G3 — Non-flatness exhibited, not asserted.**
The charter must show the spectrum actually varies across the K-types it claims to grade — at least two K-types with distinct D² values, computed, not cited. "D² varies −35/4…61/4" as a bare range inherited from memory does not clear G3; the endpoints (or any two interior points) must be reproduced from the operator on named K-types. (Companion Elie task: Toy computes the full spectrum — but the CHARTER itself must carry ≥2 worked points to be gateable independent of the toy.)

**G4 — Target-innocence + address.**
The K-types graded must be specified by address (which decomposition, which labels) BEFORE any physics target is named, and the grading must not be reverse-fit to a known quark pattern. If the assignment "K-type → generation/flavor" is chosen to match the answer, it FAILS criterion 4. State the address first; read the physics second.

**G5 — Scope declared, tier cited.**
The charter states plainly what it DERIVES vs what it IDENTIFIES. A spectral grading that *matches* a physical ladder is an **identification** (tier like K944 generations = strata integer-match), NOT a derivation, unless the map is forced by the declared verbs. Cite the line. No "the Dirac forces the ladder" without an exhibited forced map (criterion 5 / the ingredient-passes-application-smuggles guard).
