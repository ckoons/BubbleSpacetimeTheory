# K1096 — Self-correction: "Identified-permanent" was retracted; the magnitude is Identified with forced-μ OPEN

That silently re-closes exactly the door Casey asked to keep open. Corrected here.

## Why "permanent" is wrong
"Permanent" asserts the magnitude is **impossible to derive**. We have **not proven that** — and declaring un-derivability without proof is a casual over-claim, the exact mirror of casual downtiering ([[feedback_calibrate_both_directions_not_strict_pessimism]]). A running/scale-anomalous quantity (ζ(0)≠0) is **scale-ambiguous, not underivable**: it has a well-defined value once the subtraction scale μ is fixed. So:

> **Deriving Λ's magnitude = deriving the FORCED subtraction scale μ.** a₅≠0 (the scale anomaly, now ζ(0)=−0.7691 on Q⁵) is the **computed running coefficient / first ingredient**; the **forced-μ is the open second ingredient — and the reachable path to derive Λ.**

Candidates for the forced-μ (substrate fixed scales, per K1073): ℓ_B/Planck, the spectral gap λ₁=7, m_e, the Koons tick. The old banked forms — exp(−281), α⁵⁶, 7·exp(−282) — are **testable candidates for ρ_Λ(μ_forced)**, not deletions: derive μ target-blind → it lands on one (→ that form vindicated as Derived) or it doesn't (→ stays Identified). Justify-and-verify, not wrecking-ball.

## Calibrated both ways (don't over-correct either)
- **Not "permanent":** un-derivability is unproven; the forced-μ path is real and open. Re-promotion is LIVE.
- **Not "nearly Derived" either:** K1067 showed the forced-μ is **hard** — the target-aware exponent-hunt is unwinnable (constructible BST-integer exponents near S≈280 spaced ~0.43 dex in a ~3.4-dex manifold; a form always lands in-band under the null). Deriving Λ requires the **mechanism to force μ**, not a form to match 122. So: reachable in principle, not close, not imminent.
- **Honest tier: IDENTIFIED, magnitude pending the forced subtraction scale μ (open derivation target).**

## What this does and does NOT touch
- **Unaffected:** the operator ruling (K1093/K1095, unconditional, full-scalar), the hinge (one heat trace, all rungs), and the **STRUCTURE-Derived reduction** (det Δ_full → Jordan norm via Γ_Ω). Those stand.
- **Corrected:** only the **MAGNITUDE tier LABEL** — from "Identified-permanent" back to "Identified / forced-μ open."
- **Net PD split, in substance, is unchanged:** **STRUCTURE Derived, MAGNITUDE Identified (forced-μ open, re-promotion live).** The only change is I do not call the magnitude "permanent."
