# K1243 — correct K1242: no tier reduction; the mass formulas are interior computations measured in the exterior continuum; Koide is robust because it's projection-invariant

Casey corrected my K1242, and he's right — I made exactly the miscalibration I'm meant to catch, in the pessimistic direction.

## The error I made
I scored the individual lepton-mass **formulas** ((24/π²)⁶ T190, 49·71 T2003) against the **measured** lepton masses (1 part in 10⁸) in raw σ, and called them "not σ-exact." But **the formulas are discrete-INTERIOR computations, and the measured masses are EXTERIOR-continuum quantities that run.** Precision is **region-dependent** (interior / boundary / exterior — K1215). Comparing an interior computation to an exterior measurement in raw σ crosses the **interior→exterior projection** — the same gap as α's interior 1/137 vs exterior 1/137.036. So the raw ~10⁻⁴ deviation is (at least partly) the **projection residue, not a derivation failure.**

**⟹ Retract the K1242 demotion. No tier reduction.** The formulas keep their prior tier (T190 Derived, T2003 D-cand) as **interior computations.**

## The issue noted (honest — not a get-out-of-jail card)
The raw exterior-precision σ-test is **invalid** here (it crosses the projection). The **valid** test is (i) interior-vs-interior, or (ii) interior-formula **+ the interior→exterior projection** vs the exterior measurement. That projection **has not been computed** for the masses — so exterior-precision agreement is **pending, neither refuted (K1242 retracted) nor established (unshown).** The formulas stay at their tier; not upgraded to "σ-exact against exterior data," not demoted.

## Quantitative support (consistency, not proof)
α's interior→exterior fractional residue is ≈ **2.6×10⁻⁴** (0.026%). The mass-formula deviations are 0.004% and 0.05% — the **same order** as α's residue. So "the ~10⁻⁴ mass deviation *is* the projection residue" is quantitatively **consistent** with the α residue we already understand — supporting the correction, though the mass projection is still to be computed.

## Why Koide is σ-robust — now understood (the deep reason)
Koide Q = (Σm)/(Σ√m)² is a scale-clean **ratio-of-ratios**, so a common interior→exterior running/dressing factor **cancels** — Koide is **projection-invariant.** *That* is why it's directly σ-comparable to the exterior measurement and comes out ~1σ, while the **individual** masses are projection-**dependent** and can't be raw-σ-compared. Koide's robustness and the individual formulas' apparent σ-"failure" are the **same phenomenon** seen twice.

## The standing lesson (for the team)
σ-scoring is right, but must be **region-matched**: never score an interior/discrete computation against an exterior/continuum measurement in raw σ without the projection. **Projection-invariant** observables (dimensionless ratios where the running cancels — Koide) are directly σ-comparable to exterior data; **projection-dependent** ones (individual masses) need the projection applied first. A sharpening of score-σ-not-dev%.
