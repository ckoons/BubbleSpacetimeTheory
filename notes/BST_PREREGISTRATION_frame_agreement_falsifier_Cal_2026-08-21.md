# Pre-registration: the frame-agreement falsifier

**Why a pre-registration and not a note.** The corpus's standing rule is that CMB anomalies are **quote-anything — target, not evidence**. A prediction chosen after looking at dipole numbers is worthless. **This document states the prediction, its magnitude, and its falsification condition before any measurement is consulted**, so that the subsequent lookup can only confirm or kill, never shape.

- **There is ONE exterior.** The frame is a reading of a single physical state, not an average of two.

## 2. The prediction

> **P1 (which frame).** The induced 4D slice tracks the **radiation-defined** cosmic rest frame — the frame in which the absorbed photon flux is isotropic. *Derived from the mechanism's channel: absorption is of photons.*
>
> **P2 (magnitude).** The matter admixture is **ε = 0, exactly, with no free parameter.** *Not "small" — zero, because the stated mechanism contains no matter channel that could produce an admixture.*
>
> **P3 (the non-circular content — this is the testable one).** Because the radiation-defined frame and the matter-defined frame are **two independent readings of one exterior state**, BST **requires them to coincide.** Neither defines the other; both are measurements.

**P3 is the falsifier. P1 and P2 are what make P3 sharp rather than decorative.**

## 3. Why this is not construction-guaranteed

If the slice were *defined* as the radiation frame, testing "does the slice track radiation" would be empty — my own index rule #3. **It is not empty, because P3 compares two independent MEASUREMENTS of the cosmic rest frame** — the radiation dipole and the matter/large-scale-structure dipole — **neither of which is defined in terms of the other.** BST's Machian claim converts their agreement from a coincidence of common origin (as in ΛCDM) into a **structural requirement**.

## 4. Falsification condition

> **BST's Machian descent is FALSIFIED by a confirmed disagreement between the radiation-defined and matter-defined cosmic rest frames that survives astrophysical explanation.**

## 5. The escape hatch, named up front

**An astrophysical account of a matter-dipole anomaly rescues BST.** This makes the falsifier **hard, not clean** — falsification requires the discrepancy to survive scrutiny, which is a judgement call and not a threshold crossing. **A falsifier with an unnamed escape hatch is not a falsifier; this one is named here rather than discovered later by a referee.**
