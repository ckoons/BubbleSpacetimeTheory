# GATE-C-nC5 — is n_C=5 forced? (the gate, before the dig)

Block C is the deepest-foundation question and the highest over-claim risk in the program: *does commitment force the geometry, including `n_C = 5`?* Scouting the source (instrument-validated) gives a clean, honest split, and I pin the gate around it before the team digs.

## The state — type is forced, dimension is not
- **Type / rank (type-IV, rank-2): forced, mod an isotropy prior.** Commitment + "the non-arrow complement carries no preferred spatial direction" → type-IV (Grace, Phase-2, 2026-08-16). The isotropy is a *defensible addition* to bare commitment — name it every time. Node-1 (rank=2) is inverse-forced target-innocently (idempotent count).
- **Dimension (`n_C = 5`): open — an input.** The *same* result proves **type ≠ dimension**: the isotropic complement is `ℝ^{n−1}` for *any* `n`, so commitment+isotropy forces the type but leaves `n = 5` vs `7, 8` untouched. The node that once carried `n_C=5` (the spectral genus) rested on a BST-constructed formula — higher reverse-engineering risk.
- **The 22 conditions select 5, but don't force it.** `BST_22_Uniqueness_Conditions.md` exhibits 22 conditions from 7 branches all pointing at `n_C=5` — genuinely striking — but the doc *itself* says the master theorem is **open**, the joint probability is **undefined**, and these **select** among `n ≥ 4` (consistency/over-determination), not **force** from the premise.

**Honest tier: `n_C = 5` is a richly over-determined *input*, not a forced output.**

## Acceptance criterion (blind)
"`n_C = 5` is forced" requires a **minimality / selection argument from the commitment premise** (or a master theorem unifying the 22), **target-innocent** — not post-hoc. **Over-determination ≠ forcing:** many conditions pointing at 5 is evidence 5 is special; forcing means the premise *requires* 5. Distinguish them explicitly in any claim.

## Guards
1. **Don't inflate the 22 into a "22-sigma forcing"** — they are largely readings of one structure (Bar 1), of undefined joint probability by the doc's own admission. Count honestly; name which are independent.
2. **Name the isotropy prior** every time (it's an addition to bare commitment — Grace's keystone).
3. **Never "commitment forces D_IV⁵."** The forced object is "type-IV of *some* dimension, mod isotropy"; the dimension is the open.

## Expected outcome (stated so it can't be inflated later)
Most likely Block C closes as a **precise boundary** — type forced (mod isotropy), dimension an over-determined input — the arc's "container yes, mechanism open" pattern (type = container, dimension-selection = open mechanism). A genuine minimality *derivation* of `n = 5` is the promote; absent it, the honest close is the boundary — richly evidenced, not forced. Every `n=5` candidate runs the Five-Absence / target-innocence filter first (a minimality/counting argument, not numerology).
