## 2. THE RE-DRESS THEOREM — clean case PROVED

**Theorem (Re-dress, clean case).** Let w be a family word at a stuck configuration whose
stranded remnant R contains no link vertex, acting as the pure relabel τ on R (Confinement)
plus a patch change off R. Let c* be any freed coloring agreeing with c on R ∪ N(R). Then
c*′ := (τ∘c* on R; c* elsewhere) is a proper, freed coloring, and
H(w·c, c*′) = H(c, c*) + (the patch-region change). In particular the remnant contributes
IDENTICALLY before and after — its size is invisible to descent against such targets.

*Proof.* Freed-ness: R is link-free, so c*′'s link colors equal c*'s — freed. Properness:
edges inside R: τ is a bijection on colors, c* proper there. Edges from u ∈ R to x ∉ R:
c*′(u) = τ(c*(u)) = τ(c(u)) (agreement on R) and c*′(x) = c*(x) = c(x) (agreement on N(R));
τ(c(u)) ≠ c(x) is precisely the properness of w·c on that edge (w·c = τ∘c on R, = c on
N(R) ∖ patch; and N(R) ∩ patch = ∅ in the clean case since the patch is off R's neighborhood —
where it is not, see the residual case). Edges outside R: unchanged. Distance bookkeeping: on
R, (w·c)(u) = τ(c(u)) vs c*′(u) = τ(c*(u)) disagree iff c(u) ≠ c*(u) — the τ-conjugation
preserves the disagreement count exactly. ∎

**What survives as K-a2's live remainder (the residual case, named):** targets whose
difference region meets R ∪ N(R), and patches meeting N(R). The exhibits say the remnants sit
at distance ≥ 2 while nearest-target difference regions sit at the link plus blockers —
overlap is possible but confined to the annulus boundary; the residual case is a finite
boundary analysis, not a new unbounded frontier. That analysis is the next theorem attempt.
