# The Steps of Commitment
### The Standard Model flavor sector as an enumerable, ordered, forced sequence

*Casey's insight: the result beyond any value is that the process is now legible — step by step. Here it is, written out, honestly tiered.*

The flavor sector is the substrate's **commit cycle** — absorb → commit → emit — running in fermion space. Masses are **what is committed**; mixing is **what is emitted**. Five steps:

**1 · COMMIT — the record.**
A committed state is an idempotent (e²=e); an idempotent count is {0,1} — a fermion. The substrate's records *are* fermions.
→ **Derived** (item 10 / Code-Forces-Fermion; QM 10/10).

**2 · CHARGE PICKS THE SHELF — parity.**
The record's charge is the S¹ weight (m = 3|Q|); the boundary fold keeps only degree+weight even, so charge assigns the shelf: **even {0,2,4}** for up-type + neutrinos (incl. the k=0 boundary mode), **odd {1,3,5}** for down-type + charged-leptons.
→ **Derived** (T2470 + the parity fold). *Organizing principle:* even/boundary towers are anomalous, odd/bulk clean (K1185).

**3 · THE SHELF SETS THE MASS — the diagonal norm.**
Bulk (odd) towers: the FK ladder → down = 1:20:840. Boundary (even) towers: y = exp(−distance) ≈ α per shell → the top saturates at the ceiling.
→ down/lepton **Derived** (FK); up/ν **ordering forced** (T2515, no input), per-rung values carry the gen-1 cold-anomaly caveat.

**4 · THE WEAK CURRENT EMITS — one-degree hop.**
The weak charged current *is* the degree-±1 cohomology operator J_W on ℤ[h]/h⁶ — the unique even↔odd connector. It is the emit step: the transition between records.
→ J_W = degree-1 operator **Derived** (Grace/T1929); "J_W = the emit step" a **lead** (well-supported).

**5 · THE ORDERED PRODUCT — commit then emit — is the mixing.**
- **(a) Structure:** the mixing is the *non-commutativity* of the mass operator and the weak current — share a basis and CKM = identity. The order is the commit cycle: **CKM = U_up† · J_W · U_down** (mass first, weak second), not the reverse.
  → non-commutativity **exact**; commit→emit order **corpus-grounded** (SWPP + item 10) — solid.
- **(b) Values:** they fall out of the compounding. The leading Cabibbo **λ = √(m_d/m_s) = 1/√20** (the geometric mean from the one-degree hop) matches at 0.87σ, forced, no dial — but this is the *known* Gatto relation, already banked, so it **validates the mechanism**, not a new value.
