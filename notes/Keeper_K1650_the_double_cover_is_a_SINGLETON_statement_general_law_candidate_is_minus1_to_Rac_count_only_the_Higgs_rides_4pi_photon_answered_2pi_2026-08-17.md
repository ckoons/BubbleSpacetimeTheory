# K1650 — The double cover is a singleton statement; the honest general law counts Higgs-quanta; only the Higgs rides 4π

Cal's hostile read found a genuine internal contradiction, and resolving it — with Elie's Δ=4 vector — gives a *sharper* central result than the paper had.

## The contradiction, and its resolution
**§7 vs §9 (Cal):** if exp(2πiJ)=(−1)^{n_C}(−1)^F is *general*, every boson (F=0) picks up −1 and rides 4π — including the photon. But §9 marks the photon open. They can't both stand. **Elie 5326 settles it:** the vector is a **Rac⊗Rac composite at Δ=4** (the floated 5/2 was *non-unitary* — a ghost below Mack's ℓ=1 bound Δ≥4). Δ=4 is integer, so exp(8πiJ-eigenvalue)=+1 → the photon rides the **single cover**. So **the general form is refuted for composites; §7 is established only on the two singletons** (Cal's honest reading B). The boxed equation, section title, and abstract still carry the unqualified (−1)^{n_C−2} — a referee reads the box — so correct them to the sector-split *and* scope to singletons.

## ★ The honest general law (candidate — cleaner and true)
Run it through: exp(2πiJ)=exp(2πiE), and E mod 1 = (3/2)·#Rac + 2·#Di + ℓ ≡ (½)·#Rac (mod 1). So
> **exp(2πiJ) = (−1)^{#Rac}** — the parity of the number of *Rac scalar-singleton* constituents.

Check: Rac (#Rac=1) → −1; Di (#Rac=0) → +1; the vector Rac⊗Rac (#Rac=2) → +1 (matches Δ=4). So **(−1)^{n_C}(−1)^F is the singleton special case** — where #Rac-parity happens to correlate with fermion number — and that correlation **breaks for composites** (the Rac⊗Rac boson has even #Rac → +1, the opposite of what (−1)^F would give). The physical statement is then sharp and striking:
> **Among the SM particles, only the Higgs (Rac, odd #Rac) rides the 4π double cover; the fermions (Di) and the gauge bosons (Rac⊗Rac) ride the single cover.**

That is *cleaner* than "bosons 4π, fermions 2π" (which was the false general form), and it's the true content: **the double cover counts scalar-singleton (Higgs) quanta mod 2.** Tiered **candidate** — Elie verifies exp(2πiJ)=(−1)^{#Rac} blind; if it holds, it's the paper's central formula and "only the Higgs rides the double cover" is the headline. If §7 stays singleton-only, that's still honest — but the #Rac law is the natural, verifiable extension, and it *answers* §9 rather than leaving it open.
