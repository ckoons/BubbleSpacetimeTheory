# K1121 — The corpus already has this (K1040/F220), and it predicts the opposite evolution to DESI

## Reconnected: K1040 + F220 ARE Casey's model
- **K1040 (2026-07-30):** dark energy is **w = −1 from the fixed bulk volume C·π⁵** (a geometric invariant that does not run). The only deviation is the **substrate coupling ε(a)** — how imperfectly the interior currently matches the substrate — and **ε → 0** as the universe matches. So **w = −1 + ε(a), ε small and shrinking.**
- **F220 (Lyra):** the bleed fixed point is **stable and unique**, and **w relaxes to −1 on a Hubble time** (~12 Gyr). Signed, timed.

So the K1105–K1120 "two-horn fork" (flat −1 vs generic holographic) was a **re-derivation and mis-framing** of an already-banked result. It's **one model with a computed deviation**, exactly as Casey said — "w = −1 with a tension."

## The honest gap — the tension has the *opposite sign* to DESI
The corpus model's deviation **decays**: ε(past) > ε(now) > 0, so w was *more positive* in the past and *decreases toward −1* → **wₐ > 0** (quintessence, approaching −1 from above). This is precisely what **Grace and Elie computed blind** (wₐ ≈ +0.38 / +0.39).

**DESI prefers the opposite:** phantom-crossing — w < −1 in the past, w > −1 now → **wₐ < 0.**

And the magnitude: K1040 estimated the deviation ≤ ~n_C/N_max² ≈ 2.7×10⁻⁴ (w₀ ≈ −0.9997) and **explicitly rejected −0.949** (a 5% deviation) as "190× too large to be a vanishing coupling." DESI's w₀ ≈ −0.84 is a **~15% deviation** — ~590× that estimate.

**K1040 named its own kill condition:** *"Refuted by confirmed dynamical DE at the DESI scale (a ~5% deviation, or a growing / phantom-crossing w)."* DESI shows **both** — a large deviation **and** phantom-crossing — at 2.5σ–5.4σ (SNe-systematics-dependent). That is a **serious threat to this sector, not yet a definitive kill.**

## So, directly (the Keeper challenge)
"The tension explains why DESI is different" is **not supported by the banked model.** As computed, the tension has the **wrong sign** (a shrinking quintessence deviation, not a phantom-crossing one) and, on K1040's estimate, the wrong magnitude. This is the near-miss the reconnect surfaces — and near-misses get scrutiny, not defense.

## Calibration (both directions)
- The **magnitude** gap is *not* a definitive refutation: K1040's ≤10⁻⁴ was a rough "at most" scale, and F220 says the deviation relaxes on a Hubble time — so if the DE-onset/interstasis is recent (F218: onset ≈ now), we could be **mid-relaxation with ε still ~0.1 today.** Large-today is possible.
- The **sign** is the genuine hurdle: a monotone approach-from-above gives wₐ > 0; **phantom-crossing (wₐ < 0) requires w to have been below −1 in the past**, which the F220 ρ^{3/2} scalar toy does not produce.

## The path — Casey's "linear algebra on D_IV⁵"
Recompute ε(a)/w(a) from the **actual D_IV⁵ bleed operator** (the heat-semigroup generator on D_IV⁵) — **not** F220's ρ^{3/2} scalar toy, **not** K1040's scale estimate, **not** a generic holographic cutoff (all of which gave wₐ > 0 or ≤10⁻⁴). Make-or-break:
- rigorous D_IV⁵ bleed → **phantom-crossing (wₐ < 0)** → matches DESI → the value becomes **Derived**;
- rigorous D_IV⁵ bleed → **monotone-from-above (wₐ > 0)** → **K1040's own falsifier fires** → this sector is disfavored by DESI.
Run **blind** — the geometry gives what it gives; compare to DESI after. This is the precise content of tasks #54/#57/#58.
