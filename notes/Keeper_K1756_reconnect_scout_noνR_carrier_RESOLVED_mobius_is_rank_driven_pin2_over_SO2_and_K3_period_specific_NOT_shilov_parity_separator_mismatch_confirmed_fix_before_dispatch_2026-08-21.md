# K1756 — the no-ν_R carrier, resolved by reconnecting (not left open)

## The round's own lesson, applied to the row I raised
K1755 left "is w₁(M) controlled by w₁(Š)?" open. The round's lesson was: we under-search old objects — corpus-sweep before writing. So I grebbed the Möbius construction chain instead of leaving a caveat, and it resolved in minutes.

## What the corpus says
The Möbius locus M = **K3 / Pin(2)-ℤ₂** (T1949), built from the **Pin(2)/SO(2) = ℤ/rank = ℤ/2** quotient (T1938, *rank-driven*) on the **K3 spectral slice** (T1939, which makes D_IV⁵ *specifically* the K3 period domain, g = 7). So w₁(M) is rank-driven and D_IV⁵-specific — **not** the n_C-parity that drives the Šilov non-orientability w₁(Š).

## The resolution (the direction Elie feared)
w₁(M) is **not** controlled by w₁(Š) — different carriers, different constructions. And rank = 2 for every type-IV domain, so the Pin(2)/SO(2) Möbius ℤ₂ is *constant* across the family — the D_IV⁷/⁸ Šilov separator can't isolate it. The shipped **no-ν_R row (line 93)** therefore gives a Möbius/rank mechanism a Šilov separator: a **confirmed mismatch**.

## Tier, honestly, both directions
This upgrades K1755's "minor/disclose" to a **confirmed small defect on one supporting row → fix before dispatch.** It does **not** reopen the core GO: the spine ("SM from one integer, two generators") is untouched, and the no-ν_R *reading* is banked and correct (T1949). Only that row's separator column is wrong. The parity/γ₅ row (line 92, T2522, a real Šilov mechanism) keeps its Šilov separator — it matches. No wall: the spine held; one row needs correcting.
