# The H term of T1448: what "N_c colour geodesic families, each summing to ζ(3)" would have to be as a geodesic count on Γ(137)\D_IV⁵ — and why it is an assignment
**Lyra, Monday 2026-09-07, 08:01 EDT. Round 125 item (2). Cal holds 9e688fcc (B). No numerics.**

## 1. What the hyperbolic term IS on a rank-2 quotient
For Γ\G/K with G = SO₀(5,2) (ℝ-rank 2) and h a Paley–Wiener test function on i𝔞*, the hyperbolic (regular-semisimple, split) part of the trace formula is
  **H[h] = Σ_{[γ] regular hyperbolic in Γ} vol(Γ_γ\G_γ) · |det(1 − Ad(γ))|_{𝔫}|⁻¹ · (𝒜h)(ℓ(γ)),**
where γ is conjugate into A⁺ = exp(𝔞⁺), ℓ(γ) ∈ 𝔞⁺ is its length VECTOR (two coordinates: the logs of its two eigenvalue moduli along e₁, e₂), G_γ is its centraliser (a maximal ℝ-split torus times a compact part for regular γ), and 𝒜h is the Abel transform of h — the inverse spherical transform of h evaluated along A. Every object in the sum is determined by Γ; the only free input is h, and H[h] is a functional of h. Two consequences before anything else: (i) there is no h-independent number that "is the hyperbolic contribution"; (ii) the classes are indexed by a two-parameter length set in 𝔞⁺, not by an integer n.

## 2. What the length spectrum of Γ(137)\D_IV⁵ actually looks like
Regular hyperbolic classes of Γ = a congruence subgroup of SO(⟨1⁵,−1²⟩)(ℤ) are Γ-conjugacy classes of elements whose characteristic polynomial (degree 7 over ℚ) splits off two real eigenvalue pairs {ε₁^{±1}, ε₂^{±1}} with ε_i > 1 (and a 3-dimensional compact part). Their length vectors are (log ε₁, log ε₂), with ε₁, ε₂ units in the totally real étale algebras cut out by the characteristic polynomial (orders in real quadratic or quartic fields). The primitive length set is therefore a subset of pairs of regulators of orders in real quadratic/quartic fields, weighted by class-number-type volumes vol(Γ_γ\G_γ) — the rank-2 analogue of the "lengths = 2 log ε_d, weights = h(d)" picture for SL₂(ℤ), here two-dimensional and NOT of the form {n ℓ₀ : n = 1, 2, 3, …} along any direction: the powers γⁿ of one primitive γ give the ray {n·ℓ(γ)}, but each primitive class has its own ℓ(γ) and its own weight, and the prime geodesic theorem in rank 2 (Deitmar 2004: the counting function of classes with ℓ(γ) in an expanding cone of 𝔞⁺ is asymptotic to a volume-normalised exponential integral over the cone, with the Weyl group entering through the tempered spectrum's Plancherel density) says the primitive lengths are distributed in the cone with exponential density, not on N_c rays.

## 3. What T1448's sentence would have to be, if it were a count
"N_c = 3 colour families, one per short-root direction, each indexed by winding number n = 1, 2, 3, …, each term weighted 1/n³, each family summing to ζ(3), total N_c/rank² · ζ(3)" would have to mean:
(a) exactly three primitive classes γ₁, γ₂, γ₃ (or three orbits of classes under some symmetry) whose powers γ_kⁿ are the ONLY classes contributing, with ℓ(γ_k) along "the short-root directions" — but the short roots e₁, e₂ are TWO directions in 𝔞*, not three, and the length vector of a class is a point of 𝔞⁺, not a direction; there is no third short-root direction for a third family. The "3" in the sentence is the multiplicity of the short roots (n − 2 = 3 = N_c, a root-space dimension), and a root-space dimension is not a count of geodesic classes.
(b) the weights vol(Γ_γⁿ\G_γⁿ)·|det(1 − Ad(γⁿ))|⁻¹·(𝒜h)(nℓ) would have to equal 1/n³ for the chosen h — but |det(1 − Ad(γⁿ))|⁻¹ decays EXPONENTIALLY in n (like e^{−⟨ρ, nℓ⟩}), and (𝒜h)(nℓ) is exponentially small for a Paley–Wiener h; a 1/n³ weight arises for no test function and no lattice. The "2L − 1 = 3" power is the Bergman-kernel decay of the DISCRETE spectrum's index k (T1445), transplanted onto a geodesic index n it does not index.
(c) ζ(3) would have to be produced by the sum — but ζ-values on the geometric side of the trace formula for arithmetic Γ arise in the IDENTITY term through vol(Γ\G) (for SO(5,2)(ℤ)-type lattices a product of ζ(2)ζ(4)ζ(6)-type values by Siegel–Harder — even zeta values, from the B₃ absolute root system's exponents 1, 3, 5), never ζ(3) from the hyperbolic term, whose values are transcendental functionals of h with no closed form.
