# K1082 — The one forcing step, and why the coarse menu raises the stakes

## Grace's degree-ladder — the target-innocent mechanism candidate
The spectral level k tracks the **degree** of the effective-action invariant, capped at rank:
| k | degree | invariant | physics |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | trace (λ₀=0) | the catastrophe Lyra excluded (Λ=α⁰=1) |
| 1 | 1 | linear ∫R = Einstein-Hilbert | **gravity** |
| 2 = rank | 2 | **determinant / norm form** | **the vacuum** (W = ½ log det Δ) |

The vacuum energy IS a determinant; the determinant is the degree-rank invariant of a rank-r Jordan algebra; so the vacuum saturates at k=rank. This **unifies Lyra's two halves** — "off λ₀" (degree-0 trace can't carry it) + "onto λ₂" (the vacuum is the degree-rank determinant) become one statement, a **degree ladder capped at rank** — and explains the a₀↔λ₂ crossing as *leading-rung ↔ top-degree*, read off rank=2 (K1056), not off 10⁻¹²². Genuinely target-innocent-*looking*, and the strongest handle yet.

## The one forcing step (Grace self-flagged the weld — credit)
"Determinant" does double duty:
- the **analytic** functional determinant det Δ (the effective action), vs
- the **algebraic** Jordan norm form (degree = rank).
Same name, **not obviously the same object** — asserting they're one is a Rule-11 weld (the c_2=11 trap Grace has walked into before, and caught herself here *before* Cal). So:
> **THE forcing step: does the functional determinant det Δ over the K-types reduce to the rank-2 Jordan norm form?**
Discharge it **target-blind** (Lyra's test: force-the-reduction-then-read-off-λ, *never* read-off-λ-then-justify-the-reduction) → the vacuum-at-k=rank placement is forced → both Λ and Ω promote to Derived on one identification. If it can't be discharged → both stay PD, honestly.

## Cal's coarse-menu — verified, and it cuts FOR the result
α^(4λ_k): **k=1 → 10⁻⁵¹, k=2 → 10⁻¹²⁰, k=3 → 10⁻²⁰⁵** — rungs ~69–85 dex apart, and **only k=rank=2 lands near the observed 10⁻¹²²** (2 dex vs 71 and 83). This is the **opposite** of the §184 exponent-menu (0.43 dex spacing, a form always in-band). So the placement menu is *coarse*: a **forced** k=rank landing near the answer is a genuine artifact, not a fish. This **raises the stakes** of the one question — if the reduction forces k=rank blind, its landing is real evidence; and it means the temptation to *fit the placement* is checkable (only one rung is even close, so a forced derivation can't quietly pick a convenient rung).

## Integrating my K1081 catch (the Cathedral collision)
K1081 flagged: k=rank (λ₂) is banked as **3rd-gen matter** (Cathedral L2), and the vacuum's banked home is k=0/L12 (Higgs/trivial mode). So "vacuum at k=2" is not simply "the vacuum IS 3rd-gen matter" — it's "the degree-2 effective-action invariant lives at the k=2 saturation level, where the heaviest matter also resides." **But the full coherence is still owed:** why do the degree-2 determinant (vacuum) and the 3rd-gen-matter residence coherently *coexist* at k=2 rather than conflict? — that two-tower coexistence is part of the placement derivation, not yet shown.
