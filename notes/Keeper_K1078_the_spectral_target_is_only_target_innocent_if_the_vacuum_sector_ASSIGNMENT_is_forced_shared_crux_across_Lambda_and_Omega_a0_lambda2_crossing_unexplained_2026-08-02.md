# K1078 — The spectral target is only target-innocent if the assignment is forced

Cal, Grace, and Lyra all turned the knife at the moment of convergence — exactly right (the discipline fires hardest when a finding feels most elegant; [[feedback_cal_27_fires_at_peak_convergence]]). Consolidating what they surfaced into one binding condition.

## The convergence is real — but read what it rests on
Three independent handles on the Λ suppression exponent:
1. **Transmutation** — ∫dg/β (Lyra's mechanism, forced by elimination).
2. **Spectral tower** — 4λ₂ = 56 (λ₂ = 14, a Bergman eigenvalue).
3. **Two-instance** — G = α^(4λ₁), the same tower one rung down.

If all three land on 56 *blind*, that looks like forcing, not a fit. **But every one of them assumes the vacuum sector sits on λ₂** (base-α, factor 4). That **assignment** is the load-bearing thing, and it is **not yet forced.**

## The form-provenance question (Cal §191) — the deepest fishing risk
Was α^(4λ₂) chosen *because* 4λ₂=56 → 10⁻¹¹⁹·⁷ lands near the observed 10⁻¹²²? If so, "compare ∫dg/β to 56" is **"compare to the answer in a spectral disguise."** So:
> **The spectral 4λ₂ is a CANDIDATE reference, NOT yet a target-innocent one.** It earns "target-innocent" only when the assignment (Λ↔λ₂, base-α, the "4") is forced *independently of the ~120-order target*. Two clean provenances for 56 (4λ₂ = 8·genus) help but do not settle it — Rule 11: many BST integers reach 56.

## The shared crux (Lyra) — one question, two threads
- **Λ:** why does the vacuum sit on the 2nd eigenvalue λ₂?
- **Ω:** why does dark energy = c₃ (3rd Chern class, lead c₃↔N_c)?
Both are the **same TYPE of open question — the vacuum-sector assignment** — and *possibly* (not certainly; λ₂ and c₃ are different indices, selected by rank vs color) one mechanism. **Force the vacuum-sector identification once and both close; leave it and both stay Partially Derived.** They don't just rhyme — they share the load-bearing gap.

## Grace's T2539 coherence flag — the a₀↔λ₂ crossing (LOAD-BEARING, resolve before banking)
The heat-ladder (T2539) puts **Λ on a₀** (the 0-th coefficient); the spectral tower puts **Λ on λ₂** (the 2nd eigenvalue). Meanwhile **G is a₁↔λ₁** (aligned, index 1↔1). So the a_k↔λ_k indexing **crosses exactly at a₀↔λ₂**:
- a₁ ↔ λ₁ (G) — aligned.
- a₀ ↔ λ₂ (Λ) — crossed.

a₀↔λ₀ can't be right (λ₀=0 → α⁰ = no suppression), so the alignment *must* break at a₀ — but **why it lands on λ₂ specifically is unexplained.** Grace's "leading-divergence (a₀) ↔ deepest-suppression (λ₂)" inversion is a **candidate hypothesis, not shown.** If the crossing is real geometry → the assignment is forced; if the crossing was chosen so 4λ₂ hits ~120 orders → it's the fit, one level up. **This must resolve before Λ banks** — otherwise a hostile referee asks "why does your indexing cross exactly where it needs to?" and there's no answer.

## The binding audit condition
**Neither Λ nor Ω promotes to Derived on a matched exponent/ratio alone.** Required, all target-blind:
1. ∫dg/β lands on 4λ₂ (mechanism), AND
2. the **assignment is forced** — the vacuum sector's index (λ₂ / c₃ / the a₀↔λ₂ crossing) derived from a mechanism, *not* selected to make 56 / 13-19 hit their targets.
A matched exponent with an unforced assignment stays **Partially Derived** (the honest tier). Lyra already committed to this ("a matched exponent alone won't do it") — ratified.

## The tool (Casey's D_IV⁵ + linear algebra + corpus)
The **index theorem / Chern-Weil** is the bridge connecting the three faces: **a_k (heat-kernel) ↔ λ_k (spectrum) ↔ c_k (Chern classes).** The assignment question is an index-theoretic computation on D_IV⁵:
- Does one index-theoretic object force the vacuum sector's index (simultaneously fixing λ₂, c₃, and the a₀↔λ₂ crossing)? → assignment forced, both threads close, crossing explained.
- Are the three faces chosen separately to hit their numbers? → fishing three ways, both stay Partially Derived.
This is the linear-algebra + corpus route to the one crux.

## Disposition
- Spectral 4λ₂ = **candidate reference**, target-innocent only once the assignment is forced.
- **One shared crux:** the vacuum-sector assignment (λ₂ / c₃ / a₀↔λ₂ crossing) — force it target-blind and both Λ and Ω close; else both stay Partially Derived.
- **Grace's a₀↔λ₂ crossing** is the load-bearing coherence gap — resolve before Λ banks.
- Tool: index theorem (a_k↔λ_k↔c_k). I rule the assignment forced-or-fit when it lands.
