# K1065a — The cc-magnitude observational target, pinned by convention (committed blind)

Supplement to K1065 (criterion 7: *pin the observable convention before citing any exponent*). Before Lyra/Elie produce a forward a₀-coefficient magnitude, I fix the target table from the numbers — so the exponent's match cannot be retrofitted by convention-shopping after the fact.

## The target, computed from consensus cosmology (H₀=67.4 km/s/Mpc, Ω_Λ=0.685)

| Convention | Dimensionless observable | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Λ·ℓ_P²  (cosmological constant as inverse-length², × Planck area) | **10⁻¹²¹·⁵⁵** |
| **B** | ρ_Λ / ρ_Planck  (vacuum energy-density ratio) | **10⁻¹²²·⁹⁵** |
| C | (E_vac/M_Planck), E-scale = ρ_Λ^(1/4) | 10⁻³⁰·⁷⁴  (= B as a density) |

- **Convention span A↔B = 1.40 dex.**
- **Measurement uncertainty within a convention ≈ 0.01 dex** (Λ ∝ Ω_Λ H₀²; δ from H₀ ±0.7% + Ω_Λ ±1% ≈ 0.011 dex).
- **Ratio: convention freedom (1.4 dex) ≈ 130 × measurement freedom (0.01 dex).** The choice of which dimensionless observable you form dwarfs the data's precision. Convention is the dominant fishing channel — exactly why criterion 7 exists.

## The smoking gun — which BST form "wins" depends on the convention

| BST form | Value | vs Conv. A (10⁻¹²¹·⁵⁵) | vs Conv. B (10⁻¹²²·⁹⁵) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7·exp(−282) = g·exp(−C₂(g²−rank)) [T1485] | 10⁻¹²¹·⁶³ | **0.08 dex** | 1.32 dex |
| exp(−281) = rank·N_max+g | 10⁻¹²²·⁰⁴ | 0.49 dex | 0.91 dex |
| α⁵⁶ (56=8g) | 10⁻¹¹⁹·⁶⁶ | 1.89 dex | 3.29 dex |

- Under **Convention A**, the T1485 form is the winner (0.08 dex — this IS the corpus's old "0.076 dex" claim).
- Under **Convention B**, the T1485 form is 1.32 dex off and exp(−281) is the closest.
- **So the "best" form flips with the convention.** A derivation that (a) picks the form, then (b) picks the convention that form matches, has used *two* selections to hit one number. That is not a derivation; it is form × convention shopping (K1065 criteria 1, 3, 7).

## The committed rule (blind, before the forward derivation)
Any BST form offered as the cc-magnitude MUST:
1. **Declare which dimensionless observable it equals** — Λ·ℓ_P² (A) or ρ_Λ/ρ_Planck (B) — with a *mechanism* reason for that identification (does the a₀ heat-trace coefficient produce Λ-as-curvature, i.e. A, or a vacuum energy density, i.e. B?), committed **before** the numerical comparison.
2. **Hit that convention's pinned value to stated dex**, obs-uncertainty (~0.01 dex) carried. No switching to whichever convention the chosen form happens to match.
3. Clear the rest of K1065 (target-blind exponent, mechanism-not-identity, coherence with the a₀/a₁/a₂ ladder + w=−1, forced coupling-smallness).

A forced a₀-coefficient derivation naturally answers (1) *first* — the heat-kernel structure dictates whether a₀ carries a curvature (Λ) or a density (ρ_Λ) — and then the exponent must land on that convention's number without further choice. If the mechanism doesn't fix the convention, the match isn't forced.

- cc-magnitude stays **IDENTIFIED** (K1063 Guard 2 / K1065).
- Target table above is the fixed scoring reference, committed blind 2026-08-01.
- The corpus's historical "0.076 dex" precision (T1485, was in the README, now softened to Identified) is **Convention-A-specific**; under Convention B the same form is 1.3 dex off. Not a forced derivation until (1)-(3) clear.
