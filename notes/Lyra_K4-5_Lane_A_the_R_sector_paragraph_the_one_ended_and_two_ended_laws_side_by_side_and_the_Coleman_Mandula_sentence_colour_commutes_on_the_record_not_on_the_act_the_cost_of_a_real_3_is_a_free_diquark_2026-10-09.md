## 3. The Coleman–Mandula sentence on colour (Lane A item 3; owed since S1038 Addendum). Tier C.
**Antecedent (Cal S1038):** the Standard Model's SU(3) commutes with the Poincaré and conformal groups; BST's colour acts "through so(5,2)_ℂ" (T1930/K1658a: the colour 3 is the short-root multiplicity, the vector of M = Z_K(a) ≅ SO(3) on a short root space). Owed: how such an SU(3) commutes with translations on quark fields, or that it does not and what it costs.

**Answer: it does not commute on the ACT, and it does commute on the RECORD — and the corpus already says which object carries which (K1926).**
- **On the act (position space, the write):** the 3 is M's vector on g_{e₁}. M = SO(3) is the little group of the boundary's spatial directions: it rotates translations into translations ([m, n] ⊂ n, acting on g_{e₁} as the vector). So the generators that act on the colour index there ARE spatial rotations. They commute with the time translation (a) and not with spatial translations — exactly as angular momentum does. **This is not a colour symmetry failing Coleman–Mandula; it is the rotation group, which Coleman–Mandula puts on the spacetime side.** A "colour SU(3)" acting through so(5,2)_ℂ generators on quark fields would be a complexified rotation, and it cannot be an internal symmetry: R141_L1 (no complex structure on the real 3-space) and F1033/K1671a (the geometry forces the NUMBER 3, not SU(3) on that space) say the same.
- **On the record (the commit):** K1926's one condition — the commit forgets the real structure — turns the real 3 into a complex 3 with symmetry SU(3) (Elliott's oscillator SU(3) is the standard kin). The record is a density matrix on a 3-dimensional complex space with no preferred real structure and no spatial directions left; SU(3) acts on that space as a tensor factor and so **commutes with every conformal generator, translations included.** Coleman–Mandula is then satisfied in the only place it applies (the asymptotic, committed sector), and the Standard Model's "SU(3) × Poincaré" is the record's symmetry, not the act's. TD 3a's "realized on the multiplicity space, not as a spacetime symmetry" is this sentence, with the multiplicity space named: the record.
- **The cost of NOT forgetting the real structure (the horn Cal asked for).** If colour stayed the real SO(3) 3-vector all the way to asymptotic states, SO(3) has the invariant δ_ab, so **3 ⊗ 3 contains a singlet** (the diquark q^a q^a), whereas for SU(3) 3 ⊗ 3 = 6 ⊕ 3̄ has none. A real-colour world has colour-neutral two-quark states as asymptotic particles: bosons of baryon number 2/3 and electric charge ±1/3 or ±2/3 (for uu, ud, dd: +4/3, +1/3, −2/3). **Free fractional charges are excluded** by Millikan-type and bulk-matter searches to roughly one per 10²⁰ nucleons (Grace pins the PDG "free quark searches" limit; I cite it as the kind of number, not the number). So the forgetting of the real structure is not a convenience; **it is what the absence of free fractional charge measures.** Exact structural content: the existence of a quadratic invariant on the colour 3 is the whole difference between SO(3) and SU(3) here, and the three-quark singlet ε_abc exists for both (which is why baryons do not decide it and diquarks do).
- **What this does not do:** supply the REASON the commit forgets the real structure (K1926's open condition). It supplies the falsifier that the condition protects, and names the object (the diquark) that would appear if it failed. **Tier C** (conditional on K1926), with the diquark cost exact.
