## Proof (qualitative — the forced content)
**Lemma (gauge invariance).** A Majorana mass term ψ^T C ψ (ΔL = 2) is SU(3)_color-invariant iff ψ is a color singlet. *Proof:* the term lies in ψ ⊗ ψ; for a color triplet, 3 ⊗ 3 = 6 ⊕ 3̄ contains no singlet, so the term is not color-invariant and is forbidden; for a color singlet it is trivially invariant. ∎

**(a) Quark sector — small CKM, forced.** Quarks are color triplets, so by the Lemma they admit *no* Majorana mass — they are Dirac-only, with mass from the single Higgs condensate. The up- and down-type masses arise from the *same* color-triplet modes (weak-isospin partners in the Peirce off-diagonal V₁₂, differing only by the internal T₃^R weight) coupled to that *one* condensate. Hence the up and down diagonalizing frames U_up, U_down are set by one operator on one set of modes, so they nearly coincide: **V_CKM = U_up† U_down ≈ 𝟙**, small.

**(b) Lepton sector — large PMNS, forced.** Leptons are color singlets, so the Lemma *permits* a Majorana neutrino mass; the odd embedding dimension g = 7 chirality-locks out a unitary Dirac partner ν_R (F413/K673), so the neutrino mass *must* be Majorana — generated by a **second** condensate (the Weinberg operator), distinct from the charged leptons' Dirac Higgs. Thus U_charged and U_ν are the diagonalizing frames of **two different operators**, hence generically misaligned (alignment is a measure-zero coincidence): **V_PMNS = U_charged† U_ν is large.**

**Conclusion.** Color-charge determines the number of independent mass-operators per sector — one for the colored quarks (Dirac only), two for the colorless leptons (Dirac + Majorana) — and the number of operators determines whether the sector's two frames align (small mixing) or not (large mixing). The CKM-small / PMNS-large contrast is therefore forced by color. ∎ (qualitative)
