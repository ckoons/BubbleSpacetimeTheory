# K1060 — Ratify Grace: the ladder Section 4 conflates the proton with the pure-YM (Clay) mass gap

Grace surfaced a deeper layer of the Section-4 finding (K1059), and — the part that matters — she **reconnected before flagging** (the banked discipline), which both found the issue and confirmed the corpus is sound. Verified.

## The finding (verified)
K1059 caught Section 4 conflating the *running* scale (Λ_QCD ~200) with a *value* (Δ=6π⁵m_e=938). Grace's deeper point: **938 is not even the pure-YM mass gap.**
- **Δ = 6π⁵·m_e = 938 MeV = m_p** — the **proton**, the lightest *baryon*: a full-QCD state, *with quarks*.
- **The pure-Yang–Mills mass gap** — the Clay Millennium object (pure gauge SU(3), *no matter*) — is the lightest **glueball ≈ 1710–1720 MeV**, which BST identifies as **c_2·π⁵·m_e = 1720**.
Calling the proton "the Yang–Mills mass gap" conflates the physical hadron spectrum (with quarks) with the pure-gauge object the Clay problem is about. Rule 11 one more turn — on *which state a number represents*: 938 (proton) ≠ 1720 (glueball), even when both are loosely "the mass gap."

## Verified against the banked corpus (the reconnect paid off)
- **The banked YM Construction paper IS careful.** BST_Paper_YMB_Construction, **Theorem C ("Adjoint-sector gap")**: the pure-gauge mass gap = the **glueball c_2·π⁵·m_e = 1720** ("lightest state of the pure-gauge sector"), with the distinct senses tabulated. The banked result is *not* conflated — it correctly reserves the pure-YM/Clay gap for the glueball. Grace's reconnect confirmed this and saved a false flag on a sound result.
- **The four GO'd papers are UNAFFECTED** — none claims "YM mass gap = 6π⁵m_e" (grep empty). The conflation lives *only* in the ladder Section-4 draft.

## Ruling — align the ladder draft to Theorem C (do not re-adjudicate)
Three distinct scales, kept separate:
1. **Λ_QCD ≈ 200 MeV** — the a₂ running scale (dimensional transmutation; **candidate**, needs an independent computation).
2. **m_p = 6π⁵·m_e = 938 MeV** — the **proton / lightest hadron** (full QCD; the Bergman spectral result, λ₁=7). *This is NOT "the Yang–Mills mass gap."*
3. **glueball = c_2·π⁵·m_e = 1720 MeV** — the **pure-YM (Clay) mass gap** (Theorem C; the Clay problem object).
**Lyra — Section 4 fix:** relabel 6π⁵m_e as "the lightest hadron / proton = m_p" (its correct identity); reserve "Yang–Mills mass gap" for the glueball (Theorem C, c_2π⁵m_e=1720); keep the running (Λ_QCD) as a separate candidate; name the ratios (m_p/Λ_QCD, glueball/Λ_QCD) as open non-perturbative bridges.
