## Part I — The reality obstruction: color is not space (THEOREM)

The most seductive shortcut in the program is "three colors = three spatial directions." As a statement about SU(3) it is **false**, and the impossibility is one line.

- **SU(3) has no real 3-irrep (Elie 5281).** The fundamental 3 has Frobenius–Schur indicator 0 (3 ≇ 3̄, complex); the smallest real irrep of SU(3) is the adjoint **8**. So "color SU(3) acting on three real spatial directions" is **not an available group action**. What acts on a real 3-space is SO(3).
- **The reality operator J cannot rescue it.** BST's spectral-triple real structure J is **charge conjugation** (color → anticolor — the operator that builds the Yukawa/quark-mass sector), an antilinear map 3 → 3̄. It is **not** a real structure *on* the 3 (that needs an invariant 3̄ ≅ 3 = FS ≠ 0, false). Numerically, over **20,000 Haar-sampled SU(3) elements, J commutes with the color action on exactly zero.** Selecting a real SO(3) ⊂ SU(3) would break the **exact, unbroken** color gauge symmetry — physically forbidden — and J, being color-equivariant, cannot pick a point in the 5-parameter family SU(3)/SO(3). The quaternionic back-door (Spin(5)=Sp(2), J²=−1) is shut on the KO-dimension sign: a real SO(3) needs J²=+1.

- **What "three colors are three directions" correctly means.** The spatial three is **V₁₂**, the **real off-diagonal Peirce component** of the Jordan structure, with **SO(V₁₂) = SO(3)**; its **dimension n_C − 2 = 3 is forced** by the Peirce decomposition (T2545, "V₁₂ (real-SO(3)-vector) irreducibility"). **The Peirce route explains the three-ness of space.** The color SU(3) is a *separate, complex* structure that merely shares the integer 3 (the #35 discipline: never identify objects by a shared integer).

**Consequence.** Color has nothing to do with the structure of spacetime. This is a theorem, it leads the paper, and it retroactively explains why every color→space route we tried failed.

## Part II — The (3,1) signature is matter-induced (T2565)

To *derive* the descent from the 5-dimensional substrate (R×S⁴) to observed (3,1) spacetime, the geometry+dynamics must supply a distinguished direction and a velocity/scale. It supplies neither. The honest tally is **four no-go theorems plus two not-shown identifications** — not "six closed routes."

**Four no-go theorems (DERIVED):**
1. **No preferred direction (5257).** No single-valued SO(5)-equivariant map with invariant inputs yields a distinguished R⁵ direction — the only SO(5)-fixed vector is 0 (Schur).
2. **No motion (5273).** The commit generator H_B is the central Casimir of K=SO(5)×SO(2); exp(−τH_B) commutes with SO(5) (‖[H_B, L_ab]‖ = 7.5×10⁻¹⁴). A Casimir multiplies; it does not push — the induced velocity is exactly 0.
3. **No scale (5276).** The commit-induced causal order is conformal, scale-free — it fixes Δθ/Δt ≤ 1 but never a length.
4. **Color is not space (5281, Part I).** The reality obstruction closes the color→space route as a theorem.

**Two not-shown identifications (open, not theorems and not claimed as closed):**
- **Confinement identification (§513):** three indivisible color commitments ↔ three spatial directions — not shown (and Part I shows the SU(3) reading is unavailable; a non-gauge reading is unproven).
- **The corpus 4→3 chain:** SO(5,2)→SO(4,2)→SO(3,1) — either acts on the spinor/record index (γ⁵, the T2555 phase-drop, not a spatial dimension) or is Casimir-forbidden (theorem 2); not shown to reduce a spatial dimension.

**Conclusion (DERIVED, negative).** The 5→(3,1) descent is not forcible from D_IV⁵ geometry+dynamics alone; it requires a matter/observer input. **The signature is relational/Machian — matter induces it** — a *located boundary*, not a failure, exactly as GR posits its manifold. **Wording binding: "geometry-alone," never "exhaustive"** — the self-consistency route (Part III) remains open.
