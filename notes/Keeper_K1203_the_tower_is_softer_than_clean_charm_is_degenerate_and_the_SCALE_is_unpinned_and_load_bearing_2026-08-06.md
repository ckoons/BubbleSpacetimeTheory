# K1203 — the tower is softer than "clean," charm is degenerate, and the scale is un-pinned

At the convergence peak (Cal #27 fires hardest here), the honest read: **strong up-quark anchor, but a scale-soft, charm-ambiguous, suggestive tower** — not the "clean corroboration" the team converged on.

## 1. The two framings are near-degenerate and disagree (I correct K1202)
My K1202 fixed the exponent (g=7) and varied the base → {n_C, rank, 1}. Elie's 5074 fixed the base (n_C=5) and varied the exponent → weights {g, N_c, 0}. They agree on up (5^{−7}) and top (1) but **differ on charm** (my 2^{−7}=1/128 vs his 5^{−3}=1/125) — and are numerically near-identical because **2⁷=128 ≈ 5³=125**. Elie's (one base, exponent = Lyra's weight) is mechanism-backed, so it **supersedes my K1202 framing.** Do not read the tower as {n_C, rank, 1} bases.

## 2. Charm is genuinely degenerate — and n_C^{−N_c} is not its best fit
At the reference scale, **y_charm = 1/136.97 ≈ α = 1/137 to 0.02%** — far better than 5^{−3}=1/125 (9.6% off) or 2^{−7}=1/128 (7% off). So charm does *not* cleanly sit at the n_C-power the tower story needs; the data (at this scale) prefers α. **The tower is suggestive, not established** — the middle rung is ambiguous.

## 3. The scale is un-pinned and load-bearing (#1 to resolve)
The toys used the up mass at ~2 GeV → d_u = 11.28, in the window. But the Yukawa runs: at the EW/condensate scale **v**, m_u runs down to ~1.1 MeV → **d_u ≈ 11.9–12.0, above [11.0,11.4]**. The running (~0.6 in d) exceeds the window half-width (0.2). So **5^{−7} lands in the window only at a ~2 GeV scale choice that hasn't been justified.** If BST's natural scale is v (where the condensate lives), the up-quark misses. This is currently a hidden knob — pin it first.

## What survives (this is not a teardown)
The up-quark **5^{−7} at 1.2%** is the cleanest single form and the anchor; **top = 1** is solid; up + top do suggest an n_C-power family; and **Lyra's mechanism** (fermion = spinor at weight g=7, one rung above the scalar at C_2=6, energy rank·g=14) is a real candidate. The correction is only to the *strength* of the tower claim and to the hidden scale.

## Why the tower mattered — and why it's a weaker discriminator now
Cal's guard: landing ≠ forcing. Concretely, d=11.28 also admits **N_c·ln(C_2·g) = 3·ln(42) = 11.21** (in window), and charm admits α. The tower was supposed to break these degeneracies by showing up is one rung of a coherent n_C-power family — but it only does so if the family is coherent, and charm's degeneracy breaks that. So the tower doesn't yet do the discriminating work.

## What would make it real
1. **Pin the scale** — decide from BST at which renormalization scale the overlap gives the Yukawa (v? the mass scale? the domain's own?), then run **all nine** Yukawas to that **one** scale and re-check. No more scattered-scale reference masses.
2. **Mechanism predicts the weights** — Lyra derives each up-generation's weight *forward* (up=g is the claim; charm=? top=?) and we compare to the scale-pinned data. Never fit charm to N_c and call it clean.

## Tier
Up-quark y_u = n_C^{−g} = 5^{−7}: **Identified** (anchor, 1.2% at reference scale), now carrying an explicit **scale caveat**. Tower: downgraded from "clean corroboration" to **suggestive**. **Not Derived.** Forcings owed: Lyra's weight-derivation (predict all three), the ground-shell gap forward, and now the **scale-pin**. K1202's {n_C, rank, 1} framing corrected. Nothing banks Derived.
