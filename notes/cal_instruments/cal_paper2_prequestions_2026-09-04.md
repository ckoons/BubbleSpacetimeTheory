Q1 PRIORITY (Fisk). The branched double cover Σ of the fullerene's dual triangulation, branched at the 12 pentagons, is an EVEN triangulation (odd degree 5 lifts to 10) of genus 5 (Riemann–Hurwitz: 2−2g = 4−12). Fisk 1977: an even triangulation of a closed surface is 3-colourable iff a winding-number obstruction in H¹(Σ;ℤ₃) vanishes. So "confined ⟺ Σ is 3-vertex-colourable ⟺ [c]=0 ∈ H¹(Σ;ℤ₃)" IS Fisk's criterion applied to this Σ. The paper must cite Fisk and say what is new: (i) the sign/cocycle c comes from a 4-colouring's height and is colouring-independent; (ii) the count m = 60+6D; (iii) the interpretation. If the 3-colourability criterion itself is presented as new → priority error, reject.

Q2 (Mohar–Salas). Degree of a 4-colouring mod 12 is a Kempe invariant on 3-colourable triangulations. If the paper names a "charge class" of a 4-colouring or speaks of Kempe classes, the referee asks: is the charge class a function of Fisk's degree? The paper must either relate them or not claim a new Kempe invariant.
