## One-Floor Lemma — where it is construction-guaranteed, and the one place it has content
(a) Cocycle orphans (θ = 0, [ℓ] ≠ 0): the derived cover of ker[ℓ] pulls [ℓ] back to zero BY DEFINITION (restriction of a homomorphism to its own kernel); θ stays 0. Realization one floor up is a tautology; sheets = |image [ℓ]| ∈ {2, 4} likewise. Grace's cocycle rows are a check, not evidence. Empty-confirmation trap: named.
(b) Transport orphans (θ ≠ 0): the θ-cover S_θ kills θ by definition (3 sheets, connected). The CONTENT is [ℓ̃] = 0 for the labeling ℓ̃ that now exists on S_θ. Grace's half handles cycles outside ker θ (3:1 lift, ℓ + ρℓ + ρ²ℓ = 0). The owed half: cycles in ker θ.
Referee's sketch for Lyra to check (torus only): the deck generator g of S_θ → T² satisfies g*ℓ̃ = ρ^{±1}ℓ̃ (ℓ̃∘g is a labeling of the same record, so equals ρ^j ℓ̃ by R′ step 2; j = 0 would descend ℓ̃ to T², contradicting θ ≠ 0). On a TORUS a deck translation is isotopic to the identity, so g* = id on H¹(S_θ;V); hence [ℓ̃] = ρ[ℓ̃], so (1 + ρ)[ℓ̃] = 0, and 1 + ρ is invertible on V ((1+ρ)ρ = ρ + ρ² = 1). Therefore [ℓ̃] = 0 — One-Floor is FORCED on the torus, and 906/906 is confirmation of a theorem, not evidence for a conjecture.
(c) Where the argument dies: genus ≥ 2. The deck transformation of an unbranched cyclic cover of a genus-g surface acts NON-trivially on H₁ for g ≥ 2 (Chevalley–Weil: each nontrivial character has multiplicity 2g − 2 over ℂ; zero exactly at g = 1, which is why the torus works). The ρ-eigenspace of H¹(S_θ;V) can be nonzero, so [ℓ̃] ≠ 0 is not excluded. Pre-registered can-fail test: a genus-2 (or higher) triangulation with a transport orphan still cocycle-obstructed on its own 3-sheet cover — realized only at 6 or 12 sheets.
KILL NUMBER for the lemma as stated ("closed orientable surface"): ONE record with θ ≠ 0 whose S_θ-lift has [ℓ̃] ≠ 0 (floor-2 occupancy > 0; sheets 6 or 12 appear). Torus: predicted 0 by (b) — a zero that is a THEOREM's zero, not a control's. Genus 2: no prediction; the honest pre-registration is "unknown; the torus mechanism is absent."
Recommendation: state One-Floor as a TORUS lemma (provable by (b)) unless a genus-independent argument exists; the "closed orientable surface" version is a conjecture with an identified failure mechanism and no data.

## T2574 off-sphere clause — proposed rewording (realizability, not multiplicity)
"On any closed orientable surface a REALIZED sign record determines its coloring up to A₄ (Lemma R′; uses connectedness only). What positive genus adds is not multiplicity but non-realizability, in two stages: a Heawood-closed record is realized iff its transport class θ(ε) ∈ H¹(S;ℤ₃) vanishes and, given that, its labeling class [ℓ] ∈ H¹(S;V) vanishes. On the sphere both groups are zero, so closed ⟺ realized (Lemma R). Off the sphere the two obstructions are staged, not a product; each is killed on the derived cover of its own kernel (the second by construction; the first's residual V-class vanishes on the torus by the deck-isotopy argument, and is open at genus ≥ 2)."
