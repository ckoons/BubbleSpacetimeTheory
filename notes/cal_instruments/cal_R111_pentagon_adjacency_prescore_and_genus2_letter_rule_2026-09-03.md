## B. The pentagon-adjacency series — what is derived, what is measured, what the lemma may say
Objects: fullerene dual = sphere triangulation with degrees {5, 6}; its twelve degree-5 vertices are the dislocations; N_p = number of adjacent pentagon pairs (edges of the pentagon-adjacency graph); L = ℤ-span of dislocation height differences; period lattice P = 2L (Elie 5636b, measured on all 1,550 + 235); charge = x + y mod 3; A, B, C all have charge 1; the charge kernel K = {x + y ≡ 0 mod 3} is ONE of the four index-3 sublattices of ℤ².
DERIVED (Lyra 4(a), two lines): p, q adjacent odd vertices ⟹ c_q − c_p = ±L(ℓ(pq)) has charge ±1 ⟹ L ⊄ K, for EVERY colouring. Direction: adjacency ⟹ L leaves K. Nothing in it says what happens when no pair is adjacent.
MEASURED (Elie 5641, C₆₀ dual, 3,190/3,190): every rank-2 lattice IS K (1,950); rank 0 on 1,200; rank 1 on 40; no full lattice. This is UNIVERSALITY on one graph, and it is stronger than the prediction (which was existence: "index-3 drops either first appear at n = 32 or a second mechanism excludes them"). Universality is a NEW claim: "on an IPR dual every colouring has L ⊆ K." The adjacency lemma does not give it (a non-adjacent pair at distance two has charge (±1) + (±1) ∈ {0, ∓1}, nothing forces the cancellation). Derivation owed; C₆₀'s icosahedral symmetry is the suspect if C₇₀ disagrees.
THE LEMMA AS KEEPER TYPED IT ("index of L is 1 iff N_p > 0") IS ALREADY FALSE IN FRAME as a per-colouring statement: Elie 5636 has 71 colourings on nine fullerene duals with N_p > 0 whose lattice DROPS (rank 1 on 44, index 2 on 27). Adjacency kills the ODD-index drop only; 2-power drops live on colourings where the twelve carry two colours. Honest shape, asymmetric:
  (L1) N_p ≥ 1 ⟹ for every colouring, 3 ∤ [ℤ² : L] when rank 2, and L ⊄ K in general. DERIVED. Per-colouring, universal.
  (L2) N_p = 0 (IPR) ⟹ for every colouring L ⊆ K (so rank ≤ 1 or index 3·(2-power)). MEASURED on C₆₀ only. Derivation owed.
  (L3) Rank 0 ("all twelve at one height", hence one colour) ⟹ N_p = 0. DERIVED in one line: adjacent vertices have different colours, colours are heights mod 2, so adjacent dislocations have different heights. Hence rank-0 colourings are IMPOSSIBLE on every C₄₆–C₅₈ dual and possible only on IPR duals — a guaranteed zero on the series, a check not a finding.
"Does ONE adjacent pair suffice?" — for (L1), YES, for every colouring (the lemma is per pair, no colour condition: the pair's labels differ because adjacent vertices differ in colour, and any label has charge 1). For "index 1", NO: index 1 needs the 2-power drops absent too, which is the two-colour-on-twelve condition (5636), colouring-dependent.
