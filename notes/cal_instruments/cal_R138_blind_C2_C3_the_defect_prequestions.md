## C2 — the audit Keeper asked for explicitly, applied the way I applied it to myself this morning

H1. **TAUTOLOGY OF THE BOUNDARY CONDITION, in two independent senses. Neither is a defect; both bound what may be claimed.**
**(i) The resolution of the identity is inherited from the ambient sphere and is domain-independent.** Š = {e^{it}x : x ∈ S⁴} sits inside the unit sphere S⁹ of ℂ⁵, so |z|² = 1 on Š — as it does on S⁹ itself and on every subset of it. Σ_u M_{|z_u|²} = M_{|z|²} = I therefore holds for the coordinate tuple of ANY orthonormal frame on ANY subset of the unit sphere. Sweep the domain: every bounded symmetric domain whose Shilov boundary lies in the unit sphere has the same property, with the same proof, for the same reason. **So "the write tuple is a resolution of the identity" is true, and it is not a fact about D_IV⁵, about the dictionary, or about the writes — it is the statement that the boundary is where the coordinates have unit length.** It cannot be a signature and must not be written as one.
**(ii) The branching weights were DEFINED as normalised squared norms, and that is the Born formula.** P(light) = ‖Y′_{k+1}‖²/‖(u·z)Y_k‖² is a ratio of squared norms by construction (R134 L1, T2629's proof). "These are Born probabilities of the write tuple's POVM" restates the definition of how they were computed. It is a correct restatement and a good one — it names the object — but it derives nothing that was not already in the arithmetic.
H2. **What it therefore does and does not buy.** *Does:* it removes the SEARCH for a commitment effect. If the outcome of a write is already the measurement outcome, then there is no separate observable to identify, and R136/R137's whole family problem — Keeper's three columns, Lyra's two-variable family, my Σ_u W_u*W_u, her 1 − T, the two constructions that disagreed at the vacuum this morning — **dissolves rather than being solved.** That is a real gain and it is the round's best sentence. *Does not:* derive the Born rule. T754/Gleason answers why probability must be quadratic in the state; this reading uses quadratic norms from the start. **So none of T754, T2401 or the K67 chain becomes redundant** — they become unnecessary FOR THIS STEP, which is a different claim, and the row must say which. A dictionary that assumes the norm-square is a probability has not derived that it is.
H3. **Applying to this reading the treatment I applied to my own uniqueness claim at 10:37.** My §927 said the effect is constructed, not chosen, hence one operator; a second construction appeared and I struck it. The parallel strike here: **"no chosen effect, no identification, no free parameter" is right about the free parameter and misleading about the achievement — there was no free parameter in the branching to begin with.** The honest form: *the branching weights are the outcome probabilities of the write tuple's own resolution of the identity; this is a naming, not a derivation; what it eliminates is the commitment effect, which existed only because the push was treated as a separate event.*
H4. **The word "commitment," audited.** In K1860 it does work by CONTRAST: it names the one step that is not a write. Remove the contrast and the word is a synonym for "write outcome," and every sentence built on the contrast is empty — not false, empty. **Test for the row: take each sentence containing "commitment," replace it with "write outcome," and see whether the sentence still says anything.** Where it does not, the word was carrying the distinction, not a fact. My §931 C3 said commitment is not sharp enough to have a direction because nothing branches on it; Lane B gives the second half — it is not sharp enough to have a referent either, because it is not a separate event.
