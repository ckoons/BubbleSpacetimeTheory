## E2 — the redshift channel, derived
Observed redshift under the boost: z' ≈ z − (1+z)βμ. In an observed-z top-hat bin [z₁, z₂) with mean m_i and dN/dz n(z), the ℓ = 1 moment of the field z' (per-cell mean of z' fitted as ⟨z'⟩_c = m + R·n̂_c over unmasked cells, weights n_c/s_i², s_i² the bin's within-bin variance of z') is **R_i = −g_i β û** with
**g_i = (1 + m_i) − [(1+z₂) n(z₂)(z₂ − m_i) − (1+z₁) n(z₁)(z₁ − m_i)] / N_i** — the Doppler shift of every member minus the membership exchange at the edges (entering at z₂ raises the mean, leaving at z₁ raises it too). Aberration and the flux boost move counts, not the mean z, at first order. Sign: the apex is blueshifted, so R is antiparallel to û. g_i is computed from the observed-z distribution BEFORE any dipole (the same rule as x_i, α_i, B_i). The null bin has g₅ ≠ 0 (the count channel's f + B ≈ 0 does not transfer): the redshift channel keeps its lever arm where the count channel loses it.
Noise, computed first: σ_R ≈ √(3/N_i)·s_i/√(f_sky-weighted) ⟹ σ_v = σ_R c/g_i. With N_i ≈ 2–3×10⁵, s_i ≈ 0.14 (width-0.5 top hat) to 0.4 (the top bin), g_i ≈ 2–4: **σ_v ≈ 40–150 km/s per bin.** Cal's envelope is 55–110.
