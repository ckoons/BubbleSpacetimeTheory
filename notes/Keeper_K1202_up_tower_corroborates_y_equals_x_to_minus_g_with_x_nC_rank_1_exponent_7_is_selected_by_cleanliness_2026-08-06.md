# K1202 — the up tower corroborates: y = x^{−g}, x = {n_C, rank, 1}, and exponent 7 is selected by cleanliness

I ran the corroboration myself rather than wait. **The up-quark's y_u = n_C^{−g} = 5^{−7} is the first rung of a clean weight-7 tower**, and the tower independently supports genus = 7.

## The numbers (y_f = √2·m_f/v, d_f = −ln y_f, x_f = exp(d_f/g))
| | d_f | x = exp(d/7) | ≈ | 1−t² | Yukawa dev |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| up | 11.278 | 5.01 | n_C = 5 | 0.040 ≈ 1/25 | 1.2% |
| charm | 4.920 | 2.02 | rank = 2 | 0.245 ≈ 1/4 | 7.0% |
| top | 0.005 | 1.00 | 1 | 0.998 ≈ 1 | 0.5% |

So **y_f = x_f^{−g} with x = {n_C, rank, 1}**, shared exponent g = 7. The addresses are 1−t² = {1/n_C², 1/rank², 1} — the **top sits at the center (t=0, y≈1)**, the known top-Yukawa≈1. Base deviations are ~1-2%; the Yukawa deviations are larger (the exponent 7 amplifies a 1% base error to ~7%).

## Exponent 7 is selected by cleanliness (corroborates the genus itself)
With exponent **7**: bases = {5.01, 2.02, 1.00} — clean. With exponent **5** (my wrong FK-5, K1200): bases = {9.54, 2.68, 1.00} — up and charm are not BST integers. **The tower only resolves into clean integers at exponent = g = 7.** That's evidence for Casey's genus = 7 coming from the whole tower at once, not just the up-quark.

## Honest caveats (both directions)
- **Scale/scheme:** the Yukawas run; I used reference masses at inconsistent scales. Running to one scale shifts by a few % — comparable to the ~1-2% base agreements. So this is clean at the **few-% level, not sub-%.**
- **Retrofit:** nearest-of-{1..7} hits within ~7% often by chance; per-fermion this is weak. The strength is **three-in-a-row** forming {n_C, rank, 1} at a shared exponent.
- The exponent 7 is imposed (Casey's genus), not derived; the sequence {n_C, rank, 1} is fit-clean, not forced.

## The other towers (reported, not banked)
- **Leptons — suggestive:** x ≈ {C_2, N_c, rank} = {6.17, 2.88, 1.93} for {e, μ, τ}, within ~3-5% base. A descending-integer lead, too loose to bank.
- **Down — not this pattern:** x = {4.49, 2.93, 1.70}, no clean sequence at exponent 7. **Consistent** with the corpus boundary-vs-bulk split (K1185): the even/boundary up tower is clean x^{−g}; the odd/bulk down and lepton towers are different (FK ladders). A feature, not a failure.
