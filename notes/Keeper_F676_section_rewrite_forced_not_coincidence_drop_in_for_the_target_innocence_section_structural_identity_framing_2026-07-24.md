## Why this is a result: forced by the rank, with nothing tuned

They sit at the **three natural strata of the single rank-2 domain D_IV⁵**, and those three strata are fixed — in number and in kind — by the rank alone. This is a forced structure, not a numerical coincidence, and the distinction matters.

**The domain's own root data fixes both objects.** From the root system of D_IV⁵ (rank r = 2, characteristic multiplicity a = n − 2 = 3):
- the **ρ-vector** is ρ = (n/2, (n−2)/2) = (5/2, 3/2);

These are two readings of *one* root system, so their agreement where they overlap is an **identity, not a coincidence**: the middle position, 3/2, is simultaneously ρ₂ = (n−2)/2 and the Wallach edge a/2 = (n−2)/2 — the same quantity. We state this plainly rather than dress it as two independent facts converging.

**The genuine, target-innocent content is the *structure*, in three parts:**
1. **The count is forced.** A rank-2 domain has exactly two discrete Wallach points and one continuous regime — **three support strata, r + 1 = 3.** A rank-3 domain would have four; a rank-1 domain, two. The number three is the rank plus one, and the rank is fixed by BST's five integers. Nothing is chosen.
2. **The generations occupy exactly these strata.** BST assigns each generation a support-position from the field's localization structure (T2517), for reasons internal to the mass/support problem and **with no reference to the Wallach set**. Those positions — {5/2, 3/2, 0} — occupy the three strata one each: the electron in the continuous regime (5/2 > 3/2), the muon at the discrete edge (3/2), the tau at the discrete bottom point (0). The tau's position, 0, is *not* a ρ-component at all (ρ has only two); it is the Wallach bottom point k₀ = 0 — a genuinely separate fact from the ρ-arithmetic.
3. **No mass number enters either object.** Neither the ρ-positions nor the Wallach set uses a measured mass. The classification "which generation is in which phase" is therefore **target-innocent** in the sense BST requires before a structure counts: it could not have been reverse-engineered from the spectrum, because the spectrum was never used.

**So the result is stronger than a coincidence: it is forced.** The rank of the one domain fixes that there are exactly three strata; the localization structure places the three generations one per stratum; and no free parameter or measured value appears anywhere. "Forced by the geometry" is a firmer claim than "two derivations happen to agree," and it is the honest one.
