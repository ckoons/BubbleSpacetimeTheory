# K964 — Ceiling/Value Decomposition of IDENTIFIED

**Casey's insight (2026-07-28):** for m_t, rule "**Ceiling: DERIVED / Value: IDENTIFIED**" — and (his meta-point) this is a general way to make many IDENTIFIED claims more honest. Cal reached the identical split independently (§115), which is the convergence signal it's real.

## The refinement (NOT a new tier — a decomposition within the two-axis system)

A flat **IDENTIFIED** reads to a hostile physicist as "they matched a number." But most BST Identifieds are not that: the geometry **forces a real constraint** (the *ceiling* / structure), and only a **residual knob** is unforced (the *value*). Report these as two claims:

> **Ceiling: DERIVED** (the forced, falsifiable constraint) / **Value: IDENTIFIED** (the unforced residual).

This credits what is actually derived, and it locates the gap precisely instead of hiding it under one label. It also **retro-fits the mixing sector**, which has said "*mechanism DERIVED, exact form IDENTIFIED*" since 2026-07-19 — the Ceiling/Value split is the general form of that language (Structure-DERIVED / numerator-IDENTIFIED). Corpus reconnection, not a new invention.

## ★ THE THREE EARNING CONDITIONS (the anti-laundering guard — all three required)

The split is a laundering risk ("name a derived ceiling to dress up a fitted value"). Cal named the failure mode; these conditions are the teeth. A Ceiling/Value split is **earned per item ONLY when all three hold:**

1. **Target-innocent forcing route** (Cal's demand) — the ceiling rests on a geometric/topological mechanism that predates or is independent of the value. A route found *after* knowing the number is not a ceiling; it's FITTED wearing a Derived costume.
2. **★ The ceiling is falsifiable WITHOUT reference to the value** (the Keeper teeth) — you must be able to state an experiment that kills the ceiling regardless of the residual value. *m_t passes:* "no elementary fermion above ~174 GeV" (y_t ≤ 1, Cauchy-Schwarz §49, falsifier A#7) kills the ceiling independent of the top's exact mass. If no value-independent test of the ceiling exists, there is no real ceiling → flat IDENTIFIED.
3. **The residual value-gap is named and quantified** — *m_t:* the ~0.6–0.8% between y_t ≈ 0.99 (m_t = 173) and 1 (174). No hand-waving the size of the knob.

Miss any one → **flat IDENTIFIED, or FITTED.** Meeting all three → the split is airtight and survives a hostile read: *we claim the bound we forced; we do not claim the residual we didn't.*
