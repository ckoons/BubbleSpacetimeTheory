## Fix 1 (REQUIRED, moderate) — the falsifier paragraph is stale vs the direct-fit
Line 22 says "opposite DESI's present best fit, at **low significance**." Two problems:
- **"Low significance" is factually wrong and self-undercutting.** Grace verified DESI DR2 prefers wₐ<0 at **2.8–4.2σ** (arXiv:2503.14738) — moderate-to-substantial. A reviewer checks; it reads as spin.
- **It omits the direct-fit (K1145).** BST is *degenerate with the phantom fit in D_M* but makes a *distinct ~2–3% prediction in the radial D_H(z)* — that distinct D_H prediction is the honest, stronger claim.
**Rewrite** centered on the D_H(z) prediction (no phantom crossing), note the D_M degeneracy, state the tension at 2.8–4.2σ, sharp non-escapable falsifier — and **sync with the standalone A1 pre-registration** so the two tell one story.

## Fix 2 (REQUIRED, minor-moderate) — the summary sentence flattens the spin-statistics tier
Line 20: "the shape **forces** the Born rule and the reason fermions exist…". Putting "the reason fermions exist" under **forces** contradicts the bullet's own tier (spin-statistics = **Identified**, assignment open). Reword so **forces** governs the Derived items (Born, strong-CP, Λ-smallness) and spin-statistics is "gives/identifies the reason fermions exist as its own native structure, one step still open." Don't flatten the tier in the punch-line.
