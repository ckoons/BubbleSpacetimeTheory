## 2. Bias — the one factor the pin leaves free
D_cls ∝ b (C₁ ∝ b²). **Laurent et al., *Clustering of quasars in SDSS-IV eBOSS: study of potential systematics and bias determination*, arXiv:1705.04718 (abstract, fetched): b_Q = 2.45 ± 0.05 at z̄ = 1.55** (journal reference JCAP 07 (2017) 017 from memory — [pin owed]). At that bias: **D_cls ≈ 0.00024 × 2.45 = 5.9 × 10⁻⁴.**

## 3. Direction of the depth correction (CatWISE z̄ 1.2 → Quaia z_med 1.48)
The clustering dipole is generated by the nearest structure and FALLS with sample depth: Tiwari & Nusser, *Revisiting the NVSS number count dipole*, JCAP03(2016)062, arXiv:1509.02532 — abstract: the model NVSS dipole "is mostly (70%) due to structures within z ≲ 0.1"; text (lines 497–499): "The dipole signal … is largely generated at lower redshifts, z ≲ 0.1 by fluctuations with wave numbers k ∼ 0.01 h Mpc⁻¹." Gibelyou & Huterer, *Dipoles in the Sky*, MNRAS 427, 1994–2021 (2012), arXiv:1205.6476 (lines 1520–1523): at the depths of high-z tracers "the local-structure dipole should have become comparable to, or even smaller than, the kinematic dipole." So at equal bias, Quaia's deeper dN/dz gives D_cls ≤ the CatWISE value. Quaia's own dN/dz through Eq. (A1)–(A4) is a five-line camb computation on the (v) record and I have NOT run it (it is not asked; the pin is the primary's number).
