**The mechanism in the note's own terms.** A record has two factors: the Rac factor (where a source sits in the count) and the clock factor (what it reads). R148 said a boost is read by windowing one factor and reading the other; the clock-windowed clock moment cancels, the Rac-windowed clock moment survives. The channel gains a column, not a degree of freedom; the separation still lives only in the count channel's profile, and the joint fit stays (b, A, ŵ), four unknowns as before.
