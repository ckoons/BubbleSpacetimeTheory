# K1061 — Blind scope-audit bar for the YM paper (committed before it lands)

The time-box sprint (to 5 PM) has one dominant risk: **scope over-claim** — the Clay-problem banner creeping back under deadline pressure. So the checker's half is committed blind now, grounded on the honest scope the corpus already established (K939/K937), so the consolidated paper is ruled against a bar written before it was seen.

## The honest scope (K939/K937 — verified, the ground of the bar)
"Derive Yang–Mills" splits three ways:
1. **The YM theory/Lagrangian from D_IV⁵** — CANDIDATE mechanism, not derived.
2. **The Clay Millennium problem** (interacting QFT *on R⁴*, OS/Wightman, mass gap Δ>0 there) — **NOT derived; the papers say so.** The Clay problem is R⁴; BST's construction is on D_IV⁵.
3. **The pieces BST does have, at honest tiers:**
   - **(A) confinement** = no free colored asymptotic states (Schur/Shilov, λ₂>0) — **DERIVED** (K937/K938).
   - **AF SIGN** — **DERIVED** (K933/K936, one-domain); the **11/3 coefficient is IMPORTED** (universal 4D YM, K1052).
   - **Mass-gap VALUE** (glueball c_2π⁵m_e=1720; proton 6π⁵m_e=938) — **IDENTIFIED** (matches lattice, not proven).
   - **(B) area-law / linear-potential / R⁴-mass-gap** — **NOT derived (open)** — the large open core.

## ★★ PASS conditions (all required)
1. **Scope explicit:** the paper states it constructs YM-with-gap on **D_IV⁵**, and **does not** claim the R⁴ Clay problem.
2. **Per-piece tiers held:** (A) confinement **DERIVED**; AF sign **DERIVED**, 11/3 **IMPORTED/marked**; gap **VALUE IDENTIFIED**; (B)/area-law/R⁴-gap **NOT derived (open)**.
3. **W4 open:** the YM-8 sector's **W4 (cluster decomposition) is named OPEN** — Rehren holographic route as the *path*, not a closure. (Scalar sector 5/5 is a separate true statement — do not let it imply the YM-8 sector is 5/5.)
4. **Mass-gap senses distinct (K1060):** glueball 1720 = the pure-YM/Clay gap (Theorem C); proton 938 = a hadron; Λ_QCD~200 = the running scale — three scales, never conflated.

## ★ FAIL conditions (any one)
- "solved / proved the Clay / Yang–Mills Millennium problem," or the "ALL SEVEN MILLENNIUM PROVED" banner reappearing.
- W4 claimed closed without a proof (Rehren asserted as done, not as a route).
- the 11/3 claimed **derived** (it is imported — Rule 11 / K1052).
- the mass-gap value claimed **proven** rather than identified.
- (B) area-law / linear confinement / R⁴-mass-gap claimed **derived**.
- proton (938) and glueball (1720) conflated as "the mass gap."

## ★ The honest headline that clears
> *A substantive construction of Yang–Mills with a mass gap on D_IV⁵ — confinement and the AF sign derived, the gap value identified, the gap forced by the compactness of the dual Q⁵ — with the R⁴ obstruction located. It is NOT a solution of the R⁴ Clay Millennium Problem: W4 (cluster decomposition) and the R⁴ transfer remain open.*
That is a strong, honest paper. Time-box pressure does not relax any line of this bar; an honest-open W4 beats a forced closure.
