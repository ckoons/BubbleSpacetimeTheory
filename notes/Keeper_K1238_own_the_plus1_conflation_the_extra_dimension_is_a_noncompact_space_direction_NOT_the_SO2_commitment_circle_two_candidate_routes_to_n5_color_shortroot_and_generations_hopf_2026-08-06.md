# K1238 — own the +1-conflation; the extra dimension is a non-compact space direction, NOT the SO(2) commitment circle; two candidate routes to n=5

Three team self-catches this round, and one of them (Grace's) caught an error in my own K1236. Own it first.

## The correction (Grace's catch, ruled correct)
K1236 said *"the +1 = the extra 5th complex dimension = the commitment/complex circle (the SO(2))."* **That fuses two different objects.** Grace: **the off-by-one lives entirely in the n (the space count), never in the 2 (the time/conformal factor). SO(4,2) and SO(5,2) have the same SO(2).** So the **commitment circle (the compact SO(2)) is already present at n=4** — it *cannot* be the extra dimension. The **+1 (n: 4→5) is a non-compact, holographic/radial SPACE direction**, not the compact circle. Fusing them re-smuggles the +1 in new clothes.

## The crux, reframed
K1236 asked "why does recording force one extra dimension = the commitment circle." That's now wrong — **recording/commitment (the circle) is already satisfied at n=4.** The honest crux: **why does the structure force a 5th non-compact SPACE direction (n: 4→5)?** And the two surviving routes both answer through **space/color/generation** structure, not time/recording — consistent with "the off-by-one is in the n, not the 2."

## Two candidate routes to n=5 (both narrowed, neither closed; holography is dead)
- **Route A — color / short-root (Grace, = my K1237, converged).** D_IV^n has short-root multiplicity n−2; T2545 identifies that short-root space with the N_c=3 color triplet; backward, n−2 = 3 ⟹ n=5. **Open premise (Grace's discipline, sharper than my K1237):** T2545 was banked *given* n=5, so reading it backward needs **"color IS the short-root space" independently forced, not recognized after the fact.** Until exhibited, a lead.
- **Route B — generations / Hopf (Lyra, self-caught).** 3 generations = the odd loops; Lyra *counted* before shipping and found 3 generations fit **n ∈ {5,6}**, not just 5. Picking 5 over 6 needs a **third fact**: spinors closing into a clean (Hopf-type) fibration — works at n=5, fails at n=6. **Open.**

## The honest three-tier picture (Lyra's, ratified)
1. Record-requirement (commitment = Hermitian) → the **family** Type IV.
2. 3-generations → **n ∈ {5,6}**.
3. Picking 5 over 6 → a **third fact** (the Hopf/spinor-fibration condition, or Route A's color=short-root).

So **n_C=5 is narrowed hard — all domains → Type IV → {5,6} — but not forced.** Less than we wanted an hour ago, exactly what's true.

## The three guards (where a re-smuggle would hide)
- **(Cal)** the exhibit must *derive* the +1 from {4D + time + commitment} on the 4D side — **not** take n_C=5 or any 5D structure as input (else circular).
- **(Grace)** do **not** fuse the two "+1"s — the compact SO(2) commitment circle (already at n=4) vs the non-compact 5th space direction (the actual +1). Pin which one any exhibit is about.
- **(premise)** a backward-read of a banked theorem (T2545 → n=5) needs its premise (color=short-root) **independently forced**, not post-hoc.

## What stands (crisply separated)
- **Family filter** (only Type IV has a Lorentzian boundary; competitors Type IV in disguise) — sound, but selects the **family, not n.**
- **T2545's (3,1) signature** — banked, fire-independent.
- **"n_C, rank are structural (not fitted)"** — banked, and **weaker than** "n_C=5 is forced" (which it is not, today). Keeping those two apart is what keeps the pile honest.
