# K1642 — The time nature of SM particles: a table, a load-bearing fork, and yes it's a program

Casey asked the generative question: if the half-integer charge is what rides time, *which* SM particles ride the time circle, and what does it say about the family? It opens a real vein — and the round handed a fork that gates the whole thing.

## The load-bearing fork (flag it — it decides the table)
Elie (Toy 5321) derived the closure: **T = 2π/gcd{level-differences}, 4π ⟺ gcd = 1/2.** Adopt this referee-safe form (it replaces "all charges ∈ ½ℤ," which an all-integer spectrum satisfies while closing at 2π). But Elie and Cal now **disagree on the mechanism**, and the disagreement is physical:
- **Cal (absolute closure):** a *single* half-odd tower forces 4π — a charge-3/2 state returns to −1 at 2π, identity only at 4π. So the scalar tower alone forces the double cover.
- **Elie (projective, gcd-of-differences):** a single tower's differences are integers → it closes at π/2π *regardless* of half-oddness; **only the cross-tower Rac–Di gap of 1/2 gives gcd = 1/2 → 4π.** So the 4π is a *joint* property of the scalar+spinor pair, and — since the scalar-spinor weight gap is *always* 1/2 in any d — it is **dimension-independent**, not specifically odd-n_C.

**The resolution gate:** QM states are **rays** (physical states differ by a global phase), so the *physical* closure is **projective** — which points to **Elie's reading**: the double cover is a property of the **whole Rac+Di spectrum**, not any single particle, and it reframes Casey's "single object traversing the circle twice as a unit" as *the entire SM content moving as one*, not one particle. **This must be pinned** (is the substrate time projective or absolute?) before the paper or the table banks "the Higgs rides the double cover." It is exactly the kind of subtlety this session has been built to catch.

## Casey's table — the time nature of SM particles (tiered)
Two *distinct* circles a particle rides, plus proper time:
- **SPACE circle** (Spin(5) rotation): charge = spin. Fermions (½) → 4π (the usual 720° spinor). Bosons (integer) → 2π.
- **TIME circle** (the conformal Hamiltonian J): charge = conformal weight E₀. At d=n_C=5, the **scalar** (Rac, 3/2) is the half-integer, the **spinor** (Di, 2) is the integer — **inverted from the space circle.**
- **PROPER time** (worldline arc-length): massless (null) → **zero** (doesn't age); massive → nonzero.

| Particle | rep | spin (space) | weight (time) | space-nature | time-nature | proper time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Higgs / scalar sector** | Rac | 0 | **3/2** | 2π | **4π** (rides the double cover) | massive → yes |
| **charged leptons, quarks** | Di | ½ | 2 | **4π** (usual spinor) | 2π | massive → yes |
| **massive ν** | Di-type | ½ | 2 | 4π | 2π | yes |
| **massless ν (m₁=0)** | Di-type, Goldstone | ½ | 2 | 4π | 2π | **no (null worldline)** |
| **photon, gluon** (massless gauge) | Rac⊗Rac tower | 1 | (tower) | 2π | (tower) | **no (null)** |

**Tiers:** the *two-circles* structure (space-spin vs time-weight) and the *inversion at odd d* are **Structure-Derived** (Fernando–Günaydin weights + Spin(5) reps). The *proper-time = 0 for massless* is **Derived** (standard relativity — null worldlines don't age; the massless ν participates in substrate time via its weight but does not age). **The load-bearing caveat:** whether any *single* row "rides the double cover," or only the *pair* does, is the **open fork above** — so read the "4π" in the time-nature column as **provisional** until projective-vs-absolute resolves. If projective (likely), the honest statement is *the whole scalar+matter spectrum rides the double cover as one unit* — no single particle does.

## The surprise worth stating (honestly)
Under **space**, fermions are the double-valued (spin-½) objects — familiar. Under **substrate time**, at odd d, it is the **scalar sector** that carries the half-integer weight — the roles *trade*. And the massless neutrino is the sharp case Casey named: it has a weight (participates in substrate time) but a null worldline (**no proper time — it does not age**), while its massive siblings do. That is a real, non-trivial map from the SM family onto "how each particle relates to time," and it is the seed of the program.

But it's **early and gated**: the projective/absolute fork must resolve first, and most of the table is Structure-Derived/candidate, not closed.
