# K1214 — the corpus has two incompatible "Koons ticks" (101 orders apart)

Auditing the item-4 Koons-tick claim (Casey's AI question: "how does the tick get its scale") turned up a genuine internal inconsistency.

## The catch
Two definitions, both called **"the Koons tick,"** ~101 orders of magnitude apart, on opposite sides of the Planck time:
- **T1136** (Lyra, Apr 12): τ_0 = 1/(α·ν_C) = N_max·ℏ/(m_e c²) ≈ **1.77×10⁻¹⁹ s** — the observer/photon-electron interaction time (N_max Compton periods); hierarchy τ_L = N_max^L·t_Planck goes **up** from Planck.
- **T2405 / CLAUDE.md** (May 19): tick = t_Planck·α^(C_2²) = t_P·α³⁶ ≈ **6.4×10⁻¹²¹ s** — a **sub-Planck** substrate clock, **below** Planck.

Ratio ≈ 2.8×10¹⁰¹. A referee would catch this immediately.

## Reconciliation (candidate: two different objects, one name)
The coherent picture is two *levels*: a **substrate tick** (sub-Planck, D_IV⁵'s own computational clock that "produces spacetime") and an **observer tick** (the clock rate of an observer at an organizational level, ~10⁻¹⁹ s for the electron). Both can be legitimate — but calling both "the Koons tick" is the bug. **Rename them as distinct objects; don't leave two 10¹⁰¹-apart quantities sharing one name.**

## Honest per-piece tiers
- **T1136 observer tick** (N_max·Compton): physically grounded (Structural/D1), *not* numerology — it's "N_max EM-interaction times." Fine.
- **T1136 hierarchy** (N_max^L·t_P): does *not* match observed timescales — the note itself admits nuclear ~10⁻²⁴ s = N_max^3.8, "fractional powers related to g and n_C." Rough Structural; P1 (adjacent ratio = 137^(7/5) ≈ 690) is the patch, untested.
- **T2405 substrate tick** (α^(C_2²) = α³⁶): **numerology-flavored** (K1208's flag confirmed) — no stated mechanism for the exponent C_2² = 36. The weakest link.

## Casey's AI question, answered honestly
"How does the Koons tick get its scale?" — the corpus gives **two answers 10¹⁰¹ apart, conflated by name.** The observer-tick scale (10⁻¹⁹ s) is grounded; the substrate-tick scale (10⁻¹²⁰ s) is **not derived** (the α³⁶ exponent is numerology-flavored). Item 4 cannot honestly claim the tick scale is derived until (a) the two ticks are renamed as distinct objects and (b) the α³⁶ is derived (why C_2²?) or retiered.
