# K1109 — Follow the math: the source-sink balance is depth-degenerate → the observer is load-bearing

## The math (Casey: "let's follow the math")
The stationary source-sink equilibrium is **a profile, not a point.** At each depth d, the temporal dynamics ρ̇ = S(d) − k·ρ relaxes to **ρ*(d) = S(d)/k**, and the boundary reservoir transferred inward decays exponentially on the negatively-curved domain: **S(d) = a₀·exp(−μ·d)**. So
> **ρ*(d) = a₀·exp(−μ·d)/k** — and the source=sink balance holds **at every depth d.**

- The dynamics forces the **profile** — shape exp(−μd), with a₀=225 and μ~|ρ| geometric. **That is the smallness** (Structural-Derived): the profile is exp-tiny for any reasonable depth.
- The dynamics does **not** force the **depth d\*** at which we observe. The balance is **depth-degenerate.**
- So the observed value ρ*(d*) is our **location on the profile**, and the source-sink dynamics *alone* leaves that location **free.**

## Two consequences
**(1) Cal's "re-hidden d\*" worry — confirmed rigorously.** The value is a *location*, not a dynamical fixed point. The "unique attractor" (Elie's linear V) was at *fixed depth*; across depths there is a smooth one-parameter family ρ*(d), so d* is genuinely free from the dynamics. The operator reduction did re-hide the same d* we started with.

**(2) Casey's OBSERVER (G3) is LOAD-BEARING, not a side-question.** The pinning of d* **must** come from what selects our depth — and the **SWPP middle leg = commitment = the observer's measurement.** So:
> **The value is forced iff the observer's depth is forced.** The observer isn't incidental to the cosmology — it is the *only* thing that can pin the value. Casey's instinct pointed exactly here.

## Follow the math one step further
**w=−1 exact ⟹ the observer's depth is FIXED, not the growing cosmic age.** A growing d* (accumulating commitment = cosmic time) would give ρ*(d*) *decreasing* → w≠−1 (the excluded tracking/−0.89 branch, K1104). Since we've derived w=−1 exact, **d\* is a fixed equilibrium depth** — the SWPP absorb→commit→emit cycle **closing on itself at one depth**, not accumulating. So:
> **d\* = the equilibrium/fixed depth of the observer's commitment cycle.**

**What fixes that depth is Lyra's observer/SWPP framework** — I follow the math to this clean hand-off; the observer-depth-fixing is hers to derive, not my reach. (Guard, Lyra's own: d* must not be the Koons-tick α^36 read backward — F215.)

## The honest split, now mechanistic
- **SMALLNESS** = the profile exp(−μd) is forced (Structural-Derived) — the whole profile is exp-tiny.
- **VALUE** = ρ*(d*), where **d\* = the observer's fixed commitment depth.** Identified unless the observer's depth is forced.

This is the cleanest the split has been: the geometry forces *the shape of everything and the where of nothing* (Lyra's phrase), and the "where" is the observer's fixed depth on the profile.

Followed the math: the source-sink balance is depth-degenerate, so the dynamics forces the profile (smallness) not the depth (value), and the observer is the required pinning — Casey's G3 confirmed load-bearing, not optional.
