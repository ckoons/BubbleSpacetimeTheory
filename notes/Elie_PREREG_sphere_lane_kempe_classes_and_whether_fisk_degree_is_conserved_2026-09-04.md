# Pre-registration — does the SPHERE record space have a conserved charge under Kempe dynamics? (toy 5670)
**Elie, 2026-09-04 11:57 EDT (`date` before this line). Written before the instrument. The law lane's question asked on the graphs Paper 2 actually studies.**
**Why this and not another torus.** E5 answered the law question on T(3L,3M): the record is not conserved, its class is, and the class's invariant is Mohar–Salas's one bit. Mohar–Salas's theorem needs a 3-colourable (Eulerian) triangulation. **Paper 2's record space is the opposite case** — sphere triangulations WITH odd vertices (fullerene duals; k = 12), which are not Eulerian and not 3-colourable, so neither Mohar–Salas nor Fisk Thm 2.8 applies. Fisk's degree is still defined there and is NOT constant (5667: on the C₆₀ dual it takes −6, −3, 0, 3, 6), and 5667 proved it equals ¼ the cup-square of the height class. So the question has content and is cheap.
**Space:** the C₆₀ dual (pentakis dodecahedron; 32 vertices, k = 12, 3,190 colourings mod S₄) and every plantri -c5 graph n = 12…16 (six graphs, 161 colourings). Kempe classes on colourings mod S₄ (Mohar–Salas's convention), union-find over every move of every colour pair and component. Instrument reused verbatim from 5663 (validated there against Mohar–Salas's κ(T(6,6)) = 2 and against Fisk's one class on the octahedron).
**Hashed lines.**
(S1) **κ(C₆₀ dual, 4) = 1** — one Kempe class. Basis: Mohar 2006 Cor. 2.7 covers planar with q > χ, which does not apply at χ = 4; but no planar triangulation is known to me with κ > 1, and Feghali 2023 works in that direction. Kill: κ ≥ 2.
(S2) **Consequence, stated in advance so it cannot be tuned after the fact:** if (S1) holds then Fisk's degree — which takes five values on this graph — is NOT a Kempe invariant on the sphere lane, and therefore **the sphere record space has NO conserved charge under Kempe dynamics: every function of the colouring is either constant on the whole space or not conserved.** If instead κ ≥ 2, the measured question is whether deg (or deg mod 3, mod 6) separates the classes, and that would be a NEW conserved charge on the graphs Paper 2 studies.
(S3) plantri -c5 n = 12…16: κ = 1 on every graph. Kill: any graph with κ ≥ 2.
**Controls:** the octahedron (κ = 1, Fisk) and T(6,6) (κ = 2, Mohar–Salas) must come out right through the same code path in the same run.
