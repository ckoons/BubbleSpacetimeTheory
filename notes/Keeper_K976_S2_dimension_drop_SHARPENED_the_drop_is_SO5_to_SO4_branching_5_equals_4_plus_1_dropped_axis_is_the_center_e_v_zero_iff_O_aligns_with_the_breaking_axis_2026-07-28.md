# K976 — What the "5→4 dimension drop" actually is, and the exact S2 question

Lyra's S2 concern: the F603 condensate is an SO(5) vector (dim 5); the spin-factor vector part is ℝ⁴ (dim 4); the projection drops a dimension, so O could land in the dropped direction (v=0, degenerate). This note casts the drop into the actual branching so the question is exact. **It is textbook rep theory (the generic SO(5)↓SO(4) rule), NOT a reconstruction of the sourced type-IV embedding — so it is safe to state; where O actually lands stays Lyra's sourced computation.**

## The structure (standard, safe to state)
- The spin factor V = ℝe ⊕ ℝ⁴ (dim 5). Under its automorphism group **SO(4)** (= Aut of the spin factor, rotating the ℝ⁴ vector part), V decomposes as **V = (SO(4)-singlet ℝe) ⊕ (SO(4)-vector ℝ⁴) = 1 ⊕ 4.**
- The condensate O is an **SO(5)-vector 5** (K-group SO(5) ⊃ SO(4)). The standard restriction of the vector rep: **5 ↓ SO(4) = 4 ⊕ 1** (vector + singlet).
- **These match:** the SO(5)-vector's SO(4)-**vector** part 4 = the spin-factor's ℝ⁴ (the frame-selecting part); the SO(5)-vector's SO(4)-**singlet** part 1 = the center ℝe (the identity direction).

So the "dimension drop" is exactly the **SO(5)↓SO(4) branching 5 = 4 ⊕ 1**, and the **dropped direction is the center ℝe = the unique SO(4)-fixed axis** (the "5th direction" that the SO(5)→SO(4) breaking singles out).

## ★ The exact S2 question (sharp, checkable, not resolved)

Two consequences for the make-or-break:
1. **The question is now one inner product on the sourced embedding:** compute ⟨O, ê_axis⟩ where ê_axis is the SO(4)-fixed direction of the sourced type-IV embedding. |O − ⟨O,ê_axis⟩ê_axis| > 0 ⟺ v≠0. A single projection, exactly as advertised — but now with the *dropped axis identified* (the center/SO(4)-fixed direction), so Lyra knows precisely what to project against.
2. **A structural expectation (NOT a clearance):** the SO(4)-fixed axis is the *most symmetric* direction; a physical symmetry-*breaking* condensate would generically be transverse to it (v≠0). That is a prior, not a proof — the F603 O is a *specific* vector, and whether it is aligned or transverse is the sourced fact. **Do NOT clear S2 on the generic prior** (that would repeat the "O is obviously non-singlet" argument Lyra correctly refused).
