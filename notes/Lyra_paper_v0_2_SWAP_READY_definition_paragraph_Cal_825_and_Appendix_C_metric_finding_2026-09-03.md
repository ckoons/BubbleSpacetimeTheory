## Slot 1 — Section 0 definition paragraph: Cal §825 item 2, VERBATIM (replaces the bracketed paragraph at v0.1 line 63)

*Setting.* T is a 5-connected sphere triangulation (no separating 3- or 4-cycles; every minimal counterexample to the Four Color Theorem is one, Birkhoff 1913, re-derived in Appendix C); v is a vertex of degree 5; c is a proper 4-coloring of T − v. Colors are read on the link of v, a 5-cycle. Following Kittell 1935 we call c an *impasse* — we also say *stuck* — when every color appears on the link and no single Kempe swap in T − v frees a color at v; equivalently τ_v(c) = 6, every pair of colors operationally tangled (Definition 5). Stuckness forces a single local context up to symmetry (One-Context Lemma): two copies of one color r at link distance two, a middle vertex of color s_M between them, and two singletons s_i, s_j; and it forces eight link-seeded Kempe chains, which are Kittell's eight (Appendix D, three-column key; the identification is of chains at c, not of operations — Kittell's group is frame-relative and ours is not). A coloring is in the *gate phase* when a color is absent at v or τ_v ≤ 5, i.e. when at most one further swap inserts v.
*The menu.* A *move* is a Kempe swap in T − v seeded at a link vertex, with a color pair containing that vertex's color: fifteen moves. A *word* is an ordered pair of moves with distinct color pairs, applied as the fixed-seed four-stage commutator m₁ m₂ m₁ m₂; there are 186 words (225 ordered pairs less 39 same-pair pairs, which are identities). A stage is *legal* if its seed carries a color of its pair at that stage; a word is *fully legal* if all four stages act; a fully legal word's image is a proper coloring of T − v. Seeds are fixed within a word; the context is re-derived only between words. The menu is a position: its factors are the five link roles, the four colors, and the commutator shape; nothing in it is tuned, and nothing is added to it when it fails.
*The object.* A stuck coloring c is *commutator-locked at depth one* if no fully legal word of the menu carries c into the gate phase; it is *commutator-locked at depth k* if no sequence of k fully legal words does, contexts re-derived between words. These are properties of the menu — its link-seeding, its fixed seeds, its commutator shape — and not of the coloring's Kempe landscape: of the 349 colorings commutator-locked at depth one through 24 vertices, 27 leave the impasse under a single plain Kempe swap seeded away from the link, and 2 under Kempe's own two swaps (Section 3, Table T1). The frame-independent quantity is the *unrestricted Kempe depth*: the least number of plain Kempe swaps in T − v, any seed and any pair, from c to the gate phase; the paper reports it beside every commutator count. We do not use the words *Kempe-locked* (Tilley 2018: an edge property under unbounded sequences), *locked* without its qualifier, or *witness* without saying of what.
*Counts.* Three counts are distinguished throughout and never quoted unlabeled: W_legal(c), the number of fully legal words at c; W_acting(c), the number whose image differs from c; Im(c), the number of distinct images.

**C.2 Lemma (Target-Existence is the theorem).** For a sphere triangulation T and a vertex v of degree 5, the gate-phase target set 𝒯(T, v) is nonempty if and only if T is 4-colorable.
*Proof.* If c* ∈ 𝒯, some color pair is not operationally tangled at v (Definition 5), so one swap frees a color at v; assign it, and T is 4-colored. Conversely, restrict any 4-coloring of T to T − v: v's color is absent from its link, so the restriction is directly insertable and lies in 𝒯. ∎ Inputs: Definition 5 only. No census, no frame change.

**C.3 Consequence.** At a hypothetical minimal counterexample, 𝒯(T, v) = ∅, so d_gate is infinite there and any statement of the form "every stuck coloring descends toward 𝒯" is vacuous exactly where it is needed.
