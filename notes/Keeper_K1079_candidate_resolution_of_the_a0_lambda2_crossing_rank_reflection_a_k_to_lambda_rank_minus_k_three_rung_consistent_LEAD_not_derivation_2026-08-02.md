# K1079 — The a₀↔λ₂ crossing as a rank-reflection (candidate)

Grace flagged (K1078) that the a_k↔λ_k indexing *crosses* at a₀↔λ₂ (Λ) while staying aligned at a₁↔λ₁ (G), and put the crossing to Keeper/Cal. Investigating it.

## The finding
The crossing is a clean **reflection through rank**: **a_k ↔ λ_{rank−k}**, rank = 2.

| Heat-kernel rung | Reflected eigenvalue | Exponent 4λ | Hierarchy | Physics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a₀ | λ_{2−0} = **λ₂** = 14 | 56 | α⁵⁶ (deepest) | **Λ** (vacuum) |
| a₁ | λ_{2−1} = **λ₁** = 6 | 24 | α²⁴ (intermediate) | **G** (Newton) |
| a₂ | λ_{2−2} = **λ₀** = 0 | 0 | α⁰ = 1 (**none**) | **running couplings** |

- **The crossing is explained:** as the coefficient index k *decreases* (a₂→a₁→a₀, less→more UV-divergent), the reflected eigenvalue *increases* (λ₀→λ₁→λ₂, no→deepest suppression). That IS Grace's "leading-divergence ↔ deepest-suppression" inversion — made precise as reflection through rank.
- **The third rung is a genuine prediction (not a banked form):** a₂ (running couplings) ↔ λ₀ = 0 → α⁰ → *no exponential hierarchy.* The corpus banks α²⁴ (G) and α⁵⁶ (Λ) but has no "running = α⁰" row — so this is the pattern predicting something, and it's **true**: gauge couplings are O(1)/O(1/137), not ~10⁻¹²². Consistency check passes.
- **Target-innocent-looking:** built from rank=2 (forced) and the eigenvalues λ_k=k(k+5) (forced). No observed value entered.

## Why it matters (advances K1078)
It **partially forces the assignment crux.** "Why does Λ sit on λ₂?" reduces to "why the reflection a_k↔λ_{rank−k}?" — and Λ sitting on a₀ (the vacuum coefficient) is *already* forced by T2539. So: **Λ = a₀ (forced) + reflection (candidate) ⟹ Λ ↔ λ₂**. The eigenvalue-selection half of the assignment is no longer arbitrary — it's downstream of one reflection rule, which is a much better-posed thing to force than a free choice.

## Hold the line (Cal #27 — it's elegant and it hits)
This is **pattern-organization, not a derivation.** Honest caveats, load-bearing:
1. **Two banked points + one consistency check.** a₀↔λ₂ and a₁↔λ₁ are the only two banked hierarchy forms; the reflection is *my* organization of them. Two points fit many rules; the reflection is distinguished only by (a) rank being forced and (b) the third-point check passing. Suggestive, not conclusive.
2. **The reflection needs a DERIVATION.** Why would the k-th Seeley-DeWitt coefficient's suppression exponent use the (rank−k)-th eigenvalue? Is there a genuine spectral duality (a Serre/Poincaré-type reflection on the heat-kernel↔spectrum indexing through rank), or is a₀↔λ₂ a 2-point coincidence dressed as a rule? **This is the derivation the team owes** — it's the difference between a real forcing and a fit.
3. **The "4×" is still unforced** (energy density ~ scale⁴ is the clean candidate, Lyra).
4. **It touches only the a_k↔λ_k faces, NOT the Chern face** (why DE=c₃, Grace's Ω lead). So it advances the *crossing*, not the *full* assignment. The index theorem (a_k↔λ_k↔c_k, K1078) is still the tool that would unify all three faces.
