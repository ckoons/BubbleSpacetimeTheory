## What did NOT move, and why that is the point

**Every count-based verdict is untouched.** γ, η̄ and J stay retired on **discriminating power**; ρ̄ stays
**non-discriminating**. Saturation is a statement about how many BST forms fit inside an error bar, and
the density control showed that count is near-constant across band centres — **so it does not care which
centre is correct.**

> **§708's rule, now demonstrated at full strength: a competitor COUNT is target-independent; a competitor
> RANKING is not. Five rankings were in circulation today. Five changed at the primary, in both
> directions. Zero counts changed.**

**And the audit cuts for us as often as against us:** three published forms are **better** than the corpus
claimed (ρ̄ −0.10σ, η̄ +0.17σ, V_cb +0.33σ vs exclusive) and two are **worse** (A 4/5 −1.68σ, J −2.33σ).
**Under-claiming was as common as over-claiming, and both came from the same defect.**
