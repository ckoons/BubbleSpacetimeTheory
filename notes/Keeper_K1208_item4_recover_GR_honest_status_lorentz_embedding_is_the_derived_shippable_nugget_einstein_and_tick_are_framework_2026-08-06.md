# K1208 — item 4 (recover GR): honest status

Parallel-track audit while the mass tower is Lyra-gated. Read the Lorentz note, the Einstein-from-Bergman chain, and the F60-F66 summary. **Item 4 is Framework overall — with one genuinely Derived, referee-proof corner.**

## The Derived, shippable nugget — Lorentz as a subgroup
**SO(3,1) ⊂ SO₀(5,2)** is exhibited correctly: rotations J_i = {M_23, M_31, M_12}, boosts K_i = {M_15, M_25, M_35}, and the algebra checks out ([J,J]=J, [J,K]=K, [K,K]=−J). The 21 generators decompose 6 (Lorentz) + 4 (translations) + 4 (SCT) + 1 (dilatation) + 6 (internal). This is a clean, checkable Lie-algebra fact — the Lorentz group genuinely *is* a substrate subgroup. **Referee-proof; lead item 4 with this.**

## The honest gap in it (Framework)
The note asserts the extra generators "are not symmetries of the low-energy theory" but doesn't derive **why** the world sees only SO(3,1), nor **why 3+1 is selected** from the (5,2) signature. That selection is the real physics (Casey #14, via SO(5,2)→SO(4,2)→SO(3,1) — Framework). Referee objection to pre-empt: *"a big group contains SO(3,1) — so what? why is that the physical symmetry?"* Existence = Derived; selection + dynamical hiding = Framework.

## c = 1 and the kinematics — interpretive, not new derivations
"c = 1 contact per commitment step," time dilation = "commitment budget reallocation," length contraction = "projection of contact extent" — narrative glosses on standard SR that follow once the embedding is in hand. Plausible and nice, but not independent derivations. E² = p²c² + m²c⁴ = the Poincaré Casimir — correct but standard. Don't sell the interpretations as derivations.

## The Einstein equation — Framework (credible, not closed)
Induced Sakharov-style: the a_1 heat-kernel coefficient = the Einstein-Hilbert term (F63); G = κ_Bergman·ℓ_B²/π^{n_C} (F64) — on the same heat trace whose a_0 = Λ and a_2 = the running. Induced gravity is a respected mechanism and the a_1 = EH identification is genuine. But it's not a closed first-principles GR derivation — the known cutoff-sensitivity of the induced G and the a_0 (cosmological-constant) issue remain, and it gives the Einstein-Hilbert *action* as an effective term. **"GR derived" would over-claim; Framework is right.**

## The Koons-tick scale — the weakest link
