## Calibrate BOTH ways — the result splits (don't under-claim, don't over-claim)

That the cosmological vacuum energy is exponentially small **for a structural reason** (a positive spectral gap, not a fine-tuning) is a real, non-trivial partial result. Under-claiming this — dismissing the whole thing as "just a fit" — would be the opposite error from over-claiming the value. The mechanism is real.

**(B) The VALUE (the exponent) is NOT forced — stays IDENTIFIED.**

The effective scale (the "47", i.e. the number of e-foldings / effective proper-time / scale-ratio) that a genuine derivation must supply is **asserted, not derived**. The mechanism fixes the *form*; it does not fix the *exponent*.
