$$ \psi_{\nu,m}(w) = p_m(w)\, N(w)^{\nu/2}, \qquad N(w) = 1 - 2|w|^2 + |w\!\cdot\!w|^2, $$
with p_m the degree-m spherical polynomial (p_{(λ₁,λ₂)} the rank-2 Jack polynomial; single-row p_{(k,0)} = (w₁+iw₂)^k). The **cross-address two-point kernel** against the base (Lebesgue) measure dV on ℂ⁵:

— the angular polynomials of the two modes, weighted by the domain factor N(w) raised to the **average** of the two radial weights. Normalized: $\hat K_{ij} = K_{ij}/\sqrt{K_{ii}K_{jj}}$.
