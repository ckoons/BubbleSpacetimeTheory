**Which zero point unWISE's Vega magnitudes sit on — Schlafly, Meisner & Green 2019, *The unWISE Catalog: Two Billion Infrared Sources from Five Years of WISE Imaging*, ApJS 240, 30 (2019), DOI 10.3847/1538-4365/aafbea (publisher page fetched); arXiv:1901.03337:** "The unWISE Catalog absolute photometric calibration derives from the photometric calibration of the unWISE coadds (Meisner et al. 2017a), which is tied to the **original WISE zero points** through aperture fluxes"; fluxes "in Vega nanomaggies (nMgy) … m_Vega = 22.5 − 2.5 log₁₀ flux"; "**m_W1,AB = m_W1,Vega + 2.699**, m_W2,AB = m_W2,Vega + 3.339". **So Quaia's `mag_w1_vg` is on the WISE All-Sky Vega zero point — Jarrett's 309.540 Jy via the Supplement — with the AB offset 2.699.** **One convention v1.4 must freeze:** 3631 Jy × 10^(−0.4 × 2.699) = **302.0 Jy**, not 309.540 — the 2.5 % gap between "F_ν0 = 309.540 Jy" and "AB offset 2.699" is the Supplement's own colour-correction convention (the offset is defined for f_ν ∝ ν⁻², the zero point for Vega's spectrum). §3.2 must say which of the two it uses for S_W1; either is defensible, mixing them is a 2.5 % bias on S_W1 and hence on every α_i.
