# The Baryon Sector Ledger

## 0. The headline, stated first because it governs everything below

> **BST's baryon-sector departures are from BSM model space — grand unified theories, post-sphaleron baryogenesis, left–right symmetric models — NOT from the Standard Model.**

This is not a hedge. It is the accurate description, and it is load-bearing three separate times below. Every framing in this ledger that implies novelty *against the Standard Model* is wrong and will be refuted in under a minute by any referee who works in the area. What BST offers here is an **explanation of why a known regularity holds**, which is the program's actual business — not a new effect.

## 1. The rule

> **A process may violate baryon number only in multiples of N_c = 3.**
> **ΔB ≡ 0 (mod N_c)**

**Mechanism (two parts, both required):**
- **The counting constraint.** Quarks carry B = 1/N_c; a colour singlet requires N_c of them. This is what gives B a mod-N_c structure. *(Colour-based; see §4 for why it does not extend to leptons.)*
- **The integer winding per channel.** A single proton cannot untie its knot. This is what makes ΔB = 1 impossible rather than merely suppressed.

**Retracted:** *"baryon number = the ℤ₃ centre of colour."* The colour centre grades **triality**, t = (n_q − n_q̄) mod 3 = 3B mod 3 ≡ **0 identically on every physical (colour-singlet) state** — the proton has t = 0 while B mod 3 = 1. **Triality and B mod 3 are different functions.** (Cal §611; adopted as K1700b.) The mechanism is a **counting constraint**, not a **grading operator** — a distinction that has now bitten this program twice (cf. the ill-typed baryon-generator map).

## 2. Compatibility with the electroweak anomaly — and its one contingency

The anomaly is **kept in full**. Sphalerons operate. BST does not deny the SM's non-perturbative sector.

| process | ΔB | mod 3 | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| sphaleron transition | **N_gen = 3** | **0** | **invisible to the rule — anomaly retained** |
| proton decay (p → e⁺π⁰) | 1 | 1 | **forbidden, exactly** |

**⟹ Baryogenesis remains available.** B *is* violated in the early universe, by 3 units per transition; Sakharov's condition is satisfied. *(This corrects Cal §608/§609, which priced exact-B as forbidding baryogenesis — that pricing assumed integer-B conservation and did not enumerate the mod-N_c refinement.)*

**★ The contingency, which must travel with every statement of this result:**
Compatibility requires **N_gen ≡ 0 (mod N_c)** — i.e. **N_gen = N_c**. At N_gen = 4 the sphaleron would give ΔB = 4 ≡ 1, **contradicting** the exact rule; BST would be inconsistent with established physics, not merely less elegant.

**K1704 rules N_gen = N_c a coincidence-at-n_C = 5** — a boundary consequence of the one ruler, not a forcing. **So the fact holds (N_gen = N_c is true in our world), but the *explanation* of sphaleron-invisibility bottoms out in a coincidence, by the program's own ruling.**

> **Required phrasing:** *"the sphaleron is invisible to the rule because N_gen = N_c — a boundary consequence of the one ruler, not a forcing."*
> **Forbidden phrasing:** *"BST explains why the proton is stable"* without that clause.

## 3. What the rule forbids, permits, and what kills BST

| ΔB | process | status | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | proton decay | **FORBIDDEN, exactly and permanently** | GUTs predict it; BST forbids it |
| **2** | **n–n̄ oscillation**; dinucleon decay | **FORBIDDEN, exactly** | **the sharp falsifier — see below** |
| **3** | sphaleron | **permitted by the rule** | only known channel; ~e⁻³⁷² today, unsuppressed above the EW transition |

**★ Guard, verbatim and non-negotiable (Keeper): allowed-by-rule ≠ observable rate.** The rule is a *selection rule*. It supplies no mechanism and no rate for ΔB = 3. **"Trinucleon decay allowed" must never be written as "trinucleon decay predicted."**

**FALSIFIER: any observed ΔB = 1 or ΔB = 2 process kills BST.** One n–n̄ event is decisive.

**★ But n–n̄ is a shared exposure, not a unique one.** n has B−L = +1, n̄ has B−L = −1, so **Δ(B−L) = 2** — and B−L is exactly conserved in the Standard Model. **The SM already forbids n–n̄.** An observation kills BST *and* the SM's exact B−L. It is a genuine falsifier; it is **not** a discriminator between BST and the SM. *(It does discriminate against post-sphaleron baryogenesis and left–right models, which predict it — hence §0.)*

*Experimental limits are cited by Keeper with source and date. This referee has not independently pulled them and does not certify experimental values; this sign-off covers the rule and the ΔB / Δ(B−L) arithmetic.*

## 4. The baryon/lepton asymmetry — two falsifiers of two different kinds

The counting constraint is **colour-based**. Leptons carry **integer L and no colour**, so there is **no mod-N_c structure for lepton number.** The asymmetry is therefore real and has a reason.

> **ΔB = 2 (n–n̄): FORBIDDEN ABSOLUTELY.** Any signal kills BST.
> **ΔL = 2 (0νββ): PERMITTED by the baryon rule — but MAGNITUDE-BOUNDED by the neutrino sector.**

**The neutrino-sector bound (banked, corpus):** ν₁ is **exactly massless** ("it *is* the vacuum"). Therefore:

> **0νββ — a DETECTION at ~10–20 meV REFUTES BST** (m_ββ too large for m₁ = 0). **A NULL at LEGEND/nEXO does NOT refute BST** (below reach).

**★ Standing correction, already in the corpus and honoured here: never write "null 0νββ → refuted."** That error was made once and fixed. **State detection-kills and null-does-not-kill in the same sentence, every time.**

**Two falsifiers, two mechanisms, two kinds — one absolute prohibition, one quantitative bound.** Many BSM frameworks link n–n̄ and 0νββ; **BST separates them, and the separation has a reason (colour, not symmetry).** That asymmetric structure is the most distinctive thing in this sector.

## 5. Count-once obligations

- **"No proton decay" and Five-Absence's "no grand unification" are ONE MECHANISM WITH TWO CONSEQUENCES**, not two independent absences. The rule forbids ΔB = 1 operators; GUTs are what supply them. *(They are not strictly the same claim — non-GUT ΔB = 1 sources exist in principle — but they are not independent, and the Five-Absence tally must not count them twice. It is the program's most-quoted artifact and therefore the most expensive place to carry an inflated count.)*
- **N_gen = 3 and N_c = 3 are no longer independent inputs in this sector**: the rule requires their equality or matter is unstable. This *adds* structure rather than collapsing results — but it is one requirement, not two facts.
