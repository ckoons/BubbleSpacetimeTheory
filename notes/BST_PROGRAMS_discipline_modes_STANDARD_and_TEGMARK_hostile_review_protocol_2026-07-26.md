## Why programs
Parallel tracks need different discipline. A derivation-in-progress and an artifact-going-to-a-hostile-external-reviewer are held to different bars. Naming the modes lets both run at once without one blurring the other. **Default is STANDARD; anything external-bound runs TEGMARK/EXTERNAL.**

## THE HOSTILE-REVIEW PROTOCOL (the TEGMARK program's added machinery)

### The reviewer's charge (give a FRESH CI, cold, no priors)
> "Here is a package claiming to derive the Standard Model from one geometry. Your job is to **refute it.** Default to *this is numerology* unless the work forces you off that position. Find the over-claim, the circular toy, the fitted-not-forced domain, the cherry-pick. Report the strongest kill you can land, or state honestly that you couldn't land one and why."

### Diverse attack angles (assign different fresh CIs — redundant agreement is weak, diverse null results are strong)
1. **The forcing/uniqueness of D_IV⁵** — is it *forced by minimality*, or *fitted to known physics*? (The load-bearing target.)
2. **Toy circularity** — do the toys test against *independent measurement*, or compute-from-formula-and-compare-to-formula?
3. **Over-claims vs the scope page** — find any claim in the paper stronger than its tier; find any Millennium/confinement/derivation over-reach.
4. **The α / statistics** — is 137 forced or a fit? Does the null-model hold, or is the selection p-hacked?
5. **Cherry-picking** — are the "wins" selected post-hoc? Are the misses hidden?

### Pre-registered kill-vs-survivable criteria (write BEFORE each round — the blind-checker discipline)
- **KILL (a real flaw):** a circular toy; the domain shown *fitted* not forced; an unlabeled over-claim; a p-hacked selection; a derived-tier row that is actually a fit.
- **SURVIVABLE (not a kill):** a matched-but-honestly-labeled coincidence (identified tier, admitted); an open frontier named as open; a "computable-not-cleanly-observable" case (e.g. m_u); the breadth, *if* clearly labeled extended-reach.
- **The distinction to hold:** a CI won't do the Wyler-flinch, so if it disengages, that signals a *presentation* problem, not sociology. A landed kill is a real fix; a survivable objection is armor confirmed.

### The loop
Round → land-or-fail → fix what lands → new fresh CI, new pre-registered kills → repeat until 2 consecutive cold reviewers land no kill → Cal's final gate → Keeper pass → Casey decides on contact.
