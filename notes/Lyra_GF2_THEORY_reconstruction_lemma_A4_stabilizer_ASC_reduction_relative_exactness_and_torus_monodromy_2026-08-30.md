# THE GF(2) THEORY — WHY THE SIGN-PATTERN FIBER CLASSIFIES

Coordinates: ε ∈ GF(2)^F with z_t = (−1)^{ε_t}. A Kempe swap on chain S acts as
ε ↦ ε + 1_{str(S)} — exact addition (Straddle-Flip). This is the one coordinate system in which
the dynamics is literally linear per move; everything below exploits that.

## 1. THE RECONSTRUCTION LEMMA (proved) — the ε-point is complete up to realizable symmetry

**Lemma R (sphere).** On a sphere triangulation, the sign pattern ε determines the coloring up
to the 12-element group A₄ ≅ V ⋊ ℤ₃ (Klein translations and label rotations), and every element
of that group is realized by a Kempe word. Hence: ε(f) = ε(f′) ⟹ f and f′ are Kempe-reachable.

*Proof.* (i) Reconstruction: given ε, pick a face t₀ and assign one of its edges a label
(3 choices). z_{t₀} fixes the counterclockwise cyclic order of the label triple, determining the
other two labels; propagate across shared edges. Consistency around a vertex is exactly the
Heawood closure c(v) ≡ 0 (mod 3) — which holds — and the sphere's dual cycles are generated by
vertex cycles (H₁ = 0), so propagation is globally consistent. Labels determined up to ℤ₃;
colors from labels up to a Klein translation (4 choices): 12 total.
(ii) The stabilizer is A₄, by the affine structure: every color permutation σ ∈ S₄ is an affine
map x ↦ Ax + b of V = GF(2)² (the affine group has order 24 and acts faithfully on 4 points, so
it IS S₄). Labels transform by the linear part alone (ℓ′ = Aℓ, translations cancel in sums), so
z flips iff A is an odd permutation of {a,b,c}. The z-preserving subgroup is the preimage of
A₃: V ⋊ ℤ₃ ≅ A₄, order 12 — matching (i)'s count exactly.
(iii) Realizability: translations and rotations are products of two color transpositions, and
any transposition (p q) is realized by swapping every (p,q)-chain in turn (disjoint chains stay
chains; the composite transposes the colors and adds 1_F to ε). ∎

Consequence for the program: **the entire reachability question lives in the W-coset structure.**
Same coset is the only question; same point is already a theorem.

## 2. WHERE W LIVES (proved constraint), AND THE 1_F CHECK

Every straddle indicator has EVEN weight: |str(S)| ≡ Σ_str z (mod 2), and integrality of
Δdeg = −½Σ_str z forces the sum even. So **W ⊆ E, the even-weight subspace (dim F−1).** And
1_F ∈ W with a dynamical realization (the all-chains transposition above; each face straddles
exactly one (p,q)-chain — a face missing at most one color always contains p or q). Pre-registered
for the harvest (can fail): **W = E on the rich gallery graphs; the deficiency dim(E/W) counts
the graph's independent GF(2) invariants beyond the forced one.** Elie: rank(W) vs F−1 is one
number per graph, free from stored columns.

## 3. THE REDUCTION (N1's answer): complete invariant ⟺ AVAILABILITY SATURATION

**ASC (Availability Saturation Conjecture, pre-registered):** for colorings f, f′ of a sphere
triangulation: f′ reachable from f ⟺ ε(f′) − ε(f) ∈ W. (⟹ is definitional. ⟸ is the content;
Z2's 8/8 zero-over-separation is its empirical record.)

By Lemma R this IS the complete-invariant theorem: the pair (ε mod W) classifies Kempe classes.
The proof obligation splits into exactly two gaps, named so the harvest and the next proof
session attack them separately:

- **Gap A (availability/spanning):** at a given coloring f, the available indicators
  {1_{str(S)} : S a chain of f} may span only A(f) ⊊ W. Pre-registered harvest test: compute
  rank A(f) per coloring — the FROZEN objects (icosahedron's singleton classes, Fritsch's
  operational akempicity) are precisely where A(f) is small, and the conjecture's truth at 8/8
  means the COSETS there are correspondingly separated. The spanning profile
  {rank A(f) : f ∈ P} vs rank W is the single most informative table the harvest can produce
  for the theorem push.
- **Gap B (throughput/termination):** even with spanning, expressing δ ∈ W as a sum of
  STEP-AVAILABLE indicators is a dynamical walk, not a linear solve: applying w₁ changes the
  available set. The proof shape that closes it: an exchange/descent argument — a potential on
  the coset that some available indicator always decreases. (This is where the frustration
  program re-enters, POST-harvest per Cal's ruling; not chosen now.)

**Honest scope:** ASC for all sphere triangulations contains the field's open Kempe-connectivity
frontier as a sub-question; nobody should expect a same-day proof. What is new and ours today:
the exact linear coordinate system, the reduction with its gaps named, the completeness of the
ε-point (Lemma R), and the relative exactness below — none of which the field has (Keeper's

## 4. N2 — THE RELATIVE GF(2) LENS (proved exactness)

sign pattern determines the completion EXACTLY — no symmetry quotient. *Proof:* a boundary edge's
label is known (pinned colors), anchoring the ℤ₃ propagation freedom; pinned colors anchor the
translation; the disc is simply connected, so no monodromy. ∎

## 5. THE TORUS REMARK (free theory, one paragraph)

Lemma R's propagation uses H₁ = 0. On a torus the reconstruction acquires monodromy in
H¹(T²; ℤ₃): the sign pattern determines the coloring only up to a ℤ₃-twist per generator — and
this is exactly the room in which the Mohar–Salas classes live. The GF(2)-lens completeness
question is therefore GENUS-GRADED: sphere (candidate complete, Z2's 8/8), torus (provably
incomplete without the monodromy supplement — the supplement being computable from the coloring).
A sharp, publishable-shaped sentence for the eventual writeup: the complete Kempe invariant is
(ε mod W) ⊕ monodromy, with the second summand vanishing at genus 0.
