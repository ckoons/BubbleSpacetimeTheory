# K1736 — Lane D: Invariant 2 reads handedness (w₁); but the two-invariant table has double-assigned cells

## (1) Invariant 2's readability criterion (candidate)
The Shilov boundary is **(S⁴×S¹)/ℤ₂** — non-orientable (the corpus "membrane"). Its invariant is **w₁** (the orientation class). Proposed criterion, the analogue of Elie's for Invariant 1:

> **An observable is readable by Invariant 2 iff it is an orientation / handedness (w₁) fact.**

Clean candidate readings: **parity violation** (left≠right = no global orientation), **chirality** (the left-handed world), **no-ν_R ⟹ Majorana** (the ν_R cycle can't close on a non-orientable boundary, T1949). These are all "handedness" facts — the honest shape of what a non-orientable boundary forces.

## (2) ★ The load-bearing catch — double-assigned cells (resolve before Lane A ships)
K1735's clean two-column table is **not yet unambiguous** — the corpus sources two readings from *both* invariants:
- **SU(2)_L**: Invariant 1 (the ℍ block, quaternionic commutant — Grace) **and** Invariant 2 (the Pin(2) doublet on the non-orientable Möbius locus — T1949/T2138).
- **CP-existence**: Invariant 1 (ε(ℍ spinor)=−1, the quaternionic twist — Grace, Cal §398) **and** Invariant 2 (the non-orientable locus "gives parity violation — one locus, both").

Either each is **genuinely over-determined** (state both mechanisms, count once) or there's a **same-name conflation** to resolve. This is exactly the corpus's already-flagged "unify the U(1)/SU(2)/SU(3) gauge-origin stories into one consistent construction" open work. **The two-invariant theorem is citable only once every reading is assigned to exactly one invariant (or explicitly marked over-determined) — no cell claimed twice, unlabelled.**

**Likely clean resolution** (compute to confirm — Grace/Elie): Invariant 1 reads the **complex-structure** facts (CP-*existence* = the un-rotatable phase, ε(ℍ)=−1; the SU(2)_L **group** = the ℍ commutant); Invariant 2 reads the **handedness** facts (parity violation, chirality, Majorana). On this split, **CP-existence (Inv 1) and parity (Inv 2) are distinct observables**, and the double-assignment dissolves — the corpus note conflated "CP" with "the parity from the same locus." Must be computed, not asserted.

## (3) Boundary guards (adopt)
- **Baryon number is NOT Invariant 2.** It is a **discrete winding** (π₁/degree, T1945/T296) — a *different* topological invariant from the non-orientability (w₁). Cal §596 already proved they're **ill-typed to equate**. Baryon number is a separate (third) geometric fact — guard against the "it's all topology" collapse.
- **The arrow of time / no-CPT-mirror is NOT cleanly Invariant 2.** Per Cal §398, the antiuniverse-exclusion-as-Boyle-Turok is **analogy** (needs a Euclidean↔Lorentzian Wick bridge); the arrow is a heat-semigroup positivity fact + the quaternionic twist (Invariant 1). Don't fold it in without the bridge.
