# F-a — THE DESCENT-NEUTRALITY CONJECTURE (pre-registered)

## The mechanism (the re-dress bijection)

The refusal's insight inverted: J1′'s SIZE bound was never what the assembly consumes — J2
consumes DESCENT, and a stranded remnant might be descent-INVISIBLE regardless of size. The
word w's net effect = patch change P + pure relabel τ on the remnant R (the relabel is a color
transposition — moves 2/4 cannot see R, Confinement). Mechanism: RE-DRESS the freed targets —
for a freed coloring c*, define c*′ := c* with τ applied on R. If the re-dress preserves
properness and freed-ness, then H(w·c, c*′) counts the same off-R disagreements as H(c, c*)
minus the patch improvement: the remnant cancels out of the metric entirely.

## The conjecture (precise)

**F-a.** For every stuck configuration and every family word w whose stranded remnant R
contains NO link vertex: the re-dress c* ↦ c*′ maps the freed set into itself (properness holds
at ∂R because c and c* agree there off the difference region, and w·c's own properness
certifies the τ-side; freed-ness holds because R touches no link vertex, so the link multiset
of c*′ equals that of c*). Consequently d(w·c) ≤ d(c) − [patch improvement of w]: **the
remnant's size is irrelevant to descent; J1′ reframes to "some family word has improving patch
and link-free remnant"; J2's clause reduces to patch-vs-freed-word arithmetic on C_patch = 7
against the four freed words of the link arithmetic.** Both remaining joints close through one
door if this holds.

## Kill conditions (armed BEFORE contact; any one fires and F-a dies or re-scopes)

- **K-a1 (exhibit kill):** in some killer exhibit, EVERY family word with improving patch has a
  remnant CONTAINING a link vertex (the reframing then cannot cover that configuration — the
  four killers are the first test precisely because they are the hard cases).
- **K-a2 (re-dress kill):** a freed target whose re-dress fails properness at ∂R for a
  STRUCTURAL reason (not a fixable bookkeeping one) — the bijection's two certifying clauses
  (agreement at ∂R off the difference region; w·c's properness) are the exact places to attack.
- **K-a3 (tranche-2 kill, on Cal's flag):** any fresh stuck configuration where every family
  word has non-improving patch or link-touching remnant.

## What F-a is NOT claiming

Not that remnants are small (the refusal stands); not that every word's remnant is link-free
(the choice quantifier does the selecting, as everywhere else); not that patch improvement is
automatic (that is the C_patch = 7 + freed-word arithmetic, a separate obligation the
conjecture REDUCES to but does not discharge).
