## The distinguishing prediction (three sharp differences from ΛCDM)
1. **BST is NOT flat.** ΛCDM: w = −1 exactly, at all epochs. BST: **w(a) = −1 + A·a^n**, a small MONOTONE deviation relaxing to −1 (the boundary homeostat). So BST predicts a *nonzero, one-sign* departure from w=−1 where ΛCDM predicts *exactly zero*. A high-precision w(a) that is measurably ≠ −1 but **monotone and non-crossing** is BST, not ΛCDM.
2. **The FUNCTIONAL FORM is a power law, not a line or a constant.** ΛCDM = constant; DESI-CPL = linear (and permits a crossing); **BST = a power law in 1/a** (rate set by the spectral gap C_2). A *model-independent* w(a) or D_H(z) reconstruction that finds a **power-law** shape distinguishes BST from both.
3. **BST FORBIDS a w=−1 crossing** (complete monotonicity / Bernstein). ΛCDM sits *at* −1 (no crossing trivially); CPL *permits* a crossing. So the three are cleanly separable by the **sign structure of the residual**: BST = one-sign monotone (Grace's ~2–3% radial bump, largest near z≈1); ΛCDM = zero; CPL-crossing = sign-flip near z≈1.3.

## The observational discriminator (already half-built)
Grace's model-independent **radial D_H(z) residual vs ΛCDM** is the tool: **monotone one-sign** → BST (and the crossing was a CPL artifact); **zero** → ΛCDM; **sign-flip surviving non-CPL** → both BST and ΛCDM's no-crossing die, crossing real. The current radial data are consistent with **zero** (ΛCDM) at their precision — so BST vs ΛCDM needs **higher precision** (DESI DR3+, or a dedicated high-z H(z) probe) to see BST's small monotone bump if it's there.

BST makes a **more falsifiable** prediction than ΛCDM: a *specific* monotone, one-sign, power-law, non-crossing shape with a forced rate (C_2). ΛCDM only says "w=−1 is fine." So BST is distinguishable *and* killable — exactly where a real theory wants to be. If future data show BST's monotone bump → BST beats ΛCDM; if they show exact ΛCDM at high precision → BST's amplitude A is bounded small (still consistent, since A is the current-epoch occupancy, not forced to be large); if a genuine crossing survives non-CPL → BST falsified. **All three outcomes are pre-registered.**
