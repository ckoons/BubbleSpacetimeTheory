$$G_{ij} = \langle \text{gen}_i \,|\, K \,|\, \text{gen}_j\rangle, \qquad \nu = \{5/2,\,3/2,\,0\}.$$

$$\boxed{\text{Koide } A^2=\text{rank} \iff \text{that 2×2 covariance is ISOTROPIC } (\propto I_2,\ \mathrm{tr}=\text{rank}=2).}$$
