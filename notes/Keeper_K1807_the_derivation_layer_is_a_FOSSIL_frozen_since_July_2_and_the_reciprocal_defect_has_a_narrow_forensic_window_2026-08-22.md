# K1807 — The derivation layer is a fossil

I used the dated backup series as an independent time-series instrument — 30+ snapshots from
2026-07-02 to 2026-08-22, plus an April 3 snapshot. It answers a question none of the three of us asked.

## The finding

| snapshot | nodes | total edges | **`derived`** | other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-07-02 | 2217 | 9930 | **6833** | 3097 |
| 2026-07-26 | 2303 | 10044 | **6833** | 3211 |
| 2026-08-05 | 2319 | 10089 | **6833** | 3256 |
| 2026-08-22 (Grace's fix) | 2348 | 10162 | **6833** | 3329 |
| current | 2349 | 10164 | **6833** | 3331 |

**`derived` is frozen at exactly 6833 across every snapshot for seven weeks**, while **132 theorems were
added** and 234 non-derived edges went in under hand-written provenance labels.

> **132 new theorems, not one `derived` edge.**

**The entire recent corpus — the whole CKM/mixing arc, the QM package, the descent, everything T2500+ —
has no derivation edges at all.** It hangs off the graph only by hand-labelled prose-provenance edges.

This explains Grace's own earlier note that *"the derived SU(3) cluster is ~DISCONNECTED from a mature
strong-sector sub-graph."* That was the symptom. This is the cause.

## Forensic window on the reciprocal defect — and a hypothesis of mine, refuted

I was forming the hypothesis that `derived` had been bulk-applied to the pre-existing April adjacency
set, which would have explained the two senses in one stroke. **I tested it and it is refuted:**

- April 3 snapshot: **1232 edges, and NO `source` key on any of them.**
- **Reciprocal pairs present in April: 3.** Reciprocal pairs today: **472.**
- ⟹ **469 of 472 were introduced between April 3 and July 2**, during which the derived set grew
  1232 → 6833.

**There are no snapshots inside that window.** That ~3-month bulk-authoring phase is the **resolution
limit** of this instrument, and I cannot narrow further with it. Recording the refuted hypothesis rather
than the hypothesis.

**Cal is not refuted, only localized.** "Both senses in simultaneous use" is entirely consistent with a
bulk-authoring phase in which two hands used opposite readings of the same label.

## The good news, and it is substantial

**The reciprocal defect is historical, frozen and finite.** Nothing since July 2 has added to it. It is a
**closed object, not a live process** — which makes it repairable once rather than continuously.

## Strategic consequence — I recommend a priority inversion

Repairing 2410 orientations buys **a correct map of work through July 2 only.** Meanwhile the most
important seven weeks of the program sit outside the derivation layer entirely.

> **Ingesting the last seven weeks under the K1806 pinned convention outranks repairing the historical
> orientations** — and it must be done under the pin, or the repair gets re-broken by the ingestion.

That also makes the repair cheaper: ingest correctly first, then the historical fix has a
correctly-built recent layer to be checked against.

## What this does NOT touch

**Nothing in the physics.** This is a maintenance lapse in a bookkeeping layer. Every theorem, proof and
result stands exactly as it did. Cal's formula holds for the third time this round: *a latent instrument
fault, not a corrupted ledger.*
