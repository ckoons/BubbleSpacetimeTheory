## The discriminator (why the forward test is non-trivial — verified)
A **random** unit vector in 3D has ⟨cos²φ⟩ = **1/3** = 0.333 (the naive/democratic value). Observed is **0.500** — the √m vector is *more* aligned with democratic than random, by exactly 1/rank (1/2) vs 1/N_gen (1/3). **So the forward derivation must produce the "half" from the rank-2 structure, not from counting 3 generations** — a naive 3-generation democratic model gives 1/3 and fails. That 1/2-vs-1/3 gap *is* the content: Koide is evidence for the rank-2 structure specifically.

1/2 has two clean readings: **1/rank** and **N_c/C_2** (3/6). The forward mechanism must select rank by the *physics* of the tilt, not because both equal 1/2. Cal-guard it.
