# K1073 — Retracting "Identified-permanent"; the forced-μ path to derive Λ

Casey: *"I'm not sure what Identified-permanent means unless it's impossible to derive. What can we do to derive lambda? … don't take a wrecking ball to our earlier work; justify and verify."* He's right on every count. I own this.

## The retraction — and why it's the same failure as casual downtiering
I steered the cc-magnitude toward **"Identified-permanent = provably not forward-derivable."** That presumes **no forced subtraction scale can ever exist** — which we never proved. Declaring un-derivability without proof is a casual over-claim, the exact mirror of casual downtiering (feedback_calibrate_both_directions). **Retracted.** The honest tier is **Identified**, with a precise open piece named.

## What a₅≠0 actually means (now that it's computed)
Elie computed ζ_Δ(0) = a₅ − dim ker ≈ **220.64 ≠ 0**, two independent routes (cascade + Weyl-dimension rebuild), convention-robust in sign. That is a genuine result — but read it correctly:
- a₅≠0 ⟹ the vacuum energy has a **scale anomaly** ⟹ ρ_Λ = κ(μ)·V⁻¹ **runs** with the renormalization scale μ.
- A running quantity has a **well-defined value once you fix μ.** It is *scale-ambiguous*, not *underivable*.
- **Therefore: deriving Λ's magnitude = deriving the FORCED subtraction scale μ.** a₅ (the running coefficient) is the computed **first ingredient**; the forced μ is the **open second ingredient** and the derivation path.

## The derivation path — "what can we do to derive Λ"
Find the substrate's forced subtraction scale. BST has candidate fixed scales the geometry supplies:
- **ℓ_B** (the Bergman/Planck length anchor),
- the **spectral gap λ₁ = 7** of Δ,
- **m_e** (the substrate-floor mass, 6π⁵α¹²m_Pl),
- the **Koons tick** (~10⁻¹²⁰ s substrate clock).
If the geometry/substrate **forces** one of these as the subtraction point for the vacuum energy, then ρ_Λ(μ_forced) is a **number** → Λ magnitude **Derived**. This is a well-posed derivation, target-blind: derive μ from substrate structure *before* comparing to 10⁻¹²².

## Anti-wrecking-ball: the old forms become testable candidates, not casualties
The banked forms — exp(−281), α⁵⁶, 7·exp(−282) — are not "wrong fits to be deleted." Reframe them as **candidate values of ρ_Λ(μ) at specific μ.** Once the forced μ is derived target-blind, it either **lands on one of them → that form is vindicated as Derived**, or it doesn't → they stay Identified. Either way the earlier work is *justified-and-verified*, not demolished. (K1065a still holds: any such match must pin the convention first.)

## The two aspects of μ — don't conflate them (this reconciles Lyra + the magnitude)
The renormalization scale μ enters cosmology two distinct ways:
1. **Dynamics — is μ time-dependent (= H(a))?** Lyra's pivot: **NO**, μ=H is not geometry-forced (BST holography is UV, not IR; the Shilov boundary S⁴×S¹ is a *fixed compact* surface, not the growing horizon; H is not among λ_k = k(k+5)). → **w = −1 forced.** PASS.
2. **Magnitude — what fixes the constant value of μ?** **OPEN** — the forced-μ derivation above.
These are different questions with the same symbol. Lyra closed (1) cleanly (dynamics: μ≠H(a)). (2) is the magnitude derivation, still open. The DE dynamics being forced (w=−1) does not settle the magnitude, and the magnitude being open does not reopen the dynamics — Lyra's orthogonality (K1070) guarantees exactly this separation.
