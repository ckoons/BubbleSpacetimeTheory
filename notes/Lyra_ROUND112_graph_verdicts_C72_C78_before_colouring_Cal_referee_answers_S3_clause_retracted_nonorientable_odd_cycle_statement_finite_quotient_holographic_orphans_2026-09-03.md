## Cal's three referee questions
1. **Path-independence — is the parity sum a cocycle?** On S² it is NOT: around a dislocation the sum of s over one lap of the link is ±1
   (five alternating signs with one frustrated adjacency). It is well defined on the branched cover Σ (two laps cancel). What the theorem
   uses is not a cocycle but the LATTICE: L is generated by the differences along any one spanning tree of dislocation paths, and
   changing a path by a loop around a dislocation changes the difference by an element of P = 2L ⊆ L. So "all tree charges 0" ⟹ L ⊆ Λ₀
   (charge is additive along the tree; periods are doubles of differences); conversely one nonzero tree charge exhibits a charged vector
   in L. The verdict is tree-independent because L is.
2. **"Spoke j" at a degree-6 vs a degree-5 vertex.** The rule counts face-crossings between the incoming edge's left face and the outgoing
   edge's left face: j + 1 crossings, each flipping σ̃. It is the same at every vertex of Σ, including a branch point passed through once
   (the sectors are traversed on one lift). At a degree-5 vertex the only difference is that a full lap changes sheet — irrelevant to a
   path that enters and leaves.
3. **Is (L1) the j = 0 case?** Yes: a length-1 path has no intermediate vertex, so its charge is the single sign s = ±1. The adjacency lemma
   is the empty turning sequence.

## §6 — Holographic orphans (one paragraph, existence only)
An orphan needs three things: a record with a LOCAL closure law, a transport with holonomy, and a realizability condition that fails
globally. Holography as usually stated (T571 Bekenstein = Shannon converse on the Shilov boundary; T1420 Ryu–Takayanagi on D_IV⁵) gives
a boundary→bulk map that is a bijection on the states it is defined on — every boundary state IS a bulk state — so there is no
"closed but realized by no bulk" and the theory adds nothing there. Realizability could have content only where the boundary data is
given LOCALLY (a datum per boundary cell with a closure law) and the bulk is reconstructed by transport: then the obstruction class
would be the holonomy of the reconstruction around boundary loops (π₁(S⁴ × S¹) = ℤ, one generator), valued in whatever G_ops turns out
to be. Until a local boundary law is written, there is no orphan to define. Declared: no content today.
