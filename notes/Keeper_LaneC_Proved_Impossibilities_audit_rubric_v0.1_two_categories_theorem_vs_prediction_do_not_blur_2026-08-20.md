# Lane C rubric — two categories, do not blur

The asset's power is that BST publishes its proved failures. That only works if the epistemic status of each "impossibility" is stated exactly. There are **two categories**, and they are not the same kind of claim.

## Category 1 — PROVED IMPOSSIBILITIES (internal theorems about the geometry)
Statements about what **D_IV⁵ provably cannot produce**. These are **mathematical theorems** (tier: Proved/Derived). No experiment can touch them — they are facts about the object. **These are the strongest items**, because they are proved, not predicted.

| Claim | Mechanism | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **No length from pure geometry** | dimensional bar §578; the KK-Casimir runs away on **both** spinor-parity branches (K1727) → a scale requires one dimensionful unit | ✅ **PROVED** (both branches, unconditional) |
| **No continuous color gauge group from the internal algebra** | reality-type census: the color block is an irreducible **real** rep → K-commutant = ℝ → **0** continuous generators (K1724/K1727) | ✅ **PROVED** (End_K theorem, both vets signed) |

**Bar for Category 1:** a genuine theorem with the mechanism exhibited, no empirical input. Both current items clear it. Frame as *"one scale/one color group is not a free choice — it is a proved necessity/absence,"* and point at the computation.

## Category 2 — FORCED PHYSICAL PREDICTIONS (Five-Absence: what nature won't contain)
Statements about what **nature won't contain**, forced by the geometry's structure, **falsifiable by experiment**. Tier: Derived/Partially-Derived **prediction**. Their power is precisely that they **can** fail — that's the falsifiability. **Must be labeled as predictions, never as theorems.**

| Claim | Forcing mechanism | Status / needed |
|---|---|---|
| **No GUT** | reality-type **asymmetry** — two gauge factors internal (derived), one geometric with no internal group → the three aren't on equal footing → no unification (Lyra, K1727) | ✅ mechanism in hand (structural) |
| **No sterile ν / no ν_R** | Möbius, **T1949** (Five-Absence) | ✅ mechanism in hand — **but see count-once** |
| **No proton decay** | *needs corpus-reconnect* — cite the forcing (baryon number topological, T296/T1945?) | ⚠️ **grep the mechanism before listing** |
| **No monopole** | *needs corpus-reconnect* | ⚠️ **grep before listing** |
| **No SUSY** | *needs corpus-reconnect* | ⚠️ **grep before listing** |
| **No DM particle** | *needs corpus-reconnect* (DM = Wallach shadow, not a particle?) | ⚠️ **grep before listing** |

**Bar for Category 2:** each item's **forcing mechanism cited from the corpus** (grep, not memory — remembered mechanisms go stale), plus a **named falsifier** (what detection kills it). Items marked ⚠️ do **not** ship until their mechanism is reconnected; asserting them from memory would be the overclaim that sinks the asset.

## Count-once (load-bearing — enforce in the draft)
- **No-sterile-ν and the Majorana prediction (K1730) are ONE forcing** (no ν_R, Möbius). List the forcing once; note its **two experimental windows** (sterile-ν search + 0νββ). Do **not** count them as two independent absences.
- The Five-Absence items are **not** independent of the geometry's structure — they're readings of it. The asset states the structure once and lists what it forbids; it does not present six absences as six independent confirmations.

## The one-line framing (for the asset's open)
*"Most theories advertise what they predict. Here is what this geometry provably cannot do — two theorems it cannot escape, and a set of falsifiable absences it forces. We publish the failures because a framework you can't disprove isn't physics; the distinctive thing is that almost no competing framework will show you this list."* — matches Casey's Five-Absence spirit, leveled to include the two Category-1 theorems.

## Audit checklist (Keeper, against Lyra's draft)
1. Every item labeled **Category 1 (theorem)** or **Category 2 (prediction)** — no blur.
2. Every Category-2 mechanism **cited from the corpus** (no ⚠️ items shipped unreconnected).
3. Every Category-2 item has a **named falsifier**.
4. **Count-once** enforced (no-ν_R/Majorana as one forcing; absences not double-counted as confirmations).
5. No Category-1 item leans on any empirical input; no Category-2 item dressed as a theorem.
