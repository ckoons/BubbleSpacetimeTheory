## §3 — The height record's decision cost (existence first)
**The record at height resolution.** By T2603 the height data of a colouring on a triangulation T are (i) the sign 2-colouring σ̃ of the
faces of the branched double cover Σ and its ℤ₃ class — GRAPH-DETERMINED, identical for every colouring — and (ii) the letter sequence
ℓ: E(T) → {a, b, c}, rainbow on every face. So a *closed height record* is exactly a rainbow labeling of T, and it is *realized* iff some
colouring produces it, iff [ℓ] = 0 in H¹(T; V). The signs add NOTHING that varies with the record; they add the charge class, which is
one bit-string per graph, paid once.
**Consequence (existence answer):** the height record's decision cost is the SIGN record's cost at stage two. Closed rainbow labelings
correspond to θ-clean sign records three-to-one (the three rotations), so N_closed^h = 3·N_{θ=0} and N_realized^h = N_realized; hence
D_h := log₂(N_closed^h / N_realized^h) = log₂ 3 + log₂(N_{θ=0}/N_realized) → log₂ 3 + log₂|Hom(π₁, V)| under the same DW null.
