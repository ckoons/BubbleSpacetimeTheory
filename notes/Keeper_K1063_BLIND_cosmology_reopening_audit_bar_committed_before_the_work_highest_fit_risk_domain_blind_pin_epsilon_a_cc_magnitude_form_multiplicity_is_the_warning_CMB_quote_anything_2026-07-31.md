# K1063 — Blind bar for the cosmology reopening (the highest fit-risk domain)

The SM sector is complete; cosmology is the primary next domain (Casey's plan). It is also the **single highest target-awareness-risk domain** in the program — *every* dark-energy drift this session lived here (the two w₀ forms, the target-aware −0.949, the dead-R(K) leg, the CMB-quote-anything hazard). So the checker's half is committed **before the work starts**, the proven pattern (K929/K1047/K1051/K1061 — every pre-registered bar paid off).

## The four guards (pinned blind)

**1. The ε(a) / w(a) forward computation (task #54) — target-innocent or it's a fit.**
w = −1 is the fixed-C·π⁵-volume result (K1040); the deviation **ε(a) = w+1 is currently mechanism-intuition, not derived.** Deriving it forward (from the running of the Bergman matched-fraction) must be **blind-pinned**: the observed w₀ is NOT fed in, and NO w₀ form is selected to match DESI. **PASS:** ε(a) derived target-innocently — and it should come out **small, decaying, ≲ 10⁻⁴** (the fixed-volume prediction; a deviation there would be a *tension*, not a free win). **FAIL:** re-selecting a w₀ deviation to match the data — the −0.949 relapse, one domain restarted.

**2. The cc-magnitude (Λ ~ 10⁻¹²²) — the deep and dangerous one.**
It already has **multiple banked forms** — exp(−281) = rank·N_max + g (T1959, **I-tier**), plus the 10⁻¹²⁰ "3 forms." **That multiplicity IS the fishing flag** — the exact pattern of the two w₀ forms and the multiplicity-of-11 (Rule 11; a number reachable many ways proves little). So:
- The magnitude is **IDENTIFIED at best** until a mechanism forces the exponent *target-innocently* (would the exponent come out without knowing it's ~122 orders?). Is 281 = rank·N_max + g forced, or reverse-engineered to hit the observed suppression?
- **Do NOT upgrade to Derived by matching 10⁻¹²².** A clean exponent that hits the biggest number in physics is *exactly* the kind of match that invites a fit (Cal's flag).
- The **"Λ-small = substrate-coupling-small"** lead (K1040) must be a *forced mechanism* (the coupling's smallness derived), not the circular restatement "the number is small because the coupling is small."

**3. CMB — quote-anything.**
CMB anomalies fit a dozen stories. Any CMB claim is **target-not-evidence until a distinctive, PRE-REGISTERED signal** (blind-pinned before the datum). No fitting a BST story to an existing anomaly.

**4. General — blind-pin every rational; calibrate both ways.**
Every rational (Ω ratios, z_recomb, N_eff, w₀, wₐ) blind-pinned before the datum. And calibrate *both* ways: a genuinely-forced cosmology result is as real as a forced SM one (don't reflexively dismiss) — but the bar is *higher* here because the fit-temptation is higher (don't reflexively bank a clean match).

## What stands going in
- **w = −1** (K1040) — the fixed-volume equation-of-state prediction, honest Structural. The reopening derives ε(a) forward and attacks the magnitude; it does not re-litigate w=−1 by data-match.
- The **a₀/a₁/a₂ ladder** places Λ at a₀ — the cc-magnitude is the a₀ coefficient's *size* (the regularized volume term), separate from the equation of state (already w=−1).

## The bet
If the ε(a) forward lands target-innocently and the cc-magnitude gets a *forced* exponent (not a form-selected one), BST has a genuine dynamical-DE-vs-Λ prediction and a handle on the cosmological-constant problem — the real prizes. If either is fit, it stays Identified, honestly. The discipline that made the SM trustworthy is exactly what this domain needs; apply it hardest here.
