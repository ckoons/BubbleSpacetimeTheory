# K1738 — Lane D: Invariant 3 (rank) reads the strata structure; assignment cleanliness is the real remaining rigor

## Invariant 3's readability criterion (candidate)
The rank r=2 gives the Korányi–Wolf orbit strata (r+1 = 3: bulk / Cartan / Shilov), realized as the **shared 3-point ν-ladder {5/2, 3/2, 0}** that all four fermion sectors sit on (F86/F381; T2525 ratified). Criterion:

> **An observable is readable by Invariant 3 iff it is a function of the rank+1 strata structure (the count and nesting of the r+1 orbits) — not the coupling on them.**

## What it reads (structure) / doesn't read (spectrum)
- **Reads:** generation count = r+1 = 3 (T2525); mixing-matrix **dimension** 3×3 (**count-once** with generations — same reading); the **Koide** ratio Q = (2+rank)/(2(rank+1)) = 2/3 and amplitude A² = rank = 2 (K672 — a genuine *second* rank reading, the structure of the mass relation); the shared 3-strata ν-ladder (F381).
- **Does not read:** individual mass **values**, mixing **angles**, which stratum is heaviest — all **spectrum** (the coupling function on the ladder, not the ladder). Invariant 3 reads the ladder; never the numbers on the rungs.

## ★ The key realization — assignment cleanliness is the real rigor
The corpus flag "N_gen = 3 is over-determined by 4 routes" (Hodge/Q⁵, Möbius, KW/Wallach, census — Cal #35: 4-independent or rank+1 in 4 dialects?) is the **same phenomenon** as the SU(2)_L and CP **double-assignments** I flagged in K1736: **a reading claimed by multiple invariants.** And the 4 routes *span* the invariants — KW = rank (Inv 3), Möbius = non-orientability (Inv 2), Q⁵/census = Inv 1.

So the real remaining rigor for the three-invariant theorem is not only Lane A's droppability toy — it is **assignment cleanliness**: every reading mapped to exactly the invariant(s) that force it, every multi-assignment resolved (collapse to one, or mark genuinely over-determined with all mechanisms, **count once**). **Three multi-assignments to resolve before Lane B ships:**
1. **SU(2)_L** — Inv 1 (ℍ commutant) vs Inv 2 (Pin(2)-Möbius).
2. **CP-existence** — Inv 1 (ε(ℍ)) vs Inv 2 (non-orientable locus).
3. **N_gen = 3** — Inv 3 (KW) vs Inv 2 (Möbius) vs Inv 1 (census) — the "4 routes."

**Discipline** (generalizes K1736): the table is citable only when every cell names its invariant(s) unambiguously. A two-cell reading is a **consistency web** (state both mechanisms, count once) or a **conflation** (resolve to one) — compute, don't assert. Cal #35's N_gen audit and K1736's SU(2)_L/CP audit are the **same task** — do them together. Likely: N_gen is fundamentally the rank reading (Inv 3, T2525 cleanest); the other "routes" are count-once restatements or labelled corroborations.
