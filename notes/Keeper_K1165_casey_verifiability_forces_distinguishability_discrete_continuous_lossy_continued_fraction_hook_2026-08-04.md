# K1165 — verifiability forces distinguishability; discrete→continuous is lossy; the continued-fraction hook

Casey's engineer's-lever move — physical/engineering insight turning a stuck gate into a computation. The team had converged on the exact gap (Grace: *info theory forces redundancy, but the fermion needs distinguishability — rule out a bosonic record*). Casey answers it from communications/crypto.

## Contribution 1 — verifiability forces distinguishability (the clean L4 route)
*"You need more than identical redundancy — your error correction may itself fail, so you need a distinguishing format to verify it's valid."* Coding-theory-true: error **detection/verification requires minimum distance ≥ 2 = distinguishable codewords**; an identical-boson record has no addressable/distinguishing format → it can't run a syndrome check → it fails as a record. Fermionic exclusion gives **distinct, addressable states** (the Pauli addressable-memory, Elie 5049) → a distinguishing format → **verifiable** error correction.
**The L4 chain now:** a record that must stay valid ⟹ its correction must be verifiable ⟹ a distinguishing (addressable) format ⟹ fermionic. This rules out the bosonic record **by verifiability** — cleaner than Elie's "bosons condense." **Still a LEAD:** the physics identification (distinguishing code-symbol ↔ distinct fermionic mode) needs rigor; but the logical gap is now narrow.

## Contribution 2 — the discrete→continuous projection is intrinsically lossy (sources L2)
Interior and exterior are fundamentally different (discrete vs continuous); the exterior is a lossy projection needing **additional bits to cover translation/projection errors**, with the continuum's symmetries reinforcing verification. So L2's lossiness is **forced by the discrete/continuous mismatch itself** — observer-free. (The uncertainty −2/g is a candidate for the projection's intrinsic error rate / rate-distortion — tie it quantitatively.) Persistence reframes: the exterior continuum **is** the persistently re-projected record, accumulating projection error unless corrected.

## Contribution 3 — the continued-fraction hook (LEAD, deep + specific)
*"That may be why the finite continued fractions of a real number need to encode additional error correction."* Model: a real (continuum) is approached by finite CFs (rationals = the substrate's committable values); the finite CF is the codeword, the real is the message, the projection error is the CF tail → the representation carries error-correction overhead. Real substance: CF = best rational approximation; the Gauss map has KS-entropy π²/(12 ln 2); Khinchin/Lévy; Diophantine approximation quantifies the finite-CF error. Ties to **T719 Observable Closure** (observables in Q̄(primaries)[π]). New research direction.
