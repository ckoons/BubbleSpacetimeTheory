## The answer, in one breath

**One shape, and a handful of things you can do to it. Every number in physics is one of those things, done once.**

- *(twelve-year-old)* There's a single shape. You build it out of five counting numbers. Everything in physics is what you get by measuring that shape a few standard ways.
- *(referee)* A single rank-2 bounded symmetric domain, **D_IV⁵ = SO(5,2)/[SO(5)×SO(2)]**, fixed by five integers (rank 2, N_c 3, n_C 5, C₂ 6, g 7). Every dimensionless observable is a matrix element, eigenvalue, grading, or boundary invariant of that one object.

## The shape

- *(12)* Two flat directions, three "color" directions, five in all, folded so it has an inside, a rim, and an outside. That folding is forced — there's only one shape that fits the five numbers.
- *(ref)* D_IV⁵ is the unique Hermitian symmetric domain with short-root multiplicity a = N_c = 3 at dimension n_C = 5; interior (discrete K-types) / Shilov boundary / exterior continuum. The five integers are geometric invariants, not inputs.

## The five things you can do to it — the operator dictionary

**1. Turn the dial (the Casimir H_B).** *[PROVED — one operator, read three ways.]*
- *(12)* One dial. Turn it smoothly and things flow and can flow back — that's ordinary quantum physics. Turn it the hard way and things settle, once — that's a measurement. Same dial. And "things settle in order" is why time has a direction.
- *(ref)* A positive self-adjoint generator has a **unitary face** exp(iτH_B) — Schrödinger evolution — and a **contractive face** exp(−τH_B) — the heat semigroup, whose positivity is the arrow of time and whose action is the measurement commit. **Schrödinger + arrow + measurement are one operator.** A mathematical identity, not a resemblance.

**2. Weigh the possibilities (the invariant measure).** *[Born PROVED; the commit preserves it — now DERIVED.]*
- *(12)* There's only one fair way to weigh chances on this shape, and it turns out to *be* "square the amplitude." So the Born rule isn't a rule someone picked; it's the only fair scale — and when the shape settles, it keeps those exact odds.
- *(ref)* The domain has a unique automorphism-invariant measure (Bergman/FK; Lebesgue excluded) — **Born = its density (T754, proved)**. The commit is the forced composition *Wallach-drop ∘ measure-keep* (divergent modes are non-states, so the drop is forced), which **preserves the Born weights among the real states — Derived** (edge 1, closed).

**3. The odd fold (the spin-factor structure).** *[Spin-½ NATIVE — PROVED; that it is *literally* the Shilov-quotient ℤ₂ — a LEAD.]*
- *(12)* The shape is the kind mathematicians call a "spin-factor" — spinors are its native alphabet. So a half-twisted particle *is* a fermion (that's why matter is stiff), and the same spinor structure is why square-roots keep showing up all over the theory. (Whether that half-twist is *literally* the fold in the shape's rim is a promising lead, not yet proved.)
- *(ref)* n_C = 5 is odd, so the domain is a type-IV **spin-factor** (Clifford/Jordan) domain — the spinor is its algebraic backbone, so spin-½ is native and is the source of every √ in BST (ρ = (5/2,3/2), the Cabibbo √): **PROVED**. (The spin ℤ₂ is *not* the Shilov-quotient ℤ₂ — that one is the parity element −I₅, det −1, a distinct −1; F809. The domain's own "three distinct −1's" — spin, parity/charge-sign, matter/anti — are confirmed separate by determinant, not merged.) Spin + all the √'s = the spin-factor structure, **proved**.

**4. Its Gamma function (masses).** *[Down-quark ratio PROVED; Koide product a LEAD.]*
- *(12)* The shape has its own special number-machine (a Gamma function). Feed the quark generations through it as a *ratio* and out comes their mass ladder, 1 : 20 : 840 — no fitting. The leptons seem to use the same machine as a *product* — that's a promising lead, not yet proved.
- *(ref)* The Gindikin Γ_Ω. **Down-quarks = the ratio** (N_c)_k = Γ(N_c+k)/Γ(N_c) at k∈{1,3,5} → 1:20:840, **proved**. **Koide = the product** Γ(ν)Γ(ν−N_c/2) at the strata — a **lead** (its 45° tilt is a *dynamical* equal-coupling, not yet forced; the naive same-Gamma collapse is downgraded honestly).

**5. Its edge (the boundary).** *[A GROUPING of distinct forced structures, not one identity.]*
- *(12)* The rim of the shape does several jobs — it sets electric charge, it emits light, and it's why the strong force has no CP problem. These are all rim-things, but they're *different* rim-things, not one trick.
- *(ref)* Boundary structures, each forced but **distinct**: charge = the SO(2)-weight (T2470); emission = the Hardy/Szegő extension; θ_QCD = 0 = contractibility (∫c₂ ≡ 0). A grouping of forced boundary invariants — *not* collapsed to a single reading.

## The reading map

- *(12)* Learn the shape and how to count on it, and you can read any physics number off it — the way you read a clock face once you know it's a clock.
- *(ref)* Every observable = ⟨the one object | one canonical operation⟩. A grade-schooler learns the shape and the counting; a graduate student reads the operator dictionary; both are reading the *same* object, and the reduction is a proved partition, not an assertion.

## The honest boundary (what keeps this a proof, not a poster)

- **Proved and confident:** the Casimir's three faces; spin-½'s spin-factor nativeness; Born from the measure; the commit preserves Born (edge 1, closed).
- **One gate left (measurement):** the eraser/Bell statistics (CHSH, 1/2^{N_c}).
- **Leads, marked as leads:** the Koide product (dynamical); several dimensionful values (given the one anchor).
- **Settled by check (a walk-back that landed):** the "spin = Shilov-ℤ₂" identity is refuted — the two are distinct −1's by determinant (F809); spin-factor nativeness stands.
- **A grouping, not an identity:** the boundary's charge/emission/θ_QCD.
- **A boundary datum, not a shortfall:** the single measurement outcome — given, as every theory takes its initial data.

**The claim, exactly as far as it goes:** physics is fewer things than it looks — most of it is one shape read a few standard ways — and this page says precisely which readings are proved identities, which are leads, which are groupings, and which is data. That is the smallest object that can do physics, and the honesty about where the compression stops is what makes it checkable.
