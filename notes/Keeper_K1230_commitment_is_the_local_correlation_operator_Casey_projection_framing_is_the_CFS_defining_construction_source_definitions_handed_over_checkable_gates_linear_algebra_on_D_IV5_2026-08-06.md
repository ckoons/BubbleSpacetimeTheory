## Finster's actual definitions (handed to the team)
- **Data:** a causal fermion system is (H, F, ρ): H a Hilbert space; F the self-adjoint finite-rank operators with ≤ n positive and ≤ n negative eigenvalues (**n = spin dimension**); ρ the **universal measure**.
- **Local correlation operator:** F(x)_j^i = −⟨ψ_i(x) | ψ_j(x)⟩ — **built from the occupied (committed) states** ψ_i.
- **Spacetime emerges:** **M := supp ρ** (the support of the measure).
- **Spin space:** S_x = x(H), dimension ≤ 2n; spin scalar product ≺u|v≻_x = −⟨u|xv⟩, signature (p,q), p,q ≤ n.
- **Causal structure (from eigenvalues λ_j of the product xy):** **spacelike if all |λ_j| are EQUAL** (then L = 0); **timelike if the |λ_j| DIFFER and are real**; lightlike otherwise.
- **Pauli:** the Fock state = wedge product of occupied wavefunctions (**total antisymmetrization**).
- **Dynamics:** the causal action S = ∬ L(x,y) dρ dρ, L = |(xy)²| − (1/2n)|xy|², minimized (Euler-Lagrange).

The substrate (H) is projected to the 3+1 world (supp ρ) by the commitments. That is the CFS construction, verbatim.
