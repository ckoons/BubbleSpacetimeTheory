## 0. What differs from F473, or the lane does not run
F473's object was a **map**: the descent SO(5,2) → SO(4,2) → SO(3,1) was supposed to carry the three colour directions *into space*, so that SO(3)_colour = SO(3)_spatial. The direction-accounting was never computed, and F474 found a signature bug in it. Since then F4 has become a theorem: no group in the interior acts on V₁₂ as colour. That forbids F473's identification in the interior.
**This lane's object is the commitment unit on the boundary, not a descent map.** Colour is the *index* of a write inside one 3D unit. A colour singlet (ε_ijk) is a complete unit, and "confined" means only complete units commit.
**The trap, stated so it cannot pass silently:** if "the axis a write lands on" means a *spatial* direction, this is F473 in a new costume. It then dies at once, because a spatial rotation would rotate a quark's colour, and colour is invariant under rotations. So the lane runs **only** with the axes as the commit's internal coordinate labels, where the SU(3) acting on them is not the SO(3) of space. Once that is stated, "N_c = D" is no longer automatic.
