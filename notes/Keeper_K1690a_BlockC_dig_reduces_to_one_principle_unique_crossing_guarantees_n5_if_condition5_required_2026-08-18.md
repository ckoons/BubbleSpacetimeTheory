# K1690a — Block C reduces to one principle (and the crossing guarantees n=5 if it's required)

## The reduction
Condition 5 is `C₂/(2n_C) = (n+1)/(2n)` [the half-Casimir = the commitment flow's heat-kernel **decay rate**, `e^{−τD²}`] `= (n−2)/n = N_c/n_C` [the B₂ **short-root fraction**]. As functions of `n`:
- LHS `(n+1)/(2n)` is **decreasing** (`1 → 0.6 → ½`).
- RHS `(n−2)/n` is **increasing** (`0 → 0.6 → 1`).

A decreasing and an increasing function cross **at most once** — and they cross at `n=5`. **Guarantee: if the equality is a required constraint, `n=5` is the *unique* solution → forced.** So all of Block C collapses to one question.

## The one question (it's a principle, not LA)
**Is the commitment flow's decay rate *required* to equal its own geometry's short-root fraction?** The two sides are *different physical quantities* — how fast commitment decays vs the color/root content — so their equality is not a structural identity; it needs a forcing **principle**. The candidate (Lyra's frontier): a **self-referential balance** — "the commitment flow must decay at the rate set by its own root structure" — which makes Condition 5 a **fixed point** of the commitment dynamics (`e^{−τD²}` self-consistent with the geometry that generates it), with `n=5` the unique fixed point. This connects to **P2b (minimality)**: if minimality selects the `n` where the flow is self-consistent, that `n` is uniquely 5.

## Keeper's honest lean (calibrated both ways)
I could **not** derive the forcing principle from the LA alone — the two sides are independently geometry-derived quantities that lock at `n=5`, and absent a principle *requiring* the balance, Condition 5 is a deep **selection**, not a forcing → the honest close leans **boundary** (`n_C=5` over-determined input). **But the door stays open:** the self-referential-balance principle is a real candidate (the commitment flow *is* the heat flow whose decay is the half-Casimir), and if Lyra exhibits it target-innocently, `n=5` **promotes** to forced — uniquely, by the crossing.

## Route + guard
- **Lyra** — is there a target-innocent principle requiring commitment-flow-decay = short-root-fraction (a fixed point of `e^{−τD²}` with the B₂ geometry)? Does `P2b` + that principle force `n=5`?
- **Elie** — is the equality a *forced* fixed point of the commitment dynamics, or does it merely hold at 5? (Target-innocence: is the constraint independently motivated or reverse-engineered to give 5?)
- **Guard:** the crossing's *uniqueness* is **free** (any two monotone-opposite functions cross once) — do **not** mistake "n=5 is the unique crossing" for "n=5 is forced." The uniqueness is cheap; the forcing is entirely in whether the constraint is *required*. Never "commitment forces D_IV⁵" until the principle lands. Heavy resources stay held; escalate on the principle.
