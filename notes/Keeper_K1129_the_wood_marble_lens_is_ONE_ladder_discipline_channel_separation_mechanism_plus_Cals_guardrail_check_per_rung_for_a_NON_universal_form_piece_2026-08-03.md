# K1129 — one ladder, one discipline; and the guardrail that keeps the lens from over-correcting

## (1) The lens is one discipline across the whole ladder
Lyra's recognition, crystallized: Cal's G-catch, her own a₂ (11/3) Tier-2 hold, and the δτ/F781 finding are **one discipline, not three** — *never publish the wood as if it were the marble.* Every heat-trace rung has the identical structure:

| Rung | Universal FORM (wood — imported, never the claim) | D_IV⁵ COEFFICIENT (marble — the result) | Position/normalization |
|---|---|---|---|
| a₀ → Λ | volume term | 225=(N_c·n_C)², c₀=1/960 | age-set (the cosmic age) |
| a₁ → G | (1/6)R | κ_Bergman=−n_C, ℓ_B²/π^{n_C} | anchor-set (ℓ_B=tick) |
| a₂ → β | 11/3 (4D-YM) | sign, N_c, n_f | — (running) |
| a₅ → ζ(0) | (universal) | structure | — |
| F781 | exp(−τH) semigroup | spectral gap | age-set (τ_now) |

**Mechanism — the channel-separation theorem:** the forms are **orthogonal by mass dimension**, so the universal piece and the D_IV⁵ piece cannot be conflated without an error. That is *why* "publish the coefficient, never the form" is enforceable, not merely advisory. **Publication rule, all rungs:** "D_IV⁵ forces the coefficient," never "D_IV⁵ derives the [universal form]."

## (2) Cal's guardrail — ratified (the calibrate-both-ways completion)
The lens **default** is form-universal / coefficient-forced. **But** a rung could carry a genuinely **non-universal form-piece** that a generic operator does *not* produce — and that would be **distinctive forced content beyond the coefficient**:
- a geometry-specific **endomorphism E** (the operator's potential term);
- a **boundary term** — **D_IV⁵ has a Shilov boundary (S⁴×S¹/Z₂), and a manifold-with-boundary produces extra Seeley-DeWitt boundary coefficients a closed manifold does not** — a concrete place distinctive content may hide;
- a **topological term**.

**So per rung: apply the lens as the default, but check for a non-universal form-piece.** The lens brakes *over*-claiming a universal form; it must not become a brake that *under*-claims a genuinely distinctive one. This check could **reveal additional forced content** — the upside of calibrating both ways.
