# The Proved Impossibilities of D_IV⁵

## The one to lead with: a 0.0009% match we refuse

Before the list, the single sharpest item — because an honest instrument is proved honest by what it *turns away*. The charged-lepton **Koide relation**, Q = (Σm)/(Σ√m)² = 2/3, holds to **≈ 0.0009%** — the tightest coincidence in the neighbourhood. **We do not claim it here, and our own instrument forbids the claim:** the reading calculus is *structural* (it reads kinds — real/complex/quaternionic — not values), and Koide is a relation among mass *values*, provably outside what the instrument can read. We could have dressed it as a "census-and-rank" derivation; on the vet, that laundered a value through a structural instrument, and we struck it. **A device that refuses its own best-looking number because its stated rules forbid the reading is an instrument, not an advocacy tool.** (Koide is real; it may be derived by a *different* instrument — the mass/eigenvalue one — and sits on our ledger as identified-not-derived. Just not here.)

## Why publish what a theory *cannot* do

A fitted model can absorb almost anything: a new particle, a new coupling, a missing length — you add a parameter and move on. A **forced** geometry cannot. Its structure *forbids* things, and those forbiddings are not hedges — they are predictions, sharper than most positive claims, because a single violation ends the theory. So we publish our impossibilities on purpose. They are the load-bearing test of whether BST is forcing or fitting: **a theory that forbids nothing has explained nothing.**

Three kinds of impossibility follow from the single geometry D_IV⁵ (= SO₀(5,2)/[SO(5)×SO(2)]): things that *do not exist*, structures the geometry *cannot supply*, and a scale it *cannot invent*.

## II. Structural no-go — what the geometry cannot supply

These are theorems about the geometry's own limits — the honest boundaries we do not paper over.

- **No gluon generators — there is no internal color group (K1724).** Decomposing the finite internal space under K = SO(5)×SO(2) is exact (Frobenius reality-type census): the three sectors land on the three real division algebras and contribute internal gauge generators **{0, 1, 3} → 0+1+3 = 4 = dim(U(1)×SU(2))**. The color sector is a *real* 3-space; it contributes **zero** internal generators. So the geometry **cannot geometrize color** the way it geometrizes electroweak — SU(3) is the standard gauge principle applied externally, not read off D_IV⁵. We prove the negative rather than hide it: the electroweak group is derived (its dimension is a *count*), and the color group is exactly what the geometry does *not* contain. *(The geometry still fixes the color number N_c = 3 — a geometric SO(3) rotation of three real directions — and forbids ν_R; it just supplies no color gauge factor.)*

- **The complete ruler theorem — the geometry fixes neither the *scale* nor the *shape* of the ruler (K1727/K1729).** BST takes one dimensionful input ("the ruler"), and we can now prove that both halves of it are genuinely required, not shortfalls:
  - *No scale.* Zeta-regularizing the compactification (Kaluza–Klein) energy of the boundary as a function of its size *runs away* — no interior minimum, both branches — so the geometry **cannot select a length**. A dimensionless set cannot produce a quantity with units; exactly one dimensionful input (a Planck-scale unit) is forced, the same one GR pays as G and QM as ℏ. Proved.
  - *No forced shape.* The dimensionless boundary "shape" (the S⁴/S¹ ratio) has **no canonical value read off the geometry** either: the candidate ratios either slide a mode-weight into a metric (retired), or trace back to a retired target-fit — there is no kernel-free intrinsic ratio the CR/conformal boundary selects. So the shape too is handed to physics, not forced.
  We bank the whole thing as a **win**: "you cannot get a length — or a boundary shape — from pure geometry" is proved. The single ruler is a theorem, not an apology, and BST is honest about *exactly* which one thing it takes.

## Part 2 — what these proofs let us *compute*: the impossibilities are instruments

The no-scale proof is the *sorting instrument* that separates the two — it is why the ~150 dimensionless predictions are the right target and the masses-in-kilograms are not.

  - **Invariant 2 — the non-orientability of the Shilov boundary** (S⁴×S¹)/ℤ₂: two-sided or one-sided? One bit. It buys **parity violation** and **no sterile neutrino** (ν_R cannot close on a Möbius edge).
  - **Invariant 3 — the rank** of D_IV⁵ (= 2): it buys the **number of generations** (rank + 1 = **3**, and no fourth), the factor of 3 in the fermion count (45 = 15 × 3), and the 3×3 structure of the mixing matrices.
