# Cal Section 1028 — PRE-REGISTERED ruling for round 26 (Rac⊗Di's conserved spin-3/2 piece; does anything keep it; is the Forbidden list's SUSY clause a consequence or a posit?), hashed before Lyra; `date` 13:0x run immediately before writing (stamp = commit time)

**Antecedents, verbatim.**

**Group, family and action, stated first.** G = the cover of SO₀(5,2). Rac = L(scalar, 3/2), Di = L(spinor, 2). By Flato–Fronsdal (Lyra R25), Rac⊗Di ⊃ a conserved spin-3/2 module at Δ = d − 2 + 3/2 = 9/2. F(4)'s bosonic part is so(5,2) ⊕ su(2)_R (Nahm; pin owed).

**Kill line.** If a BST structure keeps the spin-3/2 current exactly conserved after the breaking, BST predicts superconformal symmetry, which contradicts the Forbidden list. One of the two goes.

**Rulings and predictions.**
1. **At the free level the spin-3/2 piece IS a conserved fermionic current, and its charge Q is a nonzero operator that maps Rac one-particle states to Di one-particle states.** It is conserved by the free equations, for any free content containing both. **So TD line 99's "two towers are not an operator Q" is FALSE at the free level.** It survives only in the scoped form "Q does not close into a superconformal algebra on BST's content, and it is broken" (points 2–3). Flag it for v1.6, on Casey's word.
2. **Is it an F(4) supercurrent? Predicted NO, by multiplicity** (count owed: Elie and Grace). The free hypermultiplet, F(4)'s supersingleton, restricted to so(5,2) ⊕ su(2)_R, is **(Rac ⊗ 2) ⊕ (Di ⊗ 1)**: the scalars form an su(2)_R doublet (4 real scalars, 4 on-shell fermionic d.o.f.). BST's content is **Rac ⊕ Di, with the Rac ONCE** and no su(2)_R. 1 real scalar d.o.f. against 4 fermionic does not match. **So Q closes into the free theory's higher-spin superalgebra, not into F(4).** BST's free singleton theory is NOT superconformal. Menu risk honoured: the Δs and spins match the hyper, and the multiplicities do not. **Unless BST supplies an su(2) doublet of Racs, which I predict it does not.**
3. **Does anything keep it after the breaking? Predicted NO; enumerated:**
   - (i) **The clock sign Z_t:** a selection rule, not a conservation law. Q has (Z_t, (−1)^F) = (−1, −1) (half-odd weight, half-integer spin), so it is W-even and nothing forbids it. But nothing protects its conservation either.
   - (ii) **The de Sitter breaking (Λ > 0):** unbroken unitary supersymmetry is incompatible with a de Sitter vacuum (no unitary positive-energy representations of the dS superalgebras; Pilch–van Nieuwenhuizen–Sohnius 1985; pin owed). **The measured Λ > 0 breaks any exact SUSY.**
   - (iii) **Interactions:** the higher-spin breaking pattern (round 10) extends. With a stress tensor and no supersymmetry, a spin-3/2 current is not protected.
   - (iv) **The commit:** no structure in the corpus is known to protect a fermionic current.
4. **Consequence or posit? Split, and the split must be stated:**
   - **"No EXACT SUSY" is a CONSEQUENCE:** of (2) (BST's singleton content is not a supermultiplet) and, independently, of (ii) (Λ > 0).
   - **"No SUSY SPECTRUM" (no superpartners at all) is a consequence of (2) ONLY.** If BST's content WERE the hyper, so that F(4) held at the free level and only Λ broke it, the partners would be degenerate up to O(H), which is excluded today (no 0.511 MeV selectron). The clause would then need interactions to break SUSY hard, and that is dynamics, which BST does not have (the third wall). It would be a posit.
   - **So the Forbidden clause is upgraded to a consequence EXACTLY IF the multiplicity count fails (Rac ×1, no su(2)_R doublet).** That count is this round's decisive item.
