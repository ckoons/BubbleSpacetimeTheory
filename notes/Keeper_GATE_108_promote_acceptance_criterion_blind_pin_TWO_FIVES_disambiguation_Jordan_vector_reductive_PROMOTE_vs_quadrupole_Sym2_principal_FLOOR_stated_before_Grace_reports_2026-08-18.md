# GATE-108-PROMOTE — the blind acceptance criterion (two-fives disambiguation)

Reconnecting to the corpus before ruling surfaced the load-bearing catch: **there are two different 5-dim reps, and color SO(3) sits oppositely in each.**

Elie's "corpus leans reductive" reasons *"color SO(3) acts on V₁₂, a 3-subspace of the 5"* — that's the **Jordan** 5.
