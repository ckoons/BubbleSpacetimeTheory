# The merged opening

**The structural decision.** A's cost was that boundaries arrived at paragraph six, after the reader had already classified the paper. B's cost was that a reader stopping early took away only negatives. **The merge puts the forces first — so there is something worth defending — and the boundaries immediately after, on the same page, before the reader has finished deciding.** The boundaries then close the package as well, so they frame rather than interrupt.

## Opening

> **One geometry, and where it stops.**
>
> This package reports what a single mathematical object — the bounded symmetric domain **D_IV⁵ = SO₀(5,2)/[SO(5)×SO(2)]** — determines about the Standard Model when nothing else is added, and, just as precisely, where its reach ends.
>
> The domain is fixed by internal criteria rather than by fitting. Its invariants are five integers — rank 2, N_c = 3, n_C = 5, C₂ = 6, g = 7 — each a property of the geometry: root multiplicities, dimensions, a Casimir. **There are no free dimensionless parameters, and exactly one dimensionful input** — a scale, as general relativity takes G and the Standard Model takes v. That is the whole premise, and it was fixed before any measurement was consulted.
>
> **From it, without further assumption:**
>
> - **the number of colours is three** — it is the short-root multiplicity of the domain's restricted root system, not a count supplied by hand;
> - **the colour symmetry the geometry provides is the real group SO(3)**, and we prove it contains no more;
> - **baryon number is a topological winding**, exactly conserved, forbidding proton decay permanently and predicting that baryon number can change only in multiples of three;
> - **the proton-to-electron mass ratio is 6π⁵ = N_c!·π^{n_C}** — 1836.12 against 1836.15, **0.002%**;
> - **the one-loop running of the strong coupling falls out of the same operator's heat kernel**, blind, with no coefficient adjusted — including the sign that makes the theory asymptotically free. That coefficient is *local*, and therefore valid on flat ℝ⁴ rather than an artifact of the compact boundary we compute on.
>
> **And here is where the geometry stops.** We state this on the first page rather than in an appendix, because the boundary is as much a result as the interior, and each of these is proved rather than conceded:
>
> - **The geometry does not give us SU(3).** It gives SO(3), and we prove it *provably does not contain* the eight gluon generators. The colour dynamics is the gauge principle, imported — the same standard input every derivation of the Standard Model takes.
> - **The boundary spectral gap we compute is not the Yang–Mills mass gap of the Clay problem.** It is a compactification gap of a free, colour-blind operator; it scales as 1/a² and vanishes as the boundary decompactifies, while the Clay gap is generated non-perturbatively and survives that limit at a fixed scale. **They behave oppositely in precisely the limit that defines the Clay problem**, and we do not offer one as evidence for the other.
> - **The charged-lepton mass forms are a patchwork**, not readings of one equation. We call a patchwork a patchwork.
> - **n_C = 5 — the dimension of the domain itself — is an input.** Four independent routes find it special rather than forced.
>
> **What would kill this.** A single neutron–antineutron oscillation event, a proton decay, a supersymmetric partner, a magnetic monopole, a sterile neutrino: any one ends it. A theory that forbids is falsifiable in a way that a theory that accommodates is not, and that asymmetry is the point of the exercise.
>
> The instrument that drew those boundaries is the same one that lowered our own count of derived results **from twenty-one to twelve while none of the numbers moved.** A device that reduces its makers' claims and leaves the data fixed is a working instrument, not an advocacy tool.

## The close (boundaries again, framing rather than interrupting)

> **What we are claiming, exactly.**
>
> One geometry, fixed target-innocently, with no free dimensionless parameter and one dimensionful scale, forces the number of colours, the real colour symmetry SO(3), exactly-conserved baryon number, the proton-to-electron mass ratio to two parts in a hundred thousand, and the correct one-loop running of the strong coupling — the last of these as a local coefficient, valid on flat space.
>
> It does not force SU(3)'s dynamics, which we import as the gauge principle. It does not solve the Yang–Mills mass gap; the gap we compute is a different object that scales oppositely. It does not yet derive the charged-lepton mass forms, which remain a patchwork. And it does not derive its own dimension.
>
> **We think the second list is why the first is worth reading.** Any framework can be made to reproduce numbers. What is rare — and expensive — is knowing exactly where your own reach ends, and being able to prove it.

**The title line does the merge's work.** *"One geometry, and where it stops"* is a forces-first promise with the boundary built into it — the reader is told in six words that this paper knows its own edge, before a single claim is made.
