# K1059 — Section 4 co-audit: the "over-determination" pairs two different scales

Casey directed the Section-4 co-audit (option a). Reconnected to the actual claim and the scales (verify, don't assert — the day's lesson), and it does not clear as written.

## The claim (Section 4, line 53)
> "The a₂ running, via dimensional transmutation, sets the confinement scale. The corpus **independently** derives the confinement scale as the Yang–Mills mass gap Δ = 6π⁵·m_e = 938 MeV."

Presented as an over-determination: two independent routes to "the confinement scale."

## The finding — they are not the same scale (verified)
- The a₂ **running**, dimensionally transmuted, sets **Λ_QCD ≈ 200–210 MeV** — the scale where α_s becomes strong. That is what the β-function delivers.
- **Δ = 6π⁵·m_e = 938 MeV = m_p** — the proton, the baryon mass gap. (Not Λ_QCD; not even the pure-gauge glueball gap ~1710 MeV.)
- **m_p / Λ_QCD ≈ 4.6** — a genuine, separate, **non-perturbative** number, delivered by *neither* the running nor the spectral gap.

So the "two independent derivations of the confinement scale" are derivations of **two different scales** (Λ_QCD ~200 vs m_p ~938). Calling both "the confinement scale" **conflates them.** This is the K1047 hazard (coincidental transmutation dressed as forced) *plus* a scale conflation — the exact "the running derives the proton mass" over-read Cal §178 warned against, made concrete.

## Ruling — reframe (does not clear)
Section 4 must not present an over-determination. Honest reframe:
1. **The a₂ running → Λ_QCD** (dimensional transmutation) — **CANDIDATE**, and only if computed *independently* of any target (Cal's K1047 point). Not yet an independent computation.
2. **Δ = 6π⁵·m_e = m_p** — a **separate** spectral result (the λ₁=7 Bergman gap), banked on its own (0.002%; W4 open).
3. **The bridge — m_p/Λ_QCD ≈ 4.6** — an **OPEN** non-perturbative computation. Until it lands, the running-scale and the mass-gap value are two *different* results, not one over-determined quantity.
So: either cut Section 4's over-determination claim, or reframe it as "two separate strong-sector results (a running scale Λ_QCD and a mass-gap value m_p), with the ratio between them an open non-perturbative target" — and drop "independently derive the [same] confinement scale."

## What clears
Sections 1–3 (with the K1057 Sym²⊥Λ² fix + the K1058 identification-tier), 5, 6, 7 are honest. **Section 6 ("what is NOT claimed") is strong** — it already lists 11/3 universal Tier-2, sign PASS pending K933, Λ/G framework, W4 open, no weld. Section 4 is the one non-clearing section.
