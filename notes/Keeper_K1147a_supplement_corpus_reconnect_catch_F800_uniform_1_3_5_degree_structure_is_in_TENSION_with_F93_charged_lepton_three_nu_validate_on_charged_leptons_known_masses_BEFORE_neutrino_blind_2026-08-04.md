## Self-correction on K1147
I wrote "F93 — the three neutrinos are the ρ-vector components." **Imprecise.** F93 is about the **charged leptons** (e/μ/τ): their three generation *weights* are ν ∈ {5/2, 3/2, 0} (the two ρ-components + 0), **one ν per generation at a fixed degree.** Corrected.

## The tension (the load-bearing catch)
- **F800** (today, the neutrino-test foundation): all three-generation sectors share the *degrees* **{1,3,5}** (the F86 strata), with **one ν per sector** (down-quark ν=N_c=3 → {3,60,2520}); it extends this to charged leptons too.
- **F93** (June): the same charged leptons are **three different ν's {5/2,3/2,0}** at fixed degree.

These are **two different structures for the same three lepton generations** — F800 = one-ν/three-degrees, F93 = three-ν/one-degree — and they generically give different mass patterns. **Unreconciled**, and the neutrino test sits on the F800 side.

Certifying "neutrino over-determination via the same FK measure as the quarks" while the **charged leptons** (also a three-generation lepton sector) use a *different* parametrization would be internally inconsistent — a referee catches it. **The neutrino claim can't be cleaner than the lepton structure it assumes.**
