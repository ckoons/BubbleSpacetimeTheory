# K1187 — the flavor sector as an ordered non-commutative product: mixing = non-commutativity, order = the commit cycle, and it may force the values

Casey's ranging shot, developed. Three parts + a payoff.

## Mixing = non-commutativity (exact linear algebra)
Three operators on the fermion space: **M_up** (up masses = the boundary ladder, α/shell, T2515), **M_down** (down masses = FK norms), **J_W** (weak current = the degree-1 cohomology step). CKM = the matrix elements of J_W between the *eigenbases* of M_up and M_down. **If they shared a basis (commuted), mixing = exactly 0.** So the mixing *is* the failure of the mass operators and the weak current to share a basis — non-commutativity made into a number. Casey's insight, precise.

## The order is the commit cycle (the sequence to deduce — not arbitrary)
The mass operator M is the **commit** — the diagonal, the stored state = the item-10 record (record's norm = mass). J_W is the **emit** — the transition between records. The SWPP cycle is fixed: absorb → **commit → emit**. Commit precedes emit → mass-first-then-weak → exactly why CKM = U_up†·U_down (mass basis, *then* the weak transition). **The non-commutative order to deduce is the SWPP cycle.**

## The unification
Flavor isn't bolted onto QM: the **masses are the norms of the committed records**, the **mixing is the emitted transitions between them**. The same commit cycle that closed QM generates the fermion masses and mixing.

## The payoff — the ordered product may force the values (the current worry)
The plan was to grind the values with a brute radial integral. But if the mixing is the **ordered product** (M_down-basis · J_W · M_up-basis), the Wolfenstein λ-tower may **fall out of the operator algebra**: M is the boundary ladder (a factor of α *per shell*), J_W steps by *one degree* — and a product of "step down by a factor per shell" and "step across by one degree" is exactly what **generates a geometric hierarchy of powers** = the λ-tower. So the order-insight may be the *forced route to the values*, not just elegant bookkeeping.

## The concrete test (runs alongside the radial fire)
Write CKM = (M_up rotation) · J_W · (M_down rotation) as an explicit ordered product of the *forced* operators; compute it; **does λ ≈ 0.22, λ², λ³, and the 0.43 corner fall out of the order, with no brute integral and no tuning?** Yes → the values are forced by the commit-cycle *order* (strongest outcome); No → the brute radial fire stands. A second, independent route.

## Honest tier (a lead / ranging shot)
**Solid:** mixing = non-commutativity (exact); the commit→emit order (SWPP + item 10). **To test:** that the ordered product produces λ, λ², λ³, and the corner; and that M=commit / J_W=emit is the right identification. Not a claim — a promising reframe with a concrete, falsifiable test. Nothing banks; the values still ride a computation (ordered-product *or* brute radial).
