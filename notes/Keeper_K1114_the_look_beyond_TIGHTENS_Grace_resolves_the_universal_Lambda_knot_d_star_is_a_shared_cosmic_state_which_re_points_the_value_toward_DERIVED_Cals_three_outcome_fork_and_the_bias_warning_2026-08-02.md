# K1114 — The look-beyond tightens: the confusion resolves, the value re-points toward Derived

## (1) Grace resolves the universal-vs-Λ knot (the confusion I flagged)
I flagged in the look-beyond that "observer-relative Λ vs universal Λ" was the concrete confusion to watch. **Grace resolved it with the geometry:** d* is the **bleed-depth from the Shilov boundary** = a property of the universe's **vacuum evolution** = a **shared cosmic state**, not a per-observer coordinate. All observers in the same universe share the same bled vacuum → **Λ is universal because d\* is a cosmic quantity.** So **"observer-relative Λ" was a misnomer** — Λ isn't per-observer; the observer's role (if any) is in *setting the shared d\**, not in making Λ per-observer. Dovetails with Cal's XOR: a fixed, cosmic-shared d* gives universal Λ, w=−1 intact.

## (2) Lyra's re-point — the audit tightened toward Derived
Universality **rules out** the pure "observer-relative" reading Lyra had been leaning on: if d* *must* be a shared cosmic quantity, it is **not a free per-observer parameter** — it's a cosmic state, which is **more likely forced** (by the cosmic dynamics) than free. So the value-tier is open **maybe more toward Derived** than "leaning Identified" said an hour ago — the observer computation could **seal Derived**, not just confirm Identified. This is the day's pattern again: **point the audit at the comfortable answer and it sharpens, not rubber-stamps.**

## (3) Cal §219 — the three-outcome fork (the decisive test, sharpened)
The independent-definition test has **three** ends, not two:
- **(a)** independent definition that **forces** d* → **value Derived** (the flip route).
- **(b)** independent definition that **doesn't** force d* → **value Identified but coherent** (the genuine observer-relative result).
- **(c)** **not** independently definable → "value = observer's depth" is a **tautology, a null result** — the reduction quietly collapses to "one free parameter relabeled the observer's scale."

So the test decides **whether the headline is a finding at all** — and the headline ("forced in everything but the observer's scale") **stays conditional on the test passing.** Cal's coherence gate (= Grace's resolution): Λ universal ⟹ d* is a cosmological constant ⟹ **the "observer" can only be a shared substrate-level structure**, not consensus-me — which *constrains* the independent-definition (define the observer as substrate structure, which is exactly what Grace's cosmic d* supplies).

## Cal's bias warning — on the record, and right
The observer-is-fundamental thesis aligning with Casey's long-held framework (antenna, "time measures us," commitment=measurement) is **not evidence it's right** — it is the **precise place bias runs strongest for all of us.** The alignment with what we've long believed is the reason to check *hardest* before celebrating, not softest. The independent-definition test is that referee check, and it **stays blind.**
