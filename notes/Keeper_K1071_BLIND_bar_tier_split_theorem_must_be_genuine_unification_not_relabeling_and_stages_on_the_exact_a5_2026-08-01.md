# K1071 — Blind bar for the tier-split theorem

Grace offered to pre-draft the **tier-split theorem** — G-Derived / running-Derived / Λ-magnitude-Identified as *one* theorem of the ζ-ladder's scale-dependence (a₀ scale-full, a₁/a₂ scale-free) — staged to register when the exact a₅ lands. It's a genuine bridges contribution and worth banking *if* it earns "theorem." I commit the bar before the draft.

## The two subtle risks, and the four criteria

**Risk A — relabeling masquerading as unification.** "Three tiers we already assigned, restated as one theorem" is not a theorem; it's a narrative. 
- **Criterion 1 — derive, don't describe.** The theorem must **predict each rung's tier from its scale-dependence**: a₀ carries a free scale ⟹ a free parameter ⟹ Identified; a₁/a₂ are scale-free invariants ⟹ forced-candidates ⟹ Derived. The implication must run structure→tier. If it only *describes* the tiers post hoc, it's a relabeling (Rule 9 flavor: locate the object, don't just re-narrate it).

**Risk B — banking on a strongly-argued input.** The "Λ Identified because a₀ is scale-full" leg rests on a₅≠0, which is currently **strongly-argued, not proven** (K1070).
- **Criterion 2 — stage on the exact a₅.** Draft and stage now; **register as a theorem only when the exact a₅ closes a₅≠0** (the closed-form symmetric-space computation, K1070). Exactly Grace's proposed staging — the bar confirms it's required, not optional.

**Two more, to keep it honest:**
- **Criterion 3 — no tier upgrade.** The theorem *explains* why G/running are Derived and Λ isn't; it upgrades nothing. G and running hold at Derived independently (a₁/a₂ are the forced invariants κ_Bergman, R(k), a₁=−1875); Λ stays Identified(-permanent). The unification is explanatory, not promotional.
- **Criterion 4 — scale-free is necessary-not-sufficient at the rung level.** Scale-freeness of a₁/a₂ is *necessary* for their Derived status; it is not what *makes* them Derived — the invariants are additionally computed forward. The theorem must not claim "scale-free ⟹ Derived" as a blanket implication (the K1069 necessary-not-sufficient point, lifted from the a₀ magnitude to the rung tiers).

## Disposition
- Valid tier-split theorem (all four criteria) → a real gain: BST's three cosmology-sector tiers become a **structural consequence of one ladder property**, not three separate rulings. Bank it (staged on the exact a₅).
- Relabeling (fails criterion 1) or premature registration (fails criterion 2) → catch it.
- I rule it against these four when Grace's draft + the exact a₅ land.
