# K1084 — Lyra's tube-type footing: sound, and the reduction has a corpus route

## The footing, audited (SOUND — credit Lyra)
The one forcing step (K1082) is: does the functional determinant det Δ reduce to the rank-2 Jordan norm? Grace flagged the shared-word weld-risk. Lyra supplied the structural footing, and it holds:
- **D_IV⁵ is tube-type.** Type-IV domains (Lie balls, SO₀(n,2)/[SO(n)×SO(2)]) are tube-type / self-dual. ✓
- **The Jordan norm is the domain's defining equation:** the Lie ball is {1 − 2|z|² + |z^Tz|² > 0, |z|²<1} — that quadratic-in-|z|² form **is** the norm form of the rank-2 spin-factor Jordan algebra. ✓
- **The Bergman kernel is a power of that norm:** K(z,w) = c·N(z,w)^{−p} (standard for tube-type bounded symmetric domains). So **N is inside K → inside the Bergman metric → inside Δ → inside det Δ.** ✓

So the two "determinants" are not strangers sharing a name — **the algebraic Jordan norm N is the seed of the analytic tower** (Bergman kernel → Laplacian → functional determinant). Grace's reduction is a **derivation-in-waiting**, not a pun. Her weld-flag instinct was right *and* there's real structure under the word — the good case.

## The bridge — Gindikin Γ_Ω, and it's already in the corpus (research hand-off)
The analytic↔algebraic bridge is the **Gindikin generalized Gamma function** Γ_Ω(s) on the symmetric cone (web-confirmed, refs below):
- For tube-type, the **Jordan determinant** det_J(z) = Δ(z,e) = **Π λ_j** — the degree-rank norm (Grace's algebraic object).
- **Γ_Ω(s) integrates powers of det_J over the cone** — the analytic machinery.
- The **functional determinant** is exp(−ζ'(0)) via ζ-regularization, and it factors through Γ_Ω on a tube-type domain.
- **The corpus ALREADY uses Γ_Ω:** the muon's **24 = Γ(5)** is a Gindikin Gamma value (F157 / K923, the π-parity theorem). So the reduction runs on **machinery already in hand** — Gindikin–Hua / Γ_Ω — not greenfield. Exactly Casey's *D_IV⁵ + linear algebra + corpus*.
