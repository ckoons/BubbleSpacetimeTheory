## Part 2 — The recognition (to verify: Elie, Cal)
**Generalized free field (GFF):** a conformal field whose correlators are all fixed by its two-point function, i.e. no interactions.
- For a GFF scalar φ of dimension Δ_φ in d dimensions, the operator product φ × φ contains exactly the "double-trace" operators [φφ]_{n,l}, with Δ = 2Δ_φ + 2n + l, each once. For identical φ, only even spin l appears.
- **Our fusion rule at Δ_φ = 5/2, d = 5 gives 5 + 2n + l.** This is Elie 5816's 5 + j + 2m with j = l and m = n, each once.
- Lyra's split lines up the same way: the symmetric part is even j and the antisymmetric part is odd j. The identical-boson GFF keeps only the symmetric part. **So the choice between Sym² and Λ² is exactly the statistics input the clock does not supply** (Cal Section 996).

**What this says, calibrated both ways:**
- **Recapitulation:** at the two-body level, BST's boundary (H² with its fusion) has the spectrum of a *free* conformal field. This holds for the whole D_IV^n family (Δ_φ = n/2), so it is structure, not a BST-specific number.
- **The honest consequence:** in conventional holography (AdS/CFT), interactions in the bulk show up on the boundary as **anomalous dimensions** γ_{n,l}, shifts of the double-trace dimensions away from 2Δ_φ + 2n + l. Heemskerk–Penedones–Polchinski–Sully (JHEP 10 (2009) 079, arXiv:0907.0151) show that, at leading order, a CFT with a gap and a planar expansion is generated by *local bulk interactions*, one per solution for γ_{n,l} (bounded spin).
- **So the question is sharp:** does anything in BST shift 5 + 2n + l? Candidates must be enumerated before any is preferred:
  - the ruler (it breaks conformal symmetry and enters as a unit);
  - the commitment/record structure (Casey's act vs record);
  - the descent (Machian);
  - N_max = 137 as a cutoff (the resolution limit);
  - a bulk vertex in D_IV⁵ (the geometry's own curvature).
- **Kill line (written first):** if no BST structure produces a nonzero γ_{n,l}, BST's boundary is a free theory at the level of the geometry, and every interaction is imported. That is the same shape as the mass-gap result (every mass = the ruler × a number) and the energy result (every coupling identified). **It would be the third wall in one day at one location, and that wall would be the program's most honest single statement about processes.**
- **Calibrate the other way:** if one structure *forces* a γ, even its sign, that is the first dynamical content derived at the boundary.
