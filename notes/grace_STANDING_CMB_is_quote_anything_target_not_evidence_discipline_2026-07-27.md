# [TEGMARK / STANDING] "The CMB is like the bible — you can quote it to say anything" (Casey). The CMB-as-target discipline, banked.

*Grace | 2026-07-27 Mon | Casey's framing, banked as a standing guard the moment the dynamical-selection lane opened — because the CMB is that idea's single biggest hostile-read vulnerability, and this is the exact discipline that defuses it. A "quote-anything" dataset is where a strong new idea gets discredited, so the guard goes down before a reviewer reaches it, not after.*

## The principle (Casey)
**The CMB anomaly set is a "quote-anything" dataset.** The large-angle anomalies — low quadrupole, quadrupole-octupole alignment (Axis of Evil), the Cold Spot, hemispherical/dipole asymmetry, the missing large-angle S₁/₂ correlation — are each ~2–3σ, individually consistent with cosmic variance, and *collectively* consistent with a dozen mutually-incompatible pre-inflationary stories (bubble collisions, topology, anisotropic inflation, cosmic textures, foregrounds, statistics-of-a-single-sky...). A dataset that fits a dozen stories confirms none of them. You can read almost any pre-inflationary hypothesis into it — like scripture, it can be quoted to support anything.

## The rule (standing, for the dynamical-selection lane + the hook/reviewer paper)
1. **CMB anomalies are a TARGET, never evidence FOR the bubble-competition / dynamical-selection picture.** "Consistent with a failed competition" is not "evidence for" — it is the null-content shared by every rival story.
2. **The only admissible use is a SPECIFIC, DISTINCTIVE, PRE-REGISTERED signal** the competition predicts and the alternatives do NOT — e.g. a bubble-collision *circular temperature scar* with a predicted profile/size, tested against the Feeney–Johnson–Peiris (and successor) searches. The prediction is committed *before* looking, and its shape is what makes it un-fakeable.
3. **A post-hoc match to an existing anomaly is worth ~nothing** for a quote-anything dataset — it is the CMB analog of the coincidence-trap (many stories, one number). Do not cite "our idea is consistent with the Axis of Evil" as support.
4. **Sigma discipline compounds the point:** at ~2–3σ with look-elsewhere across ≥5 anomalies, the effective significance is weak even before the model-flexibility. Score σ, and account for the multiplicity.

## Why this is load-bearing (the hostile-read seam)
The dynamical-selection reframing (stability = observability, geometries competed → D_IV⁵) is the day's strongest new idea AND its most over-claimable near the CMB. A hostile reviewer compresses a whole program into its weakest sentence — and "the CMB anomalies support our bubble-competition" is exactly that sentence if it's ever written as evidence. Holding CMB-as-target keeps the reframing at its honest tier (a hypothesis with a falsifiable target), which is stronger than an unfalsifiable "consistent-with" gloss. Same discipline that retired the 3/13 Weinberg coincidence and scoped confinement to (A)-not-(B).
