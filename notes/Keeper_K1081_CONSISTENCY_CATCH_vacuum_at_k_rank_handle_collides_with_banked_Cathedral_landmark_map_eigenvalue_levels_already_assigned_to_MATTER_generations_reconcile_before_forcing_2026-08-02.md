# K1081 — "Vacuum at k=rank" collides with the banked landmark map

At peak convergence (K1080) the promising handle was "the vacuum sits at k=rank = the top primitive-idempotent level of the rank-2 spin factor, so λ₂ reads off rank." Grepping the corpus before declaring (the standing discipline) catches a collision the derivation threads missed.

## The banked assignment (Cathedral Reference, T1929 + Grace W-24)
The corpus landmark map already assigns the Bergman/Wallach eigenvalue levels — **to matter generations, not the vacuum:**

| Landmark | Level | Banked observable |
|---|---|---|
| **L2** Wallach **k=rank** (Bergman saturation) | λ₂ | **3rd-gen matter** (τ, ν_τ, t, b) |
| **L5** Bergman gap (λ₁ = C₂) | λ₁ | **2nd-gen matter** (μ, ν_μ, c, s) |
| **L12** Wallach **k=0** (trivial mode) | λ₀=0 | **1st-gen matter + Higgs VACUUM** |
| **L7** Cartan subspace (rank-2 torus) | — | **QCD β coefficients, confinement Λ** (the running) |

## The collision
The cosmology thread's map (K1079/K1080) — a_k↔λ_{rank−k}: **Λ↔λ₂, G↔λ₁, running↔λ₀** — does **not** correspond to the banked residence map:
- **k=rank (λ₂) is OCCUPIED by 3rd-gen matter (L2)** — not the vacuum. "Vacuum at the top idempotent level" is wrong as a *residence* claim.
- **The (Higgs) vacuum's banked home is k=0 / L12** (the trivial mode) — which is *exactly* where Lyra argued the vacuum can't give suppression (λ₀=0). So the banked map and Lyra's argument point to the SAME place (k=0) for the vacuum, while the cosmology handle displaces it to k=rank.
- **The running couplings anchor to L7 (Cartan)**, not to "k=0" — so the cosmology "running↔λ₀" leg also doesn't match the residence map.

## What this means (sharpens, does not kill)
The cosmology thread conflated **two different towers:**
1. The **heat-kernel-coefficient suppression tower** (a₀/a₁/a₂ → which eigenvalue sets each coefficient's suppression exponent).
2. The **matter-eigenvalue residence tower** (which generation/observable lives at each λ_k) — the banked Cathedral map.

"Vacuum at k=rank" is at most a statement in tower (1) — the a₀ vacuum coefficient's *suppression exponent* is 4λ₂ — **NOT** the vacuum *residing* at the top level (tower (2), which is 3rd-gen matter). So:
- **The "reads λ₂ off rank" cleanliness is undercut** — k=rank is already spoken for by matter, so borrowing λ₂ for the vacuum's exponent **reopens** the fishing question K1080 thought it closed: *why does the vacuum's suppression borrow the λ₂ (3rd-gen-matter) level* rather than λ₁ or λ₃?
- The **a_k↔λ_{rank−k} reflection must be shown to be a genuine second structure** (a heat-kernel↔spectrum duality), *distinct* from the matter-residence map — not the matter map re-read, and not a numerical coincidence (k=2=rank).

## Disposition
- **The compression's ONE question stands** (is the vacuum placement forced?) — this catch doesn't refute it.
- **But the handle is weaker and more fish-prone than K1080 presented.** Before "vacuum at k=rank" can be forced, the derivation must: (a) **reconcile the two towers** (suppression a_k vs residence λ_k) — is the vacuum's exponent-level distinct from the matter-residence-level, and why; (b) show the **a_k↔λ_{rank−k} reflection is a real duality**, not the matter map re-read; (c) explain why the vacuum's suppression borrows λ₂ specifically, given λ₂ is 3rd-gen matter's home.
- **Both Λ and Ω stay Partially Derived.** Severity MODERATE — a sharpening/reconciliation, not a refutation.
- Handed to Grace/Lyra: the two-tower reconciliation is now part of the placement derivation.
