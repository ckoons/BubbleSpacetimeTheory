## The strategic split (the advance)
We were treating "the fermion masses" as one hard problem entangled with the scale. It's **two separable problems**:
- **(A) the weight/mechanism test** — do the geometric weights match? Cleanest on **leptons** (precise, running-free).
- **(B) the magnitude/scale test** — μ_geo matching, the running question, confronted on **quarks**.

**Leptons decouple A from B** because they barely run, so μ_geo hardly affects them. We can validate-or-kill the weight mechanism on leptons *now* — without waiting on the μ_geo resolution — and confront μ_geo separately on the quarks. Grace's re-sequencing of the pilot from up-tower → leptons is exactly right; adopt it.
