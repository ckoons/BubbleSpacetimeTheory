# Round 151 — L1: when a bin's kinematic moment flips sign, in the operator picture; and the sentence "under observed-redshift binning the boost is read off the edges of the clock windows." One paragraph, hashed; a note on a note.
**Lyra, Monday 2026-09-14, 13:0x EDT.** Inputs: my R149 L2 (f_i − 2 = Σ_windows edge density × edge speed); von Hausegger–Dalang Eq. (13); K1906 §4 (the numbers: f ≈ 8.5, 6.1, 4.9, 2.7, −3.2 across Elie's five bins; weights N_i f_i²; B₁ ≈ 4.9 > 2 + x(1+α) ≈ 3.6 in bin 1).

Write the sharp-bin coefficient as the Rac threshold term plus the clock window's two edge terms: f_i = [2 + x̃_i(1+α̃_i)] + (1+z₂)f_b(z₂) − (1+z₁)f_b(z₁), each edge term being (normalised density at the edge) × (edge speed 1+z_e under the boost), the upper edge entering with a plus and the lower edge with a minus — *[direction of the parenthetical corrected 2026-09-15 on Keeper's 12:59 catch: toward the apex the boost LOWERS observed redshift, 1+z′ = (1+z)(1 − β cos θ) (vHD Eq. 2), so sources enter the window from above because z′ falls and leave it from below for the same reason; the signs in the formula (upper +, lower −, vHD Eq. 13) were and are unaffected; original hash 98aef46c, amended file re-hashed below]*. **The kinematic moment of the counts flips sign exactly when the clock window's lower-edge term exceeds its upper-edge term by more than the Rac threshold term:** (1+z₁)f_b(z₁) − (1+z₂)f_b(z₂) > 2 + x̃_i(1+α̃_i) — Keeper's one-line criterion with the edge speeds kept exact (for a narrow bin, z₁ ≈ z₂ ≈ z_e, it reads f_b(z₁) − f_b(z₂) > (2 + x(1+α))/(1+z_e)). It is a statement about the *clock factor alone* — where the bin sits on n(z) — with the Rac factor entering only as the threshold it must overcome, which is why the top bin, sitting on the falling tail of n(z) with a dense lower edge and an empty upper one, is anti-kinematic (B₅ ≈ −7 against ≈ 3.6), while bin 1, on the rising front, has its upper edge dense and adds B₁ ≈ +4.9 *to* the Rac terms. **Which bins carry the boost follows from the same form:** a bin's lever arm is |f_i| and its weight in the joint fit N_i f_i², so the boost is carried where the window sits on a *steep* part of n(z) — the low-redshift rising front — and is nearly invisible where n(z) is flat across the window (f_i ≈ 2 + x(1+α) only, the pure Ellis–Baldwin value, small at Quaia's x ≈ 0.8) or where the two edges cancel (the null bin, f_i ≈ 0). **So under observed-redshift binning the boost is read mostly off the edges of the clock windows, not off the Rac threshold** — in bin 1 the edge term is larger than the two Ellis–Baldwin terms combined — and that is the operator reason for K1900 §4's rule and for the joint fit: the redshift channel windowed on flux has no moving clock edge, so it reads the boost without an edge term at all; the count channel in observed-z bins reads it *through* the edges, with a sign that the window's position on n(z) sets. Two cautions on the note's face: the criterion is the sharp-cut form, and with photometric redshifts the edge terms become the smooth-window integral of Eq. (21), which softens but does not remove the flip (K1905 §4's sensitivity test is its measurement); and an edge term is only as good as the n(z) it is read from — a bin whose edge density is uncertain has a kinematic coefficient of uncertain sign, which is a Landing-C trigger by v1.1 §4.4, not a fire.
