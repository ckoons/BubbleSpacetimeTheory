# LEAD — Which axioms of QM does D_IV⁵ derive?

Built for two reasons: to give **Frontier A** its target map, and to answer Casey's *derived-theory-relationships* question at the axiom level. This is a **scorecard, not a ruling** — Cal should hostile-read the tier assignments.

## The ledger (Dirac–von Neumann + the add-ons)

| # | QM postulate | BST status | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | State space = Hilbert space / superposition | **Derived-structural** | The substrate *is* the Bergman space H²(D_IV⁵); superposition = linearity of H² |
| 2 | Observables = self-adjoint operators | **Structure-derived** | The operator zoo (T2419/T2422/T2421, H_B = Casimir) are the domain's forced invariant operators |
| 3 | Born rule (probabilities) | **Derived (banked)** | Born = Bergman, T754 forced measure + Gleason |
| 4 | Unitary evolution / Schrödinger | **Structure-derived** | Native heat-eq ↔ wave-eq by Wick rotation; Schrödinger = Wick rotation of exp(−τH_B/ℏ) |
| 5 | Arrow of time | **Derived (banked)** | Positivity of spec(H_B) |
| 6 | 3+1 signature | **Derived (banked)** | SO(5,2)→SO(4,2)→SO(3,1) |
| 7 | Composite = tensor product | **Reachable** | Two-particle Bergman kernel (the object spin-statistics needs) |
| 8 | Spin-statistics / Pauli | **Frontier A (pending)** | Spin from K-type (n_C=5 odd → half-integer); statistics from exchange-monodromy — bar in K1134 |
| 9 | Uncertainty / canonical commutation | **Frontier A (pending, likely cleanest)** | Operators exist; if [x,p]=iℏ_BST forced → Robertson theorem — bar in K1134 |
| 10 | Measurement / collapse | **Genuinely open (hard)** | SWPP commitment-cycle framing exists, but a clean projection-postulate derivation *is* the measurement problem — do **not** claim it |

## Two caveats that keep this honest
- **Wood/marble:** unitary evolution and tensor products are partly universal Hilbert-space structure (wood). The D_IV⁵-**marble** is that the *specific* space, operators, and measure are all forced by **one domain** — never "we invented unitarity."
- **Smuggle (K1134):** audit each for circularity — (4) must not assume unitarity to get the semigroup; (2) must not assume self-adjointness rather than derive it from the domain.

## Why this matters for Frontier A
It reframes A from "derive a nice postulate" to **"supply the two missing pieces of an almost-complete axiomatic derivation of quantum mechanics from one geometry."** If A lands spin-statistics + uncertainty, the ledger reads: **8 of 10 QM axioms Derived/Structure-Derived from D_IV⁵, 1 reachable (composite), 1 hard-open (measurement).** Paired with Born = Bergman, that is the strongest honest hook-package framing we have.
