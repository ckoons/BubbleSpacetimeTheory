# GATE-DIRAC-H — the #108 cross-route, caged before it computes

Row H — *the gauge fields are inner fluctuations `D → D + A`* — is the live route to promote #108 from floor (the isometry route is dead: the spin-2 gluons aren't in `so(5)`, K1679). It's also exactly where I'd over-promote again, so I pin its acceptance criterion **blind**, before Lyra computes, the way GATE-108-PROMOTE was pinned.

## The trap I can see coming
In Connes' NCG the gauge group comes from the **finite algebra** whose fluctuations you take — and in the Standard Model construction that algebra `ℂ ⊕ ℍ ⊕ M₃(ℂ)` is **put in by hand.** So "the inner fluctuations of D give su(3)" is **true for free** the moment you feed it a finite algebra with an `M₃(ℂ)` factor. That is the *same cheat* as #108's "closure imposed by the 3×3 realization" (K1677 hunt-if-P) — **relocated** from "the 3×3 matrix action" to "the finite algebra." The cheat migrates to the last prose step.

## The blind acceptance criterion (to promote #108)
1. **The algebra must be forced.** The finite/noncommutative `A` whose fluctuations `Σ aᵢ[D, bᵢ]` give the gauge fields must itself be **forced by D_IV⁵** (from the FK / Peirce / Jordan structure). **Exhibit where the `M₃(ℂ)` factor comes from in the geometry** — or there is no promotion.
2. **su(3) must fall out, not be inserted.** `[D, A_forced]` must produce su(3) — including the **5 spin-2 quadrupole generators** the isometry lacked (K1679) — without those being carried in by the choice of `A`.
3. **Exactly su(3), derived closure.** Closes to su(3) (not u(3), not the full C*-algebra, not smaller), spin-2 gluons present, and the closure **derived** — not the realization-imposed closure of K1677.

## Ruling logic
- `A` forced **and** su(3) falls out → **#108 PROMOTES** — the real container→mechanism crossing.
- `A` must be **chosen** to contain `M₃(ℂ)` → the cheat migrated; **#108 stays FLOOR**, stated plainly (su(3) imposed by the algebra choice, as it was by the 3×3 realization).
- `A_forced` **commutative** (functions on the domain) → abelian fluctuations → no su(3) → **FLOOR**.

The burden is on the promote: the standard NCG construction *puts in* the algebra, so "fluctuations give su(3)" is **not evidence** unless the algebra is forced. Row H does not cross the floor on elegance — same guard that held #108.

## Bar-1 refinement (the dependency graph, sharper)
Two distinctions the first cut blurred:
- **Credential vs prediction.** Rows **A** (Kostant's `D²`-identity is a *theorem*, not a prediction), **G** (`ker D = H²` is a standard fact of Hermitian symmetric domains), **D** (the ρ-shift is a spectral property) establish the *credential* "D is the right operator" — that's **one credential, not three predictions.** The rows that could have come out wrong are **E** (muon mass), **F** (index `= 3`), **C/H** (does the gauge sector emerge with `N_c` fixed), **I** (Connes distance `=` causal order).
- **Graded vs ungraded heat trace.** **B, C** are the *ungraded* trace `Tr e^{−tD²}` — one functional, two coefficients = **one validation.** **F** (the index) is the *graded* trace `Str e^{−tD²} = Tr γ e^{−tD²}` — a **distinct** functional of the same D.
