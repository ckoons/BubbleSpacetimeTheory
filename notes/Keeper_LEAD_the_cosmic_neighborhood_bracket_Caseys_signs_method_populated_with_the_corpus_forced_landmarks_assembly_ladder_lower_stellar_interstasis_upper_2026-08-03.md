# The cosmic-neighborhood bracket

**Casey's method (chat, 2026-08-03):** *"We are somewhere; there is no 'there' to measure against. Say what our neighborhood looks like — a cow → not Times Square, maybe near a Chick-fil-A. Collect lots of signs and ask why/when would I see this. Use time, not temp."* Applied to the cc position: bracket τ_now (where we sit on the derived bleed curve) with a **set of forced signs**, read as qualitative yes/no facts — never by measuring the dark-energy value (circular) or a local temperature (untrustworthy).

## The corpus already has the landmarks

### Lower bounds — the assembly ladder (Complex Assemblies, Section 9) — "the table of whens"
A BST-forced complexity timetable, each rung a qualitative observable with a forced minimum epoch:
| Sign (qualitative) | Forced minimum | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| stars + carbon exist | ~0.5 Gyr | gravitational collapse + Hoyle resonance |
| **life exists** | ~0.8 Gyr | percolation phase transition at d_c=C₂=6 |
| **multicellular life exists** | +~2 Gyr (the long pole) | cooperation filter #2 |
| **intelligence exists** | ~4.9 Gyr | N_c management + depth-1 reasoning |
We **observe intelligence and multicellular life** → the universe is at least old enough for the whole ladder. Forced, qualitative, and non-circular (no reference to Λ). *These are the "we're past the cow pasture" signs.*

### Upper bound — the stellar mix + interstasis (Casey's stellar instinct + BST_Interstasis)
The far-future end is **interstasis/exhaustion** (BST_Interstasis): the commitment/UNC gradient → 0, star formation ceases, all stars redden and die. We observe **ongoing star formation (blue stars still forming), well past the z~2 peak but not ceased** → we are demonstrably *before* exhaustion. A qualitative sign, non-circular. *This is the hard half — the "cow that isn't there yet" — and Casey found it in the stellar populations.*

## The synthesis
Lower signs (assembly ladder) + upper sign (stellar mix / pre-exhaustion) **bracket the cosmic age**. The cosmic age brackets **τ_now** (our position on the bleed curve). The **derived bleed curve** (K-Casimir shape) then maps that bracket to a **bracket on w** — a falsifiable prediction ("given these landmarks, w lies in [a,b]"), built entirely from complexity and stellar signs, never from the dark-energy value.

## Guardrails
1. **Every sign qualitative** — a presence or an absence, never a disguised measurement of Λ (the circular temp move in costume). "Intelligence exists," "blue stars exist" are clean; "ρ_Λ^{1/4} = 2.3 meV" is not.
2. **Bracket endpoints mix forced + astrophysical.** The ladder durations are BST-forced; the star-formation-cessation epoch is astrophysical. State which is which — the bracket is only as forced as its signs.
3. **No anthropics.** The signs are *what we observe* (we see blue stars, we see intelligence), NOT "observers must exist here." Casey's method avoids the anthropic trap precisely by using signs, not necessity.
4. **Honest about tightness.** The lower bound (intelligence → ≳5 Gyr) and the upper (pre-exhaustion → ≪ trillions of Gyr) may give a LOOSE bracket → a loose w-bound. That's still falsifiable content. Tightening needs sharper signs.
