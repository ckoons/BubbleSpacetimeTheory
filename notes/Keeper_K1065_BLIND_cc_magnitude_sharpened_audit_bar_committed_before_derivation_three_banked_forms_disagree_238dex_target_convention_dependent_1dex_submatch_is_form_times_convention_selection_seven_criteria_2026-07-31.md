# K1065 — The sharpened cc-magnitude blind bar (committed before the derivation)

The ε(a) pass (K1064) was clean because five specific criteria were committed **blind, before Elie derived**, so the audit could not be retrofitted to the result. The cc-magnitude (Λ ~ 10⁻¹²²) is the next forward piece, K1063 Guard 2, and it is **the single highest-fit-temptation number in the program** — the biggest number in physics, with a fistful of clean BST forms already pointing near it. So I commit the sharpened bar now, before Lyra/Elie attempt a forced-exponent derivation.

## The concrete spine — the banked forms disagree with each other (verified this session)

| Form | BST reading | Value (Planck units) |
|---|---|---|
| exp(−281) | 281 = rank·N_max + g = 2·137+7 | 10⁻¹²²·⁰ |
| 7·exp(−282) [T1485] | g·exp(−C₂(g²−rank)); 282 = 6·47 | 10⁻¹²¹·⁶ |
| α⁵⁶ | 56 = 8·g | 10⁻¹¹⁹·⁷ |

**Mutual spread = 2.38 dex.** These three are NOT the same number — they disagree by ~240×. And the **observational target is itself convention-dependent by ~1 dex**: Λ (length⁻²) ~ 10⁻¹²², ρ_Λ ~ 10⁻¹²³, E_vac/M_Pl ~ 10⁻³⁰·⁷ (→ ρ ~ 10⁻¹²²·⁸). 

Consequence, stated plainly: **at most one banked form can be the derivation, and "matching 10⁻¹²²" to sub-dex precision requires selecting BOTH a form AND a convention.** That double-selection — form-shopping × convention-shopping — is the deepest version of the fishing flag (Rule 11 + "contested target: blind-pin every choice"). The form-multiplicity is the WARNING, not the evidence.

## The seven committed criteria (a forced-exponent derivation must clear all)

1. **Target-blindness of the exponent.** Would the exponent (281 / 282 / 56 / whatever) come out of the mechanism *without knowing* the answer is ~122 orders? A forced derivation produces the exponent from geometry/heat-kernel structure first, and *then* we observe it lands at ~122. A fit selects the integer-combination that hits 122. Test each candidate: produced-by-computation, or selected-because-it-equals-~122·ln10?

2. **Mechanism, not arithmetic identity.** The exponent must come from a *mechanism* — a regularized heat-trace / ζ-function / instanton-action computation of the a₀ vacuum-energy coefficient's suppression — not from an algebraic identity among the five integers that happens to equal the right number. "281 = rank·N_max+g" is an arithmetic identity; a derivation is "the suppression is exp(−S) where S is the on-shell/spectral quantity that *computes to* 281." **Provenance of the exponent, never its value.**

3. **Uniqueness / no form-shopping.** Pick ONE form by mechanism and either (a) show the other forms are the *same* mechanism in different notation (genuine over-determination — one mechanism, many faces, like BST's good over-determinations), or (b) admit that if unrelated forms all "work," none is forced. The 2.38-dex mutual disagreement makes (a) hard: forms that differ by 240× are not trivially "the same." A real over-determination would have the forms *agree*, not span 2+ dex.

4. **Predict the precise value, honestly.** 10⁻¹²² is a range. A forced derivation nails a *specific* number to a *stated* precision, **scored in dex against the observational uncertainty** (which is itself convention-carried). No "0.076 dex" precision claim unless the form AND the convention were pinned first and the obs-uncertainty is honestly carried. Order-of-magnitude dressed as sub-dex is the tell.

5. **Coherence with the a₀ ladder + w=−1.** The cc-magnitude IS the *size* (normalization) of the a₀ heat-trace coefficient — the regularized bulk-volume vacuum term whose EOS is already w=−1 (K1040). The magnitude derivation must be that coefficient's normalization, on the same structure the a₀→Λ / a₁→G / a₂→β ladder already uses. A magnitude mechanism that needs a *different* structure than the ladder's a₀ is incoherent.

6. **The coupling-small mechanism must be FORCED, not circular.** K1040's lead — "Λ-small = substrate-coupling-small" — must be a *derived* smallness (the coupling's magnitude computed from the geometry), NOT the circular restatement "Λ is small because the coupling is small." What *sets* the coupling's exponential smallness? If the answer is itself one of the banked exponents, it re-enters criteria (1)-(3).

7. **Pin the observable convention BEFORE citing any exponent.** Commit blind: is the target Λ (10⁻¹²²), ρ_Λ (10⁻¹²³), or E_vac/M_Pl (10⁻³⁰·⁷)? Each banked form must be matched to a *single pinned* convention, committed before the number. Form F matching convention X while form G matches convention Y is the deepest fishing (convention-shopping × form-shopping). Pin the convention to a primary source once, then hold it.

## Disposition
- The cc-magnitude stays **IDENTIFIED** (K1063 Guard 2) until a mechanism clears (1)-(7).
- **PASS** = a forced exponent, target-innocent, from a single mechanism, on the a₀ ladder's structure, matched to a pinned convention, scored honestly in dex → then BST has a genuine handle on the cosmological-constant problem (a real prize).
- **FAIL / stays-Identified** = any of: selecting the form that hits 122; citing "multiple forms all work" as *support* (it's the warning); a sub-dex precision claim without pinned form+convention; a coupling-smallness that restates rather than derives.
- The README Λ-magnitude row is being softened to Identified to match this bar (the "0.076 dex" presentation was one form × one convention — over-precise by criterion 4/7).

The bet, as with ε(a): commit the bar before the work, then the pass (if it comes) is trustworthy and the fit (if that's what it is) is caught by a criterion committed before anyone knew the answer. This is the number that most needs it.
