# K1068 — Blind bar for the ε(a) substrate-coupling correction (committed before the derivation)

The cc-magnitude bar (K1065) armed before the attempt made K1067's null clean and defensible. The ε(a) substrate-coupling correction is the second open forward cosmology piece, and it is where Guard 1 (K1063) binds hardest — so I commit its specific bar now, before Elie/Lyra derive it.

## The sharp point — a nonzero ε(a) is a NEW claim, not a refinement of ε=0

The leading result is **ε(a) = 0 exactly** (K1064), and it is exact *because the bulk volume is exactly C·π⁵* (K1040) — a fixed geometric invariant gives a constant density gives w = −1 with no deviation. Therefore:

> A nonzero ε(a) is **not** a small correction to ε=0. It is a **different claim** — that the volume is weakly *time-dependent* — and that time-dependence needs its own forced mechanism.

This is the trap, and it is subtle: the pull toward a nonzero ε(a) is **data-motivated**, not geometry-motivated. DESI hints at w₀ > −1 (dynamical); a dynamical ε(a) would (a) match that hint and (b) relax the Σm_ν bound (0.064 → 0.16 eV) — the exact double-win of the **K1033 breathing-mode lead**, which was already **refused** (K1064) and **retired** from the data layer (Grace). So the very act of "deriving ε(a)" risks re-manufacturing the refused form because we *want* the dynamical result. Guard 1 exists for precisely this.

## The five committed criteria (a nonzero ε(a) must clear all)

1. **The volume's time-dependence is geometry-forced, not assumed.** ε≠0 requires the bulk volume to depart from the fixed C·π⁵. That departure must come from a *derived* substrate-coupling mechanism. **If the volume is exactly fixed, ε=0 is the final answer and there is no correction to derive** — a legitimate, committed outcome. Do not introduce a time-dependence to *permit* a nonzero ε.

2. **Provenance = the coupling; DESI held out.** ε(a)'s form is derived from the coupling mechanism, with the observed w₀/wₐ never fed in and no CPL shape selected (same discipline that made the leading ε=0 clean, K1064).

3. **Sign AND magnitude forced.** The fixed-volume expectation (K1063) is ε small, decaying, ≲10⁻⁴ — far below DESI sensitivity. **A ε≈+0.05 that lands on DESI's w₀≈−0.8 hint is the maximal-suspicion case** — the −0.949 relapse in new clothes. Is the sign forced by the mechanism, or chosen to point at the data?

4. **The (1−f) / breathing form stays REFUSED.** ρ_DE ∝ (1−f(a)) with the fill fraction increasing — the form that gives w>−1 in DESI's direction — is not admissible unless a *forced* f(a)-evolution mechanism is derived first, justified geometrically before any comparison (K1064). No re-introduction under a new name.

5. **Blind-pin every rational; score σ after.** Any coefficient or power in ε(a) committed before the datum; comparison to DESI in σ (per sample), only after the form is fixed.

## Calibrate both ways
- **Don't dismiss a forced result:** if a geometry-forced mechanism *does* give a tiny decaying ε(a) (≲10⁻⁴), that is a **real prediction, sharper than ΛCDM's bare w=−1** — BST would predict w=−1 *plus a specific, computable, tiny correction*. A genuine dynamical-DE-vs-Λ discriminant; bank it at its honest tier.
- **Don't manufacture one:** the burden is on the geometry. Absent a forced volume-time-dependence, **ε=0 stands** and the honest report is "no correction — w=−1 exactly, and here is why the tempting dynamical form is refused."

## Disposition
- ε=0 (K1064) is the committed leading result and the default answer.
- A nonzero ε(a) is Identified-at-best until criteria (1)-(5) clear; the geometry-forced volume-time-dependence (criterion 1) is the gate.
- I audit the instant Elie/Lyra's attempt lands: is the volume-time-dependence forced by the mechanism, or introduced to allow a DESI-matching signal?
