# K1124 — the cc equation of state defaults to Identified, and the escape needs Cal's guard to be real

## (1) Default: IDENTIFIED — two independent arguments, both against the flattering horn
- **Elie (structural delicacy):** wₐ<0 ⟺ −H'/κ > Var(λ)/⟨λ⟩, and **both sides vanish at late times** (−H'/H → 0 toward de Sitter; spectral Var/⟨λ⟩ → 0 as one slow mode dominates). A 0/0-type competition → the sign is delicate and epoch-sensitive → **flips as τ_now moves → age-set → Identified.**
- **Lyra (κ non-forcing):** the only thing that could force the crossing is the tick-rate **κ, which rests on the C₂²=36 exponent — and F308 already ruled that structural, not derivation-grade.** A κ-set crossing inherits that non-forcing → **forced-except-the-age → Identified.**

Two independent roads to the same tier. So the eos **matches the value**: the whole cc sector is coherently **Identified = forced-except-the-cosmic-age** — the n=2 case, one input (the age).

## (2) Adjudication: Lyra's escape and Cal's guard are ONE test, not a conflict
They read like they might conflict (Lyra leans on F218; Cal warns against F218). They don't — they're the two halves of a single non-circular test:
- **Lyra's escape:** the DE tilt crosses −1 at the **same epoch the vacuum falls past the neutrino commitment floor** — F218's ρ_Λ^{1/4} = m₂, a condition on **two forced scales, κ-independent.** If so, the sign is pinned by physics, not our age → **Derived**, and a genuine prediction tying DE + Σm_ν + interstasis into one forced scale (the F144 gap-state).
- **Cal's guard:** **κ must come from T2405 (the Koons tick), not from F218's "onset ≈ now."** Using "now" to fix κ tunes the crossing to the observed age — the reverse-reading trap, one level up from reading d\* off the horizon.

**The resolution:** the wₐ-crossing epoch *does* depend on κ (Elie's criterion). So the escape is only **non-circular if κ is target-innocent (Cal)**. The test is therefore: derive **κ from T2405**, compute the wₐ-crossing epoch blind, compute the **m₂-floor epoch (F218) independently**, and check whether they **coincide κ-independently by forced physics** → Derived; else → Identified. A target-aware (F218-tuned) κ cannot win the verdict — Cal's guard is exactly what makes Lyra's escape a real prediction rather than a coincidence dressed as one.

## (3) The corpus-audit connection: κ is structural-tier
**κ (the tick-rate normalization) is itself a Proxy-Register item** — it rests on the C₂²=36 exponent, which is **structural-tier (F308), not forced-geometry.** That is *why* the eos defaults Identified. Lane A and the accurate-corpus audit are the same finding from two directions: the eos is Identified **because its forcing-piece κ is structural-tier.**
