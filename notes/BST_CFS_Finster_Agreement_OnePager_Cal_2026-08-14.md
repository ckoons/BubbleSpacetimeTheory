# BST and Finster's Causal Fermion Systems: the agreement, and the divergence

Bubble Spacetime Theory (BST) builds physics on one geometry, the rank-2 bounded symmetric domain **D_IV⁵ = SO₀(5,2)/[SO(5)×SO(2)]**. It carries a self-adjoint Dirac operator and a real spectral triple, so it is natural to ask: *is BST a causal fermion system?* We tested it, blind, against Finster's constructional conditions. The honest answer: **BST satisfies the constructional conditions and diverges — structurally — on the causal-structure conditions.** The divergence is informative, not a defect.

## Why the divergence is structural, not a defect

Both failed conditions reduce to one fact. The fundamental symmetry **J** that Finster's causal structure requires is **internal** — a spectral, on-fiber Krein structure carrying the causal classification of A_xy. **BST's indefiniteness is geometric**: it *moves points*; it lives in the **order in which commitments happen**, not on the fiber. A short dichotomy shows **no single J can be both geometric and internal** — so BST cannot reproduce Finster's spectral A_xy, and the 0.465 two-point-Krein-symmetry gap is structural, not a construction error. A verified factorization localizes this precisely: A_xy = ‖K(x,y)‖² · (γ·u)(γ·v), where the Bergman factor ‖K‖² is a positive, signature-blind prefactor, so the entire causal structure lives in the spinor factor — and that is exactly where the geometric/internal dichotomy bites.

## The verdict

**BST realizes causality via the commitment order — a causal set — not via Finster's spectral A_xy.** It is a **different, simpler, self-standing route**: where Finster's causality needs an internal spinor–Krein structure *plus* the A_xy spectral construction *plus* a minimization, **BST's causality is the order, full stop** — ordered pairs timelike, incomparable pairs spacelike, sequential growth the arrow of time, with the partial order *forced* by D_IV⁵'s commitment operator (the ingredient causal-set theory usually must posit).

This is **not** a claim that Finster is wrong. The two are distinct, both-legitimate routes to a causal structure on a fermionic geometry, and **D_IV⁵ carries Finster's full constructional apparatus** (finiteness, idempotent projector, self-adjoint Dirac, occupied sea) even where its own causality takes the order-based route. The one honest open problem on the BST side — the continuum limit of the commitment causal set to (3,1) Minkowski — is causal-set theory's genuine hard part; BST arrives at it with the skeleton pinned (the (3,1) signature Structure-Derived, the order forced by the geometry).
