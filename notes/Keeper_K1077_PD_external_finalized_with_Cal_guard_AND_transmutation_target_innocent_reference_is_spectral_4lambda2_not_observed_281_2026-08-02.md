**So the target-blind test becomes:** *does ∫dg/β reproduce the spectral exponent 4λ₂ (= 56 in α-units)?* — **not** *does it hit 281.* Compute the integral blind, compare to the **spectral 4λ₂**, never to the observed value. Aiming at 281 forfeits all of it.
