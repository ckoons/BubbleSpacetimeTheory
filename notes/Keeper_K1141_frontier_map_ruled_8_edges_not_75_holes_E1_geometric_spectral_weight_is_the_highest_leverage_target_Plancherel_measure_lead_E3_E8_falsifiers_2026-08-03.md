# K1141 — the frontier map: 8 edges, not 75 holes; E1 is the target

The clarity pass delivered a **strategic map**, not just a mood-fix. This rules it and sets the concentration.

## Grace's map (ruled correct, strategically decisive)
Under Rule 20, an unproved edge is the one asserted step on an otherwise core-welded result — and **constants share edges**, so the **75 load-bearing non-Derived constants collapse to 8 distinct unproved edges.**
- **Confident spine:** ~79 forced results (60 Derived + 19 mass-scales Derived-given-the-tick).
- **Frontier:** 8 distinct edges gating ~38 constants.
- **Peripheral tail:** 44 downstream numerical matches (honest-S, mechanism-TBD) — not the story.

## The dominant edge — E1, and the strategic ruling
**E1 (the per-mode / per-bond strength) gates ~13 results and is the same asserted step across four sectors:** κ_ls (nuclear magic), ε (binding ladder), n_s tilt magnitude, seesaw strength, top Yukawa. It is the **Rule-19 PD-signature (forced count × asserted strength) seen system-wide.**

**Ruling: E1 is the single highest-leverage open problem in BST.** One first-principles derivation of the geometric spectral weight — the thing that replaces "equipartition" with a *forced measure* — promotes a whole class (~13 results) at once. Elie declining to self-certify κ=1/(2C₂) was not a dead end; it **localized this target.** Concentrate the team on E1.

## Research lead (corpus-connected; hypothesis, not proof)
The geometric spectral weight is very likely the **forced Plancherel / Bergman-invariant measure** on the D_IV⁵ spectrum — **not uniform equipartition.** This is the *same forced-measure principle that gave the Born rule* (T754: the automorphism-invariant Bergman measure is forced; Lebesgue is not). Equipartition assumes uniform per-mode weight 1/N; the geometry's forced answer is each mode weighted by its **Plancherel density.**
- **Test:** does the Plancherel/Bergman weight of the relevant mode reproduce the physical strength in each sector — κ_ls≈0.083 (CP²-tensor mode), the n_s tilt, the seesaw suppression, ε, the top Yukawa?
- **Verdict rule:** forced (the invariant measure gives the strengths) → E1 proved forward, the class promotes Structure-Derived→Derived; needs a weight *other* than the invariant measure → E1 stays the asserted edge, class stays PD. Certify **blind** (team contaminated on the targets) or via a forcing-chain audit of the measure derivation.

## The two falsifiers — force them (where BST can be killed)
- **E3 — CKM CP-phase:** γ_CKM=arctan(√5) is currently a reverse-fit. Pre-registered bar: derive **arg(ε) including the quadrant**, forward.
- **E8 — dark-energy w(a) sign:** live tension with DESI (below).

## w(a) result (Lyra) — reads up under Rule 20
**w=−1 is the zero-mode** (trivial K-Casimir component, eigenvalue 0 → doesn't bleed → sits as a constant). Excited modes bleed away, so w relaxes toward −1; the **decay rate is a forced eigenvalue = the first gap = C₂ = 6**; and because the bleed is a sum of fading modes with *positive* weights (**completely-monotone**), w(a) is locked into a **constrained family** far tighter than the free (w₀,wₐ) DESI fits — **BST predicts DESI's point must land in a specific allowed region; outside → refuted.**
- **Shape = Structure-Derived** (attractor=zero-mode, form=completely-monotone, rate=C₂ eigenvalue — all proved edges); **one unproved edge = the substrate-clock→expansion map (δτ normalization)** = the age-set piece keeping the **value Identified.** *That clock edge is shared with the cc value (observer depth)* — so the earlier "4 frontier edges" partly collapse (cc value + w(a) value ride the clock edge; magic strength *is* E1).
