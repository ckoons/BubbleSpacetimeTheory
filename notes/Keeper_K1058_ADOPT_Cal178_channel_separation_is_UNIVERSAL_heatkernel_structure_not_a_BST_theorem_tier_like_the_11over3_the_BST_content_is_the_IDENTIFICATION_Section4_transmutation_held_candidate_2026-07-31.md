# K1058 — The channel-separation is universal; the BST win is the identification (Rule 11 on the ladder's own headline)

Cal §178 cleared Section 3 (the Sym²⊥Λ² fix from K1057 is in) and added two refinements. Both correct; adopted.

## ★ (1) Rule 11, applied to the ladder's own headline
The channel-separation — the a_k are orthogonal graded invariants (mass-dim 2k), and within a₂ the gauge line tr(F²) is orthogonal to the gravitational curvature-squares (the dim-4 R·F cross-term vanishing by Sym²⊥Λ², K1057) — is **universal heat-kernel structure**: Gilkey grading + power-counting + the symmetric/antisymmetric decomposition. It holds for **any** operator that induces both gravity and a gauge running. **So it is not a BST theorem, and must be tiered like the 11/3 (K1052, Rule 11):**
- **Universal (consistency):** the non-mixing itself. Reproducing/using it is not a BST derivation.
- **BST-specific (the actual win):** the **identification** that D_IV⁵'s *one* heat-trace IS the substrate operator whose a₀/a₁/a₂ *are* Λ, G, and the strong running. The headline is **"one D_IV⁵ operator, three forces"** — the identification — NOT the non-mixing.
So the ladder paper must frame Section 3 as "the non-mixing is a clean consequence of standard heat-kernel structure *on the BST identification*," never "a new BST channel-separation theorem." This is Rule 11 catching the culmination paper's own headline before it over-claims — the sharpest possible test of the rule, on our best result.

## ★ (2) Section 4 — hold the over-determination as CANDIDATE (K1047 hazard)
Section 4 pairs the a₂ *running* (via dimensional transmutation → the confinement/Λ_QCD scale) with the *already-derived* mass-gap value Δ = 6π⁵·m_e = m_p. The instinct (running-side meets value-side = over-determination) is right — **but only if the transmutation is a FORCED connection, not a numerical coincidence.** That is exactly the K1047 hazard (coincidental-transmutation → not a Tier-1). **Ruling: hold the over-determination as CANDIDATE until Elie's dimensional-transmutation is an *independent* computation** (running-scale derived without reference to the mass-gap value). Section 4 must **not** read as "the running derives the proton mass" — the running and the value stay separate results (the K1047/K1050 separation) until the transmutation forces the bridge. Cal is auditing Section 4 to exactly this bar; endorsed.
