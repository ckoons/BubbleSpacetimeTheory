# K1144 — reframe E8 per Casey (EOD)

Corrects the *framing* of the K1143 E8 disposition. The physics (wₐ>0, double-derived: Lyra F799 8/9 + Elie 5000/5001 dr/dτ=−Var(λ)≤0) is unchanged.

## Casey's insight (ruled correct)
E8 must **not** be presented as "BST on the wrong side of DESI." The robust observation — **dark energy is dynamical and diminishing** (w>−1 today, not a pure constant) — is exactly BST's core prediction: the **geometry relaxing toward the substrate floor** (w=−1, the zero-mode). ΛCDM predicts a pure constant; BST predicted a *relaxing* dark energy; DESI sees one. **That is a genuine BST win over ΛCDM, and it is the headline.**

## The divergence is narrow and parametrization-dependent
The only disagreement is the **sign of wₐ**. BST forces approach *from above* (wₐ>0, monotone to the floor, **no phantom crossing** — positive-weight completely-monotone modes physically can't cross). DESI's preferred wₐ<0 rides on the **CPL / (w₀,wₐ) linear parametrization**, which allows phantom crossing by construction. Casey's read: that assumption isn't well-founded — the wₐ<0 may be an artifact of forcing a linear w(a) onto data that is actually a monotone relaxation.

## The test that settles it (next step)
Fit BST's completely-monotone w(a) **shape directly** to the DESI data (BAO+SNe+CMB) and compare goodness-of-fit to CPL:
- BST fits comparably or better → the wₐ<0 "tension" was a **parametrization artifact**; BST **not falsified** (a cleaner fit, no phantom).
- A parametrization-independent reconstruction still requires phantom crossing → the bleed mechanism is **genuinely falsified**.

## Keeper's honest line (hold both)
The confirmation is real and is the headline; the phantom-crossing divergence is real too and must be stated — but it turns on DESI's parametrization. Honest status: **"BST predicts wₐ>0 / no phantom crossing; whether the data *requires* wₐ<0 is parametrization-dependent and open."** Pre-register wₐ>0 plainly *with* the tension stated — the honesty is the credibility (Cal §254, Grace pred_046, Lyra all concur; external on Casey's GO).

E8 tier unchanged: shape Structure-Derived, the wₐ>0 sign a forced prediction; the DESI comparison correctly framed as confirmation-of-relaxation + a parametrization-dependent sign divergence.
