# K1700 — Track ①: baryon number is the exact ℤ₃ color-grading (keep sphalerons, forbid only ΔB=1)

The team split three ways. The resolution unifies them and is cleaner than any single answer.

## What decided it (Lyra, textbook-correct)
A sphaleron does **ΔB = N_gen = 3** (ΔN_CS=1 ⟹ ΔB=ΔL=3, B−L exact — 't Hooft). Proton decay is **ΔB=1**. Different processes. Absolute proton stability (K1699 = ΔB=1 forbidden) was **never** in tension with sphalerons (ΔB=3).

⟹ The original plan's **"BST predicts no sphaleron B-violation"** (Grace's decoupling derivation aimed there too) is an **over-forbid** — it forbids ΔB=3 the ruling doesn't require, fights well-tested physics, and needlessly kills EW baryogenesis. **Retract it.** This is §609 firing in the *over*-claim direction: the brief priced a departure larger than the ruling forces.

## The actual reconciliation (unifies Lyra's mod-3 + Grace's color-object + Casey's exact-superselection)
**BST baryon number = the ℤ₃ center-grading of color** (`Z(SU(3))=ℤ₃`), conserved **mod N_c=3** and **exactly** (topological — nothing breaks it, ever). Selection rule:
- **ΔB ≡ 0 mod 3 → allowed.** Sphaleron ΔB=3 ≡ 0: **sphalerons kept, anomaly kept.** Cal's "import the gauge principle ⟹ import the anomaly" is **honored, not declined** — BST adds an exact ℤ₃ rule *consistent* with the anomaly, it does not refuse it (so this is not option (c) pick-and-choose).
- **ΔB=1 → forbidden absolutely.** Proton never decays.

**The SM already conserves B mod 3** (sphalerons do ΔB=3). Its B-conservation is *accidental* (perturbative) + mod-3 (non-perturbative) + broken to ΔB=1 only by GUT dim-6 operators (proton decays ~10³⁴ yr). **BST's departure is small and clean:** the mod-3 is *fundamental* (topological), not accidental — proton decay is *exactly* zero forever, vs GUT-suppressed.

## Cal's operational demand — met
Exhibit a configuration where the two charges assign **different** values: it is **proton decay** — SM+GUT assigns ΔB=1 *allowed* (dim-6), BST assigns ΔB=1 *forbidden* (topological ℤ₃). Different fate on the same state ⟹ the charges are **distinct**, not a relabeling. They **agree** on the sphaleron — which is exactly why "no sphaleron B-violation" was the wrong departure.

## The deep structural point (candidate)
The sphaleron is invisible to BST-B *precisely because* ΔB_sphaleron = N_gen = 3 = N_c, and BST conserves B mod N_c. **N_gen = N_c is the invisibility condition** (3 | 3) — this ties *3-generations = 3-colors* to baryon-number conservation. Candidate until Grace source-pins.

## Five-Absence bonus (candidate consistency — do not over-claim)
No sterile ν (Five-Absence) removes standard leptogenesis (heavy Majorana RH ν), which makes **baryogenesis-as-initial-data** (positive-time ontology) natural/preferred rather than merely chosen. A consistency to *check*, not a forced result.

## Owed before banking (Grace — load-bearing)
**Source-pin the winding as (a) ℤ₃/mod-N_c AND (b) exactly (topologically) conserved** — from T1945/T296/the trefoil source. This is same-name-different-object on "baryon number": SM integer-B vs BST ℤ₃-B. **The whole ruling rides on the winding being mod-3-exact.** If the source says exact-*integer* (mod-∞), sphalerons *would* violate it (ΔB=3≠0) and we fall back to Grace's decoupling picture (needs tr(B·weak²)=0) or to tension. Grace's mixed-anomaly computation is *not* the owed piece for this picture (we keep the anomaly) — the owed piece is the **mod-3-vs-integer pin**.
