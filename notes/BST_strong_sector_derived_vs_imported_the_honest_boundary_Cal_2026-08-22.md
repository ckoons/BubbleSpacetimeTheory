## Why the right-hand column is a strength

Three things make this different from a theory that simply stops where it stops.

**1. The fence is proved, not asserted.** #108 (K1673/K1677/K1724, three-way confirmed) established that the colour block contributes **zero** internal gauge generators. Elie's 5434 goes further: **no geometry-symmetry operator can confine the triplet** — the Šilov projection is SO(5)-equivariant and acts on whole K-types, and the triplet sits inside a λ₂ = 0 one. **A theory that proves where its own reach ends is in a strictly better position than one that leaves the boundary unexamined.**

**2. The division of labour is the one a spectral triple makes.** The geometry supplies the algebra and the module; the inner fluctuations supply the gauge fields. **BST's strong sector has exactly that shape** — a derived colour space with an imported connection on it. That is not a gap in the construction; it is the construction.

**3. It is the same import every derivation of the Standard Model takes.** Nobody derives the gauge principle. Saying so plainly costs nothing and buys the reader's trust for the left-hand column.
