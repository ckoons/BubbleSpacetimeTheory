# GUARDRAIL — external-claim language for the QM-axioms headline

Pinned **before** the hook-package draft, so the biggest external claim can't drift. The scorecard maps internal status; this fixes the exact **external sentence** each axiom may make, and the over-claim it must not.

## Headline-level scope sentence (the only allowed form)
> "On the single geometry D_IV⁵, most of the standard axioms of quantum mechanics are not postulated but **forced or realized** as features of the domain — with two proved/derived anchors (**Born, uncertainty**), one distinctive-but-gated result (**spin-statistics**), and the **measurement/collapse** axiom explicitly **not closed**."

**Forbidden:** "BST proves all the axioms of QM"; "QM is just the geometry of D_IV⁵"; any headline that omits the open measurement piece.

## Per-axiom: may-say / must-not-say

| Axiom | May say | Must not say |
|---|---|---|
| State space / superposition | State space *is* H²(D_IV⁵); superposition = its linearity | "derived Hilbert space from nothing" |
| Observables | Realized as the domain's invariant self-adjoint operators | self-adjointness forced without saying whence (smuggle) |
| **Born rule** | **Derived** — forced Bergman measure (T754) + Gleason; marble = the measure | "N_c=3 supplies Gleason's ≥3 dims" (retracted, Cal §238) |
| Unitary evolution | Realized via Wick-rotated Casimir heat-semigroup | present unitarity as distinctively-BST (universal wood) |
| **Arrow of time** | **Derived** — positivity of spec(H_B) | — |
| **3+1 signature** | **Derived** — SO(5,2)→SO(4,2)→SO(3,1) | — |
| **Spin-statistics** | **Identified**, distinctive origin: spin-½ = the type-IV ℤ₂ half-twist; connection derived (T1946); **field-content gate open** | "spin-statistics is Derived"; "why fermions exist" as closed; re-merging the three −1 ops; **"no spin-3/2" as an independent falsifier** (it's no-SUSY restated — circular, K1138) |
| **Uncertainty** | **Proved** (T753) — Bergman curvature −2/g, a D_IV⁵-specific value | "generic non-commutation" (undersell) |
| CPT | CPT-invariant via the standard connected-group argument, realized in BST (T1945) | headline it as distinctively-forced (universal wood) |
| **Measurement/collapse** | **Not closed** — SWPP framing exists, no projection-postulate derivation; named open | any claim BST solves/derives measurement |

## Two standing guards
- **Wood/marble per piece:** a claim that follows from *any* Hilbert-space / Lorentz-invariant theory is universal wood; the marble is *only* that **one domain fixes the specific space + operators + measure + curvature.**
- **Imported-theorem:** Born rides Gleason, spin-statistics rides the belt-trick, uncertainty rides Robertson — in each, the classical theorem is the wood, and the marble is that **D_IV⁵ supplies its hypotheses distinctively.** Never "we proved Gleason/Robertson."
