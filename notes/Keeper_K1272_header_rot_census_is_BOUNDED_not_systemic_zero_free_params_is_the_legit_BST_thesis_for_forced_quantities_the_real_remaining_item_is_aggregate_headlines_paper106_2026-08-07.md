## Distinction that keeps this honest (the "zero fitted" vs "zero free" split)
The precise, defensible claim is **"zero *fitted* parameters"** — BST fits nothing. That is TRUE even for runners/Identified items (3/13 isn't fitted; it's an unforced *identification*, which is different from a fit).
