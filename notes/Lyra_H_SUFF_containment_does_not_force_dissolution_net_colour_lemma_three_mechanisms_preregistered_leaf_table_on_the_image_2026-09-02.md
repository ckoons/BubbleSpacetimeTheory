# 0. What H-suff says, pinned (Cal §821-A; Elie 10:34 replicated)

**H-suff:** if some (r,s_M)-stage chain Z of a fully legal first word O contains a bridge cut C (C_i or C_j,
computed in c₀'s frame), then the image c′ = O·c₀ exits (some fully-legal second word reaches the gate; on
1,812/1,936 exits it is a bridge word in c′'s frame). Measured 720/720 and 586/586 with a perfect control
(0/3,632 non-exiting words contain). Not necessary (1,216 exits without containment). The containing words have
a COPY-seeded θ-letter at stage 2/4: α·θ_{B₁}, ζ·θ_{B₂}, η·θ_{B₁}, … ; the middle canonical word θ·α is NOT
among them.

# 1. The cut's original colours — predicted yesterday-evening's way, confirmed by Cal's census (derived)

Cut-Dissolution §1: a cut vertex of W_i is either type A (r in c₀, in δ) or type B (s_i in c₀, not in δ); by
the mirror, a cut vertex of W_j is r or s_j. Cal's 457 pooled cut vertices: **r 36 · s_i 209 · s_j 212** — no
other colour, exactly the two types, with type B the overwhelming majority. So the object H-suff's chain
contains is mostly s_i/s_j-material in c₀, and **an (r,s_M)-chain can contain it only after an earlier stage
has turned it r (a bridge-pair letter) or s_M (an α/β letter)** — Cal's two-step correction, now derived as the
letter table's row structure: **every containing word is [a letter whose world holds the cut's original colour]
then [θ seeded at a copy]**, which is why the list reads α·θ, ζ·θ, η·θ, δ·θ, γ·θ and never θ·α. Position, not value.

# 2. THE NET-COLOUR LEMMA (derived) — containment does not force "out of both bridge worlds"

Let O = m₁ m₂ m₁ m₂ with m₂ = θ at a copy B; Z₂, Z₄ the (r,s_M)-chains swapped at stages 2 and 4. Suppose
C ⊆ Z₂ (containment at stage 2). Track a cut vertex u through the four stages:
- stage 1 (m₁): u brought into the (r,s_M)-world (→ r by a bridge letter, or → s_M by α/β), else u ∈ Z₂ is
  impossible;
- stage 2 (Z₂ ∋ u): toggled — r → s_M or s_M → r;
- stage 3 (m₁ again): touches u only if u carries a colour of m₁'s pair NOW: a bridge letter (r,s_x) touches u
  iff u is r (i.e. u was s_M after stage 2 — it came in via α/β); α touches u iff u is s_M or s_i;
- stage 4 (Z₄ = B's (r,s_M)-chain in c₃′): toggles u iff u ∈ Z₄.
**Final colour:** u ends **s_M** iff the number of toggles landing on s_M is odd in the right way — concretely,
for the dominant shape [bridge letter]·θ: u: s_x → r (stage 1) → s_M (stage 2) → untouched at stage 3 (a
bridge letter cannot see s_M) → **s_M iff u ∉ Z₄, r iff u ∈ Z₄.** By Net-Support (T2582) the net change of
the θ-letters is supported on Z₂ △ Z₄; **a cut vertex is dissolved (ends s_M) iff u ∈ Z₂ ∖ Z₄ — excised or
stranded at stage 3 — and RETURNS to r iff u ∈ Z₂ ∩ Z₄.** By Confinement (T2583) the stage-3 chain enters Z₂
only through Z₂'s old-s_M vertices (now r): the excision that decides u's fate is the same excision T2583/T2584
describe. For the α·θ shape: u: s_i → s_M (α) → r (Z₂) → s_i or r at stage 3 (α sees r? no — α = (s_M,s_i)
sees s_M, s_i: u is r, untouched) → s_M iff u ∈ Z₄. So there u ends s_M iff u ∈ Z₂ ∩ Z₄ — the OPPOSITE
membership. **Containment at stage 4** (Z₄ ⊇ C): final colour is the toggle of c₃′'s colour: r → s_M, s_M → r —
again not uniformly "out".

**Consequence.** "Containment ⟹ every cut vertex leaves both bridge worlds" is a chain question (u ∈ Z₂ △ Z₄
in the right direction), not a bookkeeping identity. H-suff's 720/720 is therefore NOT explained by the
sentence the order asked me to derive, unless the measurement in Section 4 shows the membership is always
the right one. I decline to derive a sentence that the bookkeeping does not force.

# 3. What a bridge-word exit from c′ IS (derived: Lemma T on the image)

From c′ (if stuck — else exit-1), the bridge word in c′'s frame exits iff its leaf is Δ-NO, or Δ′-YES, or
(Q3 fails) or (Q4 fails) — T2590/T2592 applied in c′'s frame. Elie's 10:31 finding that images after exiting
words usually carry a NONEMPTY new cut (κ mostly 2–5) says most exits are NOT Δ-NO and NOT ¬Q3 (Q3 ⟹ a
separating cut, Menger); by elimination they are **¬Q4 exits: the image's middle-to-far-singleton (s_M,s_i)-
road is broken**, or Δ′-YES. An (r,s_M)-stage is precisely a stage that removes s_M-vertices from the
(s_M,·)-worlds. So the candidate mechanism the data points at is not "the cut dissolves" but **"the copy-
seeded θ-stage breaks the middle's road"** — the Q4 leg of Lemma T, not the Q3 leg.
