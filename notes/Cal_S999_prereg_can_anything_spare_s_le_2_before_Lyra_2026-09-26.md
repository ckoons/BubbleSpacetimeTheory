**Antecedent, verbatim (round-10 prompt, Cal item 1):** *"can ANY structure spare exactly s ≤ 2 without violating Maldacena–Zhiboedov / Alba–Diab's hypotheses (a unique stress tensor, unitarity)? Is 'spares 1 and 2' even a consistent CFT pattern, or does breaking s > 2 force breaking s = 1 too (only the stress tensor must stay)?"*

**Invariant first:** in a unitary CFT in d > 2, a spin-s primary is a conserved current iff Δ = d − 2 + s (the unitarity bound, saturated). A conserved spin-2 current with Δ = d is a stress tensor. Every LOCAL CFT has one; that is an axiom of locality, not a dynamical result.

**Kill line for this ruling:** it dies if there is an interacting unitary local CFT where breaking all s > 2 currents is shown to force breaking every spin-1 current, or if Lyra exhibits a BST structure whose sparing of s ≤ 2 is non-automatic, i.e. a selection that a generic interaction would not give.

**Ruling:**
- **R1. "Spare exactly s ≤ 2" is consistent, and it is the GENERIC pattern.** Any interacting unitary local CFT has an exact stress tensor (s = 2, locality). It has an exact spin-1 current iff it has a continuous global symmetry. By Maldacena–Zhiboedov / Alba–Diab it has no higher-spin current. Breaking s > 2 does NOT force breaking s = 1: the O(N) Wilson–Fisher fixed point keeps its O(N) current exact while its would-be higher-spin currents acquire γ_s ~ 1/N. The d = 5 large-N O(N) fixed point is the same kind of example (Fei–Giombi–Klebanov; pin owed). **So "sparing 1 and 2" needs no mechanism beyond (a) any interaction at all and (b) a global symmetry. It cannot be credited to a BST structure. The round-10 question reduces to round 9's: does anything interact? That is the third wall again.**
- **R2. The odd clock is not a "uniform breaking" in MZ's sense.** H²'s bilinears sit 2 above the bound at every spin, INCLUDING s = 2, so H² has no stress tensor at all (Lyra R9). A generalized free field with Δ ≠ (d−2)/2 is a non-local conformal theory. **MZ/AD's hypothesis (a local stress tensor) fails on H², so H² is outside the theorem, not a "broken higher-spin theory".** Only the Rac level (Δ = 3/2 = (d−2)/2) is local, with T and all currents. It is free by MZ, which is the wall from the other side.
- **R3. The pattern is stated in d = 5; nature's "spins 1 and 2" is a 4D statement.** A conserved 5D current on ℝ^{1,4}, restricted or branched through the descent (K3: SO(5) → SO(4) → SO(3,1)), gives 4D pieces that need not be conserved 4D currents. **The 5D pattern is not yet the observed 4D pattern.** The descent must be carried out before "BST spares 1 and 2" can be written.
- **R4. What WOULD count: the SIZE pattern, not the survival pattern.** In the slightly broken higher-spin regime (MZ arXiv:1204.3882), the anomalous dimensions γ_s of the would-be currents take a shape in s fixed by the broken algebra, times one small parameter (1/N). If BST FORCED that parameter (a candidate: 1/N_max), γ_s(s) would be zero-knob. **But nature shows no near-conserved higher-spin currents to compare with, so it is untestable today.** Pre-register the parameter before any comparison if this door is opened.
