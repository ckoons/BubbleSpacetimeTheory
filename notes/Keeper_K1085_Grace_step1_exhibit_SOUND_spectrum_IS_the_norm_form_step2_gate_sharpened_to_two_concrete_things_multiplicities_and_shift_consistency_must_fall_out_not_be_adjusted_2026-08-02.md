# K1085 — Step 1 sound; step 2's fishing risk located in the multiplicities and the shift

## Grace's step-1 exhibit — SOUND (banked)
The one forcing step: does det Δ reduce to the rank-2 Jordan norm? Grace's first exhibit:
- **λ_k = k(k+n_C) factors into exactly two Jordan eigenvalues {k, k+n_C}** — and rank=2 means the norm IS the degree-2 (product-of-two) invariant. So **the analytic eigenvalue λ_k = N(k, k+n_C)**, the rank-2 norm form evaluated level by level. (λ₀=0·5, λ₁=1·6, λ₂=2·7=14, λ₃=3·8=24.)
- The shift n_C=5 is the Gindikin parameter; the rank-2 Γ_Ω(s)=(2π)^{3/2}Γ(s)Γ(s−3/2) is **two Gammas** matching the two-factor norm.
So **the spectrum det Δ is built from *is* the algebraic norm form** — the two "determinants" share structure, not just a word. Genuine exhibit, and it's the right first step (Faraut–Korányi tube-type, Cal §195 verified).

## Step 1 ≠ the reduction (Grace's own line — ratified)
"The spectrum is the norm" is **not** "det Δ = the norm form." The reduction is **ζ'(0) through Γ_Ω** — the Barnes–Gindikin double-Gamma continuation of ∏ λ_k^{d_k} — **not yet on paper.** Grace held this line herself (naming her own failure mode: over-reaching on "56 innocent" and the two-tower conflation). Ratified: banking waits on step 2.

## The step-2 gate, sharpened to TWO concrete things (linear algebra on D_IV⁵, corpus)
The reduction is *exhibited* only if these **fall out, not get adjusted** — the concrete form of exhibit-not-infer:

**(a) The multiplicities.** det Δ = ∏_k λ_k^{d_k} (ζ-regularized), where d_k = dimension of the k-th K-type / eigenspace. For the Barnes–Gindikin continuation to collapse to the two-Gamma Γ_Ω (= det_J), the d_k must be the **forced geometric K-type dimensions** (from the discrete series — corpus machinery), **not chosen** to make the continuation work. *This is linear algebra on the K-types.* If the multiplicities have to be tuned, it's inferred.

**(b) Shift-consistency.** The spectral factoring shifts the two eigenvalues by **n_C = 5** (k vs k+5); the rank-2 Γ_Ω shifts its two Gammas by **N_c/2 = 3/2** (s vs s−3/2). These are *different parameters*. For the reduction to exhibit, the n_C spectral shift must **reconcile** with the Γ_Ω Gamma-shift under the continuation (via the variable-change / the multiplicities). If matching them requires a patch, that patch **is** the fit. **Concrete gate:** show n_C=5 ↔ 3/2 falls out of the Barnes continuation, or the reduction isn't exhibited.
