## Objects and the space each lives on
- **Colouring** f: V → GF(2)² = {d=(0,0), a=(1,0), b=(0,1), c=(1,1)} (colour indices 0,1,2,3). Kempe classes are computed on colourings mod S₄ (Mohar–Salas's convention) and the raw count reported too.
- **Height cochain** ω ∈ Z¹(T; ℤ²): ω(u→w) = σ_left(u→w) · L(f(u)+f(w)), L(a)=A=(1,0), L(b)=B=(0,1), L(c)=C=(−1,−1) (T2577, Paper 2 §1.1). Closed on every face (σ(A+B+C) = 0).
- **Our height datum** (the global datum of §1.3 at k = 0): [ω] ∈ H¹(T;ℤ²) ≅ ℤ⁴, i.e. the two periods P_x = Σ ω along a horizontal cycle, P_y along a vertical cycle (independence of the row/column chosen is a control). All periods are even (ω ≡ df mod 2). Derived quantities: the integer charges s_x = ⟨P_x,(1,1)⟩, s_y = ⟨P_y,(1,1)⟩; the ℤ₃ charge class [c] = (s_x mod 3, s_y mod 3) ∈ H¹(T;ℤ₃).
- **Fisk/Mohar–Salas degree** deg(f) = p − n over the faces of T mapped to one tetrahedron face (eq. 3.2), with ∂Δ³ oriented as ∂[0,1,2,3] = [1,2,3] − [0,2,3] + [0,1,3] − [0,1,2]; independence of the tetrahedron face is a control; the overall sign is one convention for all colourings.
- **Kempe move**: for colours a≠b and a connected component K of G_ab, swap a↔b on K (WSK at zero temperature).

## Derivations made while designing (candidates for Lyra's E4 dictionary; they are PREDICTIONS here, killed by the numbers if wrong)
D1. A Kempe swap on K changes ω only on the boundary edges of K, each by ±(L(a) − L(b)) = ±(1,−1) (labels a+x ↔ b+x, x ∈ {c,d}). Hence **s_x, s_y are exactly Kempe-invariant as integers** (charge of (1,−1) is 0), and periods change only by even multiples of (1,−1).
D2. On T(3L,3M) the 3-colouring is unique up to permutation and equals the height charge mod 3 (A, B, C all have charge ≡ 1 mod 3, so charge(h) is a proper 3-colouring); therefore **s_x ≡ s_y ≡ 0 (mod 3) for EVERY colouring — [c] = 0 always on this family; the ℤ₃ class is a constant instrument here, uninformative, not a negative.**
D3. The height map factors T → 𝕋 := ℝ²/2ℤ² (the 4-vertex, 8-face torus triangulated by A, B, C), and q: 𝕋 → ∂Δ³ (colour = h mod 2) is orientation-preserving on all 8 faces (checked face by face on paper: each colour triple appears once as an up-face and once as a down-face, both preserving), so q is the double cover branched at the four vertices (χ: 0 = 2·2 − 4). Hence **deg(f) = 2·deg(T → 𝕋) = 2·det[P_x/2, P_y/2] = det[P_x, P_y]/2** (one global sign). Consistency: Fisk's deg ≡ 0 (mod 6) follows from D2 (Q_x, Q_y ≡ t(1,−1) mod 3 ⟹ det ≡ 0 mod 3); Mohar–Salas's mod-12 bit is det[P_x/2,P_y/2] mod 2.
