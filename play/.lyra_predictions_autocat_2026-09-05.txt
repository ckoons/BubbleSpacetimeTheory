 A1 **R CANNOT SEE AUTOCATALYSIS.** A -> X and X + A -> 2X have the SAME stoichiometric matrix

    once enough product exists. **An autocatalytic reaction is a generator that switches ITSELF on.**

    an inversion suppressed its own generators and preserved a record, an autocatalyst ACTIVATES

 A4 And by Test 14's W5 (free self-modification zeroes the record), an autocatalyst that can
    activate itself without limit should end at R = 0 on its own coordinate: **an autocatalytic
    system destroys the record of how much of itself there was.**
