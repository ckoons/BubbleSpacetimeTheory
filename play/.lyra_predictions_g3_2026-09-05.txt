LYRA — TEST 27: G3, THE SECOND APERTURE AS A CERTIFICATE. Written BEFORE the run, 2026-09-05
(autonomous). Absolute path; shasum in this same command; launch separately.

THE CLAIM I HAVE ARGUED BUT NEVER COMPUTED (flagged twice as interpretation): with ONE aperture,
intake and efflux share a channel and nothing distinguishes them; with TWO they are distinct
generators, and "which end" becomes a certificate -- the through-gut as the first body axis.

WHY IT SHOULD BE A THEOREM, NOT A STORY. Certificates in this framework have been winding numbers
(dimer flux). Winding numbers live on CYCLES. By Euler, for a connected graph the cycle rank is
b1 = |E| - |V| + 1. Chamber C_m joined to environment C_n by ONE edge: |V| = m+n, |E| = m+n+1,
b1 = 2 (the two rings; the bridge contributes nothing). By TWO edges: |E| = m+n+2, b1 = 3.
**The second aperture adds EXACTLY ONE to the cycle rank -- one new winding coordinate.**

PREDICTIONS
 G3a b1 goes 2 -> 3 when the second aperture is added, for every m, n. Pure Euler; a miss means
     I have miscounted, not that the claim is wrong.
 G3b THE NEW CYCLE CARRIES RECORD: the dimer class count on the two-aperture graph EXCEEDS that
     on the one-aperture graph, at matched vertex count. Point estimate: a factor of ~2-3, since
     one new independent winding coordinate should multiply the class count by the number of
     values it takes.
 G3c **THE NEW COORDINATE IS A GENUINE CERTIFICATE BY THEOREM 9**: the winding around the
     through-loop is constant on classes, and including it in the fibre map REDUCES THE DEFECT
     H(class|fibre) relative to using the chamber winding alone.
     This is the sharp version and the one that makes G3 a theorem rather than a picture.
 G3d ONE APERTURE IS A BRIDGE. Cutting it disconnects the graph, so no cycle passes through it and
     it can carry no winding. **A dead-end cup cannot have a body axis** -- not because it is
     inefficient, but because there is no cycle for the certificate to live on.
 G3e The effect should NOT depend on chamber size: adding the second aperture buys exactly one
     coordinate whether the chamber is small or large. If the gain scales with m, the mechanism
     is not the cycle rank.

WHAT WOULD KILL IT
 G3b false (no extra classes) -> the new cycle carries no record and the topology is decorative.
 G3c false (defect not reduced) -> the winding is not a certificate and G3 stays an interpretation,
     which is how it currently sits in the note. I would then withdraw it rather than keep arguing.
