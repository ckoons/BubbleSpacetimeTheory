THE SEPARATING EXPERIMENT: run the DYNAMIC criterion on the ABELIAN substrate.
Model: n junctions, |J| active; R = n - |J|, H_thermo = |J|, H = n (exact, Theorem 8).
Growth adds g junctions of which each is active with probability f. Refusal deactivates one active
junction. Sustaining := R rising over the last steps AND |J| >= 1 (at least one degree of freedom).
