LYRA — TEST 17: A POPULATION OF COOPERATING PAIRS. Written BEFORE the run, 2026-09-05.
Absolute path; shasum in this same command; run launched separately.

CASEY (2026-09-05): "take a group of cooperating pairs; some variations will be better and those
variations will reproduce better."

WHY THIS MATTERS: Section 21 said refusal needs an observer, and I defined living as a system that
performs its OWN refusals -- too strict. Casey's picture supplies the demon from a different place:
**the act of reproduction IS the measurement.** A pair reads its own state to specify its copy.
A pair whose record melted before copying passes on noise; one whose record held passes on itself.
No external selector, and H_obs is not an extra cost because the read IS the record.

CIRCULARITY GUARD -- the thing most likely to make this worthless. If I score fitness as "record
retained" and then observe record increasing, that is construction-guaranteed and proves nothing
(cf. hunt-if-P). **THERE IS NO FITNESS FUNCTION IN THIS EXPERIMENT.** The only mechanism is:
the parameter theta that controls protection is ENCODED IN THE RECORD IT PROTECTS. Individuals
copy at interval T; junctions active at horizon T scramble; theta is read from the (possibly
scrambled) bits. Whatever concentrates, concentrates without anyone scoring it.

PREDICTIONS
 P1 NEGATIVE CONTROL, and it must be run first. With protection DISABLED for everyone (eps fixed
    at 1, so theta cannot matter), the population must stay UNIFORM over theta. If it concentrates
    anyway, the drift is an artifact of my sampling and nothing else in this test counts.
 P2 With theta free, the population CONCENTRATES on protective theta -- self-protection is
    selected for with no selector present. Point estimate: >70% of the population on the two most
    protective theta values within 200 generations.
 P3 Convergence speed depends on the copy interval T, and is fastest when T sits in the range where
    theta actually changes retention. Outside that range there is nothing for transmission to
    discriminate on and the population should drift, not converge.
 P4 THE ONE I CARE ABOUT: **selection is TIMESCALE-RELATIVE.** The theta that wins is the one that
    protects at the COPY interval, not the absolute strongest. At short T, weak protection suffices
    and there should be NO pressure toward stronger protection. If instead the population always
    runs to maximum protection regardless of T, then protection is not being tuned by transmission
    and my reading of the mechanism is wrong.

WHAT WOULD KILL IT
 P1 fails -> instrument broken, stop.
 P2 fails (no concentration) -> reproduction-as-measurement does not by itself select for
    retention, and Casey's route needs an explicit selector after all.
 P4 fails -> the mechanism is not transmission-fidelity tuning and I should say so plainly.
