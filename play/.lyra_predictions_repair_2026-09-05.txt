=== REPAIR A (Paper 1): DROP THE INDEPENDENCE ASSUMPTION ===
Paper 1's Theorem 1 assumed crossovers are independent Bernoulli(r) per junction. A referee's
first question is crossover INTERFERENCE, which breaks that. My claim is that independence was
never needed:

 A1 For ANY crossover process whatever, with switch pattern d on n junctions and uniform parent
    phase, retention = I(parent;offspring) = **n - H(d)** bits, where H(d) is the entropy of the
    CROSSOVER PATTERN. Independence only specialises H(d) = sum_i H_2(r_i).
    PROOF SKETCH: D_out = D_in XOR d with d independent of D_in, so
    I = H(D_out) - H(D_out|D_in) = n - H(d).
    To be verified by brute force on a deliberately CORRELATED d.

 A2 THEREFORE INTERFERENCE BUYS INFORMATION BACK. Exactly-one-crossover (maximal interference)
    has H(d) = log2(n); independent crossovers with the same MEAN have H(d) = n*H_2(1/n), which
    is larger. Point estimate at n=100: independent loses 8.08 bits, exactly-one loses 6.64,
    interference saves ~1.44 bits per chromosome per meiosis.

 A3 AND HOTSPOTS BUY MORE. Concentrating crossovers into a fraction f of junctions gives
    H(d) = log2(f*n) instead of log2(n), a saving of log2(1/f) bits per crossover.
    At the human figure f ~ 0.15, that is ~2.74 bits per crossover recovered.
    THIS IS A NEW PREDICTION, not a repricing of a known fact: it says hotspot CONCENTRATION
    is itself an information optimisation, separate from the obligate-crossover argument.

 A4 The information-optimal meiosis subject only to "at least one crossover" would put it at a
    FIXED position (H(d)=0, full retention). Real meiosis does not. So there must be a second
    constraint -- creation must be DISTRIBUTED, or each block ratchets. State this as an open
    problem, do not paper over it.

    fibres of the quotient map V -> V/<v_m>, and in the transitive case R = H of the pushforward
    on V/<v_m>. Adding a move with shift v' costs exactly the further quotient by <v'>.
 B2 EXACT CHECK, meiosis: V = F_2^n, crossover j has v = e_j, <e_j> has order 2, so each generator
    costs exactly log2(2) = 1 bit. Predicted to match the measured flat 1.000 EXACTLY.
 B3 DIMER CHECK: V = Z^2 (winding). Face flips have v = 0, so they are PREDICTED FREE -- this is
    why 546 compact moves retain. Wrapping cycles have v != 0 and cost the quotient.

    Measured target to beat: 4x6 faces R=2.2572 -> all-4-cycles R=0.8240, a drop of 1.4332.
