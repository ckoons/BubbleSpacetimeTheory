LYRA — TEST 25: IS THE INVARIANT AN ABSOLUTE FLOOR OF ONE DEGREE OF FREEDOM?
Written BEFORE the run, 2026-09-05. Absolute path; shasum in this same command; launch separately.

CASEY (2026-09-05): "at the cold edge where things are stable but still move -- that's balance",
and "I suspect we will always find new organisms develop near stability but with at least one
degree of freedom."

THIS REFRAMES TEST 24. I predicted balance at R/H = 1/2 (equal record and heat) and MISSED: the
peak was at 0.2-0.3 and the per-substrate means spread 0.211. Casey's claim is different and
sharper: **R as high as it can go SUBJECT TO H_thermo > 0** -- near the crystal limit, not the
middle. My own Arm B data already said so: froze at R/H = 0.991, marginal 0.958, SUSTAINED 0.863,
all crowded against the frozen end, none near 0.5.

THE KEY STRUCTURAL CONSEQUENCE: if the invariant is "at least one degree of freedom", then it is
**H_thermo, an ABSOLUTE quantity of order ONE BIT -- not a ratio.** As H grows, R/H -> 1
automatically, so the RATIO cannot be universal even when the FLOOR is. That would explain why
B3 failed across substrates while the underlying claim is still right.

PREDICTIONS
 O1 THE SURVIVAL FLOOR IS H_thermo ~ 1 BIT, and it is substrate-independent. Arm B brackets it
    already: H_th = 0.089 froze, 0.685 marginal, 2.29 sustained. Point estimate 0.8 - 1.5 bits.
 O2 H_thermo AT THE LIVING BOUNDARY IS CONSTANT IN SYSTEM SIZE, while R/H at the same boundary
    RISES TOWARD 1 as H grows. Test on dimer tori 4x4, 4x6, 6x6 and meiosis n = 6, 8, 10, 12:
    find the minimal-nonzero-H_thermo configuration in each and compare.
    **If H_thermo drifts with size and R/H does not, I have this exactly backwards.**
 O3 SPREAD COMPARISON, the decisive one: across substrates, sd(H_thermo at the boundary) should be
    SMALLER than sd(R/H at the boundary). If the ratio is tighter than the absolute, Casey's
    "one degree of freedom" is not the invariant and R/H was closer after all.
 O4 The floor should be ~1 bit and not ~0: a system with H_thermo strictly between 0 and 1 bit has
    less than one full binary degree of freedom and should NOT sustain. Predicted: the marginal
    band sits at H_th between 0.5 and 1.5.

WHAT WOULD KILL IT
 O3 reversed -> the ratio is the invariant, not the absolute floor.
 O2 false (H_thermo scales with size) -> "one degree of freedom" is not size-independent and the
    claim needs a different quantity.
