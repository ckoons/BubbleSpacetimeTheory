LYRA — TEST 20: THE CUP, THE SECOND CUP, AND THE SECOND OPENING.
Written BEFORE the run, 2026-09-05. Absolute path; shasum in this same command; launch separately.

CASEY'S SEQUENCE (2026-09-05): a cup forms naturally; a second cup at its base passes only small
objects; regurgitation makes waste accrete around the cup; one object ejects waste OPPOSITE the
intake and lives longer; there is a thermodynamic advantage to the streamlined form; later a
chemical chamber transforms ingested matter and allows growth or a replica.

WHAT IS NEW HERE FOR THE MODEL: **WASTE**. My ladder had none. The binding early constraint is not
energy but FOULING -- a one-aperture cup re-ingests its own output.

MODEL. Feeding rate i, food value p per unit, waste cost c per unit re-ingested.
One aperture: a fraction r(i) = i/(i+K) of expelled waste is re-ingested (fouling saturates).
   NET_one(i) = i*p - c*i*r(i) = i*p - c*i^2/(i+K)
Two apertures: intake and efflux are spatially separated, r = 0.
   NET_two(i) = i*p

PREDICTIONS
 G1 QUALITATIVE DIFFERENCE, not just a factor. NET_one has an INTERIOR MAXIMUM in feeding rate
    when c > p, and declines beyond it; NET_two is monotone increasing. **A sac-gut organism has
    an optimal feeding rate; a tube-gut organism does not.** If NET_one is also monotone the
    fouling story adds nothing qualitative.
 G2 The two-aperture advantage GROWS without bound in i, so it is largest for the most active
    feeders and negligible for sluggish ones. Predicts the innovation matters at high throughput.
 G3 (STRUCTURAL, an interpretation not a measurement) The second aperture SPLITS ONE GENERATOR INTO
    TWO: with one hole, in and out are the same channel and no function distinguishes them; with
    two they are distinct. By Theorem 9 the shift group changes and a certificate appears.
    **The through-gut is the first body axis.** I flag this as interpretation.
 G4 GEOMETRIC SELECTION IS FREE. A size filter carries NO maintained record -- the aperture is the
    body's own shape -- so its b-cost in Test 18's ledger is zero and it can be adopted at ANY
    decay rate mu, including mu where Test 18 said the optimal eater is indiscriminate (mu > c ln2).
    **This repairs R5: free geometric selection precedes costly chemical selection.**
    Test: sweep mu; geometric selection adoptable everywhere, chemical only below the R5 threshold.
 G5 The chemical chamber is the first form whose return is CONSTRUCTION rather than capacity, so
    it can only be adopted after surplus exists -- last in the ladder, as Test 19 found for
    'structure'.

WHAT WOULD KILL IT
 G1 false (NET_one monotone) -> fouling produces no qualitative difference and the two-aperture
    advantage is merely quantitative.
 G4 false (geometric selection also costs record) -> R5's ordering stands unrepaired and the size
    filter is not free after all.
