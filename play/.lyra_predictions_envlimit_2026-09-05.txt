CASEY (2026-09-05): "You have a 'natural' limit because the 'assembly' must interface with the
environment and environments change."

This answers L4, which Test 19 left untested (the run hit my rung cap, not a natural stall).
The limit is EXTERNAL, not internal cost growth.

MECHANISM. If the environment shifts on timescale T_env, no form's useful lifetime exceeds T_env,
so the payback condition g > m + B/T becomes **g > m + B/T_env**. Build costs grow geometrically
(B_k = B0 * beta^k) while surplus grows at best linearly, so there is a computable last rung.
Second mechanism, from this morning: **an environmental shift is a GENERATOR ACTIVATION**, so by
Test 14's W3 the loss should be a STEP, not gradual.

PREDICTIONS
 E1 THERE IS A NATURAL STALL: equilibrium rung count is FINITE for finite T_env, with no rung cap
    doing the work. Test 19 hit my cap at 16; this must stall below it on its own.
 E2 **COMPLEXITY IS LOGARITHMIC IN ENVIRONMENTAL STABILITY: rungs ~ log_beta(T_env).**
    Point estimate: plotting rungs against log10(T_env) gives a straight line, and its slope
    scales as 1/log2(beta) when beta is varied. Each doubling of stability buys a CONSTANT number
    of extra rungs, not proportionally more.
 E3 THE BUILD COST BINDS, NOT MAINTENANCE. Varying the growth rate of B should move the stall;
    varying the growth rate of m should move it much less. If maintenance dominates instead, my
    reading of the mechanism is wrong.
 E4 T_env -> infinity recovers Test 19's unbounded ladder (a control: the stall must be caused by
    the environment and by nothing else in the code).
 E5 THE TIME SERIES IS SAWTOOTH: slow climb, sudden drop, consistent with W3's step-function loss.
    If complexity decays smoothly after a shift, the generator-activation reading is wrong.

WHAT WOULD KILL IT
 E2 false (rungs linear or saturating in T_env rather than logarithmic) -> the geometric-cost
    mechanism is not what sets the ceiling.
 E4 false (stall persists at infinite T_env) -> something in my code caps the ladder and the whole
    test is an artifact.
