DEFINITION (frozen): initial conditions (x0,y0) on a uniform G x G grid of cell centres in
[0,2pi)^2. Iterate the Chirikov map x' = x + y', y' = y + K sin x with y UNWRAPPED (no mod 2pi

i.e. its orbit never crosses one full period of y within T steps. The measure m(K,T) is the
fraction of initial conditions that are non-mixing. This is an R(T)-type quantity: it depends on
the horizon T, which is stated, not hidden.
