THE BRIDGE, and it is linear algebra rather than metaphor. A reaction network has a stoichiometric
matrix S (species x reactions). Standard CRNT:
  - reachable compositions from x0 = x0 + image(S)  -> a COSET of the reaction subgroup
  - conservation laws = LEFT NULL SPACE of S       -> functions constant along every reaction
That is EXACTLY Theorem 9: classes are cosets, the certificate is the conserved vector, and by
Theorem 8 the record is the CODIMENSION of the orbit.
