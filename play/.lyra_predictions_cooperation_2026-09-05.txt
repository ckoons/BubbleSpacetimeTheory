LYRA — TEST 15: DOES COOPERATION SUPPLY THE RATE HIERARCHY? Written BEFORE the run, 2026-09-05.
Absolute path; shasum in this same command; run launched separately.

CASEY'S HYPOTHESIS (2026-09-05): pre-biological environments cheaply supply the energy an organism
needs; semi-developed objects in an environment either enter symbiosis or combine; perhaps the
COOPERATION of objects is what gives rise to life.

WHY THIS IS TESTABLE HERE. Test 14 showed (W5) that protection requires a RATE HIERARCHY -- the
gate must be slower than what it gates -- and that a system cannot supply this to itself, because
free self-modification zeroes the record. A single object must somehow contain a slow coordinate.
TWO OBJECTS WITH DIFFERENT INTRINSIC RATES HAVE A HIERARCHY FOR FREE. If that is the whole story,
cooperation is not a refinement of life but a PRECONDITION for it.

SETUP. Two record systems A (n_A junctions, rate r_A = 1) and B (n_B junctions, rate r_B varied).
State = (D_A, D_B). GATING: a set of A's junctions is suppressed by eps whenever B is in a
designated half of its state space, and/or vice versa. Score = BIT-DECADES, the sum of R(T) over
decades of observation horizon.

PREDICTIONS
 C1 CONTROL. Uncoupled, BD(A+B) = BD(A) + BD(B) exactly. Record is additive over independent
    systems. If this fails the instrument is wrong.
 C2 ONE-WAY GATING (slow B gates fast A) is SUPERADDITIVE when r_B << r_A: BD exceeds the
    uncoupled sum. Point estimate: gain of 20-50% at rate ratio 1000.
 C3 THE HIERARCHY IS NECESSARY. At r_A = r_B the same gating gives NO gain, and I expect it to be
    a LOSS, by the Test 14 result that a too-fast protector is worse than none (5.00 vs 7.00
    bit-decades) because the gate's own coordinate is a record it destroys by flipping.
 C4 MUTUAL GATING (A gates B and B gates A) is WORSE than one-way when there is a hierarchy,
    because the fast partner's gating of the slow one undermines the slowness that made the slow
    one useful. I am genuinely unsure of this one and state it to be scored.
 C5 COOPERATION BUYS TIME, NOT IMMORTALITY. The gain appears in the MIDDLE of the horizon range
    and vanishes at large T: every arrangement reaches R = 0 eventually. If some arrangement holds
    record at ALL horizons, something is wrong with the instrument.

WHAT WOULD KILL THE HYPOTHESIS
 C2 false (no superadditivity from a slow partner) -> cooperation supplies no hierarchy advantage
    and Casey's route to life does not appear in this framework.
 C3 false (gain without a hierarchy) -> W5 does not generalise from the internal-gate case and the
    rate-hierarchy claim must be narrowed to Test 14's geometry.
