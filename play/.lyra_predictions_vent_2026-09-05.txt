LYRA — TEST 23: THE VENT EDGE. Written BEFORE the run, 2026-09-05.
Absolute path; shasum in this same command; launch separately.

CASEY (2026-09-05): at geothermal vents life starts AT THE EDGE; chemicals find saturation points
and locate preferentially; interfaces become complex. And: "this will show energy, some
specialisation from geometry or position, and interfaces that grow complexity."

MODEL. A spatial gradient: position x in [0,1], move rate r(x) = r_hot * (r_cold/r_hot)^x, so the
vent (x=0) is fast and the far field (x=1) is slow. An assembly occupies a SPAN of positions
[x0, x0+w]. Its record R(T) uses only moves with rate > 1/T; rates now vary across the span.
Scores: bit-decades, and the LIVING CONDITION from Section 21 -- R > 0 AND H_thermo > 0.

PREDICTIONS
 V1 THE HOT END IS DEAD BY MELTING: at x near 0 all moves are fast, everything is one class,
    R = 0, H_thermo maximal. A gas.
 V2 THE COLD END IS DEAD BY FREEZING: at x near 1 no moves are active at the observation horizon,
    every state is its own class, R maximal, H_thermo = 0. A crystal. **R is maximal exactly where
    the system is most dead**, which is the Section 2 caveat showing up spatially.
 V3 **BIT-DECADES ARE MAXIMISED AT AN INTERIOR POSITION** -- the edge -- not at either extreme.
    Point estimate: the maximum sits where the span straddles the rate that matches the
    observation horizon.
 V4 THE LIVING CONDITION (R>0 AND H_th>0) HOLDS ONLY ON AN INTERVAL, and that interval is the
    EDGE. Neither extreme satisfies both. This is the sharpest form of the claim and the one I
    most want to be true, so I will report the interval width honestly even if it is tiny.
 V5 SPECIALISATION FROM POSITION (Casey's phrase): a WIDER span does better than a narrow one at
    the same centre, because it straddles more of the gradient and so holds fast and slow moves at
    once. If narrow spans do just as well, position is not doing the work and V3/V4 are about the
    rate value alone, not about the gradient.

WHAT WOULD KILL IT
 V3 false (monotone in position) -> there is no edge advantage and the vent story does not appear.
 V4 false (living condition holds everywhere or nowhere) -> the two-sided death is not what
    localises life at the interface.
