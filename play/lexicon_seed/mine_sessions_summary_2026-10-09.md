## 5. Suggested seed format (from the episodes)

Each episode above yields: `alias` (Casey's word, with his spelling variants: Shilove, Plank, ruller, ibserver, comityment), `readings` (the CI's candidate technical readings), `resolved_to` (Casey's gloss, verbatim where he gave one), `anchors` (K/T/F numbers, session id + timestamp), `status` (resolved / amended / open), `recurrences` (dates re-asked).
