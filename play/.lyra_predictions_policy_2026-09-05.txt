LYRA — TEST 5 predictions. THE POLICY CONTROL. Written 2026-09-05 BEFORE the run.
Registration verified by shasum immediately below, before launch (the D-test lesson).

WHY: the 0.50-0.65 exchange rate is my best number and my only "two instances" claim.
BOTH instances used the SAME greedy best-split refusal policy. Friday's own result was that
the break-even is a property of the SEARCH POLICY, not the instance. So the two instances may
be one policy measured twice. This test can kill the headline.

DESIGN: Arm A (pure selection, no growth) on the 4x6 torus, both move sets, refusal policy varied:
  GREEDY   best split among 120 sampled pairs        [what both instances used]
  EXHAUST  best split among ALL pairs
  RANDOM   first admissible pair, no selection at all
  WORST    the admissible pair that gains the LEAST record
  ANTI     the admissible pair that maximises states killed (a "wasteful" policy)

PREDICTIONS
 P1 The band MOVES with policy. RANDOM and WORST come in BELOW 0.50; ANTI lowest.
 P2 EXHAUST comes in AT or ABOVE greedy (more search, better conversion) -- if EXHAUST is
    much higher than 0.65, then 0.50-0.65 is a property of my SAMPLE SIZE of 120, which would
    be the most embarrassing possible cause.
 P3 THE ONE THAT MATTERS: if RANDOM also lands in 0.50-0.65, the rate is policy-INDEPENDENT
    and is a real property of the record space. I do NOT expect this. Point estimate for
    RANDOM: 0.30-0.45.
 P4 The FREEZING result (H_thermo hits 0 exactly at the R peak) is policy-INDEPENDENT --
    that one follows from T7 and should hold for every policy.

WHAT WOULD KILL THE HEADLINE
 P1 confirmed -> "0.50-0.65 reproduced in two record spaces" is WITHDRAWN and replaced by
 "the greedy policy converts at 0.50-0.65 in two record spaces", which is a much smaller claim.
WHAT WOULD PROMOTE IT
 P3 reversed (random lands in band) -> the rate is a property of the space, not the searcher,
 and it becomes the most interesting number in the program.
