                                                                                Abstract

                                        We discuss the size of isospin violating effects of both electromagnetic (e 6= 0) and
                                        strong (mu 6= md ) origin in P`2(γ) decays. Our results are relevant for the extraction of
                                        |Vus /Vud | from the measured ratio of the Kµ2(γ) and πµ2(γ) decay widths combined with
                                        FK /Fπ obtained in lattice calculations in the isospin limit. We point out that strong
                                        isospin-breaking corrections, neglected in previous studies, enter at the same level as
                                        the electromagnetic effects. The updated value for the ratio of CKM elements reads
                                        |Vus /Vud | = 0.2316(12).

1       Introduction
The purely leptonic decays of light pseudoscalar mesons P → `ν` (P ± = π ± , K ± ) are
theoretically extremely clean. Neglecting electroweak corrections, the hadronic physics is
encoded in one single parameter, the meson decay constant FP . Using chiral perturbation
theory and lattice QCD, P → `ν` decay rates can be predicted with high accuracy and
provide non-trivial tests of the standard model imposing stringent constraints on new
physics.
    With the advent of high precision calculations of the ratio of decay constants FK /Fπ in
lattice QCD, it was realized [1] that the combination of FK /Fπ and the ratio of measured
decay widths ΓKµ2(γ) /Γπµ2(γ) leads to a clean and competitive determination of the ratio
|Vus /Vud | of Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa (CKM) [2, 3] matrix elements. Implementing
the programme proposed in Ref. [1] requires theoretical understanding of: (i) the long-
distance electromagnetic (EM) corrections to P → `ν` ; (ii) the strong isospin breaking
correction that connects FK /Fπ (calculated within lattice QCD in the isospin limit mu =
md ) to FK ± /Fπ± that enters the ratio of Kµ2(γ) to πµ2(γ) decay widths.
    The EM corrections have been known for some time [4, 5, 6, 7]. The analysis of Ref. [1]
relied on the model-dependent treatment of Ref. [7] for the long-distance contribution
to these corrections. The long-distance EM corrections to P → `ν` (γ) have also been
analyzed in the model-independent framework of Chiral Perturbation Theory (ChPT) [8,
9, 10]. It was noted in Ref. [8] that the radiative corrections to the ratio ΓKµ2(γ) /Γπµ2(γ)
are uniquely determined to lowest non-trivial order in the chiral expansion and the final
numerical results have been reported in Refs. [11, 12]. So far, strong isospin breaking
corrections ∝ (mu − md ) have been ignored in the extraction of |Vus /Vud |. In view of the
increasing precision of lattice results on FK /Fπ , also these effects should be taken into
account and it is the purpose of this letter to fill this gap.
    In Section 2 we perform our analysis of both electromagnetic and strong isospin viola-
tion to ΓP`2(γ) in the unified framework of ChPT. In Section 3 we update the phenomeno-
logical determination of |Vus /Vud | and the global fit to |Vud | and |Vus | [12] to reflect the
new strong isospin breaking correction. We present our conclusions in Section 4.

4     Conclusions
In this letter we have discussed in detail the effect of electromagnetic and strong isospin
breaking relevant for the extraction of |Vus /Vud | from the combination of the measured
ΓKµ2(γ) /Γπµ2(γ) and the ratio of decay constants FK /Fπ calculated in lattice QCD in
the isospin limit. We have performed our analysis in the unified framework of ChPT,
providing analytic expressions and numerical estimates for the EM and strong isospin
breaking corrections δEM and δSU(2) that appear in the ratio ΓKµ2(γ) /Γπµ2(γ) (see Eq. (2.12)).
    The main new result of our work is a compact analytic expression for the correction
due to strong isospin breaking (see Eq. (2.21)). This effect, neglected in all previous
extractions of |Vus /Vud |, is comparable in size to the electromagnetic correction:

                       δEM = −0.0069(17) ,        δSU(2) = −0.0043(12) .                   (4.1)

Using the current experimental results for the decay widths and FK /Fπ = 1.193(6) [26],
we obtain |Vus /Vud | = 0.2316(12), which is about 0.2% above previous determinations
that ignored δSU(2) .
