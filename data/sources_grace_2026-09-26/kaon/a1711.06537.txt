                                                           The leading-order electromagnetic and strong isospin-breaking corrections to the ratio of Kµ2 and
                                                        πµ2 decay rates are evaluated for the first time on the lattice, following a method recently proposed.
                                                        The lattice results are obtained using the gauge ensembles produced by the European Twisted Mass
                                                        Collaboration with Nf = 2 + 1 + 1 dynamical quarks. Systematics effects are evaluated and the
                                                        impact of the quenched QED approximation is estimated. Our result for the correction to the tree-
                                                        level Kµ2 /πµ2 decay ratio is −1.22 (16)% to be compared to the estimate −1.12 (21)% based on
                                                        Chiral Perturbation Theory and adopted by the Particle Data Group.
