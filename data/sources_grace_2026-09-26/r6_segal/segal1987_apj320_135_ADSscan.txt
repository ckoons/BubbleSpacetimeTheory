                                          CORRECTION OF A STUDY OF SOURCE COUNTS IN THE CHRONOMETRIC COSMOLOGY
                                                                                                                     I. E. Segal

                                                                                             ABSTRACT
                                           A recent study of source counts in the chronométrie cosmology (Wright 1987) is flawed in several respects.
                                        It is in error (1) mathematically (2) logically, (3) statistically, and (4) in its representation of uncertain and
                                        model-dependent data as statistically cogent. Specifically, it states that in the chronométrie cosmology “a
                                        source can appear bright by being close to the antipode where z = oo,” whereas in fact its brightness must
                                        vanish at this point. (2) The study ignores the statistics of the flux-redshift relation that is fundamental for
                                        testing theories as to the nature of the redshift and is based on the logically inconclusive question of the
                                        spatial distribution of sources. (3) It indicates levels of significance derived by a posteriori reasoning, involving
                                        moreover unrealistic assumptions. (4) It ignores the smallness of the excess in the slope of the log Aí-log S
                                        over the Euclidean value, and its only marginal statistical significance, found by Kellermann (1972) and con-
                                        firmed by Kellermann and Wall (1987), in radio source counts, and cites preferentially data that have been
                                        extensively corrected and show a large increase, exaggerating their statistical reliability. These and other flaws
                                        are detailed.
                                        Subject headings: cosmology—radio sources: general
