2.B. The Lie groups.

Let us now turn to the universal covering group G of G. It is
an infinite sheeted covering and is given by a standard construction
(cp. text books, e.g. [9]): G consists of equivalence classes of
directed paths on G starting at the identity. Two paths are equiva-

one into the other. By the group action in G a path may be transported
such that it starts at any given point. Using this, group multipli-

cation in G may be defined by juxtaposition of paths.
