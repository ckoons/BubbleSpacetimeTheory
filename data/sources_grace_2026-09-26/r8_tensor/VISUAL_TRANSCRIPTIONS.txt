       of the holomorphic discrete series of SU(2,2) with its conjugate."
OZ1b: "For a general semisimple Lie group G of Hermitian type a similar problem is studied in [Re2]. It is
       shown that the tensor product π_ν ⊗ π̄_ν of a scalar holomorphic discrete series π_ν with its
       conjugate π̄_ν is unitarily equivalent to the L²-space on the corresponding Hermitian symmetric
       space, L²(G/K). Therefore we know its decomposition from the known theory of Harish-Chandra; namely
            π_ν ⊗ π̄_ν ≅ L²(G/K) ≅ ∫^⊕_{𝔞*/W} H(λ)|C(λ)|⁻² dλ
       for ν > p − 1 where p is an integer, also called genus of G/K."
OZ1c: "In this paper we take G = SU(2,2) with genus p = 4. The scalar discrete series π_ν has an analytic
       continuation, namely for ν ∈ {0,1} ∪ (1,∞) we still get a unitary representation of SU(2,2);
       this set of ν is called the Wallach set."
       [[Re2] = Repka, Can. J. Math. 31 (1979) 836-844, per reference list, text line 1094]
