                                                             Astrophysical constraints on unparticle-inspired models of gravity

                                                              We use stellar dynamics arguments to constrain the relevant parameters of ungravity inspired
                                                            models. We show that resulting bounds do constrain the parameters of the theory of unparticles,
                                                            as far as its energy scale satisfies the condition ΛU ≥ 1 T eV and dU is close to unity.
