VT-1  arxiv_2409.16555.pdf page 6 (png/arxiv_2409.16555_p-06.png); text lines arxiv_2409.16555.txt:257-277
  Bai–Hunziker, "A characterization of unitarity of some highest weight Harish-Chandra modules".
  "In [BH15], we have found a uniform expression for the GK dimensions and associated varieties
   of unitary highest weight Harish-Chandra modules.
   Proposition 3.2 ([BH15]). Suppose L(λ) is a unitary highest weight Harish-Chandra module with
   highest weight λ. We denote z = z(λ) = (λ + ρ, β∨), then
       GKdim L(λ) = r z_{r−1},   if z < z_{r−1}
                    k z_{k−1},   if z = z_k = (ρ, β∨) − kc, 1 ≤ k ≤ r − 1
                    0,           if z = z_0 = (ρ, β∨).
   Denote k = k(λ) := −(λ, β∨)/c. Then
   (1) If k > r − 1, we have GKdim L(λ) = r z_{r−1} = ½ dim(G/K).
   (2) If 0 ≤ k ≤ r − 1, then k is a non-negative integer and
       GKdim L(λ) = k((ρ, β∨) − (k − 1)c) = k z_{k−1} = dim \overline{O_{k(λ)}}.
   The associated variety of L(λ) is \overline{O_{k(λ)}}."
