                                                 We discuss kernels on complact classical groups G = U(n), O(2n), Sp(n) defined by
                                             the formula K(z, u) = | det(1 − zu∗ )|s . We obtain the explicit Plancherel formula for
                                             these kernels and the interval of positive-definiteness. We also obtain explicit models
                                             for Sahi’s ’unipotent’ representations.
                                                 In [15], the author proposed a class of natural kernels on pseudo-Riemannian
                                             symmetric spaces G/H and conjectured that these kernels admit explicit Plancherel
                                             formula. The purpose of these notes is to verify that at least in the case then
                                             G/H is a compact group G×G/G ∼ U(n), O(2n), Sp(n) this Plancherel formula
                                             can be written.1
