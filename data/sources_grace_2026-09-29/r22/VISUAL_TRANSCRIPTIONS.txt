=== ZKO 1974, Sov. Phys. JETP 40, 1 (file zko_jetp_e_040_01_0001.pdf) ===

[Z-V1] png/zko_p-1.png, Sec. 2, model Lagrangian (bottom left):
  L = (1/2)(d phi)^2 - lambda^2 (phi^2 - eta^2)^2 + psi-bar( i d-slash - m - i g gamma_5 phi ) psi
  "Here phi is a pseudoscalar field, psi is the baryon field and lambda and eta are real parameters."
  "<phi> = sigma eta, where sigma = +-1" ; m_chi = 2 sqrt(2) lambda eta.
  NOTE convention: ZKO's quartic is lambda^2 (not lambda/4). ZKO's "sigma" is the vacuum sign, NOT the wall tension; ZKO call the tension mu.

[Z-V2] png/zko_p-2.png, Sec. 3 "PROPERTIES OF THE DOMAIN WALLS":
  "the thickness of a boundary layer at rest (we shall call this layer a wall) is Delta ~ 1/m_chi ~ 1/(lambda eta), and its surface density is mu ~ lambda eta^3 [footnote 2]."
  kink: phi = eta th( sqrt(2) lambda eta x )
  mu = Integral [ (1/2)(grad phi)^2 + lambda^2 (phi^2 - eta^2)^2 ] dx
  mu = sqrt(2) lambda eta^3 Integral_{-inf}^{+inf} (ch xi)^{-4} d xi = (4 sqrt(2)/3) lambda eta^3
  "The quantity mu could be called surface tension."

[Z-V3] png/zko_p-3.png, Sec. 5 (right column):
  "the potential Phi has a discontinuity in its derivative equal to G mu, so that the change of potential within a cell of size L is of the order delta Phi = G mu L."
  "(delta T/T < 10^-3). The gravitational redshift should yield delta T/T ~ alpha delta Phi."  "In general alpha < 1, but probably alpha > 0.05"
  "Thus it is necessary that G mu L << 1."
  "Substituting epsilon = mu/L = 1/(G t^2), L = G t^2, we arrive at the conclusion that t_2 < 1/(mu G) = t_1."
  "...necessary that the superficial density of walls be mu < 0.1 g/cm^2, which seems unrealistic from the viewpoint of the theory of elementary particles, since it corresponds to lambda >~ 10^3 [sic; rendered '\lambda \gtrsim 10^3'] for m_chi ~ m_p"
  Left column: "T = m, L = m^-1, epsilon = T^4 = m^4, t_0 = 1/(m^2 sqrt(G)) = t_n^2/t_G" ; "t_1 = 1/(G m^3) = t_n^3/t_G^2 = t_0 t_n/t_G ~ 10^14 sec."
