Physical Properties of Forsterite

Cleavage: {001} Good, {010} Distinct
Color: Colorless, Green, Yellow, Yellow green, White.
Density: 3.21 - 3.33, Average = 3.27
Diaphaneity: Transparent
Fracture: Conchoidal - Fractures developed in brittle materials characterized by smoothly curving surfaces, (e.g. quartz).
Habit: Crystalline - Fine - Occurs as well-formed fine sized crystals.
Habit: Tabular - Form dimensions are thin in one direction.
Hardness: 6-7 - Orthoclase-Quartz
Luminescence: Non-fluorescent.
Luster: Vitreous (Glassy)
Magnetism: Nonmagnetic
Streak: white
