                                                             We present a precision measurement of the axial-vector coupling constant gA in the decay of
                                                           polarized free neutrons. For the first time, a pulsed cold neutron beam was used for this purpose.
                                                           By this method, leading sources of systematic uncertainty are suppressed. From the electron spectra
                                                           we obtain λ = gA /gV = −1.27641(45)stat (33)sys which confirms recent measurements with improved
                                                           precision. This corresponds to a value of the parity violating beta asymmetry parameter of A0 =
                                                           −0.11985(17)stat (12)sys . We discuss implications on the CKM matrix element Vud and derive a limit
