                                                           Neutrinoless double beta decay (0νββ) is a yet unobserved nuclear process which would demon-
                                                        strate Lepton Number violation, a clear evidence of beyond Standard Model physics. The process
                                                        two neutrino double beta decay (2νββ) is allowed by the Standard Model and has been measured
                                                        in numerous experiments. In this letter, we report a measurement of 2νββ decay half-life of 100 Mo
                                                        to the ground state of 100 Ru of (7.07 ± 0.02 (stat.) ± 0.11 (syst.)) × 1018 yr by the CUPID-Mo
                                                        experiment. With a relative precision of ± 1.6 % this is the most precise measurement to date of a
                                                        2νββ decay rate in 100 Mo. In addition, we constrain higher-order corrections to the spectral shape
                                                        which provides complementary nuclear structure information. We report a novel measurement of
                                                        the shape factor ξ3,1 = 0.45 ±0.03 (stat.) ±0.05 (syst.), which is compared to theoretical predictions
