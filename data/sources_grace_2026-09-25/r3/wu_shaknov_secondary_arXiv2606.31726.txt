                                                                                      Bell state?
                                                                                                 Paul Joos1 and Peter Kling1

                                                             Electron and positron can form a meta-stable bound state called positronium that decays via pair annihilation.
                                                          We show how polarization-dependent Compton scattering can be used to verify that the two annihilation photons
                                                          in the spin-zero case (para-positronium) are emitted in a maximally entangled Bell state. Our theoretical approach
                                                          based on two-photon density matrices connects concepts from relativistic quantum electrodynamics and quantum
                                                          information theory.
