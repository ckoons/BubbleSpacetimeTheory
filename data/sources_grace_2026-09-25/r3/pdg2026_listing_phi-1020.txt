                      PARAMETER β IN φ → P e + e − DECAYS
          In the one-pole approximation the electromagnetic transition form factor
          for φ → P e + e − (P = π,η) is given as a function of the e + e − invariant
          mass squared, q 2 , by the expression:
                                F(q 2 ) 2 = (1 − q 2 /Λ2 )−2 ,
          where vector meson dominance predicts parameter Λ ≈ 0.770 GeV (Λ−2 ≈
          1.687 GeV−2 ). The slope of this form factor, β = dF/dq 2 (q 2 =0), equals
          Λ−2 in this approximation.
