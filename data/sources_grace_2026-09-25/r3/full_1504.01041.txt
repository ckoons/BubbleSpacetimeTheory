                                                     A search for dinucleon decay into pions with the Super-Kamiokande detector has been performed
                                                   with an exposure of 282.1 kiloton-years. Dinucleon decay is a process that violates baryon number
                                                                                                                                     2

              by two units. We present the first search for dinucleon decay to pions in a large water Cherenkov
              detector. The modes 16 O(pp) → 14 Cπ + π + , 16 O(pn) → 14 Nπ + π 0 , and 16 O(nn) → 14 Oπ 0 π 0 are
              investigated. No significant excess in the Super-Kamiokande data has been found, so a lower limit
              on the lifetime of the process per oxygen nucleus is determined. These limits are: τpp→π+ π+ >
              7.22 × 1031 years, τpn→π+ π0 > 1.70 × 1032 years, and τnn→π0 π0 > 4.04 × 1032 years. The lower
              limits on each mode are about two orders of magnitude better than previous limits from searches
              for dinucleon decay in iron.
