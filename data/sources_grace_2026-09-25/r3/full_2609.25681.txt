                We report a new partial lifetime limit of 4.2 × 1032 years for the trinucleon decay mode
              16
                O(ppp) →13 Cπ + π + e+ , obtained from a search conducted using the Super-Kamiokande detector
              with 0.401 megaton-years of exposure across five operational periods (SK-I: 1996–2001, SK-II: 2002–
              2005, SK-III: 2006–2008, SK-IV: 2008–2018, SK-V: 2019–2020). This represents an improvement
              of six orders of magnitude over previous experimental constraints. The analysis utilizes a convo-
              lutional neural network (CNN) incorporating an attention mechanism—a computational technique
                                                                                                                              3

              that enables the model to focus on the most relevant regions of Cherenkov ring patterns—to enhance
              event classification, thereby improving the sensitivity of the search. This is the first application of a
              CNN to a nucleon decay search in Super-Kamiokande. Furthermore, the large dataset available in
              Super-Kamiokande (hereafter ”SK”) strengthens the statistical power of the study, enabling a more
              stringent constraint than those set by prior experiments.
