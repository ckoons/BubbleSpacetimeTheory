                                                         We have searched for proton decay via p → νK + using Super-Kamiokande data from April 1996 to February
                                                       2013, 260 kiloton·year exposure in total. No evidence for this proton decay mode is found. A lower limit of the
                                                       proton lifetime is set to τ/B(p → νK + ) > 5.9 × 1033 years at 90% confidence level.
